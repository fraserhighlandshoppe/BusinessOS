# Headline & Copy Formulas

The 7 master "Power Headline" formulas (Eben Pagan, Traffic Intensive), plus supporting
template patterns from the wiki and swipe sources. Every headline you write should map to
one of these. Fill the [slots] from the swipe_*.txt word files.

## The 7 Power Headline Formulas

### 1. Result (Benefit) Focus
`How To Get [RESULT] [QUICKLY] Without [RISK/OBJECTION]`
> "How To Get Out Of Debt In 90 Days Or Less With This Simple, Guaranteed System"

### 2. Pain Focus
`How To [ELIMINATE SPECIFIC PAIN] Without [MORE PAIN]`
> "How To Lose 33 Pounds Of Fat Without Torture Diets Or Hard Exercise"

### 3. Situation Focus (fear)
`If [EMOTIONAL HOT-BUTTON SITUATION], Then [WORST FEAR MIGHT BE TRUE]`
> "If You Experience Lower Back Pain While Sitting, Then You May Need Surgery"

### 4. Action Focus
`What To Do If [SPECIFIC HOT-BUTTON SITUATION]`
> "What To Do If You Notice Your Husband Looking At Other Women"

### 5. Customer Focus (mistakes)
`[NUMBER] Mistakes Most [CUSTOMER DESCRIPTION] Make [IN SITUATION] - And How To Avoid Them`
> "10 Mistakes Most Men Make When Approaching Women - And How To Avoid Them"

### 6. Approach Focus (myth-busting)
`Why [COMMON-SENSE APPROACH] Doesn't Work... And What To Do About It`
> "Why Diets Don't Work... And What To Do About It"

### 7. Magic (problem → benefit)
`How To [TURN PROBLEM] Into [BENEFIT]`
> "How To Use Your Credit Cards To Make Money"
> "How To Lose Weight By Eating MORE Food"

## Supporting Patterns (from wiki + swipe sources)

### Crafting-the-Headline opener
`In the next (TIMEFRAME), I'm going to share with you (BENEFIT, BENEFIT and BENEFIT) that will help you (DESIRED OUTCOME)`

### Question / Are-you
`Are You [DOING X] to [GET BENEFIT]?` — "Are You Looking for a Way to Save Over $10,000 off the Price of a New Car?"

### Secrets of
`Secrets of [GROUP/OUTCOME] That [AUTHORITY] Won't Tell You`

### Cosmo-style curiosity patterns
- `[NUMBER] Tricks/Times/Things For When You Want To [DESIRE]`
- `The Surprising [NOUN] That [UNEXPECTED RESULT]`
- `[NUMBER] Instant [OUTCOME] Boosters`
- `[NUMBER] Things All [GROUP] Keep Private (The First One's A Real Doozy)`
- `What's Your [QUIZ CATEGORY]?`
- `[NUMBER] Outrageous Facts About [SUBJECT]`

### Email subject-line types (DigitalMarketer)
Self-interest | Curiosity | Offer | Urgency | Humanity | News | Social Proof

### Headline quality rules (Crafting the Headline)
- Promise; clear not cute; compelling not wordy (10-12 words); no hype
- Purpose: pick out people you can interest (Hopkins, Scientific Advertising)
- ~6 words = highest click rate; 20 chars for email subjects, 30+ for blog
- Readers see first 3 and last 3 words when skimming
- Include ≥1 power word; emotion words 10-15%; uncommon words give substance
- Test 2-5 variations; start with "How To" — it wins ~70% of tests
