Use Case Thinking
When planning or creating a product, service, or a new category, work to get your
feeling/thinking/experiencing process as close as possible to a customer's process. We do this by
using an approach that I call "Use Case Thinking" where you put yourself in your prospect's
shoes, and talk as if you WERE the customer experiencing, feeling, thinking and communicating.
