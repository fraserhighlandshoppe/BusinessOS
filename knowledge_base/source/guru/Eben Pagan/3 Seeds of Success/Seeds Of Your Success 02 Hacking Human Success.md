Day 2:
Hacking Human Success
In this session you'll learn how the modern world controls our
mind, how to get back control by "hacking" our own brain, why
Pre-Acting is one of the most powerful things you can do to create
your own Success, and much more...
We have a highly eﬀective, hack-resistant system called the Human Mind that's
always running. It's easy to hack the human mind from the outside, as we see
governments and cultures and businesses doing it all the time, but it seems very
diﬃcult to hack from the inside. We're not designed to do it - that's why it's so hard.
We humans are built for a time and environment that doesn't exist any more.
a. We're looking for the next meal and to not become the next meal.
b. We're looking for status.
c. We're looking to reproduce.

This modern virtual world that we live in is too much. There are too many demands
on us. The current environment is designed to push our immediate gratiﬁcation
buttons... it "hacks" into us with attention grabbing, pleasure giving devices and
media. It's all tuned to our ancient animal minds and is designed to control our
attention, emotions and behavior.
1. We're over stimulated and over-distracted. We can't compete.

The metaphor we'll use for taking back control of our mind is computer hacking.
A computer hacker studies a system carefully to learn how to get inside. Once
inside, it's easy to control the system. We must hack our own minds.
The philosophy of the Samurai Warrior is "Win Beforehand."
If you wait until the battle to make your plan, you're going to lose. Instead, use the
power of your mind to "go upstream."
The world doesn't really work simply in terms of "this caused that." We're not a
victim, and we're not a cause, but we are eﬀected causes, and caused eﬀects.
Everything we cause in the world comes back to eﬀect us, and everything eﬀected
in the world comes back to cause us.

How you think eﬀects how you feel, and how you feel eﬀects how you behave, and
how you behave aﬀects how you think and how you feel.
We can't see the eﬀects because most of them are too slow to see in real time.
Health happens too slowly to see it. Plaque in our arteries happens too slowly to
see it. The moon moves too slowly to see it move. We have to use other ways of
examination and thinking to get in touch with these slow motion eﬀects.
Pre-Acting: doing the right thing ahead of time, and setting it up so you always do
the right thing, before you're called to do anything.
a. It's counterintuitive to eat before you're hungry, sleep before you're tired,
and address relationship issues before they come up.
b. If we wait until we're hungry to eat, we'll eat what we shouldn't.
Success is an Emergent. Some examples of Emergents:
a. An ant colony, cells becoming tissue, tissues becoming an organ, organs and
cells becoming a person.
To create the Emergent called Success, you have to ﬁrst master the components, so
you can then put them all together, in the right order and in the right way, so then
Success emerges.
Where are you already Pre-Acting in your life? Where have you Pre-Acted before?
