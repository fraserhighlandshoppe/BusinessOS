Day 16:
Visioning & Focusing
In this session you'll learn the danger of having too much choice,
how to create visions that motivate and excite you, why visions
have the power to make what you want Emerge, and much more...
Confucius - the person who chases two rabbits, catches none.
We humans love options and choice, but our mind becomes ineﬃcient and
confused when there are too many choices.
The Paradox of Choice:
1. Humans are wired to be attracted to choice, just for the sake of choice.
2. But we're now experiencing TOO MANY choices.
3. Bread: before we had no choice of bread. Now we can go into a grocery store

and choose from dozens.
4. Books: before there were very few books. Now we can go into a book store

and choose from thousands.
The intuitive thing is to keep our options open. The counterintuitive thing is to
choose one thing, and then focus on it. To cut oﬀ possibilities, so you only have
one.
All decisions, especially the important ones, are made with too little information. If
there was enough data, you wouldn't need to "decide." The course of action would
be obvious.
We have to draw the line in the sand, create a boundary, and move forward. This
can be stressful and challenging.
There's two ways to approach decisions (and two diﬀerent personality types):
1. The Satisﬁer: someone who ﬁgures out what elements are most important

to them, ﬁnds something that has those elements, and purchases it.
2. The Maximizer: someone who gets an idea that they want something,

compares options, and then works to maximize their purchase.
The Satisﬁcer isn't looking for the best deal, but they wind up being more satisﬁed
in life.

The Maximizer gets the best deal, but they aren't as satisﬁed, because they haven't
found the perfect option, and after they purchase they ﬁnd an even better option.
The Satisﬁer is the way to go. First, ﬁnd out what's important to you. Then ﬁnd the
thing that has what's important to you and enjoy it.
You also save the time and stress of comparing every single product.
Occasionally Maximizing does make sense: hiring someone, choosing a name for
your business, choosing a life partner. These elements are going to be with you for
a LONG time.
A vision is a picture you make in your mind.
If you can't create visions for the future, you'll be living in the visions that others
create.
Take the time to intentionally create compelling visions of the future - ones that
trigger emotions and motivation, and that are speciﬁc.
As you're creating visions, look for particular pictures that are more compelling
than the others... ones that motivate and excite you.
The human mind is a Google for visions. If you go to Google and put in a topic, you
engage the world's largest private computer network, created by the greatest
computer scientists that have ever lived, etc.
1. Our mind is like Google - but even MORE powerful. If you put visions into

your mind, your mind goes to work to create them.
The clearer you get on your vision, the more your mind takes everything
happening in your life, circumstances, resources, situations, connections with other
people, and it starts organizing them to your vision begin to Emerge from them.
Your mind is very good at putting components together in a way that an Emergent
happens.
"Opportunities multiply as they are seized." Then the game of success becomes
about pairing down opportunities and only focus on the few opportunities that are
the greatest.
