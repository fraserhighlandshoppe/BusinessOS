Day 6:
Your Foundation Of
Physical Health
Your physical health is the foundation for your emotions and your
higher thinking.
In this session you'll learn why we like sweet, salty, and fatty
foods... the 3 "time frames" of food and how to leverage them for
your health... the recipe for my own green protein shake... and
much more.
A healthy body supports positive emotions.
2/3's of our body is water. Drink plenty of water, and make sure it's as pure as
possible. Drink a big glass (ideally a half liter) every 2 1/2 hours throughout your
day.
"Eat for energy, not for entertainment."
We evolved to like sweet, salty, and fatty tastes, because historically they've been
found inside of foods. We didn't have the ability to extract those tastes from the
food they originally were part of. Until now. That's the problem.
Don't wait until you're hungry to choose your food. By then our mind is taken over
for the desire for sweet, salty, and fatty foods.
The 3 Time Frames of Food:
1. Right Now: how it tastes.
2. The Medium Term: Over the next 2-4 hours, how much energy do we get

from the food?
3. The Long Term: adding or removing fat stores, improving or repressing our

immune system, encouraging or discouraging degenerative diseases like
cancer.
Most people are biased to the Right Now food time frame... how it tastes. Instead,
Pre-Act and optimize our eating for the medium and long term.

Rate each meal in each of the three Time Frames.
1. Shoot for a 10 in the Medium and Long Terms. Consider accepting a 5, 6, or 7

in the Right Now.
Start noticing 30-60 minutes after a meal how you feel.
1. Most people are not very eﬀective after a meal, because they eat the wrong

things.
Instead of eating 2 or 3 meals a day, break foods into smaller, pre-planned meals,
one every 3 hours or so, with water 30 minutes before each meal.
I make green protein shakes with several high nutrient green vegetables, brown
rice protein powder, avocado, ﬂax seed oil, natural sweetener, and some
supplements.
1. I eat the shake ﬁrst thing in the morning, and again for my third meal.
2. I essentially no longer get sick.
3. High nutrient vegetables include baby bok choy, collard greens, kale, chard,

broccoli, and sprouts.
Get as much raw, organic, and green in your meals as you can.
My second meal every day is oat bran and oat meal, Saigon cinnamon, almond
butter, ﬂax oil, raw natural vanilla, and almond milk.
Ground ﬂax goes bad very quickly.
Cheat, and enjoy it.
1. I cheat 2 or 3 times per week. It's okay. When I do cheat, because it's not all

the time, the food tastes even BETTER than normal.
2. Another option is what Tim Ferris recommends: cheating for an entire day

once a week.
Avoid fruit, fruit juice, processed foods, sugar, etc.
Create a standardized meal system. You're eating the same foods right now over
and over anyway, so create a system designed for your health.
