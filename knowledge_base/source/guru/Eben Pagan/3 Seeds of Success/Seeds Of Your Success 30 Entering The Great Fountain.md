Day 30:
Entering The Great Fountain
In this session you'll learn what the Great Fountain of Leadership
is, why everyone needs you to develop your talent and become
massively successful, how ﬁnding your own unique purpose in life
beneﬁts the entire planet, and much more...
I this session I talk from the perspective of all people on the planet, and everyone
who has ever lived.
We need you to ﬁnd your talent, develop it into a strength, and ultimately into
mastery. Because that's when you can go out and create massive value for the
world... and become a resource for all of us.
1. Whatever you need to do to ﬁnd your talent and development, we need you

to do it. Read the books. Find the mentors. Stay on the path. You're the one
that'll be able to do this the best.
We need you to become successful. We need you to take your strength, create
massive value that turns into resources and money that you invest in assets... so
that you become successful. We need you to become successful because, just as
Maslow said we have particular needs that emerge in a particular order, until we
have our lower needs met (until we're successful), our higher needs can't emerge. If
we're too busy working about making the next buck, we can't focus on making
massive contribution. Get successful as fast as you can, so you can get to the level
where you can contribute.
1. When I was young, I got the message that rich people exploit others. Later, I

did meet some people like this, but I've met many people who have gone
past this selﬁsh level and have begun contributing in a BIG way. The two
richest people in the world right now are Bill Gates and Warren Buﬀet.
They're most concerned right now with giving all their money back to the
world to help people. They've also been going around asking billionaires to
contribute half of their money to the world.
We need you to have your "We Moment." At some point of success, you'll realize
that more success won't make you any happier, that there's more you can do for
others with your talents. You also realize that you're the same as everyone else. We
have the same ancestors, the same emotions, the same challenges. You become
part of the group of humanity.

We need you to have your "Purpose Moment." As you create massive value in the
world, you'll see something that is wrong, a big problem, an injustice. You may be
the only person who can see it in exactly the way you see it. You'll realize that your
talent, connections, etc., are exactly what can solve that problem. And you realize
maybe you can have a Purpose in this life, and solving this huge problem is it. You
realize there's something more important than you that's going on, and that you
can make a diﬀerence.
1. When you do, you'll get a sense of calling and energy that you won’t ﬁnd

anywhere else.
Finally, we need you to enter the Great Fountain of Leadership. It's where we all can
go if we choose to accept the challenge. Something magical is going on in the
fountain, but most of us are scared to go in. A few of us allow ourselves to be pulled
into the fountain, facing our fear with courage. As you get drawn in, you realize
there's many more people above you, and you feel the weight, and you realize
you're supporting their weight and you're pushing them up. It's challenging and it
feels unfair. But as you stay in, you look down, and you realize those that are
coming in behind you are pushing you up and supporting you, as well as those
above you. And you realize that all of you are acting together to push the water up
the fountain. Then you relax in your role, and you realize your time is coming.
Eventually, as you support others, you reach the top and you get your time to
shine. And the process goes on and on.
We need you to be part of the Great Fountain of Leadership.
