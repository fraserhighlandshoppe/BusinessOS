Day 7:
Moving Your Body
Consciously
In this session you'll learn why not exercising can literally make
you depressed, why I stand while I work, how to breath the way
our bodies were meant to (most people do the OPPOSITE), and
much more...
Trees that grow with wind become strong and ﬂexible. If we want a strong body,
we need to expose it to wind (resistance).
Your body is the foundation for happy emotions and a high functioning mind.
We talked about what we put into out body, now we'll talk about what we do with
it.
We are made to move a lot, that is, to exercise.
Exercise improves brain function and memory, too.
Not exercising is a depressant. We're made to move - and we become not happy
when we don't.
If you don't exercise and you start, start out slowly, say 10 or 15 minutes of light
exercise. Work your way up to 20-30 minutes of moderate exercise each day.
1. Your body will get stronger and it will get easier.

Most people don't pay attention to how they hold themselves and their posture.
Hold yourself upright.
Sitting is generally bad for us, especially when we hunch over. It's better to stand.
1. I stand while I work. I stand on 3 inches of memory foam, without shoes. I

keep my legs about shoulder width apart.
2. It's done wonders for me. I no longer get pain in the middle of my back.

Another beneﬁt of standing more during the day is you sleep better at night.
One type of exercise is aerobic, where you get your heart rate up and keep it up for
20-30 minutes.

Another type of exercise is anaerobic exercise - short bursts of 20-30 seconds to get
your heart rate up, then rests of 20-30 seconds, and then repeat. (Check with your
health expert before starting or changing your health regimen).
Breathing is also important to your body, obviously. But we don't normally breathe
consciously, so our breathing is less than optimum.
Andrew Weil says if he could recommend one thing to improve someone's health
and vitality, it would be breathing.
We can take conscious control over our breath.
Breathe with your belly, not so much with your chest. To do this, make your belly
fat.
Occasionally throughout the day, take 4 or 5 deep breaths from your belly.
Deep, slow breaths will also calm you down.
If you want a strong, healthy body, you need to move it, hold it, and exercise it
consciously.
