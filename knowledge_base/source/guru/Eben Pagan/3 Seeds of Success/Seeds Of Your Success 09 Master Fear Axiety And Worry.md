Day 9:
Master Fear, Anxiety & Worry
In this session we tackle the emotion of Fear.
You'll learn why it's hard to make good decisions when you're
afraid, how Fear gets in the way of our success, 4 speciﬁc action
steps to make friends with Fear, and much more...
Fear may have been the ﬁrst emotion, and the emotion all other emotions evolved
from.
Fear gets us into reptile brain, and gets us out of consequential thinking.
A little fear over a long period of time creates an underlying sense of anxiety ALL
the time.
Fear allowed us to survive in our old environment, but in the modern environment
it holds us back.
Fear is triggered. It's an emotional response.
One way fear is triggered is by the "Neural Back Alley." With this mechanism, fear is
triggered instantly and there's very little opportunity to stop the reaction with
rational thought. This mechanism bypasses the rational centers of the brain.
Negative emotions like Fear are twice as motivating as positive emotions, like
Desire.
Fear motivates us to move away from what we don't want, but you can't be
successful by moving away from things.
1. Example: You can't get to England by simply avoiding Australia.

Focusing on the fear can bring more of what we fear into our lives. Instead, make
friends with it, see it as the gift that it is, and work on creating more of the positive
emotions we want.
Spence: Fear aﬃrms that I am alive.
Courage: Acting in the face of fear.
90-95% of the time the thing we fear don't happen.

Action steps to reduce fear and make friends with it:
1. Stop watching the NEWS. It magniﬁes fear and makes it seem like bad things

happen around you much more often than they really do.
2. Practice pausing before you respond to anything.
3. Face your worst fear. What if the worse thing happens? Walk through what

you will do if it does. When you plan for it, you'll realize you're going to be
okay. People across the world deal with this every day. It could be worse.
4. Dissociate.

If you have a nightmare, turn on the light, close your eyes, and invite the monster
to come out to the sunlight and talk to you.
