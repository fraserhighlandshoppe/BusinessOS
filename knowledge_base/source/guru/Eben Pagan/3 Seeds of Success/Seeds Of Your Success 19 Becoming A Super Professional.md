Day 19:
Becoming A
Super Professional
In this session you'll learn the 5 qualities of a "Super Professional,"
why you should make fewer commitments but keep them all, the
#1 attitude that creates real results in your business, and much
more...
How does a CEO look? How do they dress? How do they think? Can you rely on
them? What's their level of expertise?
People tend to trust "real excellence." When a person takes their strengths and truly
turns them into excellence, it inspires trust.
The business world values very speciﬁc qualities and behavior.
Super Professional Quality #1: a Performer.
1. Someone who gets the job done. Always does what they say.
2. Stop making a lot of promises. High performers only make a few

commitments, but keep all of them.
3. Stop saying things like "I'll do that soon" or "I'll never do that again."

Super Professional Quality #2: a Driver.
1. A driver is someone who likes to own the responsibility for delivering

speciﬁc results. They are persistent and keep pushing to get the results they
want.
2. Most people try to do their job and not screw up so they won't get in

trouble. They think "just doing their job" is what's required.
3. But some people do more - they want to own the responsibility for

delivering the result. They understand that "eﬀort" and "work" are diﬀerent
than "outcomes" and "results."
4. To be a driver, you have to have initiative, self-esteem, be willing to face

conﬂict, etc.

Super Professional Quality #3: On Time & Synchronized With Time.
1. Half of people are Judgers. They like to plan. They are on time.
2. Half of people are Perceivers. They are the free spirits. They are late.
3. Each type drives the other nuts.

When it comes to what matters, it pays oﬀ to do your research and know your
topic. The super professionals in the world can tell if you're prepared... or not.
Super Professional Quality #4: Over Communication.
1. Super professionals let everyone know what's going on with them,

continually.
Super Professional Quality #5: Result Oriented.
1. Achievement and results are in the physical world. You either got the results

out in the world, or not.
2. If you're biased toward emotions or logic, you'll need to shift your emphasis

to creating physical results that everyone can see.
3. "Get The Job Done."

Understand the distinction between eﬀort and results.
