Day 20:
Mastering The
Knowledge World
In this session you'll learn about the shift from working with our
hands to working with knowledge, 3 core skills to become a
"knowledge master," how improving your communication skills
will boost your job security, and much more...
Someone graduating college right now will have 10-14 diﬀerent jobs and 3-5
career changes by age 38.
These career changes won't be totally diﬀerent - they'll have something in common
- we're all becoming "Knowledge Workers."
Peter Drucker coined the term "Knowledge Worker."
Throughout history humans have worked with their hands. Know that the world is
so prosperous, and we have extra time, we're becoming interested in knowledge.
Because we're consuming knowledge, we need people to create that knowledge.
All of us can participate in creating this new knowledge.
In business knowledge is giving us power - the power to see patterns of how our
customer think and behave, and that knowledge can fuel the growth of business.
The new world is about creating new resources, particularly Knowledge and
Technology resources.
A Knowledge Master is someone who can "go meta" and learn any knowledge
quickly... connect knowledge from diﬀerent areas quickly... teach others
eﬀectively... and distribute knowledge eﬃciently.
You need specialized deep knowledge on a topic that allows you to create value.
1. Underwater basket weaving: isn't valuable.
