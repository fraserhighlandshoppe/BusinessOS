Day 3:
Making Your Success
Inevitable
In this session you'll learn how to align your natural drives to
create success, how to make success inevitable, the drives that
keep us from the success we truly deserve, and much more...
For Inevitability Thinking, think of the image of setting up a row of dominos so you
can push one over, and then they all fall in a beautiful pattern.
Animals have all sorts of specialized features for speciﬁc functions. Humans have
several very powerful mechanisms, and we seem highly adapted to the world we
evolved again.
We come preprogrammed with speciﬁc motives and drives, and programmed
decision preferences. They are shaped by our experiences in life, but we don't start
out with a blank slate as some people used to think.
What are our programmed drives?
1. Most of us are familiar with Survival and Reproduction. These are very strong

drives.
2. But most of us aren't familiar with the more subtle drives, like the drive to

achieve social status or avoid losing status and our position in the hierarchy.
3. We come wired to get social status and avoid losing social status.
4. We come wired to avoid failure
5. We come wired to get other people to approve of us, and avoid rejection.
6. We come wired to be attracted to certain feelings like happiness and

meaning, and avoid states like despair and meaninglessness.
7. We come wired to acquire and display material things.
8. Our choice of mate establishes in part our social status.

100's of generations ago, we lived in a tribe of 100-150, most died before reaching
adulthood, and we knew everyone in our tribe and group, and their levels of status
in the group, and their relationship to each other socially and sexually.

We're wired for a simple game of survival, sex, and social status. That's what got
passed on. But now we live in modern society, known as "the human zoo."
The human zoo idea: take animals out of natural environment, they get depressed
and don't act like themselves, because they don't know what to do.
The human zoo is that we humans have built zoos called cities and houses, and we
voluntarily move into a zoo that's unlike our natural environment. And we're
getting depressed.
Most businesses do things not because it's the best thing long term for individuals,
communities, or the planet. It's because humans want things in the short term,
even though they’re bad for you.
1. They're not doing it because they want us to be healthier. They're doing it

because it works to make their business secure.
Most people trade short term pleasure for long term disease, cancer, bad feelings,
etc.
This happens because we can't do the math. We're not wired to handle this level of
temptation and promise of instant gratiﬁcation.
Success killers:
1. Foods: concentrated sugars, fat, salt.
2. Empty entertainment (TV, video games, movies).
3. General news: most is concentrated fear, conﬂict, bad news, tragedy. (When

we were in tribes watching conﬂict was important because it meant
something to us. Now, it makes little diﬀerence to us and makes us
unrealistic about negative events)
4. Internet, Phone, Texting, Email, Etc: creates continual partial attention.
5. Fashion, Beauty, and Pornography Industry: it's all about sex, gives us

unrealistic expectations, obsession with beauty and sex.
6. Credit and Credit "Cards": creates burden and worry. It's hard to resist.
7. Drugs: legal and illegal. Dependency on instant ﬁx they give us. Nicotine,

alcohol, and coﬀee are drugs. They give us the instant gratiﬁcation of feeling
good.
8. Decisions Being Made For Us: we're becoming programmed more and more.

The common problem for all these is the instant pop of feeling good. The "chemical
squirt." They ultimately take away from your life, because while you're doing these
things to feel good in the short term, you're not doing the things to feel good and
create a rewarding life in the long term.

Each of our drives can be consciously aligned to create a successful life.
If you don't consciously learn how your human animal works, then you can be sure
that others will get you to do what they want... and you'll end up unhealthy and
unhappy.
Inevitability Thinking:
1. There were 2 major ways of accomplishing things in success literature: goal

setting & problem solving.
These are both direct methods - but success is an indirect game. You do a lot of
diﬀerent things, then success Emerges.
The 3 ways of accomplishing things is what I call Inevitability Thinking: What is the
condition that I need to put in place so that what I want to have happen, happens
naturally and automatically?
1. How can you take the natural drives that we all come born with, that your

ancestors used to survive and reproduce and gain status, to actually make
success more likely in today's world?
