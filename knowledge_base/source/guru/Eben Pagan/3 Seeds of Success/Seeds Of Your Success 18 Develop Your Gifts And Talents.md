# Seeds Of Your Success 18 Develop Your Gifts And Talents.pdf

```markdown
Day 18:
Develop Your Gifts & Talents
In this session you'll learn why the strategies for business success
are so diﬀerent from personal success, 3 steps to uncovering your
natural talent, the danger of trying to learn everything in the
modern environment (and what to do instead), and much more...
This session marks a transition into a new paradigm of the program... where we
start focusing on the outer world.
If you look at great accomplishments, and the people who did them, you'll ﬁnd
people who identiﬁed and developed their natural gifts... turned them into
strengths, excellence, and mastery... and focused them on creating massive value
and innovation.
The Poor Farmer Story - The thing that had caused him so much despair turned out
to be his greatest treasure.
Business Success & Personal Success are very diﬀerent.
1. Business requires you to be strong, almost machinelike.
2. Personal success requires you to look in places that are painful to look,

where you're psychological unhealthy.
The two strategies are very diﬀerent.
1. The strategy to business success is to strengthen your strength.
2. The strategy to personal success is to develop your weaknesses.

How to strengthen your strength:
1. Start with your natural talent or gift.
2. Develop it into a strength.
3. Use it in the world.
4. Develop it into excellence and mastery. This develops long term

commitment. You can't "cram" for it.

The downside of having a gift is that if you don't have mentors there to push you,
you will skate through life on your talent. This can be even worse than not having it
in the ﬁrst place. When you go out to the real world, you'll need to take it to the
next level, you won't be able to. People will say you have the talent, but why can't
you develop it.
1. The documentary Hoop Dreams. Leaning on your talent too much, not

staying focused and not having the discipline to go on to turn their strength
into mastery.
Adam Smith's pin factory: a group of people, each making a product from start to
ﬁnish, won't create nearly as much as a group of people who divide the labor and
each then each person repeats a specialized task over and over.
We now live in an environment of ultra-specialization and ultra-interdependence.
Stop trying to learn everything, instead go deep in a highly specialized area, in a
niche where you have some talent that will allow you to create specialized value,
then focus single-mindedly on this at work... and study the best in your ﬁeld.
It can feel like we're cheating making money with our natural gifts, because it's so
fun.
How to ﬁnd your talents:
1. Ask yourself, "Do you perceive yourself as more of a physical, emotional, or

mental genius?"
a. What's your secondary genius?
2. What do people with this combination tend to be good at?
a. Physical/Emotional: healer.
b. Physical/Mental: project managers.
c. Emotional/Physical: artists, performers.
d. Emotional/Mental: psychologists.
e. Logical/Physical: architects, systems thinkers.
f. Logical/Emotional: philosophers.
1. If you don't know what your strength/talent is, ask 10 people who know you.

Once you identify your talent, get some great mentoring. Find others that are
highly developed - read their books, watch their programs, go study with them,
hire them or talk them into being your mentor.
Show that you have talent and the drive to learn, and that you're applying what
they're telling you, and they'll teach you more.
```
