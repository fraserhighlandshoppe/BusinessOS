Day 26:
Sex
In this session you'll learn why it's important to have sex regularly
(even when you don't feel like it), how to use sex to deepen the
connection with your partner, why sex can resolve conﬂict, and
much more...
Sex can make or break a relationship. Like pH, if the balance isn't right, things can
get sour ﬁrst.
Use sex as a conscious tool in your relationship development. Use it to prevent
problems, resolve conﬂict, communicate love, and explore the shadow aspects of
yourself.
Make sex a habit, like food or sleep. It's not a luxury. Have sex regularly whether you
like it or not.
Ritualize your love making. Make it a sacred conversation that deepens your
connection through regular practice.
Learn how to generate your own sex drive. Waiting for the right mood is a dead end
game. It's like exercise. If you don't consciously set aside time and space, it won't
happen regularly.
Foreplay is everything you're doing when you're not having sex.
Look for beauty in your lover. Do you focus on ﬂaws, or gifts? Look for things to
appreciate and admire.
Sex ﬂoods the body with hormones and endorphins that reduce stress.
During stress, sex can be the fastest way to get back to baseline.
Sex is a pre-rational problem solver. Many problems are from childhood and talk
doesn't help. But touch can.
Sex can be a salve. Physical touch is the ﬁrst language we learn.
Sex can be a playground for the imagination. The more creativity we bring to our
conversations and sex, the more alive we feel. Become a great experimenter - try
out new and interesting ideas, positions, role plays, fantasies, costumes, and
fetishes.

Shame: a painful feeling of having lost the respect of others because of improper
behavior.
Back in tribal days, showing improper behavior could have meant being banished
from the tribe and dying alone out on the savanna.
Shame is the biggest obstacles to fun and freedom in sex. But we're afraid of
wanting, needing, and pleasure itself.
Shame loves to live in the dark. Secrecy is oxygen to shame.
Sex is a conversation. Conversation is where you get to say what you think, share
what you feel, and hear what it provokes on the other side. Good conversations
cover any topic with no adherence to propriety. Polite conversation is boring. Polite
sex is boring. So stop being polite. We want to be with the messy, untamed, human
animal. Make noise, move your body, bite, scratch, and be wild.
Your job as a lover is to unleash your partner's animal. Help them be free, make
mistakes, and expand into their most audacious self.
The level of openness your partner has in the world, is an indicator of how well you
love them (not of how much you love them).
You already love as much as you can. The task is to channel that love in speciﬁc
ways that help them grow.
Have lots of sex, often, especially when you don't feel like it.
