Day 11:
The Enlightened Emotion
Gratitude is a magic, higher order emotion that has the ability to
transform your life.
In this session you'll learn how to use Gratitude to neutralize a
negative emotion, the secret to changing someone else's
behavior, the 3 steps for turning diﬃcult events into positive
outcomes for yourself, and more...
Gratitude is magic, because it has the ability to turn around almost any emotional
situation very rapidly.
Here's a metaphor: Life is a big pristine white circle. Little dots start showing up they're the conﬂicts. They're very tiny compared to the big white circle. But we start
focusing on them more and more, and soon we get so tuned into them that they
are all we can see.
Besides focusing on the negative, another challenge is arguing with reality. Most
people go through their lives not accepting what is going on around them. If they
get home and the dog made a mess in the house, instead of accepting it, they say it
shouldn't have happened, that the dog was bad for doing it... but the reality is, it
happened. Often what we get out of arguing with reality is indignance or
righteousness, which could be a chemical we're addicted to.
Carl Rogers and Carl Jung both said in order to change something, you ﬁrst must
accept it. If your partner does something you don't like, and you start yelling at
them, they won't change because you haven't accepted what they did. Instead, ask
them what they meant by what they did, discuss it... and that's probably your best
chance to change their behavior.
Werner Erhard: Choose what you got. Choose to say yes to the events that happen
in your life. Don't argue with reality.
3 Steps for dealing with "stuﬀ":
1. Step 1: We need to face the thing that's happening that we wish wasn't

happening. "I'm going to stare this thing down, I'm going to understand it,
and I’m going to work with it." When you face your demons, it brings great
power.

2. Step 2: After we accept it, the next level is to welcome it. Not that you're glad

about it, but to welcome the circumstance into your life.
3. Step 3: The next level is Utilization - take the event and you utilize it to help

you grow and become more successful, and to help others grown and
become more successful.
Many of us fear that if we stop focusing on the black dots and fears, something bad
will happen because we're not "watching out." But we no longer live in that ancient
environment where we have to constantly be fearful. You can reduce your vigilance
by 90% and you'll still be ok, plus you'll be a lot more fulﬁlled.
We can neutralize negative emotions by going into Gratitude.
Gratitude is a "higher order emotion." Lower order emotions like fear are simply
triggered. Higher order emotions take higher level thinking and perspective.
Gratitude requires Pre-Action because it requires you to reﬂect on and appreciate
the miracle of what's happening. Appreciation takes practice. Appreciation is about
focusing on what's working and how amazing that is, on what's good in the
situation.
When you stub your toes on a tree root, instead of getting angry, ask yourself,
"What can I appreciate about this tree?"
1. It's evolved over billions of years.
2. It's just trying to survive.
3. It breathes the carbon dioxide that I exhale. I inhale the oxygen it exhales.
4. Keep looking in this way, and you may be blown away by how amazing all of

life is.
Gratitude requires you to look deeper into the processes of how things are
working. It's something we need to practice.
"Now-ing": Most of us live in the past or future, or in a map of the now instead of
the actual experience of the present moment.
