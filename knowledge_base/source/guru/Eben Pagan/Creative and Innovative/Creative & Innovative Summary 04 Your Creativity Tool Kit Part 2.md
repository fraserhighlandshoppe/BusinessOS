Session 4:
Your Creativity Tool Kit Part 2
In session 4 you going to learn 3 more tools for being creative and innovative. You’ll learn how to
zoom in and zoom out, how to take multiple perspectives, and how to put conditions in place that
will make your visions happen on their own. You’ll also be getting powerful exercises to use to
practice these tools so you can apply them to your visions in the key areas of life: Health,
Relationships, and Success.
• Zooming In & Zooming Out - In NLP (Neuro-linguistic Programming) there’s an idea called
“going meta”. This is when you go up a level and look at everything “zoomed out”. Example:
when you go meta to a tree, you zoom out and look at the whole forest full of trees.
• One way to go meta is to talk about something. Once something is not just happening to you
and you can talk about it, you gain a lot of power over it.
• Just like zooming out or going meta, you can also zoom in and examine something at a much
finer level of detail.
• EXERCISE:
• Look at the 3 visions for your future.
• Your Health Vision:
• Zoom in and consider what you need to eat to achieve it.
• Now zoom in another level. What do you need to eat at each meal?
• Now zoom out. Consider what the overall balance of fats, proteins, and carbs will be of
all the food that you eat over the next 3 years.
• Your Relationship Vision:
• What are the words you need to say to your romantic partner to get the outcome you
want to create?
• Now zoom another level. Consider the most important individual word you need to say.
Zoom in even further, slow down the saying of the word and find the perfect facial
expressions and gestures to convey how you’re feeling to your partner.
• Now zoom out. What’s the general tone of your conversation that now includes all of the
words you’ll say to your partner?
• Zoom out again and see the general patterns of interaction with your partner over 3
years.
• If you use this insight, do some research, and find out what really works in romantic
relationships, it will be much more likely that you’ll realize that 3 year vision. You’ll actually
be creating it.

• Your Success Vision:
• Zoom in and decide exactly how much money you’d like to be making per year in 3
years. Zoom in again and decide how much money you’d like to be making per hour.
• Now zoom out. How much money would you like to make in you entire life?
• If you get really clear on these different levels, you’ll automatically come up with ideas
for how to make that much money on each level.
• Write down the biggest insight you’ve gotten from practicing zooming in & out.
• Then, write down one action step that you’re going to take that will make it much more
likely that your visions will become real.
• Taking Multiple Perspectives or “Perspectiving” - When working to create an outcome that
you want in the world, factor that make the biggest difference are...
• The perspective from which you’re viewing the situation
• Who’s influencing you, who’s involved with you, and who’s around you
• By switching your view or taking other perspectives, you can become much more effective, get
insights in how to create the results that you want, attract the people you want into your life, and
get a lot of power and influence.
• Example #1:
• First, imagine telling someone that you love them. Then, imagine being told by that person
that they love you.
• Next, imagine actually being the other person saying “I love you” to you.
• Imagine you (person A) hearing a story from person B about a third person (person C) telling
them that they love them. Then imagine being person C.
• Now zoom out and imagine being up above all 3 of these figures and watching person A as
he or she imagines being person C saying I love you to person B.
• This exercise is teaching us to take different person perspectives.
• Example #2:
• Visualize something that you want to accomplish in your life.
• Then imagine what you’ll have to do next, next, next, etc. from the perspective of now.
Imagine it like dominoes falling from now into the future. Notice how you feel about those
actions.
• Next, mentally go out 1 day past completion of this goal. Look back at what you had to do
the day before, then 2 days before, etc. like the dominoes falling into the past back to now.
Now notice how you fell about taking those actions.
• EXERCISE:
• Look at your 3-year health vision.
• Imagine it from the perspectives of the three closest people to you.
• Then, imagine that you’re each of those three people looking at the new you that you’re
going to become when you’ve realized this vision.

• Notice which one is going to be the most influential and impactful to you.
• Can you go get that person involved or get some coaching or accountability from them?
• Write down the biggest insight you got from this exercise and an action step you’re going to

• Now take your other visions of relationships and success and repeat this exercise with each.
• Creating Conditions - To create a particular outcome you can set up conditions so the outcome
can happen on it’s own. This is one of the most highly leveraged things that you can do because
you can create the conditions once and have them “manufacture” the outcome over and over.
• The general rule is that the context determines the content. If you put a context around
something it dramatically affects the content.
• Example: A seed and the soil it’s planted in. If a seed is planted in fertile soil it will grow into a
healthy plant. If a seed falls on some rocks with no nutrients, it will never grow in the first place.
• EXERCISE:
• What is a key condition that has been set in your life that led you to where you are now?
• What condition could you put in place that would...
• Give you more energy and health?
• Give you more love, intimacy, connection, and affection?
• Help you learn, grow, contribute, and create more success in your life?
• Review your 3 visions of health, relationships, and success.
• What’s one condition you could put in place for each that would make it so they would
happen automatically?
• Then, write down your insight about these conditions and an action step to put each
condition in place.
