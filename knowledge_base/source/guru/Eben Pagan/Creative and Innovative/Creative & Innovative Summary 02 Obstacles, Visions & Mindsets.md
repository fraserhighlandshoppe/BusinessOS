Session 2:
Obstacles, Visions, & Mindsets
In session 2 you’ll learn some of the challenges that prevent us from being more creative and
innovative, and a set of mindsets that help and facilitate creativity and innovation. You’ll also learn
to create visions and objectives for key areas of your life so you can learn the tools for creativity
and innovation and apply them to those key areas.
• Some of the obstacles and challenges that we face when trying to become more creative &
innovative:
• The old habits we have of thinking, feeling, and behaving really prevent creativity and
innovation.
• Low self-esteem is an obstacle. Dr. Nathaniel Branden says that self-esteem is a combination
of feeling like we can cope with all of the challenges that life throws at us, and feeling that we
deserve to be happy. One step further is feeling that we can also succeed with the challenges
that life has for us and ultimately thrive from them. One of the most important ways to
increase self-esteem is to face increasing levels of challenge in your life and see that you can
handle them. All growth happens outside of your comfort zone.
• Another obstacle is the idea that each of use deserves happiness. It’s counter-intuitive to
think that it’s ok to do things just to make yourself happy.
• Another obstacle is just having a baseline belief that it “won’t work” when the reality is that
most of our experiments don’t work. Instead of the mindset, “it probably won’t work so I
won’t even try”, use the mindset, “it probably won’t work and that’s great news.” Because,
when you start adding up and integrating the 1 in 100 things you try that are true
innovations, you’ll get unimagined levels of being able to create and be successful.
• Not seeing the connections and relationships between things.
• Not being able to think “upstream” and “downstream” or not being able to think multiple
levels of cause and effect, before and after.
• Another key obstacle is just plain fear.
• It’s important to name the obstacles and separate ourselves from them so we don’t identify with
them. They are not us, just things that we experience. Ken Wilbur says that when you objectify
your demons, you rob them of their power to control you.
• 5 mindsets that are valuable as you work to become more creative and innovative:
• We’re all creative geniuses.
• Creativity is a skill that can be developed like a muscle over time.
• Creativity and innovation skills are important to develop in order to achieve all types of
success.
• My creativity and ability to innovate is my personal advantage in the future that’s coming.
• Creativity and innovation reach their highest value when they include physical, emotional,
and logical aspects.

• Relax and give yourself permission to go for it. The greatest meditation is a mind that lets go.
• Our minds can make pictures and simulate experiences that don’t yet exist. The better we get at
visualizing the way we want to create the world, the better we’ll be at actually creating that
world.
• Emergence - Emergent properties arise beyond the level of the individual elements. This is
important because creativity and innovation are about adding pieces together and then seeing
what’s beyond the pieces themselves.
• Success isn’t a result, it’s an emergent.
• EXERCISE:
• Make visions of how your life is going to be better in 3 years, in these key areas: Health,
Relationships, and Business/Financial Success.
• Take a few minutes, close your eyes, and imagine how your health will be in 3 years. What
state would you like your health to be 3 years from now?
• Next, visualize where your primary love relationship will be in 3 years.
• Then, visualize where you would like to be on the success path in 3 years; financially and in
business.
• Then write down your vision as if it was the day that it happened in 3 years.
