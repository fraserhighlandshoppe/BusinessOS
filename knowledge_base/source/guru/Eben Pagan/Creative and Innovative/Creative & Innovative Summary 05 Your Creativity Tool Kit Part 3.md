Session 5:
Your Creativity Tool Kit Part 3
In session 5 you’ll learn the next set of tools for being creative and innovative. These include
Categorizing and Un-Categorizing, Inverting, Cause and Effect, and Stylizing. You’ll also be
getting powerful exercises to use to practice these tools so you can apply them to your visions in
the key areas of life: Health, Relationships, and Success.
• Categorizing and Un-categorizing - The human mind seems to naturally group items into
categories based on similarities and we un-categorize things based on differences.
• This is very useful in one way because it allows us to take massive shortcuts. If we see something
with huge jaw muscles and sharp teeth we don’t have to look at a biology book to know that it
looks like a predator and we should run. We can just make the decision based on knowing the
category.
• It can also be very limiting. The more these categories become rigid the more our minds jumps
to conclusions, and even forces things into our pre-conceived categories.
• EXERCISE:
• Look at your 3-year visions and focus on one of them.
• Really see it as being separate from the other two.
• Then, expand your awareness and see all 3 visions as a category. What would you call that
category?
• Do this with each of your three visions.
• Also, try playing with the categories. For example, take your health vision and your
relationship vision. Try to see how you can categorize the two of them together and call that
category your “Health Vision”. Then try it the other way around and call it your “Relationship
Vision”.
• Now, look at your 3 visions and name the category that those 3 visions are a part of.
• Then, let’s say you call that category a “well lived life”. Could you zoom out and see that
category as part of a bigger category? What would that category be called?
• Zoom out again. Imagine many other people living “well lived lives” and that yours is part of
that category. That category might be called a “healthy community”.
• Zoom out once more. What could “healthy community” be a part of and what would you
call that category?
• Now, write down the insight you got from this exercise. What’s the specific action step you
can take, now that you have this tool, to make it much more likely that your visions will
become real in 3 years?
• Inverting - It’s taking a very special perspective which often leads to an instant “ah-ha”.
Example: Let’s say you’re Warren Buffet and you’re considering buying a company at a certain
price. If you wanted to invert, you could imagine that you owned the company. Then, ask
yourself if you’d want to sell it if you owned it.

• EXERCISE:
• Imagine your health vision.
• What would have to happen to prevent your health vision from becoming real?
• Then, what key action step could you take to prevent that thing from happening?
• Repeat this exercise with your relationship and success visions. Write down your insights and
an action step for each.
• When you think of what would prevent your visions from happening it often turns out to be
more of what you’re already doing. But, when you invert and say “how could I prevent the
thing that’s preventing it?”, you’re using creative thinking and getting a lot more leverage.
• We have minds that can think in terms of Cause and Effect. What we don’t realize is that
everything that seems like a cause was actually caused by something before it. And, there are
typically multiple “upstream” causes for each effect.
• Every effect also triggers other effects “downstream”.
• The more granular we can get and the more we can zoom out and see the bigger picture, the
more we can influence the entire system.
• EXERCISE:
• What level of energy do you want to feel when you’re doing your highest value of work?
• What will you need to eat in order to feel that level of energy? What will you need to do to
make sure you eat that food consistently?
• Will will happen as a result of feeling that level of energy while doing your highest value
work? Will are the other results of that work being done?
• What will happen when you interact with people you love with your energy high and clear
What will happen as a result of that difference?
• What action steps can you take to make sure that you get that high level of energy?
• What emotions do you want to feel in your romantic relationship? If you felt those emotions,
what other emotions would they trigger? How will that affect the emotional state of your
partner?
• What emotions do you want your partner to feel? How would it make you feel if your partner
feels those emotions?
• What action step should you take to help feel the emotions that you and your partner want
to feel?
• What would you want your income to be in 3 years? What will you do with that income when
it happens? Then, what will that allow you to do?
• What action step do you need to take right now so that your income gets to that level in 3
years? And, what action step can you take to make sure that you do that?
• Write down your insights and the actions steps for each of your 3 visions.

• Stylizing - Find the essential features or high leverage parts, and featuring them in a stripped
down, simplified version.
• It often helps to simplify what you’re working with in a way that maintains the critical aspects but
clears away all of the distractions.
• EXERCISE:
• What is the one key indicator of health to you?
• What is the key indicator of a successful relationship to you?
• What is the key indicator of material success?
• Use the stylized, simplified, or “dumbed down” versions of these three indicators.
• Write each one down, then to the left of them, an action step that will cause each of them to
happen. Then put an arrow between the indicator and the action step to indicate that it’s the
trigger.
