Session 4
How To Communicate Powerfully

TM

When Charging For Information,
You Must Make It Valuable
✦ All information isn’t created equally
✦ Some books sell for $10, and some

books sell for $1,000
✦ What’s the difference?

The Difference Is In The
Way Value Is Communicated
✦ Our highest-leverage tool when teaching

and sharing knowledge is the way we
communicate
✦ You can increase perceived value by

10x-100x by refining your communication

Key Mindset: You’re
Teaching One Person Alone
✦ It’s “natural” to think of groups of people as a

“mass”
✦ Instead, remember that you’re teaching

individuals who are alone from their perspective
✦ Talk to, not at - and speak like you would to a

friend who was sitting in the same room with you

Create It For One, Then
Market It To All Of Them
✦ When you create your product for one

person, you make something that’s much
higher value for your customer
✦ Once you’ve created something that works

for individuals - by working 1-on-1, then go
to work selling it to “the masses”

Begin With A BANG!
✦ Grab attention immediately whenever

teaching or sharing information
✦ Use the most powerful words, ideas and

action-oriented language you can to
GET ATTENTION

Simplify What You Say
✦ Use short words and sentences, explain

everything, eliminate the possibility of
misinterpretation and misunderstanding
✦ One idea at a time
✦ Separate ideas with punctuation

Specify - Make What You’re
Saying Concrete & Tangible
✦ Bring every idea, concept, technique or method

all the way to the most specific, real-world
ACTION and effect
✦ Go past the “what” all the way into the “how”...

and leave nothing to the imagination.

Speak-Write: Use A Very
Conversational, Casual Tone
✦ Write and teach the way you speak

conversationally
✦ “You” when pointing to strengths, asking

questions etc. and “We” when pointing to
weaknesses, mistakes, fears, etc.

Stay In Rapport
✦ Use conversational language that keeps

you “the same reality,” and avoid saying
anything that puts distance between
you and your customer
✦ Speak THEIR LANGUAGE - the language

that they’re using in their head

Tell Stories
✦ Include as many stories, examples and

experiences as you can possibly fit into your
product
✦ Minds think in stories, and they will make your

product far more valuable
✦ This is how “regular humans” relate to and learn

“information”

EXERCISE
Opening The Communication Portal
