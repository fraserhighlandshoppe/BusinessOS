```markdown
Session 3
Learning Directly From Customers

TM

Most Products Fail Nevermind Sell Themselves
✦ Most new products and new businesses fail -

meaning that they don’t make sustainable profit
✦ A key cause of this failure is not understanding

exactly what customers want & need
✦ It doesn’t have to be this way...

The Fastest, Cheapest Way
To “Test” Your Niche
✦ The fastest, cheapest and best way to test your

niche is to TALK to prospective customers!
✦ Nothing will get you “up to speed” and “in the

know” faster than live conversations with real
people who have real needs

This Requires You To
Face Your Issues
✦ It sounds “well and good” to talk to customers

and learn directly from them
✦ But to do it, you must overcome fear of rejection,

shyness and other “issues”
✦ It’s important that you do this NOW, because your

long-term business success DEPENDS on it

What To Ask Your
Prospective Customers
✦ The most important discoveries you need to

make are about what’s driving your customer
and what your customer WANTS
✦ Ask “What’s your biggest fear or frustration?”
✦ Ask “What’s your dream scenario or outcome?”

AGAIN: You Must Overcome
Yourself In Order To Do This
✦ We’re taught that it’s “rude” to ask “personal”

questions
✦ Most of us have a natural resistance to asking

questions that might be potentially painful or
embarrassing to answer
✦ People will answer “personal” questions if they think

that it’s going to get them what they WANT

Talk To At Least 1 Prospective
Customer Per Day From Now On
✦ The things that make money in business are

PRODUCTS & MARKETING
✦ To create both of them well, you must practice

understanding your customers at a deep level
✦ Talking to one prospective customers and

customers per day - and ideally 3 or more

Listen For The Conversation
They’re Having In Their Head
✦ Each of us is walking around having an

endless conversation in our heads
✦ Your job is to find out what that

conversation is, then JOIN it!

Pay Attention For “Power
Words” & Power Phrases
✦ As you interview prospective customers and learn

about their fears, desires, wants, aspirations you’ll
notice something
✦ Patterns of emotional “Power Words” & phrases will

begin to become obvious
✦ Make not of these and USE them in your products and

marketing - they have massive VALUE

Use This Format As The Basis
For A Coaching Program
✦ Great coaching is about understanding the specific

outcome your client is trying to achieve
✦ Most people know what to do, they just haven’t

done it
✦ Start a coaching practice, and get paid to research!

EXERCISE
```
