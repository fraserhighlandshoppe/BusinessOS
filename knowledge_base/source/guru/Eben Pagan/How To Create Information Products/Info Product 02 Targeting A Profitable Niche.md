If Your Niche Is Wrong,
Nothing Can Help You
✦ Your niche or “Market” is the combination of

your target customers and the products &
services you sell them
✦ It’s critical that you know who your customer is

and what their needs are, so you can offer
products and services that they want & need

Niches Aren’t “Chosen,”
Niches Are NEEDS
✦ Successful niches aren’t “chosen” - they’re

identified, discovered, developed
✦ It’s much more useful to think of “Niches” as

“Needs” - instead of just “the people you’d
like to have as customers”

You Must Get Outside Of
Yourself To Win This Game
✦ It’s all about thinking from the Customer’s

perspective, not your own
✦ The “intuitive” thing to do is to do what makes

sense to YOU
✦ The successful thing to do is to think like a

CUSTOMER - from their perspective

Why Empathy Is The Most
Profitable Business Skill
✦ Empathy is about SEEKING to understand

another person
✦ Compassion is an even higher form of this idea
✦ These skills must be practiced, developed and

used regularly to succeed as a “conscious”
business owner and creator

Your Product Is Actually An
Obstacle To Their Success
✦ As you think about it, you realize that

your product isn’t the solution
✦ In fact, your product or service is a

barrier, an obstacle - it’s in the way

The “Niche Test”
✦ Is your customer experiencing pain and

urgency or irrational passion?
✦ Is your customer pro-actively looking for

solutions?
✦ Does your customer have few or no

perceived options?

The Big 3 “Mega-Niches”
