Session 5
How To Organize Your Ideas

TM

Building Blocks Of Products
✦ If you want your information, advice and

techniques to be used, you must organize
and communicate them well
✦ We’ll start by learning basic “building

blocks” and then learn how to combine
these elements to create complete products

Development Stages...
1) Concept...
2) Content...
3) Complete Product

Start By Turning Every Idea
Into A “Complete Concept”
✦ A “Complete Concept” is an idea, approach or

technique that has been “fleshed out” and
developed into a stand-alone, useful method
✦ In order to create a Complete Concept, you must

introduce the concept, explain it, describe how it
works, then give actionable steps

Why Humans Need Ideas
Presented As Complete Concepts
✦ We’re all “time poor” in the modern age, and we

don’t need any more “random information”
wasting our time and mind-space
✦ In order to see a piece of information as valuable,

it must be connected to our wants, desires and
needs - in a way that makes it easy to "get”

A Complete Concept Shows Me
“What It Is And How It Works”
✦ First tell me what it is that I’m learning - so I can

set my mind “on that channel”
✦ Next, show me how it works, so I can understand

it and see myself doing it
✦ A person will only do something they’ve first seen

themselves doing in their MIND

Focus On Creating Concepts That
Solve Tangible Challenges
✦ Use what you’ve learned so far to make sure you

always focus on creating Concepts that offer
benefits, results, relief and solutions
✦ If you want to create products that sell

themselves, you must always keep your
customer’s need in mind - it’s the key

An Example Of Creating
A Complete Concept
✦ Let’s say that you were going to teach the

technique of losing weight by eating smaller meals
more often
✦ You could just say “Eat smaller meals more often if

you want to lose weight” - but that’s not very
interesting or compelling (and remember, you’re
trying to make your information VALUABLE)

Presented As Complete Concept
✦ We get fat because of the way that we eat
✦ When we eat 2-3 “regular” meals per day, we’re

likely to keep getting fatter, because we start
out hungry at each meal, then eat too much
✦ If, instead you eat 5 smaller, planned, healthy

meals each day, and eat every 2 1/2 hours, you’ll
actually eat less total food

Continued...
✦ The net result is that you’ll lose fat around your belly

and thighs
✦ As an added bonus: You’ll never get to the point in the

day where you feel hungry and you’ll have more
sustained energy
✦ The best way to start is by planning out exactly what

you’re going to eat for each meal, then schedule your
meals so you always eat before you’re hungry

Notice How The Complete
Concept “Connects The Dots”
✦ People understand ideas, approaches and

techniques when given context and content
together
✦ Take each of your key approaches, techniques,

etc. and “flesh them out” to turn them into
Complete Concepts
