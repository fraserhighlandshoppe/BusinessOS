Session 6
How To Create A Piece Of “Content”

TM

Turning Concepts Into
Valuable Content
✦ Once you have a set of usable Concepts, it’s

time to start turning them into Content (and
then into Products)
✦ Think of a complete piece of Content as the

logical extension of a Complete Concept

Remember, Content Is The
Building Block Of Products
✦ First you learn to create Complete Concepts,

then you develop your Concepts further and create pieces of Content with them
✦ Finally, we’ll put several pieces of Content

together to create full Products

Examples Of “Content”
✦ Email newsletter

✦ Teleclass

✦ Blog post

✦ Tutorial

✦ Podcast

✦ Free Report

✦ Product “chapter”

✦ Launch video

✦ Webinar

✦ Article

Use “Frameworks” To Turn
Concepts Into Content
✦ I like to use what I call “Frameworks” to

develop my Concepts - and turn them into
entire pieces of Content
✦ Here are some of my favorite

Frameworks...

The 3 Brain Model...
✦ Physical
✦ Emotional
✦ Logical

The 4 Learning Styles
✦ Why?
✦ What?
✦ How?
✦ What If?

A 3 Mistakes Piece
✦ Start with 3 mistakes
✦ Include partial solutions
✦ Lead into product offer

Fear, Desire & Motivation
✦ Humans are approximately twice as motivated by

fear as they are by desire
✦ Explain what will happen if your Prospect takes

action - and also what will happen if they don’t
take action - it’s a powerful motivator!

Practice Creating
Concepts & Content Daily
✦ Your ability to make information valuable is

directly related to how much practice you get
creating Content
✦ Create Concepts and Content DAILY
✦ Start a blog, newsletter, podcast, etc. immediately

EXERCISE
Creating Your First 3 Mistakes Report
