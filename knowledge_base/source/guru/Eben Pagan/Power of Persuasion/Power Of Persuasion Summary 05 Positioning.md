Session 5:
Positioning - How To Create A
Reputation
In session 5 you’ll learn how to use positioning to create a reputation in the minds of the people that you
want to persuade. You’ll also be doing an exercise to help you position yourself, your product, and your
business.
• Positioning - Creating a category in the mind in which you can be first. Al Reese & Jack Trout say, “It’s
better to be first than it is to be better.” It’s better to be first in the mind of your customer than to have a
better product.
• When you’re the first one in the category, people perceive you as the leader.
• Once you create the category you need to name it. Don’t just promote your business, but promote
the category itself.
• Most people want to learn from the creator of the category.
• Positioning Yourself – Look out into the market you’re in, and position yourself and your business in a
way that makes you the original solution to a problem or need.
• EXERCISE:
• Think about where you’d like yourself, your product, and your business to be in 3-5 years.
• What is the category that you can create in which you would be first?
