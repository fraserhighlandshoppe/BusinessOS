Creating Your Customer Avatar
One	
  of	
  the	
  most	
  important	
  exercises	
  you	
  can	
  do	
  to	
  create	
  a	
  successful	
  marketing	
  strategy	
  and	
  
successful	
  marketing	
  communications	
  is	
  to	
  develop	
  a	
  “character”	
  or	
  mental	
  image	
  that	
  
represents	
  the	
  “average”	
  of	
  all	
  of	
  your	
  prospective	
  customers.	
  I	
  call	
  this	
  character	
  your	
  
“Customer	
  Avatar.”	
  By	
  creating	
  a	
  Customer	
  Avatar,	
  you	
  can	
  then	
  imagine	
  what	
  it	
  would	
  be	
  
like	
  to	
  experience	
  life	
  as	
  that	
  person	
  -­‐	
  which	
  gives	
  you	
  the	
  opportunity	
  to	
  “walk	
  in	
  their	
  shoes”	
  
-­‐	
  and	
  also	
  allows	
  you	
  to	
  discover	
  their	
  fears,	
  frustrations,	
  wants,	
  aspirations	
  and	
  “emotional	
  
hot	
  buttons.”	
  Begin	
  by	
  answering	
  the	
  following	
  questions...
1. What are the “demographic” commonalities of your ideal customers? Are more of them
male or female? How old are they? What kinds of jobs do they have? How much money
do they make?

2. What are the “psychographic” commonalities of your ideal customers? What are their
fears and frustrations in life? What are their desires and aspirations?

3. What do your customers have in common? (Get creative here. Do they like the same
music? Do they share a political view? Do they have an experience that they’ve all
shared?)

4. If you had to sum up the “story” of your ideal customer’s life - as they tell it - how would
it go?

5. What would be an ideal NAME for your customer? What name epitomizes your
customer?
