Session 6: Key Marketing
Elements To Include In Any
Marketing Piece
In Session 6, you’re going to learn what key ingredients you should include in your marketing to make
sure that your customer buys from you. You’ll also be doing an exercise that walks your through the
creation of your marketing piece.

•

Elements of any good marketing piece:
‣

A powerful headline - Communicate a powerful benefit right away.

‣

Your conversion story - Tell the whole story of how you discovered & created this information
or product.

‣

The product or service - Introduce your product as the result you customers want, the solution
to their problems.

‣

Summarize all of the benefits of your product into marketing bullets.

‣

Introduce the price - Take every opportunity to frame the price into terms your prospect can
understand.

‣

Take away the risk - Oﬀer a money back guarantee or better than money back if possible.

‣

Call them to action - Most prospects have never been through your buying process so they
need to be told exactly what to do, step-by-step.
