Session 5: Content Marketing
In Session 5, you’re going to learn how to follow-up with your customers and about a new form of
marketing - Content Marketing. You’ll be doing an important exercise to help you start creating your
content immediately.

•

Content Marketing is blending education, editorial, and marketing into one piece of content.

•

If you want people to read or watch your marketing, it’s important to format them so they look like
something that oﬀers value - like an article. And we don’t just want them to look like value, but
actually add value to the person watching or reading.

•

Blogs, podcasts, e-mail newsletters, downloadable reports, and surveys are now all examples of
content marketing - where the prospect can first learn something valuable, then be led nicely into
some marketing and a conversion.

•

A great way to follow up is with e-mail auto-responders. With e-mail you can put a lot of
information into it, then have them delivered automatically in a specific sequence.

•

Remember: As you’re creating your follow-up sequence make sure your content is really valuable,
make sure you’re interacting with your customers, and make sure you’re tuning all of it so it really
meets their needs.
