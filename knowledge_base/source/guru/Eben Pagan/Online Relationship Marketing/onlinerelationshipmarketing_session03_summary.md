Session 3: The World Of
Relationship Based Marketing
In Session 3, you’ll learn 3 powerful marketing models you can use to create strong & lasting
relationships with your customers. You’ll also do 2 exercises to start putting these in place now.

•

Marketing Models:
‣

Stair-Step Marketing - Breaking up the big commitments you would ask a customer to make
into small commitments that ultimately lead to them buying your products.

‣

Education Marketing - One of the most valuable things you can do is educate your prospective
customers. They don’t know what they don’t know and you take the role of educator. They will
come to see you as an expert and someone they trust.

‣

Customer Life-Cycle Marketing - We’re all at diﬀerent stages at diﬀerent times. But, we all go
through predictable steps at each level of growth. By creating a marketing process that your
customer can join at any stage they’re at, you can create something accessible to many people
but still feels personal to each of them.
