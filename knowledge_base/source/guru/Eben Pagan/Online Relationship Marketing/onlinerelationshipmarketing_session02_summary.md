Session 2: Your Customer Avatar
In Session 2, you’ll learn all about creating your Customer Avatar and really getting inside the mind of
your prospective customers. You’ll also be doing two important exercises to take what you’ve learned
and put it into action right away.

•

How do you build individual relationships, en mass? A Customer Avatar.

•

The Customer Avatar is an individual that you create in your mind to “stand in” for your individual
customer. Imagine taking all of your best customers and combining them into a single person that
has all the key traits of people who buy from you.

•

This is a figure you use mentally to visualize all of the important aspects of a customer. Then, use
this figure to build a friendship & a relationship.

•

We’re combining the traits your customers share into a single person, and leaving out all the traits
they don’t share.

•

In order to really find these traits & help your customers, you must invest time actually interacting
with your customers. Most people want to skip this step but it’s critical.

•

Another mistake that business owners make when creating a Customer Avatar is imagining that all
their customers are just like them.

•

Give your Customer Avatar a name. This helps you talk to your avatar one-to-one.

•

Understand your customer’s Ultimate Scenario. This is their ideal situation or perfect outcome.
