Week #4: The Beliefs of
Wealth (Using Your Mind to
Change Your Mind)
Your beliefs are the “filter” between you and the “real world.” They affect
how you perceive the world, make meaning out of your experiences, make
decisions, and act. In this session, we’re going to do some powerful “belief
work” to “reframe” your limiting beliefs – and install a set of EMPOWERING
beliefs that will support your value-creation and wealth-creation focus.
1.

Our beliefs shape the whole way we see the world. When you change your beliefs you can change
your ability to generate wealth.

2.

Learn how to take out negative wealth-limiting beliefs and install positive, wealth-generating
beliefs

3.

By choosing your beliefs, you allow any results you want in life. Change ANY belief, any time.

4.

There are two types of goals:

• Achievement-oriented external goals (make $100, buy this car, meet this person).
• Mastery goals (learning a skill, mastering a part of life, learning something so you can achieve it
over and over).

5.

Focusing on “fairness” could block you from creating wealth. You need to focus on mastery and
creating value instead.

6.

Master the art of creating value and you can automate skill and do it over and over.

7.

Master creating value for others.

8.

Looking for “fairness” could be robbing you from making money, because you aren’t focusing on
creating the maximum amount of value, without focusing on what you get in return.

9.

When you are mastering a skill, that IS the payback, because you can use that skill for the rest of
your life.

10. “Re-framing” is the easiest, simplest and most powerful tool you can use to change your beliefs.
Take a concept or situation and look at it from a diﬀerent perspective. By learning the skill of
“reframing” you can take control of your life.

11. The concept of “failure” is imaginary. Using this simple mental trick can immediately eliminate
every failure or problem in your life: say, “What can I learn from this?”

12. Learn how to use the diﬃcult things in your life as fuel for creating success and wealth.
13. Napoleon Hill said, “For the World’s richest people, their greatest success came right after their
greatest failures.”
