Session #10: Creating
Massive Value
In this session, you’re going to learn some of the most important mindsets,
strategies and techniques for creating MASSIVE value for others.
1.

The “Magic formula” for wealth creation:

• Stop self sabotaging (debt, instant gratification, payment systems).
• Create massive value.
• Accumulate assets.
• Take those assets and generate long-term wealth.
2.

3 Part Wealth Creation Formula = Value > Assets > Wealth

3.

Value is 100% external, and it’s based on others perception. You create value by giving
convenience, joy, security, and taking away fear, anxiety and pain for others.

4.

Assets are real estate, stock, and gold. Appreciating assets actually hold and grow in value over
time. Appreciating assets that throw oﬀ cash flow (i.e. a real estate that is appreciating and
throwing oﬀ income).

5.

If money ISNT an asset, then what is an asset?

• Appreciating asset - Gold, stock, real estate.
• An appreciating asset that throws oﬀ a stream of income – profitable business, rental, stocks
with dividends.

6.

The dangerous (yet common) mistake people in debt make, when they are looking for wealth, is
to focus on where they are financially sick. Keep focused on creating massive value at all times for
other people.

7.

The “give and take” trade equation is losing its power to create wealth. Instead the power of
“creating value all the time, without focusing on taking” is key to creating wealth.

8.

What is creation? It is manifesting something that is new in some respect.

9.

What is value? Value is an “abstract anomilization.” It’s a process. And it is based on what other
people consider valuable. So you have to get inside the heads and hearts of other people, and
create value from their perspective.

10. The ultimate business and value-creation skill is compassion – the proactive seeking to
understand where you can create joy or alleviate pain for another person.

11. Entrepreneurs combine and synthesize materials into new combinations and put them together
to create another level of value. And when that higher level gets put together, the entrepreneur
gets a cut of it.

12. You need to create at least 10x the value that you’re getting paid for.

13. What is the key distinction between value and veneer? Veneer is a quick fix, but not the deep
value. Don’t confuse creating value with creating veneer. We want the value we create to look
good though. Veneer does not create long term assets and wealth.

14. Imitate >> Assimilate >> Innovation.
15. There are a few critical types of value creation that you need to master. You need to be an expert
at them and do them on a daily basis, but you won’t get paid for them:

• Teaching.
• Coaching (help them develop a skill, helping them be accountable).
• Mentoring (helping another actualize who they are).
• Fusion (fusing humans together, Tribal Leadership).
16. As you create value for yourself, invest back in yourself too: your health, psychology and
relationships.

17. Strengthen your strengths; don’t focus on your weaknesses. Superstars are willing to admit their
weaknesses.

18. Make a list of talents. Which could be a talent creating a lot of value? What’s an action step that
you can take to develop your natural talent into an external value creation strength.
