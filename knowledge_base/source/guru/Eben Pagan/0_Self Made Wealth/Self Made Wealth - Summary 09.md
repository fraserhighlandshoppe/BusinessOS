Week #9: Stop Self-Sabotage Why Habits are the Key to
Long-term Financial Success
We’re now moving into the “physical” aspects of creating WEALTH – and in
this session we’re going to start out by learning about the actions and
habits that prevent us from ever accumulating assets and creating wealth
for ourselves.
1.

In this session we transition to learning what to do to create wealth in the world. We’re moving
on from the Inner Game.

2.

Can anyone create wealth? Is it really possible for an average human being, like you or me, to start
from nothing and create wealth? YES! 80% of millionaires are first-generation millionaires. Learn
the habits and mindsets of self-made millionaires.

3.

The “get rich quick” mindset encouraged by our culture takes away from wealth. Let’s get away
from getting rich quick by being lazy.

4.

Anyone can create wealth, including you… NOW.

5.

“No matter how far down the wrong road you’ve gone, turn back.”

• Seven Habits that will definitely work to prevent you from becoming wealthy:
• Buying with credit cards and debt.
• Buying new cars.
• Car payments.
• Buying rapidly depreciating assets (anything that when you buy it, it still has some value).
• Buying liabilities.
• Impulse spending.
• Fear-based credit card accumulation.
6.

Think “I’ll see it when I believe it… in fact I’ll create it,” instead of “I’ll believe it when I see it.”

7.

Humans are not made to think in long-term time frames. We’re used to instant gratification. This
can make it hard to achieve wealth long-term, where you have to keep yourself on track for 10, 20,
40 years…

8.

“Cognitive Dissonance” is when we hold two diﬀerent beliefs in our head, and one is about the
way we want to behave, but the other actually runs counter to that desire.

9.

Change your behavior to change your beliefs. Behave the way you want to believe.

10. Put new rituals and habits in place, that you do every day that help you to accumulate wealth.
11. Impulse control is essential to building wealth. You have to be able to resist desires that come up.
12. You can survive on much less.

13. Short-term gratification is the biggest habit that all of us have that prevents of from wealth
creation.

14. Learn how to identify the rituals and habits in life that are preventing your wealth.
15. The more wealth you want to create in your future, the more you need to accept that you can
survive on much less today. You do not need most of the things you buy.

16. The most dangerous concept that has ever been invented that halts wealth creation is debt. It’s

very hard to switch oﬀ the debt mechanism, and thinking in terms of buying on credit. Avoid debt
like the plague.

17. Turn oﬀ the “debt logic” and stop thinking in terms of monthly payments.
18. Credit card “teaser rates” seem GREAT, but they are actually like a mortgage or credit company
drug dealers, giving you your first “hit” for free, then getting you hooked.

19. Did you know you’ll get struck by lightening 10 times for every time you win the lottery, and yet
“winning the lotto” is the highest percentage way young people say they’ll get wealthy.

20. Warning, debt consolidation can be a good idea… but only use it to pay oﬀ debt more quickly,
not to reduce monthly payments.

21. Stop “retail therapy” and feeling good by spending.
22. “Purchase Pause” – wait at least a week before minor purchases, and at least a month before major
purchases.

23. Call credit card companies and look to reduce interest by suggesting a balance transfer to another
card or from another card.

24. Build habits that get you out of debt, and take the value you create and invest it in assets over
time.
