Week #7: The Difference
Between Wealth and Happiness
(& How to Create Both)
In this session, you’re going to learn how we create our own emotional and
financial SUFFERING – and how to escape from this “vicious circle” of selfcreated pain… so you can accelerate the creation of wealth in your life.
1.

Alignment is very important – for most of us our mind, body and spirit is out of alignment or
incongruent. Aligning thoughts and emotions are crucial to developing long-term wealth.

2.

Happy is an emotion that happens when good things happen to us. It’s temporary. Happiness is a
long-term emotion. It is not physically possible to have long-term happiness, but we can have
fulfillment. Fulfillment is not a temporary emotion, but a combination of how you see yourself,
your world, learning and self-reliance.

3.

Learn to let go of the “justice mechanism” and to give away your best ideas to others and learn
from that.

4.

Learn why there is a HUGE diﬀerence between “avoiding being poor or in debt or being
controlled by our circumstances” and “creating wealth. “ And the critical reason why only the latter
leads to wealth.

5.

Why do you really want wealth? Your reason will determine whether you’ll EVER have it.

6.

It’s critical to align your thoughts and emotions for the long-term, so you can be aligned for the
long term in creating wealth. Learn a system for auto-tuning your long-term alignment.

7.

The road to fulfillment is not through “happiness” and it isn’t through wealth. If you want true
fulfillment, you can’t get it through emotion and money. If you are trying to get to fulfillment and
peace, it is better to focus on reducing suﬀering.

8.

There is a diﬀerence between pain and suﬀering (attachment). You can create pain over things
that haven’t even happened (worrying), or by holding on or resisting things in our life. Pain x
Resistance = Suﬀering.

9.

There are two specific strategies for dealing with choice:

• “Maximizers” take a lot of time to make a choice, and after they make the purchase they tend
to feel less satisfied, compare it to everything else and second-guess or wish they could
upgrade to new.

• “Satisfiers” look at their options, pick the best, then they are happy with what they picked.
• Learn to be more satisfied with choices. Make choices based on needs, and not getting the
absolute best possible thing out there.

10. The proliferation of choice is making us less happy. This is the “paradox of choice.”
