Week #3: Taking Control of
Your Self-Image
In this session, we’re going to learn how our self-image affects our financial
success – and how to take CONTROL of our self image so we see ourselves
as CREATORS of wealth.
1.

Your “Personal Money Myth” is the story you created in your mind about your relationship to
money. Your story is often diﬀerent than what actually happened. You will find situations that
confirm your view of reality.

2.

“Self Image” is our imaginary idea of who we are in the World. Our image of ourselves acts on our
map of the World (rather than operating directly). Your self-image is made up of ideas you got
from other people. Most of our self-image was created unintentionally; we just take what comes
in and limit ourselves in unimaginable ways.

3.

“Identification” is when we identify ourselves with an idea, concept or thing, and we think we are
that thing.

4.

Change your deepest fears about money into “how to” actions you can use to generate money.

5.

“Denominalization” is the process of taking something from a static, frozen thing into a changing,
flowing process:

• This allows for change instead of freezing something “as is.”
• Avoid saying, “I am…” or “the world is…” Instead say, “I am-ing.”
• See yourself as a growing person that can construct your world and thoughts consciously.
6.

Are you a “frozen getter” or “evolving creator?” You can live in a dynamic view of reality, where you
are in control of your ability to evolve.

7.

“I am a poor person” v. “Until now I have acted out behaviors that have resulted in less money
than I would like to have.” This frees the mind like a seized up motor moving again.

8.

Avoid saying, “I AM this way…” When we say “I” or “Me,” we freeze ourselves and we can’t change.

9.

Learn which “money maps” to use to create wealth.

10. Become a “Creator of Value.” What does the other person find valuable?
11. Shift from an “extract money” mindset to a “create value” mindset. Create new value for other
people.

12. Plant the seeds of a self that creates value for others.
