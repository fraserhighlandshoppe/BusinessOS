YOUR MARKETING: Session 7
Long Term Success
In this session, you'll learn how to begin thinking in terms of long-term success (instead of instant
gratification), what it takes to truly master any area of your life and, why compassion is the greatest
business skill.

• The answer that you're proposing to your clients involves them investing some amount of money,
energy, or eﬀort. But what they want is instant gratification. It's like the medical industry
programming us with the notion of taking a little pill and whatever problem we have will be
gone. We in western culture are particularly spoiled. No one comes up to me at a program like this
one and is thankful for scratching the surface of an area that will take them years to develop.
Instead, people come up to me, frustrated, that they don't have all the answers right now.

• What I'm doing is putting you on the track that in a few years can make you RICH. But people
want their instant gratification now. If it's this hard for me who has at least a little skill at teaching
this stuﬀ, and it's hard for you to get it, then how hard will it be for you to explain to your
customers, through the internet?

• That's why it's so important for you to think of this as a new skill to develop, and a practice to do
every day. If you spent 30 minutes a day, for the rest of your life, practicing this you will become
far more successful in business. What's more, if you really start doing this, you will realize that this
is the essence of relationships, friendships, management, running a company, hiring people, and
managing the relationship with yourself.

• When you really start understanding this stuﬀ, you start asking yourself "How can I motivate
myself?" The way you motivate yourself is to go inside and do all this stuﬀ on you, and you follow
the trail down to the thing that really motivates you.

• In friendships and relationships with other people, I think we're on the verge of a pretty good
break through in our species and our cultural evolution. We went for most of our history as a
species being pretty selfish and having some unconscious drives that made us protect the ones
we love. But just in the very last sliver of our evolution, we've now gotten to the point where we
can say "I'm going to rise above the pure animal thing. Let's set some societal rules in place that
aren't perfect but allow us to interact with each other on a higher level."

• This has really allowed us to get to a much higher level. We now value fairness and justice. But
there's something past this. And that's where we give our greatest gifts at all times. But we have
to go through the being able to exchange value level to get to that next level. There aren't a lot of
models of giving our greatest gifts all the time. But when you understand this piece of seeing
where the other person is coming from, then you can give your greatest gift because you can help
them get what they truly want much faster and easier.

• This is a philosophy. It's about compassion as the most important business skill. It's about
challenging yourself to evolve past where you are and really work on this as a practice over time.

• When you're feeling frustrated, immediately go into empathy. Ask yourself how can you be
patient with yourself as you learn to do this thing, and how can you manufacture confidence and
optimism, seeing how far you've come in life, so you can stay on the course? How can you get
support from other people? This is critical and will help you immensely along the way.
