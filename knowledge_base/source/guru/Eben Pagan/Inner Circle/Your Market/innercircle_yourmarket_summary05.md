YOUR MARKET: Session 5
Power Words That Boost Sales II
In this session, you'll learn the very first thing you need to understand about your prospect, why
certain words have more "sales punch" than others, and a famous copywriters #1 "power word" that
you'll want to start using right away.

• One way to really hone in on your market is to realize that people have a wide range of definitions
for words we commonly use. Take the word "success" for example. For some people, success in
the emotional realm means controlling their emotions. But for others it's being totally in the flow
of emotions. For some people, success in the mental realm is always learning and thinking. To
others it's to cease their thinking altogether. If the continuums are that wide for a word like
"success," you imagine how diﬀerent they are for other words... words that you THINK mean the
same thing to you and your prospect. The individuals words you use make a huge diﬀerence in
the impact on your market. More specifically, the right individual words can dramatically take the
relationship you have with your prospects to the next level. Most words don't connect.

• If I'm engaging in a sales activity, the first thing I want to understand is what are your hot button
words and concepts. What are the situations you've been in that trigger your positive and
negative emotions... and what are the words you use to describe these experiences? Then I'm
going to weave these words into what I'm saying. What's amazing is I can use words and phrases
that sound totally weird to me as I'm saying them, but because the other person uses them, they
make perfect sense and the other person goes right along with me. It's not weird to them. It's the
words they actually use.

• Here's an exercise: Take a minute and write down the individual words that your prospect uses
that you believe have the highest emotional value for them (that relate to the problem their
having, of course). Then go back through and rank each word 1-10 in terms of it's emotional
punch from the perspective of your prospect.

• Which word is more powerful: "ugly" or "love"? The answer is ugly. Why? Ugly is specific - we can
SEE it. Love is abstract and general. That's why it has less impact. Focus on concrete, specific
words in your copy.

• John Carlton is one of the world's most respected and admired copywriters. One of this favorite
words is "humiliate." It's specific and triggers powerful emotions. Are your prospects humiliated
by something related to the problem you can solve? If so, use it.

• I also highly recommend you get Frank Luntz's book “Words That Work”. It's a killer resource for
discovering words that really trigger emotional response.
