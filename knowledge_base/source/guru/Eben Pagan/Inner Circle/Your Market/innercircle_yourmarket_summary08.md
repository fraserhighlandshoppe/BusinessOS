YOUR MARKET: Session 8
Hot Seat: Crafting Your Unique
Selling Proposition
In this session, I work one on one with two Inner Circle members helping them to craft killer Unique
Selling Propositions for their businesses. The first member is an auto part business, the other is a
fulfillment service for information marketers. With both of these hot seats, you'll learn a ton about
crafting a USP for your own business that can take it to the next level.

• One of the concepts I introduce here I call The Straight Line principle. If your prospect is in one
spot, and where they want to go is somewhere else, your product or service should be a straight
line from where they are to where they want to go.

• Ideally in their mind, they almost equate their result with your stuﬀ. Whatever it is you're selling IS
the result they're looking for or the thing they want to avoid.

• So when we talk about USP's like "Fresh Hot Pizza Delivered In 30 Minutes, Guaranteed," it's a
straight line to the outcome the prospect is trying to achieve.

• Even some business names, like 3 Day Blinds, is a straight line to the thing the prospects want.
And there are product names too, like a bug killer called "Kills Bugs Dead." This gets right to the
bottom line.

• How can you word your USP for your prospects, and how can you word the name of your product,
in a way that uses the specific words and phrases that your prospect uses?
