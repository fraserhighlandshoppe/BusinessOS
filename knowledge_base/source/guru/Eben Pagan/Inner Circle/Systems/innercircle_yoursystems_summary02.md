YOUR SYSTEMS: Session 2
Using Systems Thinking To Change
Your Life
In this session, you'll learn the danger of quick fixes and instant gratification, the 2 levels of thinking
people use to solve problems (one of these actually makes things worse - you'll discover which one),
and why doing the more of the thing you want to stop doing can be the answer to banishing it from
your life forever.

• People tend to miss the system in which they're operating, and instead flip back and forth
between individual components that like or don't like.

• For example, a woman is in relationship with a man, but there's one area where she's not happy:
he's more of a homebody than she'd like. So, she breaks up and quickly finds a guy who loves
adventure. But after a couple of years she sees he's a complete mess in other areas, then she
realizes how good she had it before with the homebody. So, she breaks up and quickly finds a
diﬀerent guy who's more of a homebody. We bounce and go to the thing we feel is missing, but
we miss out on the bigger picture of what's going on.

• The human system is spectacular at idealizing and rationalizing and saying this is the thing that's
missing and that's why it's not working. So be careful of quick fixes, instant gratification, and takea- magic-pill outcomes. Remember that long term eﬀects are diﬀerent and often opposite of
short term eﬀects. You're sick, you take a pill, then another, and another, and pretty soon the pills
themselves make you even more sick. But what's going on at a system level is you're looking for a
quick fix. That's often the core issue.

• I used to know someone who was a little idealistic. She'd talk about health issues, and always
mentioned "laser" solutions for everything. That was her metaphor. We need metaphors to
perceive things in certain ways. Metaphors allow us to perceive things in way we wouldn't be able
to without them. If you get a metaphor that's not useful and it spreads out to everything else like instant gratification thinking - it can cause you to not go to work at systemic issues. You'll
think "They'll be a quick fix - I'll work on that down the road."

• When you're facing a challenge, problem or opportunity, and you'd like to achieve results, you
can think about in on 2 levels. One is the Action Level, the other is the Condition Level, conditions
you can set up.

• If you focus on the Action Level, you're typically sub-optimized. You're just doing one thing to try
to push against a system, and usually the system is going to push back. Whereas if you work on
the Condition Level, you're setting up conditions that will ideally change the system and change
it in a more long term way.

challenge you've faced in your life that you've tried to solve a few times. Next, write down 3 ways

• Milton Erickson used to do something very interesting when people would come in to talk to him.
People would say something like, "You know Milton, I've been to all these diﬀerent doctors, I've
tried every diet, and I can't lose weight. I weigh 300 pounds. I just can't live my life like this
anymore. I should way 150 pounds." Milton would say, "Well, I can help you. But you have to
commit to executing the treatment exactly as I prescribe, or else I won't work with you." They say,
"Of course, I'll do anything you tell me to do. I've tried so hard and nothing works."

• Milton would say, "You're not going to like what I'm going to tell you to do. The first thing you
need to do is get up to 310 pounds. So you need to go and eat even more than what you're
eating right now. And then come back and see me when you weigh 310 pounds." So the person
would go home and eat and eat and get themselves to 310 pounds. Then they'd come back.
Milton would say, "Ok. Perfect. You've done it. Now leave." They'd leave, and they'd then lose all
their weight without doing anything else. It's super counterintuitive.

• This is what Milton called "Prescribe the Symptom." In human chimp systems, we're often out of
control of our instant gratification mechanisms, our belief systems, our behaviors, our cravings...
just about everything. With the person who can't lose the weight and has been to specialists,
there's something more that's going on here. Each of the specialists probably gave them a plan
that would have worked, but they couldn't execute it, they couldn't control themselves. Milton
realized this. So he prescribed the system - he told them to do something they were probably
already going to do - gain more weight - but by doing it intentionally they took control of
themselves and got to experience being in control of their weight. They now felt they had some
control over their weight, making easier for them to lose the weight they couldn't before.
