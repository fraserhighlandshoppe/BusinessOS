YOUR SYSTEMS: Session 10
The Importance Of Key "Levers"
In this session, you'll learn the power of having a few key "levers" in your business (and what they
are), how to get a massive boost in your bottom line very rapidly and, how to focus the creative
energies of your team on real problems and real results.

• We're getting to the end of Inner Circle. If you’ve noticed, I'm telling you to do just a couple of
simple things. They include putting a few numbers up on a dashboard and tracking them daily,
doing daily updates with your team, getting together and have a daily meeting, etc.

• This is all stuﬀ I said way back in the first Altitude program, it's stuﬀ I teach in some of our
beginner free programs that I give away, and it's stuﬀ I talk about all the time. Why am I sharing
them with you now? Because, for the most part, we don't DO these things RITUALISTICALLY and
we don't do them with intention.

• What I want to communicate is that I haven't figured out a lot of things in life that accomplish as
much stuﬀ that you want to accomplish as these little levers do. They are THE "trim tabs" of
business. They are the conditions of your business lead to all kinds of other good stuﬀ happening.
1. Working in solid chunks of uninterrupted time.
2. Owning your niche by creating a new category.
3. Identifying your avatars.
4. Using direct response marketing techniques.
5. Moving the free line.
6. Hiring only A players.
7. Implementing systems like a dashboard, daily meetings, and daily updates.

• If you do these mindsets and practices in your business, you'll get so much. And these final 3 daily
rituals are so critical to this. They're so simple that most people don't do them, even after I talk to
people about them. They think it's too simple, or they don't like meetings, or whatever, but they
can really change your whole business.

• I'm not recommending these 3 simple things because I can't come up with anything better. It's
because these are really the things that push the whole system. If you do this stuﬀ, so much of the
other stuﬀ takes care of itself. Not only that, but you avoid so many problems that you can't avoid
otherwise.

• These systems are simple - each one only takes 10-15 minutes to do every day. Little eﬀort =
massive results.

• Something else to remember is your role as a leader. Because you're a strategic thinker, you can
zoom out to a level that most people in your business can't. You have the mindset of create value
and innovate and improve... mindsets that most people in your business won't have. When you
put these few parameters in place, it prevents the people in your business from meaning well but
doing things that are counter-productive.

• For example, we used to have people come up with all sorts of well intentioned but random ideas
on how to improve the business. They weren't really connected to our daily reality. But if you have
daily meetings and metrics, a lot of the these random ideas and brainstorms don't come up. Why?
Because everyone is moving toward something, so everyone's creativity is used in real time,
working on real projects to create real results.

• If you're working on a project and someone has an idea, like perhaps the business should shift
gears and focus on a new area, you can say "Great, when we're done with this cycle of products,
keep bringing it up, and if it's a good idea maybe it'll see the light of day." Michael Eisner was
famous for not considering an idea anyone brought to him unless they brought it up to him 4 or 5
times. After that many times he knew the person was serious about making it happen.

• It's also important to work on each of the 5 areas we've been working on here in Inner Circle. Way
back when I was first marketing Altitude, one of the things I would say on the partner teleclasses
is when you're growing a business from one to the ten million dollar level, if you're not working
on all 5 of these area simultaneously, you'll run into snags. And you won't be able to see what it is.
You'll blame it on something, but you can't see what it is because you don't have the frame of
reference for it.

• But, when you do each of these 5 things, you're firing on all cylinders and all the rest of the stuﬀ
will take care of itself. You'll have so many plants in your garden that the weeds won't have any
place to take root. That's how important these simple things are. Sometimes you have to really
think about them and experience a lot until you realize how important they are.

• I used to get really frustrated when people in my life would come to me and say "I have this
challenge." I'd tell them what to do, but they wouldn't do it, and the problem wouldn't go away,
and they'd come back to me with the same problem. But then I learned about the idea that the
way we sometimes need to learn is by screwing things up multiple times, and then we realize we
have to do something diﬀerent.

• So, now I'll give them a metaphor and tell them to keep it tucked away in the back of their head.
Then I tell them to keep trying the way they're trying things, and if it keeps not working,
remember the metaphor... pull it out... and try it at some point. This has been working much
better. They get to own the success more this way too, instead of me hammering at them about
the way I think they should do it.
