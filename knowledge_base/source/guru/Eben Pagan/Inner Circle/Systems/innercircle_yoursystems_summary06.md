YOUR SYSTEMS: Session 6
The Highest Leverage Systems
In Any Business
In this session, you'll learn how to unleash the power of rituals in your business, the 3 systems I
recommend you begin in your business IMMEDIATELY, and a fascinating cycle of human
development that explains why most people struggle so much in life (and how you can avoid this).

• Joe Stumpf used to share an interesting model. According to this model, we start out idealistic in
any situation. We think we know the way things are - our view is often simple and utopian. Next
we move to frustrated. That person or the world isn't what we thought it was. Next, we become
defiant. We won't take it anymore. We get angry and blame. Some people get stuck here and even
become arrogant about their defiance. This is where most people live in life.

• But, if you want to move past it, you move on to Resigned. We give in. We're exhausted. And then
we move on to Aware. We recognize there's a new way to see this and that there are other options
we didn't see before. Things makes sense in a new way. Then we become Decisive. Now that we're
aware of other ways of seeing, we choose one perspective and get on a new path. Then we
become Committed to that new path.

• After looking at this model for awhile, I noticed that the people who are the most committed are
often the most idealistic. I think the sweet spot to be in between Aware and Decisive as much as
possible. If you want to stay out of the challenges of this whole cycle, try to stay here. Look at all
the options you have, choose something, but immediately have the attitude of simply wanting to
learn a lot, not being too committed or attached, because new ways of seeing things are going to
come up.

• I'm always asking myself where's the leverage - where do you exert the smallest amount of force
and get the biggest result? My experience so far is that there are a few key systems you can set up
in your business, and if you understand the theoretical aspects behind them, you can make them
even more powerful. One of these systems is rituals... doing things ritually.

• What most people ask is, "What do I do?" What I ask is "What ritual do I do?" In other words, what
do I do not once, but every day? Human beings have a challenging time doing things that they
don't do every day. We're challenged to do anything with any type of skill or mastery that we
don't do daily. I once heard a great guitar player say "If I skip a day of practice, I can tell. If I skip
two days, other people can tell." If you're not doing the thing daily in your business - and other
things weekly and monthly as well - both as an individual and as a group, you're missing a lot of
leverage and you're gong to have a hard time "herding the cats."

• At the end of this training I'm going to be encouraging you to do basically 3 things: a morning
meeting, daily updates, and a daily dashboard. This is about setting up the conditions that'll have
the most leverage, and making it inevitable that you get the results you want. Take morning
meetings, example. Well, people hate meetings. But if you do a meeting in the morning - even a
short 15 minute meeting - and you just have each person check in and give a quick update on
