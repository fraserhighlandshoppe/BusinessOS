YOU: Session 11
Leveraging The Power Of Your Mind
In this session, you'll learn how to stop sabotaging your own success, what it means to
"communicate with the transcendent," and the three gems we all have that are the keys to taking our
entire life to the next level.

• Some people claim that their mind sabotages their success. I believe this isn't exactly what's
happening. I think what's really going on is that success, almost by definition, is always some
where other than where we are. It always entails going someplace else, to another level. But the
mind loves things the way they are. It's not that it’s sabotage, it's that it avoids anything that
creates change.

• So, the question then becomes, are you your mind? Can you observer you mind? What part of you
is observing your mind? I don't have an answer. I just live my life, being aware while I'm living. But
I do believe that the mind resists change.

• Your mind tries to protect you by attracting you to doing the same things over and over. And
that's helpful, up to a certain point. After that, we have to realize the areas where it's no longer
serving us, and make new choices.

• Sometimes we avoid what we fear, but other times we actively seek out fear (think of skydiving).
The thing is, we can seek out fear in our everyday interactions with other people, and get a similar
"rush" without spending money on professionally organized events (like skydiving).

• Wyatt said something that I'm going to "geek out" on for years. He said "communicate with the
transcendent." What does this mean? I think it means communicate with something that is
beyond you, at a higher developmental level.

• When you get to a higher level of evolution, you start having a hard time finding people who are
transcendent... who are way beyond you. And when you do find them, it's hard to get their
attention, because so many other people are trying to get their attention. But try to find your next
level, whether it's an idea, or a process, or spirit, something that is at least one development level
higher than where you are now. Then figure out how to communicate with the transcendent.

• There are precious few golden eggs that your mind/body system creates. One is Awareness, or the
"ah ha" experience. This is when you see things diﬀerently. The awareness window is open. You
see what you need to do in at least one area to transcend to the next level. The window is open.
Tomorrow morning when you'll wake up, you'll feel refreshed, like you made progress, but it won't
be the same feeling of awareness as it is now. The next day you'll still feel good, but the feeling
will be weaker and weaker.

• The solution is when that window of awareness opens, right there create the new ritual or put the
new attractor in place so you change your patterns of behavior... so you turn the momentary
higher level of awareness into a new habitual way of feeling and behaving that serves you at a
higher level.

• In addition to Awareness, another gem we get is Will Power. We get very little of it. Most of us
squander the little we have, through resisting temptations, stress, fighting our body's natural
rhythm, etc.

• So, the next step is to focus our Will Power where we get the highest pay oﬀ - making new
empowering habits. We all do the same things every day, essentially, so if we want to go to the
next level one of the most powerful things we can do is to upgrade the quality of our habits.
Focus our Will Power on one new behavior every 30 days.

• Finally, we have the ability to Focus... to pay attention to one thing at a time. It's important to
eliminate distractions and interruptions, and focus our mind on our biggest opportunity in
chunks of at least 90-120 minutes.

• One example of this is to focus the first 2 hours of every day on your most important task. Then
completely unplug from that activity, so you avoid the feeling that the task is controlling you.
