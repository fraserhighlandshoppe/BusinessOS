```markdown
Move The Free Line Exercise
What is your prospect’s biggest fear or frustration - and what is
the specific outcome or result that they want to achieve?
Start with the biggest motivator that your prospect has, and make sure that it is tangible,
situational, and concrete - not abstract or subjective.

What is the most valuable insight you’ve had for getting that
outcome or result? How do you implement it?
Take your insight, then create a series of steps to go through in order to get the result that
your prospect wants... and build a simple method with these action steps.

Create a piece of educational content for achieving that result,
then put it into a report, video, webinar or other format that
allows you to give it away to the masses for free...
And use this piece as your “magnet” to get people to visit your website and opt-in,
subscribe or otherwise follow you. Remember that you’ll still need to use your marketing
super-powers to “sell” this valuable free advice.
```
