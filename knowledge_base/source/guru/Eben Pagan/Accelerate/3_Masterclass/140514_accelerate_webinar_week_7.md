What Is Marketing, Anyway?
★ Marketing: Communication that gets customers
★ Marketing can be very direct, very subtle, very simple,
very complex...
★ Great marketing, whatever its form, is typically the result
of deep understanding of customer needs combined
with a knowledge of how to use psychology

Words Are The “Bridge”...
★ When trying to reach people with your marketing
message, the words you choose will ultimately be the
most powerful tools you have
★ Let’s learn about how people use (and misuse) words and how to find the most powerful words to motivate
our customers to buy what we’re selling...

The Real vs. The Intangible
★ In marketing, we are very interested in what actually works
to get customers to buy - not what we hope works, or
what we think might work
★ A key distinction to make in your mind is the difference
between “real, tangible” things and “abstract,
intangible” things
★ Knowing this distinction allows you to communicate with
customers far more powerfully

Words vs. “Real” Things...
★ Until around age 11, children cannot distinguish between a
word and the thing it describes
★ For instance, a child believes that the sun itself, as well as the
name “sun” are the same thing (they also tend to believe
that they think with their mouths)
★ Even after we grow up, most people don’t differentiate
between names and things... most of the time

Which One Is Talking About “Reality”?
★ An advertisement telling you that you’ll get into
better shape and feel better about yourself...
★ Or an advertisement telling you that you’ll lose 20
pounds of belly fat
★ ...and which one would be more motivating to a
person who wanted to lose weight?

Product Features vs. Results
★ As the creators of our products and services, we tend to “fall
in love” with what we’re selling
★ As part of the falling in love process, we become convinced
that our product’s “features” are magic jewels that
everyone covets
★ Instead of talking about our product and its features, it’s
much more powerful to talk about the result that your
customer will enjoy

Emotional “Trigger Words”
★ As adults, we tend to have multiple words to describe the
same thing - my wife is wife, Annie, partner, best friend,
lover, mother of my daughter, beauty itself, etc.
★ Some words will have a higher “emotion value” than other
words - these are the words we’re after
★ In particular, we’re looking for words that trigger the
strongest emotions inside of our customers

Project Into Customer
★ Imagine what’s happening in their lives one minute
before they realize that they need your product or
service
★ What emotion are they feeling?
★ What words and phrases would trigger that same
emotion if they read them or heard them in your
marketing?

Nightmares
★ When you consider what would be a “nightmare
scenario” for your customer, what are the words that
they would use to describe it?
★ Pay careful attention to use words that are about real,
tangible things and situations - not abstract,
intangible things and situations

Fantasies
★ When you consider what would be a “fantasy
scenario” for your customer, what are the words that
they would use to describe it?
★ Again, pay careful attention to use words that are
about real, tangible things and situations - not
abstract, intangible things and situations

Ranking Your Trigger Words

words and phrases you believe will trigger the highest
emotional response from your customers
★ Now rank them on a scale of 1-100, with 100 being
the highest rating, meaning it triggers the highest
level of emotional response
★ Use the highest value words in your marketing...
