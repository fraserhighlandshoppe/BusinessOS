```markdown
The Rhythms Of Success Exercise
Daily Huddle
When can you have your daily huddle each day, and what is your simple outline to align and
organize everyone on your team? TIP: Repeat your top company priorities at the beginning,
to get maximum alignment value.

Daily Updates
Things to have included in daily updates: 1) What I did today, results I got, 2) Obstacle and
challenges I ran into, 3) Questions I have, 4) My current priorities. Who could you have start
sending you a daily update starting immediately?

Daily Dashboards
When you look at daily numbers, view them over time in a chart or graph, so you can see the
trends over time. If possible, have each person on your team update at least one “number”
or metric that they personally drive or impact. Who could be the owners of the key metrics in
your business?
```
