Seeing The Future
★ As an Entrepreneurial leader, you are the keeper of a
very special flame - the vision of a better future
★ It’s my experience that most people don’t have the
natural ability to envision a future that’s different
from the one that exists now
★ As the leader, your ability to envision a better future,
then translate that vision to others, is priceless

The Challenge Of Leading
★ As an Entrepreneurial leader, you must see and create a
future that doesn’t yet exist - for you and for others
★ One of the biggest psychological challenges you’ll face is
the living into a reality that others cannot yet understand
or imagine, which creates pressure on you
★ The ability to keep yourself believing in the future
you’re creating, even when others don’t, is key

Add Another “Believer”
★ To keep yourself motivated and sane, find at least one
person who sees and believes in the future vision that you
can see for yourself and for your customers
★ Have a regular conversation with that person - ideally at
least once a week - to talk about that future you’re in the
process of creating
★ You just need one other believer to keep yourself on track!

Helping Others Create & Produce
★ We’ve learned about the importance of creativity
and productivity in your role as an Entrepreneur
★ As an Entrepreneurial leader today, I believe that your
key role is helping others to increase their creativity
and productivity
★ If others are more creative and productive when they
are around you, they’ll seek you out as leader

Creativity & Productivity Contexts
★ As we’ve learned, the context, setting or environment you
create for yourself and others is critical to producing high
levels of creativity and productivity
★ The vision that you set, and the values that you establish,
are the most important contexts that you will create inside
your business - for yourself and others
★ Let’s use leadership skills to clarify our vision and values...

Decisions & Values
★ In your role as a leader, you must continually make decisions
about what is most important, and what is most valuable
★ The job of deciding is about “chopping up” reality into little
pieces, then choosing which ones are important
★ The job of establishing and reinforcing values is also about
choosing what’s most important, but at a more abstract
level - for your customers, team members, and business

The Dreaded 49/51 Decision
★ Many decisions are challenging to make because there’s no
obvious “winner” to choose
★ I call these “49/51 decisions” because you stand to gain
49% if you go one way, and 51% if you go the other way
★ In these cases, it’s important to get on with making the
decision, because the delay becomes the most expensive
part of the game (our experience with email delivery)

Decision Making Is A Practice
★ Each time you make a decision in your business, you are
practicing a skill that you’ll use for your entire life
★ Most people avoid making decisions until they are forced to
make them - which usually puts them at great disadvantage
★ As you become more pro-active about making decisions,
you’ll become more confident, and build esteem about your
ability to make good decisions

What Do You Value Most?
★ Clarifying your own values - so you clearly identify and
acknowledge what you want most from your business - is
key to actually achieving it
★ Remember: Your values change over time, so don’t get
“hung up” on them, but do clarify them regularly
★ Do you want more money to buy things? More free time?
More control over your life? More security for your family?

What Do Your Customers Value Most?
★ When you “zoom out” and look at what your customers
want to move toward or away from most, what do you
see?
★ When you talk to your customers, what do they ask for
most - or complain about most?
★ What are you currently working on for them, that you
assume is the most important value that they have?

What Are Your 49/51 Decisions?
★ What are the decisions in your life right now that
are 49/51 decisions - that probably won’t make
a huge difference either way if you choose?
★ What does your gut tell you is the best decision,
based on your own intuition of what will
probably work out best in the long-run?

Choose Your #1 Value
★ Make a list of all of the benefits you’d like to get
from your business
★ Pair them up into decision contests, and then actually
choose the most important priority from each pair,
and go on to the next levels until you’re left with one
★ Write down your #1 value on a post-it, and put it on
your computer or in your work space to see every day

Choose Your Customer’s #1 Value
★ Write a list of the key outcomes, results, relief and
benefits that your customers want
★ Pair them into decision contests, and consciously
choose which you believe is most important
★ Talk live with a minimum of a dozen customers in the
next week, and ask them to choose their highest value
result or benefit that they want, and compare to your list

Choose Your #1 Priority
★ What are the most important projects that you
have to complete in your business right now?
★ Make a decision contest by pairing them, and
find the single most important priority you have
★ Put conditions and contexts in place to make it
inevitable that you’ll complete your priority!

Find Another Co-Believer
★ Who could you add as a “co-believer” - that
would help keep you inspired about creating the
future you’re building for yourself, your business,
and your customers?
★ How can you reach out to them right now to
set up a meeting to talk about the future?
