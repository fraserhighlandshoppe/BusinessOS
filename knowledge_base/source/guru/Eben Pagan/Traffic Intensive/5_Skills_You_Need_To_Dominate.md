The 5 Skills You Need to Dominate Any
Marketing Channel

Key Points:
1.

In this program you will focus on 5 key skills that are the foundation for your marketing,
coaching and information product business:
• Identifying the hot button motivators of your prospects
• Stating those motivators in compelling ways
• Turning those powerful hot button statements and motivators into ads and content
that draw people in
• Designing those ads and content so they are trustable
• Taking those ads and content and getting them out to the world, and in front of lots
of partners

2.

If you want to get people to put your ads and content on their websites and blogs, in
front of a lot of people, then you need to design great ads and content. You need to script
great ads and content. You need to make compelling statements that draw people in.
You need to know your prospects’ emotional motivators.

3.

Getting customers is about creating ads - that first thing someone sees from you. It has
to get their attention and get them to say, “Hey, that is what I need. That looks like it
could solve my problem.”

4.

“Psychological Biases”: we come wired to interpret things in one way and not another.
We walk around in the world seeing some things and not others, responding to some
things and not noticing others. These cognitive biases running subconsciously are
driving you.
• Tangibility bias: humans are more motivated when others speak about something
out in the real world that is meaningful to them. The more tangible you are in your
communication, the less likely you are to be misunderstood
• Abstracting: you see something happen, then you make a word or symbol out of it
that is a shortcut for the things that actually happened - an “abstraction.” The
symbol in your head isn’t the same as the real thing. The more abstract a person’s
communication, the less likely another person will understand what they are
talking about. You could be thinking about a totally different thing than the
recipient of your message

5.

“Perspectiving”: using the simulator of your mind to to look at a situation from as many
angles as possible. Imagine what a situation is like from another’s perspective... in
detail.
