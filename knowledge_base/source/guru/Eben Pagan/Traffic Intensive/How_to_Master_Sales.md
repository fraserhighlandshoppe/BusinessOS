How do sales and marketing fit together? Sales is typically one-on-one. Marketing is
typically one piece going out to a large number of people. Advertising is salesmanship in
print.
It is important to start out selling and coaching. You will learn a lot, very quickly. Most
importantly, look for the common denominators in how clients learn and behave. You
will learn what motivates clients and what they are likely to do when you work with
many one-to-one.
Once you learn this, take it, and create your marketing/advertising. The first words in
your marketing piece MUST reach through the clutter, grab the prospects attention, and
talk about the tangible things they want.
Also, when you think about your coaching offer, you must consider three things:
1. Can they do it?
2. Will they do it?
3. Are they likely to do it?
It’s not “it works if humans do it.” You need to find out “if humans will actually do it.”
What is the ultimate benefit that you are delivering to your customer that is like money
or cash, and as tangible as a $100 bill. One word. Write 5 headlines that start with the
word “Get” and end with the word “Now” (with the tangible benefit in between).
For “away motivated people” start with the word “Stop.”
In advertising, you can talk about the conceptual and abstract IF you’ve already talked
about tangible and concrete. Especially when you are first getting a prospects attention.
