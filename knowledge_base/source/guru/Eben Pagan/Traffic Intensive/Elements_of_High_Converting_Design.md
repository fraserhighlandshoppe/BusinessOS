The Elements of High-Converting Design

Key Points:
1.

Design is about enhancing whatever it is that you are trying to do, and making an ad
work better.

2.

Don’t overuse caps, underlines, exclamation points, etc. without purpose.

3.

Professional design principles include:
• Simplicity - strip out anything that doesn’t have purpose
• Dominant visual elements and hierarchy - there should be a large, main thing that
first catches the eye, then a secondary thing
• Eye gravity layouts - read top to bottom or left to right. Most important thing
should be top left
• Typography principles - legibility (can read a word), readable (able to read a long
block of copy)
• Drop letters and indents are easier to read
• Don’t use more than one or two fonts
• Don’t use more than one or two text alignments
• An appealing video template that enhances your marketing message
