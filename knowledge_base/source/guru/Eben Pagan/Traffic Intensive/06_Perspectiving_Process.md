Identifying The “Ultimate Influencer”
In any situation, there are many “players.” Your customer has many different people around them who
influence their self‐image, emotions and decision‐making process. Your power of these influencers is
essentially entirely unknown to your customer. One of these influencers exerts MUCH stronger
influence than the others. In this exercise, we’ll identify the strongest influencer and then focus in on
the part of their influence that you can use to create powerful marketing and advertising.
This exercise involves using the skill of “perspectiving” ‐ looking at the world, a person or a situation
from the perspective of several different other people.
Answer the questions below ‐ again, from the perspective of your customer...

The “Perspectiving Process”
Who are all of the other people in the “Situational Drama” around your current
problem or desire? (List at least 7 different people, roles, etc.)

Choose the most influential person from the list above, then ask yourself: What
do you FEAR that this person will find out, think or feel if they “find out” the
“truth” about your situation?

What do you HOPE they think and feel - what is the facade that you’re trying to
keep up to maintain their approval, keep your power in the relationship, and
avoid rejection and insignificance?

What are the words and phrases that would best trigger your “emotional hot
button” around this influential person and what they think or feel about you - or
what they might do if they find out “the truth”?
