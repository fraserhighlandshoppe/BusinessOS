Interview With Opher Brayer
1. Methodology: a set of necessary formulas in a necessary order to get a specific result.
2. Good networkers have Charisma. People want to be around them. Part of Charisma is
1) Having a vision you're passionate about and a plan to get there.
2) The ability to tell good stories, especially as they relate to your vision.
1. Which words you put an accent on makes a diﬀerence on your impact.
2. Start out conversations with telling a great story about your vision. When the person is listening to
your story, listen to what their body is telling you... and ask them questions about them.
◦ Example: "I went with this good friend to get sushi last week - do you like sushi? - we went to this
sushi place called Zenbu - you even been there? - we were talking about a book by Eben - do you
know Eben? - he wrote book called The Laws Of Success - have you read it? - ...."
3. As you're talking to people, do a "needs analysis" to uncover needs they have so you may suggest
people who can help them.
◦ Common needs are: money, career, health, family, love relationships, sports, etc.
◦ Focus your mind on how to contribute to the person in front of you. Give it for free
4. Use this phrase: "I know someone who can help you..."
5. Opher's MasterMind Methodology:
◦ Meetings are exactly 90 minutes
◦ Before the session, send an overview of the situation to the group: what the problem is (in 7 words
or less), strengths you bring to it, weaknesses, opportunities, etc.
◦ 1 person in the group is taking time. 1 person is making sure the group follows the rules. 1 person
is taking notes. The rest of the 6 people actively participate
◦ First three minutes: you re-state the problem
◦ Next 15 minutes: you explain the rest of what you already sent to the group. No one else talks. Talk
for 15 minutes. The note taker has two columns on her paper: on the right side is what you say, on
the left side is questions and reactions to what you say. You are quiet for the rest of the session
◦ Next 10 minutes: each of the 6 people must ask a question. Then no more questions
◦ Next 10 minutes: people are as negative as they'd like about the information they have. Don't sugar
coat. Don't lead with positive. Just be negative. 90 seconds each of the 6 people
◦ Next 10 minutes: be as positive as possible. But don't give ideas
◦ Next 10 minutes: build up ideas/solutions
◦ Then look at ideas, what is the emotional reaction to these solutions
◦ Then the group suggest which solutions should come first, second etc.
◦ Then, people share what their intuition says - will it succeed or not?

6. You don't need 10,000 people to be your friends, you only need a very small but highly influential
group of friends. It's OK to have a ton of Twitter or Facebook friends, but it's not as important as
having your small group of close friends. It's the quality of people in your network, not number. For
example, if you have Bill Gates and Obama in your network, that's all you'd need, and that's just
TWO people.
7. Do anything for the people in your close network, and in exchange have them be on your advisory
board to help them with your career. They are all at least as smart or experienced as you. Your
purpose is to have the best advisory board in the world helping you with your career.
8. Action Steps: Create your vision, write your stories that relate to your stories, tell your stories to
people you meet, study personality types, practice doing needs analysis, suggest people who can help
them, find people to start your mastermind group.
