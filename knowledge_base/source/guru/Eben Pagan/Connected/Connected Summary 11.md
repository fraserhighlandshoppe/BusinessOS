SESSION
11:
Interview With James Owens
1. Tell people of the cool, contribution based project you're working on, and ask them if they know
anyone who has interests or skills in that area.
2. Most people don't network... they take. Not cool, and not eﬀective. Networking is a two way street,
even if it's just a sincere thank you after the meeting.
3. Be cognizant that famous people are extremely busy and many people want things from them.
Realize you're not the most important thing in their life. Be "politely persistent." Listen for
opportunities to give back to them, even something small, like a book. Show that you appreciate
talking to them. Write a personal thank you note afterwards. Write thank you notes to the assistants
too.
◦ Hand-write your thank you notes. No one takes the time to do this, so it makes you stand out
◦ Thank you notes have been huge for James. Some people even comment that his thank you note
was the FIRST they had ever received
4. Most people are afraid of networking because they see it as selling. But it's not selling... so long as it
isn't focused excessively on YOU.
5. Have a strong 30-second Elevator Pitch: why you admire them, and why you're interested in getting
them specifically to contribute to a project.
◦ It helps to always have a current project that acts as a conduit for future relationships. Projects
create a reason why to connect and work together
6. "Use People To Connect You To More People": At the end of James' interviews, he asks if they know
other people who would make for good interviews. About 97% of people are willing to give him
names. So although the first few interviews were tough to get, now they're much easier because
they're referral based. You can do the same.
7. If you're looking for a job, instead of spending 80% of your time posting your resume online, spend
80% of your time doing information interviews about other people's jobs and how they became
successful in their job. This is a great work to build your connections.
8. Don't just thank people because you want something from them. Thank them because you mean it.
9. If you have the passion for something - if you're not faking it - if you really care - other people will feel
it. You will attract them because they will see you're genuine.
