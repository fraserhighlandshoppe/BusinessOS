# Guru Product Plan Summary 07 Crystal Clear Communication(1).pdf

```markdown
Session 7: Crystal Clear
Communication (and How
Most Experts Mess It Up)
1. There are 2 Big Mistakes we make when evaluating people:
a. We think everyone is the same, or...
b. We think everyone is different. In reality we are all a combination of
same and different.
2. “Show your client a spoon.” Get out of abstract world of signs, and focus
instead on tangible, concrete and observable results in the real world.
3. What are the emotional and irrational, but concrete and tangible moments
that demonstrate that something is wrong? What is the specific evidence in
the real world that the problem is occurring? For example, “He doesn’t look
me in the eyes any more when we argue.”
4. What is the sign in the “real world” that the problem is solved, that shows
proof of the desired result? What evidence can your prospect look for to know
your solution worked?
5. Get less abstract in your teaching and marketing - drill down and get more
specific.
6. Human beings become small minded when they become emotional – they are
drawn irrationally to specific solutions they are sure will make them happy.
They can’t see the big picture. As a marketer, you have to enter at their reality.
```
