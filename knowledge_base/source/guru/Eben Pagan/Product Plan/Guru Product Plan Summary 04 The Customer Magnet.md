# Guru Product Plan Summary 04 The Customer Magnet.pdf

```markdown
Session 4: The Customer
Magnet: Your "Magic
Bullet" Technique
1. “Perspectiving” is switching from your perspective to another’s in a fluid way.
Turning yourself off and imagining what it is like to be another person.
2. What technique or method could you design that is as close to an idealized
“magic pill” as possible? A magic bullet technique so targeted and compelling
that your prospect would say, “That was designed for me!”
3. Match the technique you create to what they would imagine is the ultimate
solution.
4. Humans want solutions to their problems – solutions that sound like they were
created by someone just like them. Someone who understands them.
5. Get into the reality of the other human being; don’t demand them to come in
to your reality.
6. Here’s an example of a Magic Bullet: “The Kiss Test: How to Know When a
Woman is Ready to be Kissed.”
7. Behind every issue you are working on are two underlying drives: 1) survival
and 2) reproduction. These are deep-seeded reptilian brain drives.
8. What can you teach the prospect to get them as close as possible to the
“Priceless Answer” you created in Session 3’s exercise?
9. What can you call the technique you teach (in the language of the Prospect)?
```
