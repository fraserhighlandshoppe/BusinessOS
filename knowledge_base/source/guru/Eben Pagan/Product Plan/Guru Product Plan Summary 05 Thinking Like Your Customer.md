# Guru Product Plan Summary 05 Thinking Like Your Customer.pdf

```markdown
Session 5: Thinking Like
Your Customer
1. “Cognitive Biases” occur when we see behavior in others that we don’t see in
ourselves.
2. A good marketer asks: “What is going on with my customer, and how can I
help them get their perceived need met?”
3. “Content Marketing” is a new type of marketing based on giving high-value
information to the prospect.
4. A “Self Contained Concept” takes an idea, unpacks it and transfers it in a
NEW way to the Prospect. It gives the Prospect an “ah hah” as they see their
situation in a new way.
5. Ask your prospect, “What is it like to be you?”
6. “The Self-Contained Concept Formula” includes everything you need to
include in a concept to have it stand alone:
a. Introduce the concept by talking about the problem it solves (in terms
of the problem THEY want solved and the result THEY are looking for).
b. Mention why your new solution is uniquely appealing.
c. Describe the specific tangible result and the ease and speed to expect it
in.
d. Describe the problem or need in more detail, in a voice the prospect can
relate to. This is where you demonstrate that you “get” what’s going on.
e. Describe the insight or trick that allows your prospect to get a new view
into the problem. What is the power point within the technique or
concept? The “Aaaaah…you just showed me a new perspective!”
f. Direct with specific action steps about how to calibrate whether
application of your concept is working or not. Tell them what to expect
and what feedback loops to look for.
7. Give your customer the feeling of being understood. They should feel as
though you are talking to them in their native tongue. That’s when you
become attractive and persuasive to them.
```
