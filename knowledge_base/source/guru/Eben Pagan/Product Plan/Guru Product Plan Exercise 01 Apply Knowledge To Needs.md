# Guru Product Plan Exercise 01 Apply Knowledge To Needs.pdf

```markdown
Apply Knowledge To Needs,
Not Needs To Knowledge
Most	
  “Experts”	
  create	
  their	
  content	
  and	
  products	
  in	
  exactly	
  the	
  “wrong	
  direction.”	
  They	
  take	
  all	
  of	
  
their	
  knowledge	
  and	
  try	
  to	
  explain	
  it	
  in	
  a	
  way...	
  so	
  that	
  a	
  customer	
  could	
  take	
  it	
  and	
  then	
  apply	
  the	
  
knowledge	
  to	
  their	
  situation.	
  This	
  requires	
  the	
  customer	
  to	
  do	
  a	
  lot	
  of	
  the	
  work	
  of	
  interpretation	
  and	
  
application.	
  Instead,	
  organize	
  your	
  concepts,	
  each	
  part	
  of	
  your	
  content	
  and	
  ultimately	
  each	
  of	
  your	
  
products	
  from	
  the	
  perspective	
  of	
  your	
  customer’s	
  NEED.	
  In	
  other	
  words,	
  choose	
  one	
  specific	
  need	
  that	
  
your	
  customer	
  has,	
  then	
  focus	
  all	
  of	
  your	
  knowledge	
  on	
  THAT	
  need.	
  Apply	
  your	
  knowledge	
  to	
  
individual	
  needs,	
  not	
  individual	
  needs	
  to	
  your	
  knowledge.

What is your customer’s biggest need right now?

Brainstorm how each of the different things you’ve learned as an expert in your
life... and in this area... applies to this ONE need that your customer has:
```
