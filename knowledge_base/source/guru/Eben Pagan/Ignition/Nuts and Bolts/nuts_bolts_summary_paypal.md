Nuts & Bolts:

Setting Up PayPal
Key Points:
1. The traditional way to accept customer payments online is to set up a "merchant
account"... the only problem is this takes a while and it's a big hassle. The easier,
faster way is to use PayPal "merchant services". Your customers won't need a PayPal
account - they can pay by credit card as well. The money shows up in your PayPal
account.
2. With PayPal, there is no credit application, no set-up charge, no monthly fee, and the
transaction fees are low. Plus the system is VERY secure.
3. When you first sign up with PayPal, you may need to wait 3-5 days to verify your
account. Just follow the instructions they give you.
4. To make a PayPal "buy now" button, just go to merchant services, buy now button,
enter the price, thank you page, and other info... then take the code and put it on
your website. When you're done, do a test purchase to make sure everything works
correctly.
5. To make a PayPal "subscribe" button (so people can pay you monthly), go to
merchant services, subscribe, and enter information... then take the code and put it on
your website.
6. To offer a free trial, the limitation with PayPal is that you must charge them a minimum
of TWICE after the free trial. If this works for you...
• Make a "subscribe" button... click "I want to offer a trial period"... enter zero for
the amount to bill for the trial period... then choose the length of the trial period.
• After that period, the customer will be billed the billing amount you entered for
each cycle. The limitation here is that you must charge them twice... kind of a
bummer.
7. It's important to make the PayPal Check Out Page look similar to your own site. If it
looks completely different, it will scare some of your customers away. So create a
"header" that matches your site as closely as possible.
• To do this, go to merchant services - website payments standard - feature list customer checkout experience - custom checkout pages - and follow the
instructions there.
• You may need to get a graphic designer to design your header, or just create
one using your html editor.
8. PayPal's support number is (402) 952-8899.

Action Steps:
1. If you have a product to sell, make a “buy now” or “subscribe” button... put it on
your sales page... put a header on the PayPal check out page.... and you're done!
