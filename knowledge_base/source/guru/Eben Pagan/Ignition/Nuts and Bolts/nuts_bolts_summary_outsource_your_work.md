Nuts & Bolts:

How to Outsource Your Work
to Elance and Other Cool
Sites
With Allie Longoria
Key Points:
1. Why hire/outsource now?
• You are starting or running a business…NOT a hobby
• You need to start somewhere – sooner rather than later
• If you’re serious about this, you need to treat it like a business
• Working from home doesn’t have to mean working alone
• There’s way too much to learn and way too much to do…you can’t do it all on
your own
2. Who to hire vs. who NOT to hire
• Making the decision WHO to hire is more important than the decision TO hire
• The cost of hiring the wrong person is 3 to 10 times what you pay them annually
• Traits of the right person…an “A-Player”
a. Reduces stress
b. Frees your time for more important things
c. Keeps you on track
d. Makes you money…directly and indirectly
e. Adds value to your business – your business is worth more if you’re not the
only person in the business
f. Adds value to your customers – you KNOW they’re taken care of
g. Increases performance of team
h. Attracts other A-Players
• Wrong person: “C-Players”
a. Causes energy drain
b. Increases stress
c. Increases frustration
d. Increases overwhelm

e. Makes you lose money
f. Destroys customer relationships
g. Destroys partnerships
h. Slows down work flow
i. Makes you look bad
j. Repels and/or drags down “A-Players”
k. Hides mistakes and create fires
l. Sucks the life out of you!
• A-Player traits: diligent, follow directions, goal oriented, resourceful, don’t blame
others, and have a consistent track record of upward movement
• C-Player traits: don’t follow directions, late to interviews, no longevity in work
history, bad attitude, complain or blame others in mishaps, and have little or no
rise in rank in past history
3. Why you need a hiring system
• Need to sift, sort and screen people
• Hiring system needs to automatically and systematically get candidates to reveal
their true colors
• C-players will not respond and/or complain about filtering process – celebrate
this! They just saved you thousands of dollars
• Having a system protects you from your biases, protects you from your team’s
biases, saves you time and money, makes it easy for you to succeed, and puts
success on automatic
4. Define your outsourcing needs – know exactly what you need in advance because
great people won’t be attracted to you if you’re unfocused, and C and D players will
take advantage of you if you aren’t prepared for hiring them
5. Where to find A-Players
• Best bet: colleagues, friends, and referrals
• Craigslist.com, Elance.com, eDesk.com, RentACoder.com, SCriptlance.com,
Hiremymom.com, Virtual Assistant Companies, Schools, Universities, etc.
6. How to write a killer ad to attract great people
a. Your goal with the ad is to attract great people and repel losers
b. Your ad should be detailed and written like good sales copy
c. The qualities you are looking for should be stated first
d. The general tasks you are looking for someone to fulfill should be listed
second
e. Your ad should state that your company is growing and successful, and
you’re looking to add the right kind of team member…sell yourself and the
company!

f. State in your ad:
“This will be an easy task for someone who knows what they are doing”
“Looking for a [fill in the blank] wiz”
“For the right person this could lead to more work”
7. Filters you can use to sort through applicants and find the best ones
• Put in a “secret code” – this gives you the power to delete people who didn’t
really read through your ad and confirms that the candidate follows directions.
Example: If you have read this add, put “amazing organizer” in the title line of
your response
• Give them test tasks and projects
• Send to additional filters: 800 number, survey, web with audio or videos and/or
training
8. How to create an automated pre-interview questionnaire
• Tools and resources: surveymonkey.com and Google Docs
• Create general and custom questions based on who you are hiring because most
positions have different qualifications
• Write questions about special equipment and skills required (i.e. high speed
internet for virtual employees)
• Look for hidden talents people have as well as traits that might not fit the position
you’re hiring for
9. Interview process
• Your best candidates need to go through at least 2 interviews and 2 tests on the
front end
• Always on the phone
• record it…if they say “no”…end interview
• Outsource 1st interview
• Always take notes during and after interview when information is fresh in your
mind
• Review survey questions, clarify compensation, use version of Brad Smart’s “The
Smart Interviewer” – these questions force candidates to go far back in their
lives and answer specific questions
• Second, third, fourth interview, etc. should be on the phone or in person, and
should include test tasks, projects and/or training (expensive but worth it to find
the right person)
• Hire slow…fire fast!
10.After hiring someone
• Probation periods should last no less than 90 days. At the end of 90 days, give
feedback to your new hire on how they’re doing
• Celebrate a good hire!

Action Steps:
1. Nail down exactly what you are looking for.
2. Ask colleagues and friends for referrals.
3. Create a killer ad to attract great people and post in places that are a good match for
the person you want to hire.
4. Set-up an automated system with filters that collects responses and sorts through
applicants to find the best ones.
5. Interview potential hires at least twice, and use tasks, projects and/or training to make
sure you are hiring the right person.
6. Have probation period that lasts at least 90 days. If at the end of 90 days you can
confirm that the person you chose is a great hire…celebrate!
