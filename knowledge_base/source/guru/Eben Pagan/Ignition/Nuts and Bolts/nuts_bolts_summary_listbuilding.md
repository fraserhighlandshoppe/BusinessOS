Nuts & Bolts:

Setting Up Your Email List
Key Points:
1. One online business model that works is to "break even" on your first product, then
make your profit with the products you sell later to those same people (these products
are known as your "back end").
• Also, many people need to hear from you several times before they trust you
enough to even buy their first product from you. If you don't have their email,
then they won't hear from you enough to trust you and buy from you
2. The answer is to capture their email address.
• This is referred to as "building your list," which means building a list of emails
from your prospects
3. You need to choose which company you will use to store the list of emails you get.
• Aweber has one of the best deliverability rates around, which is important,
because with all the spam filters these days, it's easy to get filtered out
• Icontact is another reputable company, and may not require a double opt in
4. To get started with Aweber:
• Sign up at www.aweber.com
• Click on create and manage lists
• Click on the default list name
• Change the list name
• In "from address", put the name and email address you want to show up when
someone receives your newsletter or message. You can have a different “from”
name and email for each list, if you want.
5. You can send two kinds of messages: Broadcasts and Follow up
• Broadcast: a message that gets sent to all your subscibers at one time
• Follow Up: a series of messages that each new subscriber will get, in order, in
intervals you decide. You might set it up so each new subscriber gets one
message on day 1, another message on day 3, another message on day 6, etc.
You write these messages ahead of time, then each new subscriber receives them
in sequence, no matter when they sign up (these are also called Autoresponders)
6. Should you send HTML messages, or Plain Text?
• HTML messages: allows you to change the font and make it look pretty, and
allows you to track statistics

7. To write a message
• Enter the subject line (you can personalize if you want, for example, so the
person's first name shows up in the subject line)
• Keep lines to 50 characters across
