Key Points:
1. A blog is not just someone’s diary, with a long list of articles, newest on top, and
oldest on the bottom never to be read again. A blog (contraction of the term weblog)
is a type of website, usually maintained by an individual, with regular entries of
commentary, description of events, or other materials such as graphics or video.
Entries are commonly displayed in reverse-chronological order. Blogs can have pages
as well – just like regular sites.
2. Advertisers realize the value of blogs. A blogger’s loyal audience is more likely to
respond to ads and recommendations on the blog. Blogs are a great traffic source.
They are used by professionals to share new content (and to make money). See
tuaw.com, lifehacker.com, ZDNet’s Mary-Jo Foley, BusyMom.net, PostSecret.com,
and WineLibraryTV for examples.
3. When you create a blog and posts, Google will show you in the listings much more
quickly than other types of sites.
4. Types of blogs include:
• Tech, Industry, Funny, Political, Software, Teen, Parenting, Gossip/Entertainment,
Personal, Corporate, Science, Causes, How-to, Mommy, Daddy, and News.
• Blogs on business websites help create community, get people to interact and
comment, and become the key traffic magnets on the site. Blogs become the
FACE of that business on the web where most traffic goes.
5. How Pro Bloggers Make Money
• Affiliate programs – sell other people's stuff on your site and earn a commission.
This is the easiest and fastest way to make money.
• Advertising – sell banner space on your site to other companies. This comes
when you’re popular (and generates HOT leads)
• Products / Services – sell your own. Do this once you're an established authority
• ProBlogger.net has the biggest site on the net showing people how to make
money from Blogging, with blogger Darren Rowse. ProBlogger makes $100K+
per year.
• Follow SethGodin.com to learn about good blogging too.

6. Blogging Jargon
• RSS Feed – a way of syndicating content (or getting your content spread to
many different webistes fast). It's a list of pieces of content organized like your
blog (latest to oldest). It’s not pretty to look at, because it’s not for us to
consume, it’s for computers to consume in a nice looking format. RSS Feeds are a
way to take content on the web and display it anywhere else on the web in a
variety of formats.
• Feed Subscribers – Google Reader is an RSS reader where you can subscribe to
all the blogs you want, and all their info comes to one central place where you
can filter your info. Feed Subscribers are the people who subscribe to your RSS
Feed. They are prized more than email subscribers by advertisers.
• Social Marketing - a blog may only be one component of your presence on the
web. You may also be on social media sites like Twitter & Facebook. Twitter is a
micro-blog. Your RSS Feed will update all of your social media sites
automatically.
• Linkbait – bloggers will make a post that is so valuable other sites will want to
link to it right away. Links are what drive traffic on the web. Google ranks you
based on how many links you get. Bloggers share traffic a lot. If you link to
someone else, they link back in the form of a “Track Back.”
• Post – the message or article that you put on your blog.
7. 5 Principles of Pro Blogging
• Mindset: Be a giver, be dependable, be social
• Content: Give exceptional content, and install your personality into your writing
• Traffic: Get traffic through the search engines, social media, & getting back links
• Monetization: Through affiliate marketing, advertising, and/or your own
products and services.
• Technical: Use Wordpress, host it on your own domain (recommended)
