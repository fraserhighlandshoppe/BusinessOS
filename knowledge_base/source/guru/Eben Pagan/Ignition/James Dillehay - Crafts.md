```markdown
The Top Ten Ideas for Starting an Online Crafts Business
By James Dillehay http://www.craftmarketer.com/webinar/
1. Find ideas for crafts to make and sell by browsing popular craft sites.

2. Consider making crafts for the home decor market, because it is one of the few
industries that grow during recessions.

3. Set up a storefront (free or low-cost) on one or more sites for selling crafts from.

4. Avoid the common mistake of under pricing.

5. Maximize your profits by learning how much customers are willing to pay for crafts like
yours.

6. Use the best images you can when posting pictures of your crafts online.

7. Promote your online craft store through social networks, blogs, video sites, samples to
the media, and online review sites.

8. Learn from successful craft sellers by reading the forums on the popular craft selling
sites.

9. Selling crafts can lead to spin-off opportunities like teaching, writing books, publishing
your designs, and more.

10. Lower your material costs by buying craft supplies at wholesale prices and then sell
your excess supplies to other craft artists.
```
