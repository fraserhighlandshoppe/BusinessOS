Creating a Profitably
Successful Business on eBay
with Janelle Elms
Key Points:
1. eBay isn’t just a “yard sell.” It can be a business (instead of a hobby).
2. You must register to be a Seller. It’s free.
3. What is the Unique Selling Proposition of what you want to sell?
4. How much competition is there selling it on eBay? Do your research. You must get the
product right on eBay if you want to make a profit.
5. Put the terms people search in the TITLE of your listing. Use the same words as your
prospect to explain the item.
6. The PICTURE in your listing and the quality and clarity of the photo is essential.
7. Write your description as though you don’t have a photo; it should be that
descriptive. Show features, benefits, condition, measurements, and terms of service.
Be honest.
8. Terapeak.com tells you everything you need to know about specific products.
9. Free shipping sells!
10.Under “help” you can search for “selling & seller fees” associated with selling.
11.Your fees shouldn’t go over 10 to 15% of your sales (including PayPal)
12.eBay Stores is one of the most powerful e-commerce tools you could possibly have in
your business, especially for the price. People can find you much easier than an
independent site because eBay attracts traffic. In addition, Google gives eBay priority
listings.
13.To set up your store you will:
• Choose a name
• Select a theme
• Upload a logo
• Optimize your Store descriptions
• Use up to 300 custom categories for merchandising
14.Think of giving the “spiders” (robots that go and scan through the information on
websites) lots of information to see and take back to Google, so your rankings will go
up. Don’t starve your spiders.
