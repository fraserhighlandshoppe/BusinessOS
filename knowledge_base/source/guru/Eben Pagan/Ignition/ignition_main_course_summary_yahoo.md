e-Commerce Made Easy with
Yahoo! Stores
with Shawna Fennell
Key Points:
1. Design Wizard
• Step-by-Step Guide
• Customize your store
• Multiple options
• Choose a pre-designed theme
• Create the look and feel you want to create
• Search engine-friendly website
2. Five steps to opening a store. Go to the Yahoo! Small Business Site and Create a
Store:
• Design your site with templates and layouts, and color schemes
• Easily add products with step-by-step wizard
• Upload multiple products using a database
• Upload a spreadsheet with product details
• Manage up to 50,000 products
3. Tips for adding products:
• Should contain enough information to answer buyer questions about the product:
Who is this product for? What is this product? When do they need to use this
product? Why do they need to use this product? Where do they use this product?
How do they use this product?
• Product descriptions are for Google (and other search engines)
• It can take 3-6 months for the search engines to find your site
4. Getting the Sale
• Customize your shopping cart
• Accept debit, credit and PayPal payments
• Within the shipping manager decide how you want to ship. All major carriers
work, and you can create multiple shipping rates.
• Sales tax manager (make sure to set it up within your Yahoo! Store)
• Secure Cart

5. Patience, patience, patience. It can take 3-6 months for search engines to find you,
and sales will start slowly but build over time. Don’t give up.
6. You get Free Search Marketing by automatically getting into Yahoo! Search, Yahoo!
Local, Google Search, Google Product Search and Search Engine Friendly Pages,
and Yahoo! Shopping. It may take 3-6 months for search engines to naturally find
your site.
7. Paid Search Marketing starts with a $100 Credit on Yahoo! Search Marketing, $50
Credit on Google Adwords, Submitnet – Free 90-Day Trial, Yahoo! Keyword
Generator. This is a way to kick start traffic to your site while your natural search
listings are waiting to show up.
8. Shopping Search Engines get you traffic too. You can get into Yahoo! shopping for
free and get listings on the 2nd or 3rd page, but you can pay Yahoo! Shopping to get
higher listings. You’ll also get a $50 Nextag credit, listing in Google product search,
Shopzilla $50 credit and 1 month free on Shopping.com.
• Tip: run searches for the products you are selling and see what shopping search
engines routinely come up for that type of product. These are the shopping
search engines you can think of working with
9. Favorite Tools in the Yahoo! Stores are the *places where you can measure
performance!
• View sales, traffic, referrals (Shawna’s favorite), keywords, reports and graphs
• If it feels overwhelming to look at all your “analytics,” then just go to the
“statistics” and look at “referrals” and you can see where people came from to
find you and exactly what they type to find you (keywords)

First Action Steps:
1. Go to the Yahoo! Small Business Site and create a store.
2. Research, research, research product niches, competitors, prices, and competition
until you have decided on a product niche to test.
3. Add products with the step-by-step wizard (if you need a source for products, check
out the IGNITION presentation by Colette Marshall from World Wide Brands on
Retailing). Upload a spreadsheet with the product details, using Shawna’s writing
Tips.
4. Open your Store!!
