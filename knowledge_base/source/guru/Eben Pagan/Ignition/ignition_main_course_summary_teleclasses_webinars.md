How to Make Money with
Teleclasses and Webinars
How I Got $1,000,000 in 90 Minutes with

with Shawn M. Casey, J.D.
Key Points:
1. Being lazy is good: you can work hard, or you can work smart. Or you can work
really smart and leverage (multiply) your efforts with Teleseminars/Teleclasses or
Webinars. With these tools you can speak to a lot of people at once.
2. A teleseminar is when the person is speaking and the group is listening on a
conference phone line.
3. A webinar uses online software to share your desktop presentation and the audio of
you speaking to a group. Attendees can listen either on the phone or using their
computer.
4. The most popular webinar service is GoToWebinar.com for up to 1,000 people at
$99 per month.
5. Teleseminars are easier than webinars because all you have to do it talk, and all the
attendees have to do is get on the phone and listen.
6. Teleseminars are the second easiest way to make massive profits in minimum time with
the least amount of work. Sell a product priced from $19 to $10,000 but the simple
truth is that highpriced products are better.
7. If you deliver a strong value proposition you can sell 5% to 15% of attendees at
prices starting at $1,000; get 100 people on a teleseminar, sell 10% of them, that’s
10 sales at $1,000 = $10,000 in sales!
8. Here’s how a good promotion works:
• You need a short page with info on the teleseminar that explains why people
should register
• That page will have an opt-in form that people can use to register their name,
email address and (optional) phone number
• Then, you need a confirmation page with the teleseminar information
• You’ll use email promotions to send people to the registration page
• Send at least 3-4 emails for each event
9. Record the teleseminar and post the recording online ASAP.
10.The number one easiest way to make money is play pre-recorded teleseminars!
