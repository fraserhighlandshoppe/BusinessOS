PART II
1. After conducting a PPC Test, take the data and apply it to SEO.
2. SEO is all about “reputation”
3. Off-page factors matter (the links from OTHER Sites that are pointing to YOUR
site). Other people are saying “YOU are the expert.”
4. An On-page factor is something on your site that shows YOU are the expert.
It’s the thing that you control and show people.
5. On-Page Reputation Objects include:
• The Page Title (title tag), Page Content (body) and most everything else is
outside the 80/20 rule.
• The “Meta Description Tag” tells the search engines what brief description
to give of your site in the search engine results page listing.
• The Meta-Tag doesn’t make a big difference to the Search engines, but it
means a LOT to customers (“sell the click”).
• Body Copy is where you focus on your customer’s behavior. Use it to get the
most wanted response from the prospect. Write for CONVERSION.
6. Search engines rank INDIVIDUAL PAGES, not Sites (this is why it is helpful for you
to point to other pages within your site, i.e. internal linking)
7. Off-Page Reputation Objects include: hyperlinks from other pages (called “anchor
text” or “link text”)
8. Off-Page Goals include: getting links that lead to your pages (link text that
includes your target keywords), just about any link to your page is a good link.
More links = GREAT. Also get links through your blog, Twitter, Facebook, Squidoo
Lens’, HubPages, press releases, Get Dug (Digg.com), StumbleUpon, article
marketing (distribute your articles in article directories), partner with other web
masters. Make cool videos, articles and podcasts that people talk about.
9. Google “Page Rank” is a measurement of page popularity.

Action Steps:

3. Build and run your first campaign, pointing to another person’s product (a topselling product on ClickBank for example) if you don’t have your own site and
products yet.
