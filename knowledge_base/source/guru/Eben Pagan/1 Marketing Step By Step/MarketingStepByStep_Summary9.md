Session 9: Naming Your Product or Service
Key Points...
1. Naming - The Ultimate Leverage:
• Nowhere do you get so much "bang for your buck" than choosing a name for your
business, your product or your service.
2. Create a name that’s impossible for customers to forget.
• Don't focus on creating a name that's "nice" or "easy to remember."
• Instead, focus on creating a name that's impossible to FORGET.
3. The mind remembers name by SOUND, not by sight.
• Names are sounds before they are printed words - so focus on the SOUND primarily.
• Repetitive sounds and rhythmic sounds win in this domain - so use them.
4. Avoid "Cute" or "Funny" names.
• Spending money is serious business - and most people don't want to laugh when they're
doing it.
• Rarely does a cute or funny name work - so avoid them.
5. Use names that promise results, benefits, and/or solutions if possible.
• Most people think that a name is something very diﬀerent from their "other marketing."
• Not so - use everything you've learned so far to create a name that promises a BENEFIT.
6. Rhythm makes names much more “sticky” in the mind.
• We have a system called the "phonological loop" that acts as an auditory buﬀer.
• Use repetitive and rhythmic sounds to keep your name bouncing around LONGER.
7. Do use and include:
• Alliteration
• Rhyme
• Rhythm
• Power Associations
8. Favorite Name Examples:
• Coca Cola
• 3 Day Blinds
• YouTube
• Bed, Bath & Beyond
9. Names that I've created:
• Double Your Dating
• Self-Made Wealth
• Wake Up Productive
