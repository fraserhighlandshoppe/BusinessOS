Look For Customers Who Are
Already Looking For YOU
Most people aren’t looking for the Result, Benefit or Solution that you’re offering in your product or service. In order to
succeed in Marketing, it’s important to identify that segment or sub-group of people who have realized that they have a
challenge, are emotionally motivated to find a solution, and who are pro-actively looking for YOU. Use this 3 question test to
make sure that your Marketing appeal has a high probability of
success before investing money and time testing it.

Is my Prospective Customer emotionally driven to find solve a
problem or get a specific benefit that I offer?

Is my Prospective Customer so motivated that they’ve begun
proactively seeking to solve their challenge?

Does my Prospective Customer have few or no perceived options?
