How To “Serialize” Your Content
When creating your content - whether it be newsletters, videos or products - it’s important to remember
that people don’t learn something the first time they hear it. In fact, they must not only hear it and see it
several times, but they must also hear and see it many different WAYS before they really “get it” and put a
technique or method into action. Instead of creating “all new” ideas continually, it’s much better to take
your BEST concepts and then SERIALIZE them. In other words, develop many perspectives, angles and
ways of explaining and teaching the same core concepts. Use this exercise to identify one of your best
strategies or techniques, then to serialize it into several pieces of content. Next, create a piece of content
based on each of your “serialized” ideas.
