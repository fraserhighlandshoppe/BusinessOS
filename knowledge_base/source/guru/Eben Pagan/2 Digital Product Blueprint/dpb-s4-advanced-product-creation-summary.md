“Advanced Product Creation”
Key Points...
1. Your product is actually an OBSTACLE to your customer geN̂ing their needs fulfilled. Your customer doesn't want
your product - they want a solution.
2. Imagine if your customer hired you to do research on their problem, figure out a solution, and then present your
results back to them in the way they want. That's essentially what's happening - your customers are paying you to
find a solution to their problem.
3. You can create the outline of your book based on your customers' wants and needs. If it makes sense, address
the most important needs at the BEGINNING of the book.
4. Your customer can't hear anything except the pain they are in and their needs. And your customer wants results,
not ideas.
5. Win beforehand: think about your customers' needs BEFORE you make your product. That way you'll waste as liN̂
le time, energy, and effort as possible... and you'll avoid creating a product people don't want... and don't buy.
6. With an information product, you can easily avoid creating something that won't help your customer. You do this
by creating a piece of content, seeing if people respond positively, and if they do, only then go ahead with creating
a product. Don't create your product first before you test the content you want to teach.
7. "I'm about to teach you how to (result your customer wants). In order to do this, we must first learn one of the
most important reasons why you're in so much pain. That'll help you immediately. You'll know why you got in the
pain, and how to avoid it in the future."
8. People don't care how much you know until they know how much you care. One way to do this is by exploring
what they want and what is frustrating them. By doing this, you get to know your market personally... and it shows
them that you really do care.
9. When someone is in pain, they are open to you probing for their needs and hot buN̂ons. Say something like, "I'd
like to help you get out of [this pain], and in order to do that I need to find out a liN̂le more about your situation."
Then ask for their challenges, fears, frustrations, aspiration, etc.
10.Even if you're introverted, you learn best about your market by talking to your prospects LIVE. It's the fastest way
to learn your customer's hot buN̂ons and what's important to them. You'll get dramatic insights about how to help
them with their problems.
11. If you're hesitant to do live consultations, come at it like this: "I'm trying to figure out how to help people solve
this problem. And I want to create a product so people can read about it in a book and solve it. If you have this
problem, I'd like to know how you got in, what have you tried so far, what your biggest fears and frustrations are,
etc." Look for the emotional, irrational hot-buN̂ons and the words they use to describe them.
