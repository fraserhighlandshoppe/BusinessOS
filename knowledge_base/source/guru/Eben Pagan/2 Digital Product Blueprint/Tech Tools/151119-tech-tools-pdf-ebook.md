Overview

Why PDF?
• While the number of ebook formats proliferates,

the one format that is still most universally visible
across all platforms and devices is: PDF
• Also compatible with many ePub, Mobi and other

digital readers (e.g. Kindle, iBooks, etc.

Producing PDF eBooks
• Good news:
• Used to need Adobe Acrobat or third party

software to produce PDF
• Now, most word processors will output, print, or

export to PDF format directly from original
document.

Producing PDF eBooks
• Caution:
• Whatever word processor you use - just be sure

that when you publish to PDF, the layout stays
intact and the hyperlinks are active (e.g. Microsoft
Word shows hyperlinks in the PDF, but they are
not clickable).

Styles and Templates
• The technology of producing PDF ebooks is so

straightforward that today
• So, in addition, we’ll look at how to use styles and

templates to make your eBook creation process
faster and easier too

Styles and Templates
• Style = predefined format and layout characteristics of

page elements and the document
• e.g. A “Header” style may be “24px; Bold Georgia Font;

left aligned; with page-width underline”
• e.g. Document style may be A4; grey background; border

on each page; page number and copyright in footer;
2.5cm margin all around; 1.25cm header and footer

Styles and Templates
• Template = a collection of document and page

element styles used to produce a particular format
of document
• e.g. “Newsletter”; “Novel”; “Weekly Report”
• Allows fast and easy creation of documents that

have the same look and feel, but different content

Styles and Templates
• Templates are sometimes included with the word

processing software (e.g. Word, Pages)
• Third party templates are available (free / paid)
• You can design your own (from scratch, or by

modifying existing template)
