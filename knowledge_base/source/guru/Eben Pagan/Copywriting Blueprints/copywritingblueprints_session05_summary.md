Session 5: Arousing Your
Prospect’s Interest
In Session 5, you’ll learn about the importance of telling your story and the 7 elements that will transform
your customer’s mindset. You’ll also be doing an exercise to take what you’ve learned and put it into action.

• In each of the parts of the sales piece your prospect is asking questions. In this second area, your
prospect is essentially asking 2 questions:
‣

Who are you and why should I trust you?

‣

Do you know what you’re talking about?

• Tell your prospect your own story. You’re doing this to build trust so they feel you know what you’re
talking about.

• Rapport is the idea of feeling “in sync” with someone. Another aspect of rapport is that the more similar
a person feels to you, the more influenced they are by you.

• Talk as much as you can in their voice – tone, sentence structure, words, etc.
• Trust between people is a big deal. If you don’t have trust, then your prospect won’t take action. To build
trust, tell your prospect who you are, how you figured out how to solve the problem they have, and how
to get the result that they want.

• Humans tend to think in stories.
• The 7 elements of your story:
‣

Where you were when you started out

‣

How you tried and you failed

‣

The breakthrough

‣

Consistent results

‣

Created a method, system, or product

‣

Others did it too

‣

Now you can learn it

• When you create your story using this format it can completely transform your customer’s mindset.
