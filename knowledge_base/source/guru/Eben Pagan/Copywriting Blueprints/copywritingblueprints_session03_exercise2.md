Formal Tone vs.
Conversational Tone
When writing marketing copy, many people go into “formal” mode - and begin using words and
language in a way that feels detached, academic and abstract. Success in marketing requires you to
always keep in mind that you’re communicating with an individual on the “other side”... and real people
feel more comfortable when communication is conversational. Use this exercise to “translate” formal
ideas into conversational language, then use it in your marketing copy.

Formal Tone
“Our product offers many benefits, and it is
a logical choice for the serious golfer.”

Conversational Tone
“This new driver will add 10 yards to every
drive - and help you hit the ball further and
straighter than you’ve ever hit it.”
