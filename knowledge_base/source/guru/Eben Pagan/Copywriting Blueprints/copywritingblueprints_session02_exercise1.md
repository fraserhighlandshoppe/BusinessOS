Fears & Frustrations,
Wants & Aspirations
This is the exercise I use when creating a new marketing campaign. It’s the best place to start when
writing your copy.
Keep in mind, the focus of this exercise is to discover emotional needs. We’re looking for irrational
drivers that are “hidden” behind the logical explanations and rationalizations.
List as many as you can in each group. Go for at least 10. 20 is better. The more you list, the more work
you’ll have to do - and the more you’ll discover the “gems” that are really motivating your prospect.
