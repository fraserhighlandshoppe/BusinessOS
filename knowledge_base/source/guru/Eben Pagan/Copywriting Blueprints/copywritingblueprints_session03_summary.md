Session 3: Your Marketing Voice
In Session 3, you’ll learn the key tools to creating a new voice when writing your marketing copy. You’ll also
be doing two critical exercises to help you build your marketing voice.

• When selling products and services it requires you to use a different voice, style, and tone than in other
areas of life.

• Start by building a new voice based on the tone of familiarity and friendliness.
• Writing copy is counterintuitive to most people – don’t go into abstract or academic writing but talk as if
you’re having a conversation with an old friend.

• Remember that when you’re writing copy, you’re talking to someone who is probably alone. Take a oneto-one voice.

• When you combine familiarity & friendliness with talking to a person one-to-one you get “speak-write”.
You write the way you speak.

• Keep in mind that you’re talking TO one person, not AT a mass of people.
• The most powerful words in marketing are YOU and YOUR.
• Speak to specifics, not to generalities.
• Talk about concrete things that are real, not abstract ideas.
• Talk to things that are irrational, which trigger emotions instead of logic.
• When we combine these together, our voice and tone changes. Then, we start relating to others in a way
that grabs their attention.

• Rapport:
‣

When you’re in rapport you’re connected to the other person. When you’re out of rapport you’re not
connected.

• The more you do all of this, the more you’ll realize how important it is to actually talk to your customers
one-on-one. They will give you the words and concepts that you’ll need to create your marketing.
