Directing Your Prospect To
Take ACTION
In order to get the benefit that your product or service offers, your prospect must take action. They
must place their order, take specific steps in a specific sequence, etc. Make sure you tell your prospect
exactly what they need to do, and ask them to DO IT. Connect taking action with getting the benefit
they want, and incentivize your prospect to take action RIGHT NOW.

What are the specific action steps your prospect needs to take in order to buy
your product or service - and get the benefits they want?

How could you “word-smith” the end of your marketing piece to incentivize and
motivate your prospect to take action immediately?
