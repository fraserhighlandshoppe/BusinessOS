The Sales Follow-Up Sequence
When to Implement:
After a sales call has been completed with a prospect.
Who is Included:
Any prospect who was a fit for your product or service, but did not move
forward. We don’t recommend including prospects who flat out said ‘No.’
But this is for the group that is ‘Maybe’ or ‘check back in with me’ or ‘Let me
think about it’ or ‘Send me some details after the call.’ You know the type.
:-)
Set-up Process:
1. Create a Tag that will organize the contacts intended for this
sequence.

messages that will be sent out.

follow-up

through Zapier to ‘Remove a Contact’s Tag if they purchased or
Booked a Call’.
This will eliminate them from further follow-up.
