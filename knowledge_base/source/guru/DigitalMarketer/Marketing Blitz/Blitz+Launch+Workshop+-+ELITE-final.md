A stand-alone piece of high-value
content (blog post, video, article,
manifesto, etc.) that causes your

action based on this new belief.

PIVOTS

In the event that your offer is failing…

Do not panic.

• Before you launch, define your
revenue/trial/lead goals.

PIVOT

• Identify your pivot day.
• Have a plan B for your main mailing

list (something that’s going to hit
comparable metrics to what you were
expecting).

• If chances of success aren’t looking good

after your pivot day, you have two options:

PIVOT

1. Stop mailing EVERYone on your list
and switch to your plan B offer.
2. Continue mailing your offer to anyone
who has ENGAGED with at least one of
your offer emails, then mail the plan B
offer to everyone else.

DETERMINE
PIVOT DAY

• Be aware that day 2 or 3 should be your
pivot day (that way you can collect
metrics on the first day/day and a half).

• TRACK your opens, clicks, and sales

page conversion rates as CLOSELY
as possible… ideally 3 times a day
(morning, afternoon, end of day) - this
will help you determine where there are
any massive drop-off.

WHAT WE
EXPECT

• 15-20% of total sales to come in on day 1
• 30-40% to come in on days 2-5
• 50% to come in on 6 and 7

Pivot PAID ADS
• Paid ads and content need time to ramp up,

so it can be difficult to know when (or how) to
pivot

• Paid ads are detrimental (because $$$)
• Overall, your ads will do better with the more
assets you deploy (awareness)

• Keep budget low, since the campaign is
short
