# essential-podcast-tool-list.pdf

```markdown
At DigitalMarketer, we’ve tested a lot of tools to figure out what works best to
produce and publish Perpetual Traffic.
This page contains links and descriptions of the tools, software, and hardware that
helps to make our podcast what it is. Some are free tools, others are paid.
Tools with asterisks (*) are alternative options to tools DM uses.

Podcast Episode Idea Generating Tools
Buzzsumo – Use Buzzsumo to analyze what content performs best for any topic or
competitor. This will help you find the most popular content people are searching
for, and can serve as inspiration for podcast episodes.
Google AdWords – This tool helps you see what people are searching for most. Find
well performing keywords to use in the SEO of your show notes. AdWords may also
spark some ideas to build an episode around.

Podcast Collaboration Tools
Skype – We use Skype to connect when we’re planning an episode or about to
record a new episode. This is very effective since the hosts of Perpetual Traffic all
live in separate states.
Google Docs – With Google docs, you can write, edit, and collaborate a document
wherever you are, all for free. We use this tool when planning episodes.

Podcast Recording Tools
ScreenFlow – If you’re using Mac, it’s very easy to create screencast video and edit
within this program. Some of the Perpetual Traffic hosts use this program for
podcast recordings.
Ecamm Recorder – Some of our hosts also use this tool to record our podcasting
Skype recordings directly to their Macs. It’s very simple to use.

We use Rode Podcaster microphones to record our podcast.
This high quality microphone rejects a lot of ambient noise.

An alternative mic is the ATR-2100. It is a far cheaper mic than
the Rode Podcaster, but still of good quality; this is the type of
mic we used at first for Perpetual Traffic.

Podcast Audio Transfer Tools
Dropbox – Once the podcast recording is finished, we upload the recordings to a
shared file in Dropbox so the editing process can then begin.
WeTransfer – You can use this platform to send audio files. Our Audio Editor uses
WeTransfer to send the completed podcast to us.

Podcast Editing Tools
Logic Pro – Digital audio editor to make the podcast sound professional. In our
opinion, this is one of the best audio editors out there.
*Pro Tools – Digital audio editor to make the podcast sound professional.
*Audacity – Free digital audio editor to make the podcast sound professional.
*Adobe Audition – Digital audio editor to make the podcast sound professional.
*AudioJungle – Royalty free music and sound effects you can buy for your podcast
music needs, such as transitions and segues.
Izotope RX 5 – Repair, enhance, restore problematic audio, and dramatically
improve your podcasts clarity and sound quality with this tool.

Podcast Hosting Sites
Libsyn – We use Libsyn for our podcasting hosting site. It’s a very easy platform we
can use to upload, track, and share Perpetual Traffic.
*PodBean – Easy and free podcasting hosting site.
*Blubrry – Podcasting hosting site.

Podcast Distribution Tools
Bitly – We use Bitly to shorten and share our podcast’s link over our channels. Bitly
also allows us to track the link’s traffic.
PushCrew – Website push notification you can implement on Chrome and Firefox.
MeetEdgar – Use this tool for social automation. Create a library of posts that can
be published on a future date.
```
