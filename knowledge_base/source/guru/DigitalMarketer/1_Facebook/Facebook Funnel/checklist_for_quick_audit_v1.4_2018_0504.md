# checklist_for_quick_audit_v1.4_2018_0504.pdf

```markdown
QUICK AUDIT CHECKLIST
1. Download Soapbox To film Quick Audit.
2. Check to see if Client has a Strategy Assessment form filled out (GCT).
Go over goals and steps that we can take to help reach their goals.
Go over targeting and suggest how we can use those audiences to help their
brand.

3. Note if their website has site wide HTTPS or not.
4. Analyze client’s digital plumbing using Google Tag Assistance and
Facebook Pixel Helper.
Introduce digital plumbing giving a brief overview of what digital plumbing is.
Use your Facebook Pixel Helper to see if there is a Facebook pixel on their
website.
Explain the importance of Facebook Pixel especially when you are running ads
using Facebook.
Use Google Tag Assistant to see that they have AdWords remarketing tag,
Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager on their website.

5. Plug in Client’s URL and analyze results on both of the site speed pages
being used. (Page Speed Insights and Test My Site with Google).
Note the speed and optimization for both Mobile and for Desktop using Page
Speed Insights.
Recommend the optimization suggestions given by Google if needed.
Note the speed of loading time over 3G as well as the percentage of visitors
lost. (Test my site).
Go over the industry comparison and give a good idea of a good site speed for
the client.
Recommend fixes that could reduce their loading time.

6. Analyze their content being posted on their Facebook.
Go through their content and see how far apart the posts are from each other.
Explain why it is important to have consistent content, and how it affects the
client.
Check to see if the client has a high authority pinned post.
Give an example of what exactly word of mouth means.
Recommend the type of content that they should create more (top, middle, or
bottom).
Provide analysis on how you can utilize content using some of our strategies.
Give examples of how and why you can use the client content in a sequence.

7. Look over their Facebook videos and note important metrics.
Note the overall length of their videos, note the longest and note the shortest.
Explain why we would recommend 1:00-1:20 minute videos.
Note the engagement on their video, are they getting a few views, okay views,
or really great views.
Give a brief explanation of how they can use the Facebook for a dollar a day
strategy on their top video.
Give them a few standards of excellence methods.

8. Go over the 3x3 video grid.
Introduce the 3x3 video grid.
Explain the funnel and the parts that it is broken down into (WHY, HOW,
WHAT).
Explain what type of content belongs in the WHY, HOW, WHAT part of
the funnel.
Mention why they should not try and sell their product in the Awareness and in
the Engagement part of the funnel.
Explain how they can use the 3x3 video grid to create if/then sequences.
Explain the importance and give an example of using if/then sequences.

9. Analyze content being posted on their Instagram.
Note their follower to following ratio.
Look at their content and note the engagement that they are getting on
their post.
Explain why it is important to utilize one minute videos on Instagram.
Explain how Instagram and Facebook tie together when running ads on
Facebook.
Take note on what they are creating and where it belongs in the funnel.

10. Analyze content being posted on their Twitter.
Note their follower to following ratio.
Look to see if they have a pinned post and if they do, note engagements on it.
Look at their content and note the engagement that they are getting on
their post.
Explain why it is important to utilize one minute videos on Twitter.
Explain how Instagram and Facebook tie together when running ads on
Facebook.
Take note on what they are creating and where it belongs in the funnel.
```
