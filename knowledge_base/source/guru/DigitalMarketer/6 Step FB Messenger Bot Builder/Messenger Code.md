Messenger Code
The last growth tool I’m going to cover in this EP is the Messenger Code growth tool. And this allows you to send people to Facebook Messenger from an offline source like a print ad, a business card, a postcard, etc.
It works through something called a “Messenger Code.” Messenger codes are similar to QR codes, but instead of opening a website… they send the person directly to Messenger, where you can engage them with a custom-made message and follow-up sequence.
So in a lot of ways, this growth tool is similar to the Ref URL growth tool—but where the Ref URL sends people to Messenger from another online source, Messenger code sends people to Messenger from an offline source.
To set this up in ManyChat, just choose the Facebook Messenger Code growth tool:
And from there, set up your opt-in message (just like you did for the Ref URL or embeddable widgets growth tools).
This will be the message the person receives after they scan your Messenger code.
And you might notice that unlike the Ref URL tool, this doesn’t give you the option to subscribe the user to a sequence. But you can still do this if you want to.
Just click Edit next to the opt-in message, and add a button. From there, you can automatically add people to a sequence once they click on that button. Here’s what that looks like:
Now a word of warning: make sure your opt-in message is set up the way you want it BEFORE you get the Messenger code (because if you go back later and edit the opt-in message, it will also change the code).
Here’s what the image looks like:
The image in the center will be your Facebook page’s profile picture, and the lines and dots around that image will be the unique part that Facebook will read to send that person your unique opt-in message.
Here’s an example of how you could use this if you ran a restaurant:
Include a picture of your Messenger code near the register, along with a sign that says “Scan this image in Facebook to get 15% off your order.”
The customer scans the image and is sent to your Messenger bot. They click “Get Started” and then click on your opt-in message to subscribe to your Messenger list.
Then your bot messages them with a coupon code, which they show the cashier for 15% off their order.
Pretty cool, huh? Then you will be able to follow up with those people for sales, promotions, and special events.
This is a great tool to use for:
Live event booths
Inside brick and mortar stores
Business cards
Physical fliers (for bands, local businesses, etc.)
And more!
When you use this growth tool, just make sure you’re placing it somewhere people can actually scan it with their phones. (In other words, it has to be CLOSE—you can’t put it on a billboard or anything like that.)
And give them instructions on how to scan it.
To do that, they just open the Facebook app… click on their picture… click on the big image at the top… and then click “Scan Code” at the top of the screen:
Then they just have to put that circle over the Facebook code, and that’s it! Your opt-in message will automatically open in the Messenger app.
