MLM industry.

mildly.

organization. I believe that an MLM can be great training for the
future entrepreneur. However, the truth is that very few people are
successful enough to tout MLM’s as the “end-all” to success beyond
your wildest dreams.

place as I advise him about the pro’s and con’s of MLM’s as well as the
best alternative that I can think of for earning an honest dollar –
namely, creating an information product that you, yourself, own and
control.
