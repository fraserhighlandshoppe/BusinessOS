Here is a free 15-minute consultation that I recently did with a gentleman named
Bal. Bal is an experienced technical writer who also does freelance work in
copywriting. Bal contacted me because, although he enjoys both his job and
freelance copywriting, he felt that he could do better. He wanted my advice on
building a business of his own and how to be more focused on bringing products
to market in order to enjoy success.
