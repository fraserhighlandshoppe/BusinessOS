How To Be Positive… Regardless:

An Interview With Legendary Cookie-Maker
Famous Amos
Wally “Famous” Amos has had his share of ups and downs. As a high
school dropout who was sent to live with his aunt when his parents
divorced, he could have chosen to see life negatively. Instead he
realized the power of being positive, regardless. And in this interview,
he shares how that attitude has helped him in business and in life.
According to Wally, in order to be successful you have to commit to an
idea as soon as it comes to you. You can’t wait for “the best time” to
do something or for someone to do it for you. You have to go for it
right away, or someone else will beat you to it.
So in this audio, you’ll hear how Famous Amos took his simple idea
and made a fortune with it – and how he stayed positive throughout
the process.
