The Real Reason Most People Struggle With Weight
If you go to a doctor when you’re overweight, they’ll probably tell you to “eat right and
exercise” in order to become healthy. But since most people have no idea what that
means, they usually go home, watch TV and sign up for the first diet they see on a
commercial – without doing any research to find out if it’s actually good for them.
And since most of these diets involve starvation as a way to lose weight, you won’t just
be losing fat when you’re on them; you’ll also be losing muscle. So when you go off that
diet, you can expect to gain your weight back quicker, and even more of it.
The good news is -- this spiral effect doesn’t have to happen. And in this interview with
KC Craichy, author of The Super Health Diet: The Last Diet You Will Ever Need, you’ll
hear how to stop the crazy yo-yo dieting, lose weight the right way – and keep it all off
for good.

drink with 9 grams of sugar in it, it’s basically like driving up to gas station:
you fill your car until it clicks a few times, and then it runs down the side of
the car, and you roll the back window down and you fill the back seat. So
the body has about as much capacity to deal with the extra sugar and
extra fuel that people put in their body as a car does the gas in the back
seat.
