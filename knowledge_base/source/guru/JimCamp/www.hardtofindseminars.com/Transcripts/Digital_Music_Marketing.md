agent is responsible for getting the artist out there look why that
performance is. The problem with booking, you see this is one of
the inherent problems with the music business Michael, is you have
to have booking, radio and distribution all on the same page with

ultimate goal in the ideal world is to get the artist out there live, get
them on the radio and get the music in the stores. Music industries
have become so complicated booking doesn’t want anything to do
with you unless people can hear you on the radio and the music is
in the stores because who’s going to go see the shows live if they
haven’t either heard you on the radio and/or they can’t buy you in
the store.

fans don’t already know about you live and/or on the radio. And

corporate as it is. There was a time where you use to be able to
get a DJ, especially a night time DJ, to play just about anything and
