this endeavor – and it wouldn’t be Roy. This kind of business never sounds like a
good idea to me. Listen as I try to sway Roy into creating his own Information
Product where he would have total control of the product and would receive 100%
of the profit.
We discuss reasons why Roy should spend his money and time developing and
creating his own Information Product. We explore potential topics for his
Information Product and how to determine what types of topics would be best for
him to research.
