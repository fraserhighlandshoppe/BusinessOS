==Maximizing Virtual Meetings==
 Keith Leech

Some people will object to an in-camera (online/virtual) meeting.
*Let's just grab a phone call instead.
===Phone Meetings===
#No Speakerphone.
##Tells client that "I don't care enough about this conversation to hold the phone to my ear"
##Use a headset instead.
#Stand up (or sit up as straight as possible)
##Energy level drops if slouched
#Have a smile on your face.
##Tone comes across better
#Simplify your language
##We can't get the visual cues that the person doesn't understand.
===Video===
 Using video helps advance the relationship.
 *Seeing someone say the words, and the body language, is more powerful.
Power of video
*89% of people say video makes them feel more connected.
*82% of people say they will multitask less on a video meeting
**Everybody knows everyone can see them.
*We process visuals 60000x faster than text.

Network Speed
*The more your lips match your words the better
*The more your movements match your words the better.
*Cost of upgraded network is usually pennies on the dollar.

Lighting
*If they can't see you you lose the advantage of the video

Audio
*Built in microphones aren't as good as a good headset.
*Podcast mic's are also an option.

Appearance
*Traditional meetings you can see waist to above head.
*We also never sat against the back wall of a conference room.
*Is the camera at/about eye level?
**Don't have the camera angle make it look like you are staring down at them.

When someone on the other side is speaking, continue to look at the camera.
*regardless of where on the screen the person is, from their side they are equivalent to camera
**Looks like you are looking away of you are looking at them on the screen

====Virtual Meeting Best Practices====
[[File:RussellInvestmentsVirtualMeetings.pdf]]
#DO A TEST RUN 
##Take the time to test the technology, sound and camera—ask a friend to be the guest and you the host and vice versa.
#SIGN IN EARLY
##Sign in 10-15 minutes early. You will feel more comfortable when the meeting starts.
#LOOK AT THE CAMERA
##This is not easy but your clients will be more engaged if they see you looking at them.
#LIGHTING IS IMPORTANT!
##Face the window to avoid silhouetting. Ensure that once the camera is on, you are clearly seen.
#USE THE CAMERA
##It is much more engaging for both the host and guest to see and also hear each other. Body language and facial expressions are just as important as words.
#USE AN AGENDA 
##Set expectations before the meeting. Sending an agenda in advance creates structure and order.
#SHORTER IS BETTER
##Virtual meetings tend to be more efficient and conscious of time. Shorten 1 hour meetings to 45 minutes.
#SHOW A VISUAL 
##A welcome slide with the client’s name will show that they have joined the right meeting. 
##Visuals illustrating your points will keep them engaged.
#BE DELIBERATE WHEN USING A MOUSE
##Avoid moving your cursor too much on the screen as this can be distracting to the viewer.
#USE A WHITEBOARD
##Most apps have a whiteboard feature – use it. 
##You can write notes live on it, have the agenda typed up ahead of time, etc. 

====Best Practices on Camera====
Even today, not everyone is comfortable in front of a camera. Consider these 12 best practices to help you feel like a natural when hosting virtual meetings before and after the camera is turned on:
*Practice. Practice. Practice
*Record and watch yourself
*Have a plan, not a teleprompter
*Get hydrated and stretch
*Be in the middle of the frame
*Turn on the lights!
*Look into the camera
*Limit distractions
*Posture is power
*Be animated in the best way
*Speak slowly and smile
*Be natural and authentic—not perfect and scripted

===Converting In Person Meetings to Online===
 Need to be more strategic at the start of the meeting.
*Get people speaking on camera as early as possible.
**Before the agenda is started in a trad meeting everyone will speak.
**People don't like to speak on camera.  The more we get them talking pre-agenda the more they will participate on camera.
**Have pre-planned open ended questions i.e. what did you do this weekend vs how was your weekend.
*Need to check in more with clients during meeting
**Easier in person to catch the body language.
**Add check-in here to your agendas.
***Don't say CLIENT do you understand --or You need explained.
***Instead say is there anything I need to explain better
*Silence where no-one is speaking in a virtual meeting is OK.
**This occurs during in person meetings too
**Don't feel the need to fill silence.
Acknowledge up front that virtual meetings are more tiring.
*In person we would probably cover off these 5 things.
*these are more exhausting, lets see how far we get.  Let's expect to get a followup to cover what we miss.

====Screen Sharing====
If you share a slide you shrink in size and the body language benefit decreases.
*People remember 10% of what they see or hear 3 days later.
*Graphics are remembered 65% 3 days later
Only have the slide shared while it is providing value.

Attract eye with color.
*use 2 slides
**one with greyscale
**2nd slide has emphasis change to color
**Can also use font size to emphasize color change.
