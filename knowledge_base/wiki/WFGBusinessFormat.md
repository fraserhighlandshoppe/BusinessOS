==Inventory List==
Your list of potentials and leads.

Monthly review - list should always be growing.

When you contact someone, keep notes of what they said.

When growing the list, you need both their name, phone and email.

You want to track your potential's ability to get to a BPM.  You want to be present the nights they are!

==Approach and Contact==
We are all self employed.  We need to talk about the business or nobody will know we are in it.
*Get people's contact info.  They almost never call back if you give them a card.

When asked what do we do, people will shy away from "sell insurance" or "financial services" (people don't like to talk about money).  Instead, we educate people about how money works in Canada and assist them in preparing for the future!

It is only as difficult as you make it in your mind.

If you tell yourself you will be rejected, the contact will feel your uncertainty and almost certainly reject.  The moment you second guess yourself is when you fail.  The hardest step is the first - actually starting.  

Instead, be confident and excited, knowing in your mind that you WILL close (mindset).

You need to know the end GOAL, and have it firmly in mind:
*It is to educate and to understand.
*It is not to recruit nor sell! (these turn people off)
Know that we bring value to people.  Focus 100% on the value we bring people.

Keep it simple

You will have more success if you schedule (and keep the schedule) your phoning times.  This becomes a routine.  Expect it of yourself.

===At the BPM===
*At BPM don't walk to them, let them come to you once they have had a moment to adjust to the crowd and energy.
*At the end, don't talk about presentation - ask if they have had a chance to fill out the followup contact card.  Introduce to your senior partner.

==Presentation==
The WFG's prefered method is the Business Process Meeting.  If the client doesn't want to come into the ''open house'' then you have the meeting at their place (a.k.a. kitchen table meeting).

The reason to use the BPM is time efficiency - if you can get a few people into the BPM at once then they get the presentation while you get to do training etc rather than you sitting at their kitchen table doing the presentation.

The Kitchen Table Presentation is where we get clients.  The BPM is where we get recruits!
*They see the office,
*They meet the team,
*They meet the leadership,
*They see that this isn't a fly by night operation!

==Follow-up==
The followup is  where you (or your senior leadership) collects information from the potential client in order to create a plan for them.

It is also where you pursue your prospect until they tell you to stop!  Consider, if someone upline of you had not followed up on their recruit who is up the chain from you, then you would not be in this position.  The entire chain below that 1 link would not exist, at least not as that chain.

When we call back when we promised to do so, we demonstrate professionalism, commitment, integrity.

If we get their voicemail during followup, leave a message saying "As I promised to followup, here is my call.  Please call me back".

==Duplicate==
The process starts all over.  As a team leader, the duplicate is assisting one of your downlines to run the earlier steps here for themselves.

Your team will do 1/2 of what you do right, and 1/1 of everything you do wrong.

The ultimate duplication in WFG is recruiting your team.

There will be a time in the future where someone needs your time, money and attention.  Without a solid team in place to carry on, you will not have the financial ability to step back.

You need to duplicate:
*environment
*growth mentality
*gratitude
*the system

You only have 24 hours in a day.  By building the team you are tapping into Other People's Time.
