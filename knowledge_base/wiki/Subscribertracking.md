==Smart URL==
 https://www.mautic.org/blog/developer/tracking-visitor-data-by-smart-url/
This is the difference between http://example.com and http://example.com/?email=jdoe@example.net.  With the later you can grab $_GET['email'].

Just remember that you are using personal information, so hashing email=xxx@uuu.com to be h3,kas98uw2rtnsfd and then de-hashing it on the form handler side might be better.
