==Generating Leads==
 https://onlinemlmcommunity.com/get-more-mlm-leads/

 Also see [[Social Selling]]

===LinkedIn===
*Phone Number
*Email
*Website
*Good Picture
*Bio
*Interests
*Experience
*Certifications
*DO NOT mention MLM or company (WFG) name.  Want people to contact you out of curiosity.
Once the profile is up, reach out:
*Join groups
*contact old friends
*post articles
*search for specific interests
**Search your target market and reach out
===Email Signatures===
*name
*email addr
*Link to website
*phone
*Catchy phrase and CALL TO ACTION
*DO NOT MENTION MLM...
===Facebook===
*Don't spam
*Don't mention name of company or product
*Post fun and interesting:
**spending time with team mates
**Attending company events
**recognizing a teammate
**lifestyle photo
**The benefits of your product (but not product itself)
You are trying to get people's attention and curiosity.
===Twitter===
Best is to actively use it, but you can also sync from Facebook, Youtube, Instagram etc.
===Youtube===
Minimum 1-2 video posts per week

Educational or entertaining.  Short and to the point. NOT SALES PITCH.

Separate channel.
===Drop Card===
*Combination Business Card and Advertising
*Headline, Bullet Points, Call to Action
*Name, email, website
===Online Forums and Blogs===
Find ones where your target market hangs out, and comment on articles/posts.
*Don't spam the forum.  Turns people off.
*Ad value.  Comment on the article.  Let your profile signature sell you/business/product.
*Ask questions
*Answer Questions
