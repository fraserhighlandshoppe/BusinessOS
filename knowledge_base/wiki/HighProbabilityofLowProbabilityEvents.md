===High Probability of Low Probability Events===
We all have a series of low probability events (Sick on any given day - e.g. 1 day a month).  We all start the day with good intentions.  But there is a high probability one of the low probability events will occur.  If we allow ourselves to be distracted from our plan by these events (it's just this once) it becomes a pattern where every day this one-off takes precedence over the regular important event, and the regular important event never gets accomplished.

We need to protect ourselves against these low probability events by telling everyone that here is my schedule, I am going to stick to it.  And then we need to stick to it.  This way the random one-offs don't take over.

 The interested do the thing when it is convenient.  The committed do it always!
