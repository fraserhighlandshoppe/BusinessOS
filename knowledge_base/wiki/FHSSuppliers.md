===Round Stone Industries===
Located in Pakistan.
*Kilts
*Vests
*Sporrans
*Sgian Dhub
*Scarves
*Blankets
*Cap Badges
*Kilt Pins

Prices do not include shipping.  Most of the time a box costs us 50-60USD.

Ordered a box of kilts (about 25) and shipping was 250USD.  This was about 20% of order's cost.  That said, any time I've looked at shipping cost between Pakistan and Airdrie DHL starts at 300USD regardless of weight.  I assume this larger order will cost more, so we aren't paying the entire shipping cost.

Leather Sporrans were good (some cantels were poorly cut).  Adnan is sending replacement sporrans gratis.  Haven't seen a sample of a fur sporran.

Dance shoes were not suitable for Highland.

Dance Vests are iffy.

Charlies and Argyll are OK.  Not braithworth wool, so lower price, but lower cost.
===Mudassar Sporrans===
*Sporrans
*Hornpipe Hats
*Braid
*Buttons
Mudassar tried to supply us "Argyl" socks but sourced men's dress socks.  Charged us 25USD for a 2$ pair of socks and blamed difficuly in getting a "sample".

Sporrans and Hornpipe hats were decent.  

Silver braid and buttons were fine.  Gold buttons were orange.  Replacement buttons were the same orange.

Prices doubled from 2016 to 2017.  Blamed sending us a 6 year old price list last time.
