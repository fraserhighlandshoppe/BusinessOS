==Advisor Practice Review==
 Desjardin presentation
===What is and APR===
Insurance councils are increasing their monitoring.  This means Insurance Companies need to increase their monitoring.

APR is an assessment of an agent's compliance regime.
*Contractual obligation
*Regulations

===CLHIA Questionnaire===
Series of questions to assess compliance.

Part 1 - Common between insurers.
*Full Legal Name of advisor
*List of jurisdictions where licensed
**Disclosures
**CE
*Confirmation of CEs required for the jurisdictions
**Copies of certificates will be required
*E&O up to date.
*Privacy Policy
*Privacy Breach Procedures
*AML-ATF Policies and Procedures (FINTRACK)
*Copy of Disclosure Document template.
*Needs Based Selling
**Fact Find
**Reason Why Letter (and/or Template)

Part 2 - unique to each insurer.
*Insurer Policies and Procedure Manual
*Confirmation of sound practices
**Info needed to make an informed decision to purchase product
*Confirm training recvd for Sound Practices
*How long agent has been licensed
**Is agent still under period of supervision
*Home jurisdiction for license purposes
*Confirm if they have maintained improper paperwork
**pre-signed form
**signature falsifictation
*Confirm advisor gets DesJardin permission for marketing/ads/SM that references DesJardin
*Any privacy breaches in last 12 months.

===Common Deficiencies===
====CE Credits (Certificates)====
*An accredited course can only produce one CE certificate
**Attending 2x does not produce 2 certs.
*Misplaced or destroyed certificates cannot be counted.
Must provide copies of certs, not just a list.
====Privacy Policy====
*Must review every 2 years for effectiveness
*Tracking sheet for Training Sessions
*Must include Pipeda's 10 fair info principles
*Name a compliance officer
*Includes a privacy form
*Accountability
*Purpose
*Accuracy
*Safeguard
*Access
*recourse
Most insurers have a template that ensures all requirements are met.
====AML-AFT Policy====
Must review every 2 years for effectiveness.
*Tracking sheet for training
*Name a compliance officer
*Risk Assessment review every 2 years.
*Changes to policy should be logged.
Most insurers have a template that ensures all requirements are met.

Highly recommended that advisor also signs!
====File Reviews====
*Disclosure Doc must include all 7 elements.
**Common missed area is CoI.
**MGA/AGA
**Personal corporations
**List of and Nature with represented companies
**Compensation
*Needs space for Advisor and Client signatures.

Highly recommended that advisor also signs!
====Privacy Consent====
*What personal info is collected
*Who its shared with
*Purpose of collection, use, disclosure.
Client needs a copy once they have signed.

Highly recommended that advisor also signs!

====Client Notes====
*Document all fact finding and needs assessments notes
**What was discussed
**Recommendations made, accepted or declined
*Electronic or hand witten.
Every interaction with client should be noted, even if seems irrelevant.
*Calls
*Emails
*Discussions
Also note any concerns the client raises 
*disatisfaction

FNA
*Evidence of suitability of recommendation
*A formal Fact Find questionaire / FNA
*Key facts
Don't accept the clients pre-determined need

If client declines to do an FNA, note in client notes and Reason Why.

If FNA says 1M but client purchases 250k Reason Why needs to explain the discreptioncy.

====Life Insurance Replacement====
*Required when a purchase will result in the termination, cancellation or reduction of benefits of another contract.
*Advantages and Disadvantages need to be provided
**To client
**To new insurer
**To old insurer. (ON, NL)

====Reason Why====
Contains
*Date
*Type, Amount, name of insurer
*Key facts that led to recommendation
**FNA or decline of doing FNA
*How the recommendation meets client needs
*Other topics discussed
*Invitation to contact agent if anything incorrect/omitted.

===Receiving a Report===
Any deficiencies noted by auditor will be in summary report.

May also include recommendations which are not necessarily be deficiencies.
===Closing the APR===
Agent is required to provide missing documents, confirmations and explanations to address deficiencies.

Once all deficiencies are adequately addressed the APR will be closed.
