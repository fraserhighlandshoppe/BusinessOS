*Accountability
*Identify the Purpose
*Consent
*Limiting collection
*Limiting use, disclosure, retention
*Accuracy
*Safeguards
*Openness

===Meaningful Consent===
*Emphasize key elements
*Allow individuals to control the level of detail they get and when
**They must understand what they are consenting to .
*Provide individuals with clear options to say yes or no.
*Be innovative and creative.
**Should be specific to context.
*Consider the consumers perspective.
**Friendly.  Understandable.
*Make consent a dynamic and ongoing process.
**Obtain new consent when making changes to practice.
*Be accountable,  be ready to demonstrate compliance.

===Privacy Breach===
Loss, unauthorized access to, or disclosure of, personal Information.

It is unauthorized if it occurs in contravention of applicable legislation.
*Stolen
*Lost
*Mistakenly shared.

===Responding===
#Containt breach.  Preliminary Assessment
#Evaaluate the risk associated with the breach
##How many people affected.  What information at risk
##What caused the breach.  Extent?
###Stolen?
###Misdirected email?
#What harm to individuals will result from the breach?
##Is there a risk of significant harm?
##Is the risk real?
#Notification
#Prevention

===Significant Harm===
*identity theft
*Financial loss
*Negative efects to credit score or credit record
*Loss of employment
*Loss of business or personal opportunities
*Damange to reputation or relationships
*Humiliation
*Loss or damage to property
*Bodily harm

===Notification===
*Customer
*Police
*Privacy commissioner
*MGA
*Insurance carriers
*E&O Provider
**Cyber coverage?
When?
*As soon as possible, subject to contrary direction from law enforcement
How?
*Letter, email, phone call or in person.
*In some cases, privacy commissioner may allow indirect notification
**Website information, posted notices, media
*Consider using multiple methods to ensure actual notification
What?
*A description of the circumstances
*Day, or period when breach occurred.
*Description of personal information that is subject to the breach
*Steps that you are taking to reduce the risk
*A description of steps that affected individuals could take to reduce the risk of harm
*Contact info the affected can use to obtain further info.
===Prevention===
*Take time to thoroughly investigate the cause
**Security audit.  Physical, tech
*Develop/improve adequate long term safeguards
*Review policies and procedures.  Update to reflect lessons learned
*Additional training
