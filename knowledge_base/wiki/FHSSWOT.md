===SWOT Analysis===

 The following SWOT analysis captures the key strengths and weaknesses within the company, 
 and describes the opportunities and threats that face BOG.

 If having difficulties, look at the following:
 #company's philosophy or mission?
 #product features, benefits or quality?
 #product's competitive advantage? (Is there a competitive advantage?)
 #distribution methods or distributor satisfaction?
 #pricing structures? Is it priced much higher or lower than the competition?
 #target market's awareness of your product?
 #target market's attitudes toward the product (or its category)?
 #target market's brand loyalty?
 #competition's activities? (new product launches, price changes, new companies, etc.)
 #overall market? (shifts in needs, trends, behaviors, etc.)

[[Media: SWOT Analysis.pdf]]
====Strengths====
*Low overhead that provides excellent margins and flexibility in pricing.
*Availability during waking hours so that customers can purchase when they want to.
*Experience and active participation in the Piping and Drumming world.
*Experience in the Dance world.

====Weaknesses====
*As a home-based start-up, if sales really spike it might be difficult to fulfill a large increase in orders.
*A limited marketing budget to generate awareness of FHS and its service/product offerings.
*Outside commitments can limited the number of hours a day that can be spent on sales/business development relative to order fulfillment.
*The company lacks visibility in the eyes of our target market.
*The requirement to generate awareness, not only about the company, but for the industry as a whole.
*Limited cash.
*Little experience with local politics.
*The struggle to continually forecast buying trends and stock inventory accordingly.
*Currently a lack of Company reputation
*Product reputation (need to research the various brands)
*Lack of Market share
*Lethargy in marketing.
**analysis paralysis

 *Value your company brings to the market
 *Quality of your product
 *Quality of your customer service and support (or other area of service)
 *Quality/effectiveness of past promotions and other marketing efforts
 *Pricing
 *Distribution
 *Geographical advantages
 *Operational leadership
 *Financial strength
 *Manufacturing capabilities
 *Responsiveness of workforce

====Opportunities and Trends====
*Participation in an industry that is not well served locally.
*No well-focused, well-marketed competition.
*The cost of Internet and other direct marketing opportunities have decreased in recent years.
*Pipe band community is cyclic as well as economic dependant.

 Significant changes in:
 *tech
 *distribution channels
 *markets
 *consumer and social trends
 *product innovation

====Threats====
*Competition from industry gorillas like TartanTown.  
**They have experience, reputation.  
**They have world class pipers running (owning) it.
*A slump in the economy decreasing people's discretionary spending.
*A significant increase in shipping costs.
*General trepidation of Internet-based business.
