==Fees==
Questrade running ads that suggest that advisors aren't worth the fees.

Questrade is using a small set of ETFs for their investment choices.  They are also using a "basket" of 80 Mutual Funds to get their rates of return, MER, etc to compare against.  So they aren't comparing apples to apples.

Questrade admits in its small print that there is no statement of reasonableness of their assumptions in their math.

===Approaches===
*Proactive - approach client first
*Reactive - be prepared to present strong couter argument.

Distinguish who to be proactive with versus reactive.
You can be reactive if the client meets all 5 below:
*Has a personal relationship with you
*Takes your advice
*Respects your process
*Understand they have deeper needs
*Willing to pay for value

====Being proactive====
Best time to broach is when client is still happy.
#set up a progress report meeting
#Revisit your value proposition
##Remind them that you are there to help them achieve ALL of their financial goals, not just investing.
#Then discuss Questrade ads.
