==Ideal Client Objectives==
Even with a good-sized book, most advisors want to continue growing their practice. One way to retain clients, win more of their business, and attract new clients is to communicate your value. Clients need to know that you’re helping them reach their goals by providing personalized service and valuable advice.

===Here are the top 10 reasons to focus on an ideal client===
*Simplifies your Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
*Simplifies your prospecting activities
*Triggers your Reticular Activating System (RAS)
*Makes you more referable
*Simplifies your product & strategy offering
*Simplifies your client servicing offer
*Simplifies your client segmentation process
*Makes you “the expert” in a specific field
*Creates value more easily for your clients
*Makes your business more scalable

===Your brand is what others say about you===
*Are people talking about you, our benefits and differentiators?
*What do you want people to say about you?
*Have you provided something memorable to share? Something that stands you apart?
*Have you clarified your USP?
 Why do my clients work with me?

I’m a financial advisor. We work with individuals, families and businesses to help them reach their goals through financial planning and sound investing.
 vs
“I’m a financial advisor. I specialize in helping corporate executives monetize their executive compensation benefits—many from companies or industries like (fill in the blank)…”
 This statement is much more specific—and therefore memorable and effective.

"I'm an insurance advisor. I specialize in risk protection."
 vs
“I’m an insurance advisor. I specialize in helping early retirees prepare to have the right health plans in place."

===Turn a feature into a differentiator===
Feature. "I've been in this business for 20 years."

Connector statement. "Let me share with you why that may be important to you…”

Benefit. “In 20 years, I’ve experienced 13 major downturns of the market, and I understand how to help protect our clients. We’ve been here before.”
 Always include a connector statement and the client benefit.
 
 I'm a financial advisor.  We specialize in helping executives monetize complex executive compensation programs, including stock options and deferred comp benefits.  In fact, to date we’ve been able to uncover/maximize, on average, an additional 13%1 in overall benefits for our executive clients.

====Core Story====
You do investment planning, financial planning, retirement planning, estate planning, insurance planning, banking and lending, tax planning, financial planning, succession planning and on and on AND MORE!

This makes up YOU. The question is, how do you organize all of this into your core story and make it memorable so that someone who doesn’t know you will remember why they should work with you? 

And the WHY is key.


====3 Competencies Funnels====
Well, in this case it’s providing “Goals-based wealth management services,” providing “Personalized investment advice”, and finally “A Qualified Team – with real Intellectual Capital”.  

Under Goals Based Wealth Management – they will discuss items such as developing a goals based financial plan, retirement planning, providing estate planning and tax planning support, longevity planning, business succession planning, strategic philanthropy planning, executive compensation planning, and banking and lending services.

Under Personalized Investment Advice – they will discuss items such as developing a formal investment policy statement and investment philosophy, delivering personalized portfolio construction and customized reporting, monitoring performance and results - vs. client’s stated goals and not an arbitrary benchmark They let their clients and prospects know that they stress test the portfolio 2-3 times per year, regularly provide a quarterly investment outlook and finally engage in quarterly strategy meetings.

And, under A Qualified Team with Intellectual Capital – they will discuss items like:  The team’s experience & focus or their niches, they will detail the team’s designations and experience – and why that is important to the client, they will make sure their clients know they consider themselves financial professionals to the entire family – a financial coach if you will. They are also a technology assistant – providing digital access to the clients’ accounts. They are risk managers, and finally they are their clients’ personal CFO.  If a decision involves anything with a $ - they ask to be involved in the conversation.

====Communicate your Process====
Begin by identifying, at a high level, the services that you deliver (or want to deliver) in your practice.

These would be your 3 core competencies.

Brainstorm with your team the step-by-step process that you want your clients to experience.

Turn each process into a visual graphic you can show your clients.

You will want to add the same language to your website and your LinkedIn profiles and ensure everyone on your team is saying the same thing. 

This is where consistency is key!

===Talking Fees===
#What is the cost for delivering the service
##initial
##ongoing
#What is considered part of the AUM fee.  What should have a separate fee structure.
#What are the competitors charging.  How do their service levels differ.
#What are online platforms charging for advice (for comparison)
#What are the total costs to the client including investment fees and my fee
#What is the minimum level of revenue that will cover expenses and contribute to profit?

The most important thing to do when communicating your fees is to be completely transparent and communicate with confidence. If you have done the work to communicate your value in a meaningful way, fees are rarely an issue.

However, it is important to note that there is a lot of experimentation going on with fee models. AUM fees still account for the majority of fee models however, flat retainer fees, hourly fees, and subscription fees continue to gain momentum. And, many advisors are starting to employ a hybrid approach of AUM fees + one of the other fee models.

You need to think critically about aligning the value and services you provide with the fees you charge. Be clear, be consistent, and be transparent.
