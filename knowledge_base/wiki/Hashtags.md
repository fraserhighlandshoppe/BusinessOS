==Hashtags==
===What are hashtags===
A hashtag is a keyword or keywords jammed together with a hash mark (#) in front of them.  An example would be #FHS or #fraserhighlandshoppe.

===Finding clients through hashtags===
Search the hashtags for the niche you want to hit. 

Then start networking to the people that are commenting/liking/posting with these tags.

===Posting with hashtags===
*Facebook penalizes you for using hashtags.  They don't want your content to be cross posted.  They want to control it all!
*Instagram searches on hashtag.  However, less is more. (Travelex)
*Twitter searches on hashtag.  16-22 a good number to use on a post.
