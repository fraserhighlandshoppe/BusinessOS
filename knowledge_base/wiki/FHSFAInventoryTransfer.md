==Transferring Inventory from one warehouse to another==
#Login to FrontAccounting
#Go to Items and Inventory.
#Click on Inventory Location Transfer

[[File: FA InventoryLocationTransfer_Select.png]]

===Inventory Location Transfer===
 This creates a transaction within the item.
#Select the From and To locations
#Confirm Date
#Confirm Transfer Type (Typically Adjustment)
#Enter Item Codes and Quantities.  Click ADD.
#Enter a Memo
#Click Process Transfer
#on the next page, click View this transfer (to print)
#Print the transfer.
