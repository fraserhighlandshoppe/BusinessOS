If you had 2 minutes to impress someone, what would you say, show or give them that would blow their mind?

You can talk about how awesome you are, or you can show it.  Is there a way to demonstrate the results they will get from your tripwire without letting the cat out of the bag? What is their burning question?

Hooks:
*What does the end user have after taking the action that they didn't have before
*How do they feel after consuming the offer.
*Average day - how is the average day better after consuming the offer
*Status - how are you elevating their status.
*Proven results - how can you show that you should do this because others have and it works.
*Speed and Automation - how do you make something quicker or easier.

Use as many hooks as you can.

Try to enter the conversation going on in their brain...
