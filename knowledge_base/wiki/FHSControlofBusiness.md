====Control of the operation of the business====

Unless otherwise specified, the equity holders are non controlling share holders.  They are allowed to voice their concerns at any time, and may provide input during annual share holder meetings, but they will have no authority over the day to day operations of the business.

Strategic control will rest with the directors.  Daily operation control will rest with the [[FHS Management of Business|operation management team]] employed to run the business.

 Note: shareholder may have a legal meaning that may be inappropriate here due to 
 the resulting legal structure.  Substitute the equivalent word.

When shareholders are called to vote, there shall be two components required for the vote to pass:

    60% of the shares held must vote for the resolution
    51% of the share holders (rounded up to the nearest whole person) must vote for the resolution.

Example:  The following shareholders exist

    Shareholder 1 – 35%
    Shareholder 2 – 35%
    Shareholder 3 – 10%
    Shareholder 4 – 10%
    Shareholder 5 – 10%

For a vote, at least two shareholders (depending on which ones) must vote for the resolution for the 60%, but 3 members must also vote for the resolution for the motion to pass.  This provision is to ensure that a minority of share holders can’t hold captive the rest of the shareholders.
