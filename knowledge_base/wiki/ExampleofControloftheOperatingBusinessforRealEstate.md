===Control of the operation of the business===

Unless otherwise specified, the equity holders are non controlling share holders.  They are allowed to voice their concerns at any time, and may provide input during annual share holder meetings, but they will have no authority over the day to day operations of the business.

Daily operation control will rest with the designated share holders (directors), and the operation management team employed to run the business.

Note: shareholder may have a legal meaning that is inappropriate here due to the resulting legal structure.

When shareholders are called to vote, there shall be two components required for the vote to pass:

    60% of the shares held must vote for the resolution
    51% of the share holders (rounded up to the nearest whole person) must vote for the resolution.

Example:  The following shareholders exist

    Deal maker – 50%
    Shareholder 1 – 10%
    Shareholder 2 – 10%
    Shareholder 3 – 10%
    Shareholder 4 – 10%
    Shareholder 5 – 10%

For a vote, the deal maker and at least one other shareholder must vote for the resolution for the 60%, but 4 members (shareholders + deal maker) must also vote for the resolution for the motion to pass.  This provision is to ensure the deal maker and 1 share holder can’t hold captive the rest of the shareholders.
