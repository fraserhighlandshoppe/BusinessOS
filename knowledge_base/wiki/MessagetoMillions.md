===Powerful Types of Stories===
Every story has:
*The Call
**What disrupted your life
**The Challenge/Opportunity presented to you that "Calls" you into the journey
*The Pit
**The very low almost immediately after the Call
*The Search
**Pulling yourself out of the PIT
**Searching for answers.
***Probably obstacles along the way
*The Breakthrough
When selling, you need the results after the Breakthrough to give credibility.

You need the PIT or they won't buy.

===Package your message into a profitable program===
At this point we've enhanced our message from our life story
*What were we thinking.  What was going through our mind during the journey
Now we need to write down the skillsets we learned at each stage of the journey
*What did we learn before, that got us to that stage.
*What skills did we then need to go learn at that stage.

Look at the story and the events.
*What were the searches
*What were the breaktrhoughs and lessons
*Organize them into a sequence.
Now we have the steps to package into a program.
