==Building a Storyline==
===Attributes of the storyline===
*Simple to Understand
**clear structure and timeline
**anyone can tell the story in 2 minutes
*Relevant, Interesting and attractive
**start by thinking about your audience
**consider entry level audience versus decision maker
*positively surprise the audience
*challenge traditional and prevalent thinking
*Specific in terms of benefits and return
*Underline that we are committed
*told in plain language.
**no tech buzzwords
===Essential parts of the storyline===
#our understanding of the situation
##show that we understand the big picture
##do not try to explain their business to them
##show sources of numbers and key information
#our value proposition
##outline what we suggest to achieve
##list the key initiatives and dependencies
##focus on benefits (quantity, quality)
##demonstrate our commitment
#next steps
##clearly state what we believe are the most sensible next steps
##outline objectives and benefits of these next steps
##outline time and client resources required
##make it an easy decision
Point out that the numbers are preliminary and that the data needs confirmation.

Be prepared to tell the entire story without a slide.

==Selling the value proposition==
You can show the client, or you can sell the client.  This forms a matrix:

===Selective Sell Selective Show===
This is a focused scope approach.
*low risk
*used where we don't have a strong relationship in place
*can be used for a start small and grow approach
*requires a mid to long term view
===Selective Sell Full Show===
Here we outline the overall roadmpa but focus on entry point or clearly defined scope
*medium risk strategy
*demonstrates that we understand and are capable of delivering a full scope value proposition
*works well in a storyline
===Full Sell Full Show===
Outline full roadmap and demonstrate the full scope value proposition.
*highest risk strategy
*usually the largest business benefits
*requires a good relationship with CxOs.
*requires that we have an excellent reputation
===Full Sell Selective Show===
Only useful as a sales strategy to specific stakeholders at different levels
*dangerous strategy
*might be necessary to keep certain players in the dark about the full scope
*depends on client culture and politics
*only use when no substantial existing business at risk.
*works best with clients that have a compelling reason to act.

==Evaluating options==
#Suitability
#Feasibility
#Acceptability

List each option, grade for these 3 questions.  As soon as there is either a No or a question mark, that option is no longer an option.

Can also add other questions (rating H/M/L).  Examples include:
#business at risk
#business risk
#keeping market position
#flexibility and architectural fit
#mid term cost base
