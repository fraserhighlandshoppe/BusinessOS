==Elevator Pitch==
You need to practice your elevator pitch so that it is fluid, and comes naturally.  You need to have [[Determine Your Brand|determined your brand]] before you can successfully build your elevator pitch.  However, you should be considering the points in the template below while determining the brand!

Your elevator pitch should be no longer than 30 seconds.  It should also be intriguing, funny and specific.  However, it needs to be a conversation starter rather than something that lets your audience mentally file you into a category of all the other people they know that does the same thing.  (See [[Why the Elevator Pitch Hurts Your Chances of Winning New Clients]] ).

[[Category: TravelEx]]

===Building the Elevator pitch===
Take something complicated (your product or service) and make it sound simple, compelling and inevitable!  (See [[Elevator Pitch to get Funding]]).

Potential employees need to understand your vision of the future to decide whether, against all other choices, they will join you. 
Customers need to stop being satisfied with the status quo and queue up for whatever you are going to deliver. Your elevator pitch is a proxy for all of these things. 

*Complex products need a simple summary
*Tell me why I should quit my job to join you
*Tell me why I should invest in you rather than the line outside my door
*Tell me why I should buy from you rather than the existing suppliers
*Do it in 100 words or less
