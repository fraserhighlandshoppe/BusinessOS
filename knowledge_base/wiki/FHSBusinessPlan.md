====Incorporation of the business====

The Business shall be opened as a Partnership. (Service Alberta definition http://www.servicealberta.com/730.cfm).  The [[FHS Partnership Registration|partnership is registered]] with the Province of Alberta through an authorized registry.

The business shall have only 1 class of shares. The business shall have 100 shares that it may issue.  If "shares" is a legal term that does not apply to the business structure (i.e. Partnership), the use of the term shares shall represent percentage ownership.

Beyond the voting, dividend and dissolution rights prescribed by the Business Corporations Act or Partnership Act, there are no special rights or restrictions attached to these shares.  

These shares may not be offered to the public.

There is a maximum of 50 shareholders permitted under the Act.

The ownership of shares and transfer of shares shall be controlled.  Transfer of shares is allowed only within the constrains of the Exit Plan as described below.  All sale and transfer of shares must be approved by holders of a majority of the shares.

Due to the nature of the business, share holders will not have their names added to the title of the property.  Income, Dividends and resulting tax obligations shall be resolved as specified below.

Upon dissolution of the business, assets shall be split as described in the Exit Plan.

The business shall be dissolved only after a vote of shareholders, with the requirements for a successful vote as specified below (percentage of shares, percentage of share holders).

===Reserve Funds===

The business will provide a Reserve Fund budget.  The Reserve Fund shall be funded to such a level that over a 3 year time period of the budget the fund will never have a negative cash balance.

If the reserve fund reaches less than 50% of the projected value for the given year of the projection, a cash call shall be required where each share holder must contribute cash to the reserve fund to bring it back up to projected levels.

Any time that the reserve fund is below the projected level from the budget, any free cash flow shall be used to top up the reserve fund.  This will impact the amount of cash available to pay out as dividends.
===Payment of Dividends===

The board of directors may recommend to the share holders to issue a dividend to the share holders.  The amount of cash available to pay the dividends shall be calculated by:

    ensure all debt obligations have been paid.  This includes but is not limited to:
        interest (mortgage, loans, etc) payments,
        debt interest holder payments
        all operating expenses
        All of the next year’s capital reserve fund
    taking the net income after all expenses and current obligations have been paid, and set aside 50% as an operations reserve.
    The remainder is what is available for dividends.

===Tax Implications===
 http://www.cra-arc.gc.ca/tx/bsnss/tpcs/slprtnr/prtnrshp/rprtng-eng.html

Generally, a Partnership does not pay income tax on its Income and does not file an income tax return.

Instead, each partner files an income tax return to report their share of the partnership's net income or loss.

This requirement for each partner to report their share of the partnership's net income is the same whether the share of income was received in cash or as a credit to one of the partnership's capital accounts.

====Tax Forms====
 http://www.cra-arc.gc.ca/E/pub/tg/t5013-inst/t5013-inst-e.html  T5013 Statement of Partnership Income

Small scale partnerships only need to fill out the T2125

====Losses====
A Partnership can have a loss. However, apply the loss carry-over rules to each partner, and not to the partnership.

For example, when you complete your own income tax return, combine your share of the partnership non-capital losses with any other non-capital losses you have in the year. Apply this amount against your Income.

The loss carry-forward period is 20 years for:

    non-capital losses, farm losses, restricted farm losses and life insurer's Canadian life investment losses incurred; and
    investment tax credits earned for Scientific Research and Experimental Development (SR&ED).

For information about the loss carry-forward period for non-capital losses, go to Line 252 – Non-capital losses of other years. 

====GST Rebates====
 http://www.cra-arc.gc.ca/tx/bsnss/tpcs/slprtnr/prtnrshp/rbt-eng.html

If you are an individual who is a member of a Partnership, you may be able to get a rebate for the GST/HST you paid on certain expenses.

You may qualify for this rebate if:

    the partnership is a GST/HST registrant; and
    you personally paid GST/HST on Expenses that:
        you did not incur on the account of the partnership; and
        you deducted from your share of the partnership Income on your income tax return.

However, special rules apply if your partnership paid you an allowance for those expenses.

Examples of expenses subject to the GST/HST are vehicle costs, meals, and entertainment.

The rebate may also apply to the GST/HST you paid on motor vehicles, musical instruments, and aircraft, for which you deducted Capital cost allowance (CCA).

The eligible portion of CCA is the part of the CCA that you deduct on your income tax return in the tax year, that relates specifically to a motor vehicle, musical instrument, or aircraft on which you paid GST/HST and that is eligible for the rebate to the extent that the partnership used the property to make taxable supplies.

If you deduct CCA on more than one property of the same class, you have to separate the portion of the CCA for the property that qualifies for the rebate from the CCA for the other property. If any part of the rebate relates to the CCA deduction for a motor vehicle, a musical instrument, or an aircraft, you have to reduce the Undepreciated capital cost (UCC) of the related property by that part of the rebate.

To claim your GST/HST rebate for partners, complete Form GST370, Employee and Partner GST/HST Rebate Application. If you receive this rebate, you have to include it in your income for the tax year in which you receive it.
Example

If in 2013 you receive a GST/HST rebate relating to the 2012 tax year (on your 2012 notice of assessment), you have to include the amount of the rebate on your income tax return for 2013:

    Report the amount of the GST/HST rebate for partners that relates to eligible expenses other than CCA on Line 9974 – GST/HST rebate for partners received in the year in Part 6 of your 2013 Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities; and
    In column 2 of Area A on page 5 of your 2013 Form T2125, reduce the UCC for the beginning of 2013 by the portion of the rebate that relates to the eligible CCA.

===Investor participation levels===
====Equity Interest====

An equity interest holder shares in the equity of the business.   The equity holder does not receive any interest payments as they own a share of the business rather than having loaned money to the business.

If the business finds itself in a situation where it requires an injection of cash, each equity holder will need to fund the injection to the percentage of their share holding.  If an equity holder is unable to fund their portion, they will have their percentage of interest reduced appropriately to compensate the other holders for the increased funding they will be required to provide.  

The investing partners will share the equity based upon how much of the cash their share of the total cash is.  This is done to balance the risk/reward ratio.

====Investor Exit situations====
=====Investor is moving=====

The investor does not gain any special exit rights due to a relocation.
=====Investor wants to sell their equity=====

If the investor wants to sell their equity in the partnership, they must first offer it to the remaining members of the partnership.  The members shall negotiate amongst their selves the split of the offered equity.

If the partners either do not want the additional equity, or can not afford to purchase the equity, the offering partner must obtain the remaining partners agreement to offer the equity to a new partner.  All remaining partners will need to sign off on the new partner joining the partnership.

All rights and obligations attached to the equity shall transfer to the new owner upon the sale of said equity.
=====Investor passes away=====

If the investor passes away, the equity must be offered to the members of the partnership.  The members may decide to purchase that equity by returning funds to the estate (or heir) of the partner that passed away.  

If the partners don't want to purchase the equity it may be offered to a new investor.  Similar to a straight sale, the remaining members will need to sign off on the new partner.  If the new partner isn’t approved by all remaining members, the potential partner shall need to sell the equity to someone who the partners can approve of.

===Irreconcillable Disputes===

In the case of a dispute between 2 members that can not be resolved, a shotgun approach shall be used to remove one member from the partnership.  One member shall buy out the other member’s shares.  The first member shall offer to buy out the other member for $x per percent.  The second member has a decision – do they accept the offer and be bought out, or do they instead pay that amount per share to buy out the other member.
