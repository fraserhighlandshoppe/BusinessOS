==To lead many, just show them the way==

KARL MOORE – This is Karl Moore of the Desautels Faculty of Management at McGIll University with Talking Management for The Globe and Mail. Today I am delighted to speak to Marc Poulin who is the CEO of Empire Co. Ltd., the parent of Sobeys, one of Canada’s top retail supermarket chains.

You have almost 100,000 people working for you now, how can you possibly lead 100,000 people? How do you do that?

MARC POULIN – I guess at the end of the day everyone has to understand what we are here for. That is part of what we are trying to do at Sobeys, is to make sure that everyone understands what a great food experience should be, in our minds, and how can we contribute in everything that we do to make that food experience for the customer what it should be.

People have brains, people know what they can do, but if they know what we want to do you have to have the confidence to put the tools in their hands to actually deliver that. The first and most important thing is to make sure people understand – I usually refer to the phrase, ‘What do we want to be when we grow up?’ Well, once that is secured in someone’s head, then you can unleash the power of 100,000 people. So it is not a question of what can I do for 100,000 people, it’s what can 100,000 people can do for all Canadians?

KARL MOORE – So what is the role of the CEO? The CEO doesn’t provide all the answers? But what is the role of the CEO, what’s the job of the CEO?

MARC POULIN – Well, the job of the CEO is, in my mind, to create the environment where people can actually perform but also to make sure everybody performs with the common goal and the same goal so that actually as a team there is cohesion.

Business is a series of small decisions being taken along the way, you just want to make sure that everybody who has to make a decision first does it, because it is so easy nowadays to postpone, and that the decision is being made in a context [so] that will advance the greater cause.
