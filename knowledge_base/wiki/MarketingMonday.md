==Social Media==
Television is the new radio; Smart Phones are the new TV. 

People are spending 5-7 hours a day on their phones.

If you can get a bit of time/space on their platform, how much space would you want?  As much as possible.

Need to build your brand.  It is not about vanity/selfies.
*Make videos
*Take selfies that build on your brand
40-50% engagement bump when your face in your pics - you are the brand.

Stop worrying about what everyone else thinks about you.  In 5 years you will be glad.

This will keep you excited in your business.  You will be vocal about your business.  It is difficult to quit the business once you start talking about it online.  You lock yourself in.

 I am a Brand!

Personal branding is managing and optimizing how you present yourself to other people.

Social media is not a replacement for going and talking to people.  SM doesn't cover up your flaws.  It can enhance an already strong culture.

To see how you are branded, google your First Name + Last Name + location.

Increase your presence:
*Prospect new people
*Get your name out
*Retention tool
**Builds a community around your team and business.
*Gives you presence.
**Give someone your instagram handle + get phone number rather than ONLY get phone number.
Benefit to having good content where people check you out before hand.

People trust SM more than corporate website.

===Developing a culture===
Every team has a culture.  It depends on who is casting the vision and who is leading the team.

Every champion team has developed a winning culture.  This is the things that the leader has determined what is important and shone a light upon.

There is no accidental winning culture.  It isn't just a matter of time.  What can I do to improve the team culture?

#The culture needs to be clearly defined.  Define what is acceptable and what is not.
##Develop your brand early.
##Why does this team exist?  
##Why are we all here?  
##What is this for?  
##What is it going to do for me in my life?
##Get everyone speaking about the culture.
##Get people to ask "Is what I am doing now in line with the team culture?"
#Communication is everything.  Need to communicate the culture to the team.
##Welcome to the team - our culture is...
##Communication is a big hole in 99% of organizations.
##Be continously meeting with the team mates and communicate what the culture is.
#Create structure
##Structure creates certainty.
##Structure is the walls that contain the culture
###people need to know where they need to be and what they need to be doing.
#Accountability
##There needs to be open accountability
##People that don't want to be accountability are probably not a good fit for your team.
##If this is the thing you are spending the majority of time and effort on, there must be accountibility
#Get your team involved
##Give your teammates a role/job
##Let them share experiences related to the core values.
#De-clutter and a fresh coat of paint
##physically change your surroundings
##When you change your environment you change your culture.
#Stay on it
##It can take 3 years to change a culture.
##Most people start-stop-start-stop.
##In the beginning you will be the only one talking about the culture.  
###Many leaders introduce flavors of the month.
###This is the team testing whether this will last.
##Have to give it time to compound.
##If in doubt, check with your mentor/leader to review whether you are on track.
