====What MLM top earners know that the rest of us don't====
Direct Sellers Association reports that only 1/4 of 1% makes significant residual income.  They also report:
*80% will quit within 90 days
*70% feel disenfranchised lacking upline support
*97% will never break even.
 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GuUzUgW0fIw
At the start of their business, people have the highest level of anxiety, uncertainty and discomfort while their skills and knowledge is at the lowest.

Companies "address" this by saying follow the start-up kit.
*make your list
*call
New distributors - Reasons for anxiety
*Don't know what to say or how to say it
*Scary reactions (from friends and family)
*Overwhelmed by amount of information
*Unmet expectations
*Fear and frustration -> stop talking.
Mid Range Earners
*don't see recruits advancing
*transitioning from hobbyist to professional is challenging.
*No business experience
*no time management system in place so reacting to everything
*confidence starts waining due to reacting rather than pro-active
*trying to out-recruit loss of retention.
Top earners
*feel horrible
*don't know how to pass along the knowledge.
*Not training next set of leaders
