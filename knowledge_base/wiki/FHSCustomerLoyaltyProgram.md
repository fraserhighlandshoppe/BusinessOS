==Customer Loyalty Program==

To become a member of our Loyalty Program, you need to register with us.  The easiest way is to subscribe to our newsletter.

As a member of the Loyalty Program, you will receive discounts on virtually all products on our site/in-store and extra discounts via our newsletter.  You will also receive advanced notice of sales and promotions.

Members of our Loyalty Program can receive additional benefits (Non Refundable Instore Credits) through our Referrals Program.

Members of our Loyalty Program will receive special offers not available to the general public via our newsletter.  Sign up now!
