==Receive a Shipment==

Assumption the Purchase Order exists in Front Accounting.  See [[FHS FA Creating a Purchase Order]]

#Log in to Front Accounting
#Click Purchases Tab at the top of the screen
#Click Outstanding Purchase Orders link
#Select the appropriate Purchase Order (you may have to change the date range)

[[Image: FA Purchase Tab.png]]

[[Image: FA Purchase Link.png]]
===Select Oustanding Purchase Order===
#Click on the Receive Icon at the right end of the row
##Alternatively, you can alter the PO by clicking on Edit (pencil).

[[Image: FA Select PO.png]]

===Receive Items===
#Select Appropriate Data
##Change the Deliver Into Location
##Confirm the Receive Date
##Confirm This Delivery numbers and update as necessary
#Click on Process Receive Items
