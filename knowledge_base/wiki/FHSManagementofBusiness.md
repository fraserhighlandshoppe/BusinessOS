*experience, 
*service, 
*professionalism, 
*excellence, 
*communication and 
*timeliness.
====Decision Makers====
Strategic decisions will be made by the board of directors.

Daily operating decisions shall be made by the department heads in consultation with the Chief Executive Officer.

A management agreement shall be drawn up and reviewed by Legal Counsel.  The CEO shall supervise the General Partners.  

The COO shall obtain insurance for the business.

The COO shall ensure the business has copies of all documents relevant to the business.  This includes blueprints, specifications, title, permits, inspection certificates, warranties, guarantees, and contracts.

The CFO shall ensure all receivables are collected on time and any payables are paid before due dates but as late as possible.  The CFO shall ensure a budget is prepared as well as bank accounts being established and managed.

The General Partners shall meet at a minimum once a quarter to discuss the operation of the business.  They shall review a consolidated summary of the reports, book keeper reports, legal summaries and accountant advisories.  The PAO shall produce a meeting minutes to be distributed to the equity and debt interest holders for review.

The General Partners as well as the equity and debt interest holders shall meet at a minimum on an annual basis to review the operation of the business. At this Annual General Meeting (AGM) the following shall occur:

    The COO shall discuss the issues and risks experienced over the course of the year as well as any new risks, and likely risks for the coming year.
    The COO shall discuss any maintenance performed, capital improvements conducted as well as what capital items are on the schedule for the following year.
    The CFO shall present a summarized expense report as well as a projected budget for the following year.
    The CFO shall confirm that insurance is in place at appropriate levels.
    The PAO shall address the marketing of the property.
    The COO shall advise of any legal issues experienced or anticipated.

Equity and Debt interest holders shall be provided the opportunity to address any questions or concerns they have over the business at the AGM.
