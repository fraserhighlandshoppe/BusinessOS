 FrontAccounting allows us to pay a supplier before or after we've created the [[FHS FA Invoicing a Supplier Shipment|Supplier Invoice]] in FA.  
 If we are charged before invoiced,  we pay, and later [[FA Allocate Supplier Payment|Allocate Payments]].
===Paying a Supplier in FA===
#Go to the Purchase tab.
#Select Payment to Suppliers

[[Image: FA Purchase Tab Transactions.png]]

===Supplier Payment Entry===
#Select the correct vendor
#Select the correct date payment was made
#select the bank account the payment was made out of
#Enter a bank charge if appropriate
#Select the invoices you are sending payment for, and enter the allocations.
#Enter a memo
#Hit Process

[[Image: FA Supplier Payment.png]]

On the next screen
#Select Print this Remittance.  1 copy must go to the book keeper (gets attached to the invoice)
