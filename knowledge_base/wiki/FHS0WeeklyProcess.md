==FHS End of Week Process==
#Balance Cash Box (Wallet)
##Journal Entry using 1001 Magic Cash as opposite account
#Check [https://www.atbonlinebusiness.com/CorporateBankingWeb/core/login.aspx| bank accounts].  Reconcile into FA
##Check cash flow - do we have enough to cover next week?  Next 4 weeks?
#Prep [http://shop.fraserhighlandshoppe.ca/wp-admin/post-new.php article] for next month's newsletter
#Prep next week's weekly sale item.
##Create [http://shop.fraserhighlandshoppe.ca/wp-admin/edit.php?post_type=shop_coupon coupon] or [http://shop.fraserhighlandshoppe.ca/wp-admin/post-new.php?post_type=o-discount Discount]
##Publish to [http://shop.fraserhighlandshoppe.ca/wp-admin/admin.php?page=wysija_campaigns weekly sales letter]
#Accounts Payable
##Tax Report (Submit GST as required)
##Setup transfer for [https://www.atbonlinebusiness.com/CorporateBankingWeb/core/login.aspx | Mastercard]
##Ensure all received shipments have been entered and invoiced
##Annotate invoices to indicate entry (in our case print out entries and staple)
##File invoices
##Pay invoices that have terms (we currently pay at time of purchase via CC to ALL suppliers).
