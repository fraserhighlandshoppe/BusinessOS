==Stories that Sell==
===The Story of You===
====What is this story?====
This is the ‘here’s who I am’ story – the story that makes an immediate impression. It’s your ‘impact’ story.
====Why should we tell it?====
We get the opportunity to talk to people dozens of times every day. Whether it’s a formal meeting at a networking event or just a chance meeting on a train, 
people will often ask you what you are up to, what do you do? 

Usually our response is stalled, unplanned, different every time, and not very inspiring.

The ‘story of you’ is the initial way of having powerful impact when you talk to people, and they get to know you.

====The Formula====
There is a simple formula for the impact story. You start with a statement about your passion. Then you add the story about what happened to you that gave you the awareness of why this is important. 

And finally, you sum up with a clear statement of who you serve, what they experience and how you offer transformation.
#Statement of Passion
#What Happened
#Statement of 
##Who, 
##What, 
##How
====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
I’m passionate about the power of storytelling. 

I remember as a corporate trainer, I saw the transformation in my students when I told a story,
there was amazing engagement and connection.... – that’s when I knew I was on to something. 

So now, I support entrepreneurs to attract clients and build their business with the power of storytelling”.

=====Jeff’s Story=====
I’m passionate about mentoring teens. 

I was viciously bullied, and I don’t know where I would have ended up if I didn’t have an older cousin who took an interest and helped me find myself again. 

So, I’ve dedicated my life and my business to providing a support network to kids who are suffering.
====What to expect====
When you have a powerful ‘story of you’, you can expect people to remember what you do and who you are long after you have met. 

You can also expect interest in your topic and if there’s a fit, a request for continued contact or even to work with you!
===The Story of What===
====What is this story?====
This is the story that answers the question – what happened to you? What was that pivotal experience that made you realize that this is what you need to be doing in your life and in your work? It’s your business’ ‘origin story’.

====Why should we tell it?====
When you are creating a business that matters to you, not just a way to make money, there’s a compelling reason for doing so. 
Usually that’s a super interesting story and it reflects your values, your experience and what you truly stand for.

This story builds your credibility, you haven’t just developed a skill around the topic, but you have a passion for it and a real reason for driving forward. 
It has a fairy tale quality in that it’s set way back and yet is powerful enough to have been a driver for your passion and motivation.

====The Formula====
The formula for the ‘story of what’ starts by taking us back to a long time ago,
perhaps far, far away. 

Start by bringing your listener/reader back to the time and place.   
Share the specific moment of the event, what happened and why it was so surprising.  
Finally, you must share how it made you feel and what you had to do as a result. There is always an urgency in the ‘story of what’, because it became such a powerful driver.


#WAY BACK WHEN (TIME & PLACE)
#EVENT - WHY IT WAS A SURPRISE
#HOW IT MADE YOU FEEL
#WHAT YOU KNEW YOU HAD TO DO

====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
I was invited to a storytelling evening. I walked into the crowded hall,
the storytellers were on the stage. As they began to share their stories
I felt a visceral feeling of connection, my senses were heightened, I
felt a tightening in my stomach and intense excitement. I realized, this
is what I’ve been doing my whole life, I just didn’t know what it was
called. As a trainer, I was telling stories. And in my personal life, I was
always telling stories. I knew in that moment, I had to learn more, I had
to become a professional storyteller. And later, when it was me telling
stories from the stage, I wanted to share it with others, so they could
tell powerful stories too!

=====Judy’s Story=====

As a young lawyer, I thought I was like all the other guys who also graduated and were hired by the biggest firm in my town. That is, until I got married. 
It’s not that the guys didn’t get married too, they did. It was a Monday morning, I had arrived early, I needed to complete a report
that was due by 10am. I was at the copy machine when I overheard two partners in the next room talking about the newbies, who had potential
and who didn’t. My name came up and there was a laugh. “Well, we can forget her, she’ll be having babies soon enough, no serious future there”.

Ever since that day, I’ve been committed to creating equal and diverse workplaces where no one has to feel discriminated against.
====What to expect====
When you have a powerful ‘story of what’, you create allies in your work. People connect to your passion, your drive... and you attract like-minded prospects who are similarly driven but need and crave your guidance. 
It’s like a magnet for your best clients.
===The Story of Who===
====What is this story?====
This is the story that explores who your work is for. 
*Who would be most impacted by what you offer? 
*Who does this affect? 
*Who is your customer?

====Why should we tell it?====
There is an important reason for telling the story of who: It helps your ideal
client recognize themselves in what you are telling and self-select for your
program or services. 

When a person hears a story and sees themselves in it they understand that the person telling the story understands them and their
situation, has had relevant experience and cares about the issues that are most important to them. They easily determine that this is the perfect
person to provide a service specifically tailored to them.

In preparing to tell the ‘Story of Who’, you need to thoroughly understand your ideal client, get into their head, and see the world through their eyes.

You should see their biggest challenge and understand their greatest desire.

====The Formula====
The formula for the ‘Story of Who’ begins with a description of the ideal client that is simple, clear, specific and places them in a typical place doing something quite normal.

Then you must describe their frustration or pain in doing something that is a critical part of their day to day activity. The story will conclude when you have shown the possibility for this person and the promise of what is possible when they get your help.

#DESCRIPTION OF IDEAL CLIENT
#THEIR FRUSTRATION/PAIN
#THE POSSIBILITY
====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
I loved meeting people at the Coach Conference. These were passionate, mission driven folk who wanted to make the world a better place. 
I loved their ideals and their drive. It was just after lunch, she sat down beside me. 
I asked her “what do you do?” She said, “I’m a coach, I help people find transformation”. I almost sighed out loud. Why were
coaches so bad at telling their story? Almost every coach in that place (and there were thousands there) were saying the same thing. It told
me nothing about who she was and what she does, never mind, why I should hire her. From that moment, I knew I needed to help coaches find
their story so they can attract clients and make the business and mission they’re passionate about thrive.
=====Susy’s Story=====
Susy heard about me through a mutual friend. She had been married
for more than 20 years, had 3 kids and was at her wit’s end. It was her
oldest son’s graduation from high school and she and her partner had
been estranged for months by then. She didn’t know how she could
continue the façade for the kids that everything was okay when she
felt her life was falling apart. At first, her husband didn’t want to come
to therapy but after some time he noticed the change in her when we
worked together. Finally, he agreed to try. When their second child
reached her graduation, there was no façade to keep up, they were
doing really well.
====What to expect====
When you have a strong ‘Story of Who’, you end up attracting ideal clients
and your close-rate in sales conversations increases. It is much easier
to sell to someone who has self-selected, who already feels convinced that
you’re the person they need to work with.
===The Experience Story===
====What is this story?====
This is the ‘what’ story, where you share the powerful moments of lived experience that describes what happened to you that made this journey so impactful. 
And by sharing this your audience understands that this could happen to them too. It establishes the possibility of transformation from your work.

====Why should we tell it?====
The experience story illustrates the journey you’ve been on while creating your
business. It builds your credibility and can be quite relatable. This is the story
that makes your prospect think, well if she can do this, why can’t I? It answers
the concern that we all have that maybe this is a great service for someone
else, but will it work for me?

The experience story needs to be carefully suited so that it doesn’t feel
out of reach for the listener; it should describe a normal situation they would
recognize even though something unexpected happens. Your story will show how you 
overcame and managed to get beyond the big challenge.
====The Formula====
We start by describing the situation before the event, the context. Then there
is a dramatic unexpected occurrence that is personal and challenging. We
need to describe it clearly. The outcome of the story shows us our resilience
and helps us step into our strengths.
#THE CONTEXT
#DRAMATIC OCCURRENCE
#OUTCOME & RESILIENCE/STRENGTHS
====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
I had been raising my babies, and slowly but surely tinkering with my
business. A client here and there, a speaking gig, a website – very slow
and not a lot of growth. I had lots of ideas, I was quite diligent about
following through on creative projects, but I was not consistent about
getting clients.

I thought the writing was on the wall for quite some time, but we were
all ignoring it. My partner was given 2 weeks’ notice. The next 6 months
we tried to figure out what to do but there was not a lot out there and
he was fed up with his career path. That’s when I realized, I needed to
make this so-called business (that felt more like a hobby) work. I began
to reach out to contacts and colleagues. I started to hustle. I found
some opportunities and I created some more. And it worked. Over the
next two years, I built a substantial business, and I’ve never looked back.
=====Jessica’s Story=====
I was on a stellar career path, meeting everyone’s expectations, and
climbing the corporate ladder. I liked the work and put in long hours.
I was away at a conference. It was intense and I was quite tired. It
was the end of the day, I jumped into the shower, the water felt blissful, 
that’s when I found the lump. Within a few days, my surgery was 
scheduled. It was the worst kind, and the treatments began right away.

I couldn’t believe that this was happening to me. I was fit and reasonably healthy, it just didn’t make sense. But I got through it, actually it
was quite a gift. I made a lot of changes because of my journey with cancer.
====What to expect====
When you have a strong experience story, you connect with your prospect
at the heart level. They understand that you’ve been through something
intense and challenging but you have prevailed, and you have gained knowledge and wisdom through
through the journey. It’s a powerful way of sharing at a deep level.
===The Failure Story===
====What is this story?==
This is the story of when things went really wrong, the pain, the shame, the
vulnerability. It’s where your humanity shows up. And it’s always accompanied
by how you picked yourself up, brushed yourself down and started all over.

====Why should we tell it?====
This is the ultimate story that shares that you are a real person... you make
mistakes, things go wrong, life happens but you can still work things out and
keep going. It shares your humanity and also your perseverance and commitment to getting the job done.

This is the story that tells your prospects that you’ll stick around, that you’ll be
there through thick and thin, that what to expect is not all roses but you’ve
got their back.

The failure story is a powerful connector between you and your prospect. It
shows your humility, your fallibility, and your resilience. If you can get through
this difficult situation, you can guide others through something difficult and
they don’t have to be afraid anymore.

====The Formula===
The failure story starts with a presumption of success. You start by sharing
how you seemed so confident, that you were sure of your ability to do well
and had no inclination that failure was even possible. Then the unexpected
failure happens, and you share the surprise and the pain. Finally, the failure
story describes what you did to get back up again, to keep going and the
lesson you learned.

#CONFIDENCE 
#UNEXPECTED FAILURE
#SHOCK/PAIN
#RECOVERY
#LESSONS LEARNED
====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
I had been preparing for a big launch, created the program, set up the
marketing and was sure that I would have tens of people register. The
big day arrived, the emails started to go out, I even had a large number
of people show up for the webinar. Instead of 30 people registering
for my program, 3 signed up. I was devastated. I had invested time and
resources, and this was my only plan for the business. I didn’t know how
I would go on, I was on the floor, maybe it was time to give up. A few
days later a colleague reached out to ask how the launch was. I was
tempted to lie, to tell her that it was fine, and the results were good, I
was so ashamed of my failure. Instead, I told her the truth and she was
really supportive. “It happens to all of us,” she said, and she offered to
help me understand what had gone wrong and design a better marketing plan for next time. It was the only thing that kept me going.

=====Marc’s Story=====
I was in the public sector for years. Going between various government
jobs and educational institutions. I had a great reputation, and I really
knew how to build educational programs. So when I left my job to go
out on my own, I knew the exact program I needed to build. I spent the
next 18 months researching, designing, and building the perfect pro-
gram. It had the topic covered perfectly. When I started to think about
selling it, I wasn’t quite sure who would be interested. For the next 8
months I knocked on doors, reached out to all my contacts, and though
everyone was very friendly and encouraging, that was it. They said they
like the idea, but no one wanted to buy my perfect program. I realized,
I had created it in a bubble, there was no demand. I would need to
start all over. This time, I worked out what people wanted and sold the
idea before I created the program – and what a difference! My business is finally taking off.
====What to expect====
When you share your failure story, you are more relatable and appealing
to prospects. It tells them that you’ve put in the hard work, you’ve dealt with
tough situations, and you’ve stood your ground, you’ve recovered. The prospect knows that they can trust you to see them through tough times too.
===The Vision Story===
====What is this story?====
This is the story that describes your desired future for yourself and your business. It’s a story your readers/listeners can also envision for themselves.

====Why should we tell it?====
The vision story holds space for possibility but also creates commitment for a
better future. It is the story of hope, and we all need that! It’s the story that
often motivates us to keep moving forward, to keep striving for improvement.
It’s the promise that if we keep clear and work hard, we’ll be able to create
something wonderful.

====The Formula====
The vision story has a simple formula that starts with ‘what if’ or ‘imagine..’.
It’s perhaps more whimsical by nature and yet must be grounded and clear in
order to have impact. Once we have our ‘what if’ statement, we need to add
tangible details to make the story feel real. The final part of the formula is the
invitation to co-create this vision through participation.

#WHAT IF / IMAGINE 
#TANGIBLE DETAILS 
#INVITATION TO THE VISION
====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
I had just finished my coaching course. I was newly certified as a professional coach and yet, I couldn’t find any clients. It seems that they
don’t teach that in coach school. And it was really hard. I’m guessing that was the same for you too! Then I found that the training I had as a
professional storyteller, well, I could use it for my business. I could tell a compelling story and people remembered it and searched me out. Well,
what if you could reach out to potential clients and they immediately wanted to work with you? What if your ideal clients found you...yes, that’s
right, they heard about you and came looking for you? It’s possible. When you start telling compelling stories about what you do – people
notice, they self-select and come looking for you.
=====Julie’s Story=====
For years, I struggled when buying produce that I could tell had been
grown with toxins and in a manner that was damaging to the environment. It really bothered me but I wasn’t a farmer, I didn’t know what
difference I could make. Then I asked myself, what if I could source
organic produce and make it affordable to my local community? I’m
not an activist and yet I couldn’t let go of this vision. Well, I organized a
local farmer’s market and it’s completely changed how I shop and how
I eat. Now I’m helping others do the same. What if you could revolutionize your local community while improving your health and that of your
family?
====What to expect====
When you have a powerful vision story, you become a huge inspiration to
others. Not only do you impress them with your vision, your brilliance and
what you have achieved, you also inspire them to take action. Ideally, that
means they are inspired to hire you or buy your service.
===The Proof Story===
====What is this story?====
These are stories told by you, in the form of client experiences or stories told
by clients in the form of testimonials. It’s the proof that your service or program works, that it gets results. It’s the voice of the people who have been
impacted by your work.

====Why should we tell it?====
Most people who are considering investing in a service or program have a
few simple questions. Does it work? And will it work for me? Your proof story will give tangible evidence that what you do works for a specific person...
someone you’ll hopefully resonate with, who understands that it can work
for them too. The proof story should be clear, detailed and completely outcome-focused. 
It should speak to the greatest desires of your target audience and show, in detail, how their lives can be changed.

====The Formula====
The proof story should start by describing the situation before the work began, helping your prospect recognize themselves and their concerns in your
client’s story. They should feel like you’re describing their situation. Then you
give the details of how you worked together and what happened as a result.
This final part should be tangible, showing the real-life impact the work had
for your clients. Ideally, if you can speak to increases in revenue, savings, time
or health, this is highly desirable.

#CONTEXT/SITUATION
#WHAT YOU DID
#TANGIBLE RESULTS
====Example====
=====Lisa’s Story=====
Susan was starting her coaching and consulting business and was full of
doubt about her credibility and relatability for her target audience. We
worked together and I helped her see that her professional experience
in her career was directly related to her current coaching offering. With
that clarity, we worked on the stories she could tell in her meetings, her
materials and her networking. Within 3 months, her business was at capacity and she had to start a waiting list for clients.

=====Robert’s Story=====
When Robert came to work with me, he had clients but was exhausted and almost burned out. He only met people one-to-one, and had
thought about creating a group coaching offer but didn’t have the
time, energy, or clarity to move forward with it. He was beginning to
question if this whole business was the right path for him. Very quickly we re-structured his one-on-one offerings and explored different
ways he could offer value to his clients. Within six months, he was
working 5 hours less per week and had increased his revenue by 20% - and we were only getting started.

====What to expect====
The proof story is a great driver for decision making. A prospect could de-
liberate about working with you for quite some time. When they hear a proof
story, it’s often the moment that they decide to go ahead. You want to have
several proof stories on your website, your sales page, and ready to tell. It’s a
powerful and persuasive resource in your business.
