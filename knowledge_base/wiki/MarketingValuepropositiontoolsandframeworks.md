==Economical Value Add==
The EVA is a function of:
#Magnitude (size of cashflow in/out)
#Timing (distribution of cash flow)
#Risk (uncertainty of cash flow)

EVA is the profit as economists measure it - after tax net operating profit less a charge for using all capital equity as well as debt.

We need to address:
*Improving operating efficiency
**cut costs
**add to profit without adding to capital
*Enhance asset management
**reduce assets that earn less than the cost of capital
*Increase profit growth
**new investments that earn more than the cost of capital
*reduce the cost of capital

use sensible assumptions for missing data
