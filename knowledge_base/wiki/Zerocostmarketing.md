==Zero Cost Marketing==
Companies are able to market at no cost to themselves using techniques similar to viral marketing.  Their trick is to spread word of mouth via their existing customers through blogs and social networks.  Online communities have a "contagious" effect.  People that are enthusiastic about something have a sub-conciuous affect on others.
*The actions of a person trigger similar actions in another person.
*Collaborate with existing and potential customers over products
*Consumer generated content
*Focus on the consumer's behaviour, not the number of consumers

The downside to the contagious or viral marketing is the loss of control.  Because the users (and detractors) become involved, they now have control of the message they pass along.
