==Formulate the sales campaign==
Stakeholders can be neutral, friendly, or unfriendly.

The stakeholders can be categorized as:
*Enemies 
*bottlenecks
*allies
*influencers

You can reduce your enemies by finding shared interests so that you can move them from enemy to neutral.

Seek out the "hero" - this is the client executive who likes us, and can gain by implementing our solution.  This person will be willing to help us sell the proposition.

Required items
#elevator pitch
#hero
#set of concise key messages
#stakeholder specific key messages
#leverage or create events to get the message across
