==Business Model Innovation==
Economic cycles create different opportunities.

Enterprise model innovation is most prominent during a downturn.

Industry model innovation is seen after long periods of economic growth.

The degree of industry and environmental change impacts the extent to which companies need to pursue the BMI.
*there needs to be a balance between the amount of environmental change and the business change.
*Misalignment - the longer the drift, the less likely the company will survive.
Transform or perish!
*during the transformation, companies will either disrupt (change the market) or be disrupted (change the wrong way and perish)

Internal factors can trigger the need for BMI:
#product or service innovation
##bringing a new product, or to a new market segment often drives the need for a new business model
#performance issues
##declining performance compared to competition will drive change in business model
#resource availability
##Without the resources available it will be difficult to make the bold moves.
===Industry Model Innovation===
====move into new industries====
*Apple moved into the music industry with iTunes
**Made complex tech easier and simpler
**entirely new way of delivery entertainment.
**new collaboration with partners in media and advertising
====redefine existing industries====
*Dell moved to direct to customer sales
**Effecient path to customer
**Single point of accountability
**Build to order
**low cost leadership
**standards based
====create entirely new value chain====
*Virgin moved into Airline, Media, Telecom
*took their expertise in one market, and moved into a similar role in a different market
**manufacturer -> wholesaler -> retailer (Virgin) -> Customer.

===Revenue Model Innovation===
Alter your revenue model and value proposition to respond to a different set of customer behaviors and market requirements.
====new value propositions====
Change your value proposition or the way you price products and services
*Cirque du Soleil transformed the circus
**Circuses were generally aimed at Kids and was losing attractiveness
**Cirque changed the rules of the game by changing the target audience
***no spoken dialogue so appeals to diverse audiences
***Appeals to high income adult audience
**promote the show and not the easily replaceable performers

Value innovation - create value while reducing costs
*open new uncontested spaces
*generate a leap in customer value
*reconfigure factors of competition and the value they bring to the customer
**Reduce the gap where the average competitor is better
**eliminate the gap where possible
**raise your value over the competition
**create new value where the competition doesn't.
====pricing models====
Produce new pricing structures and models
*Gillete gave away the razor and makes money on the blades
**Developed higher prestige products when BIC introduced disposables that were much cheaper
**developed a patent wall to prevent duplication
*Netflix shifted from product/rental based to subscription based income

====3 Factors driving timing====
#Macro factors - current economic environment
##macro economic - external disruptions such as downturn or the financial crisis
##regulatory/legal - significant changes to regulations
##environment - are there sustainability factors impacting your current model?
#Industry transformations - drives the need for more radical innovation in fast changing industries
##value chain - have there been shifts in your value chain such as introduction of "direct" models
##new entrants - new market entrants introducing models that would disrupt your industry?
##customer preferences - are they changing (goods, services, delivery channels)
##customer segment - new customer segments emerging that would require different products/services or a new delivery model?
##technology - disruptive new tech?
#internal factors - new products, services, resources available
##products - are you bringing a new product to market that requires a new set of skills, capabilities, processes, etc?
##performance - are you in a declining growth relative to industry?
##resource availability - do you have the resources for bold moves?
