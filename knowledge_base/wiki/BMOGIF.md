===Probate===
*Public process
**increase risk of court challenge
*Executors, Lawyers, accountants
*Probate Taxes
*Unintended Beneficiaries
*Executor fees 3-5%


Have a Seg Fund contract open.  POA can't open a new fund or designate benficiaries
but they can transfer funds in.  So having it open protects against incapacity

===Estate Wedge===
Portion of individuals non registered portfolio to hold outside the estate.  Usually introduced during decumulation stage.
Since outside of probate can provide cash flow to beneficiaries during estate settlement.
