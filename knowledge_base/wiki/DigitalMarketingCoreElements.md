==Content Marketing==
 More than blogging
Content Marketing is the full funnel tactic delivery valuable content at every stage of the [[Customer Value Journey]].

==Customer Value Journey==
 Helps automate the selling process
8 Stages that help you sell without being pushy or creepy.

Helps build trust and loyalty.

==Digital Advertising==
 Paid Ads
You need to have a plan.  How do you separate the winning ads from the losing ones?

==Social Media Marketing==
 More than Facebook and Twitter
You need to be where your customers are.  You need to engage with them.  You need to build relationships with them.

==Email Marketing==
Keys to ROI:
*Ensure emails are delivered (and not into Junk/Spam)
*Ensure they get opened
*Generate engagement so readers take action
*Automate

==Search Marketing==
 Long game strategy
SEO can help get organic traffic.

Boost visitors trust.

Need high quality content.

Optimize your website for the visitors first.

==Website Analytics==
 helps figure out what is working and what isn't.
*What types of visitors are failing to convert?
*Are we producing content our audience wants?
*Which pages produce more leads?

==Conversion Rate Optimization==
 help turns visitors into leads/customers
