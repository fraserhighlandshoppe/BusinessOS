*Don't Fall in love with a profile
*Don't be dumb
*Pick a good screen name.  It is your brand.
*Don't write the same typical profile.  This is all Marketing.  Don't use old personal ad wording.
*Create a mystery that only a conversation with you can answer.
*Don't send an email with a bad subject line.  88% of subject lines are a complete disaster.
**Never wink.  Cheap, timid, places the burden on the women.
**Don't forget a subject line
**Don't use "Hi" or "Hey" etc.
**Don't use a smiley.
**Don't send "Wow"
*Open Loop.  Creates curiosity until action is taken.  Starts completion anxiety.
**The top 10 reasons Julei never ...
**Nessie spotted in Plano Texas, Eats ...
**Eat, Pray, Love and respond to
**Must see frisco charmer, lots up upgrades... (for realtors)
*Don't send an email to a woman that hasn't been active in the last 48 hours.  You might as well write to Santa Claus.
**Your email will get lost in the crowd.
*When to email is just as important as what.
**Sweet spot is first 2 weeks they are online or back online.
**If she has been online within the last 24 hours, email now
**If within 3 days, add 1 days to check again
**if within 5 days, add 2 days to check again
