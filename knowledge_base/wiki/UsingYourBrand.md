===Using Your Brand===
Need to play to win, not just to "not lose".

Organize your business around your brand promise.

There is about 5% of customers who will try to take dishonest advantage of your brand promise.

If you have a problem and go in and fix it, you get a 6% increased loyalty from these people than from the people who didn't have any issues (Discovered by UPS).  THIS BECOMES AN UNFAIR ADVANTAGE FOR YOU.

You probably can't hit 100% on any claim.  However, if you stand behind your claim and make good on it, you will stand out from the competition.

====Selling your brand====
Take a look at who your customers are.  
*Which of them like you the most?  
*Which of them best fit your ideal customer, of whom you want more customers like them?
Once you have identified the customers of whom you want more of, those are the ones you want to network with further
*Attend their events
*ask them for referals
*ask them to tag you on social media
*invite them to your events
Know your message!
*convey your expertise
*Speak with intention
Observe others for feedback
*watch their facial expressions
*observe their body language
*look for confirmation
*take cues from questions and objections
When seen, be seen for that which you want to be seen!

Focus on others
*team
*clients
Engage people
*monitor participation
*manage challenges

Listen!
