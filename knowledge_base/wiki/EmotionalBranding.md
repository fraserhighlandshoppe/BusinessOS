==Dos and Don'ts of Emotional Branding==
Engagement ->Emotions->Experience->

No longer in factory economy.  Now in people based economy where people
(customer) are in the seat of power.

Need to make the deep lasting relationship
===Emotional Branding===
*focusing on the most compelling aspect of human character.
*emotional fulfilment because of a product or service.
*Make them understand we are there for them no matter what.
===Deciding to buy===
====Buying for need====
*price
*practicality
*rational
*convenience
====buying for desire====
*emotional
*no price tag, but evaluate value
*their self-interest
===Brands that are using emotional branding===
product or service makes a client feel the key differences between
consumers ultimate choice and the price they are willing to pay.

We know what to expet when we deal with these brands.  We feel connected
to the brand; we feel we need that brand to fulfill our need.

===How to use===
*Look in the mirror.  What are we trying to sell?
====Build Foundation====
*Understand who your core clients are
*Understand what you sell best
*What promises can you make to your clients
====Build a relationship with your client====
*understand their preferences
*earn their trust
====Stand by your promises====
*If you don't keep your commitments you will lose the relationships.
*How will you handle problems?  Even things you can't control like
weather.  They need to feel important.

==From consumers to People==
*Don't worry about the sale.  Focus on the individual needs.
*Consumers buy but people live.
*be genuine and caring.  Don't fake it.
**Need to be open.  Need to connect.
*Products fulfill needs; experiences fulfill desires
*Improve the shopping experience.
**followup before use and after.
==Honesty is expected.  Trust needs to be earned==
*High standards and QoS
*Set your customer expectations, and then exceed them (under promise and over deliver)
*Foundation = commitment
===level of commitment===
*low level >>>> high level
*wish-hope-like-try-want-commit

===Quality for the right price is a given.  Preference is what makes the sale===
*Customer service and quality assurances expected.
*Everyone claims they have excellent CS
*Intimate marketing and engagement
*Vanilla/generic marketing will not be successful.  Needs to be highly targeted.
*Identity is about recognition
*Personality is about character and charisman
*being known doesn't mean you are loved
*identity is descriptive
*personality shows unqieness and why you are special
===Storytelling===
use new words and techniques
*get rid of transformational
*replace vendor with partner
*instead of booking - use create/design/build/craft
*agent replaced by advisor/expert
*sell -> work with

When stories are told our brains react in a different way. (Facts tell, stories sell)
*Information leads to analysis
*emotion leads to action

Paint a picture using emotional trigger words
*Delightful
*exotic

Hit on the 5 senses

Your stories are as important as their stories

Have 3 stories ready.

Use your specialties and experiences for crafting the story
*search for trigger
**blissful, magical, energised, easy
*Use a story structure - setting, action, result

Learn their stories.  Be a good listener

Use the power of visualization with your client
*ask them to close their eyes
*Instruct them to go to their vacation sweat spot
*use all 5 senses
*Ask what they are feeling, seeing, heaering, tasting, smelling, touching.
Reflect back what you heard.  Trying to get to the emotional root as to why ''they want to travel.''

===One to One===
*At the desk
*On the phone
*Email
===one to many===
*newsletters
*social media
*public presentations
