==Customer Service as part of your Brand==
Customer Service is an expectation by your customer.  If you don't provide service, they will drop you as a vendor/supplier.

===Customer Service===
Everyone makes this claim (their business is customer service).

What distinguishes your brand is if you actually provide good customer service.  
*What does your service provide?
*How well do you follow through on your promises/commitments?
*How well does satisfaction reps followup with the customer after the service?

People don't make the claims because they are afraid.  The *Little Voice* prevents you from taking the position in the market.
*Will work for free to ensure the obligation is met as well as the client meets their side of the obligation as well.
