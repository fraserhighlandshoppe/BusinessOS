====Kevin Fraser Scottish Biography====
Kevin has 30+ years participating in Scottish Culture.  He has played the bagpipes since the age of ten.

Kevin has played in a number of pipe bands.  He is currently the Pipe Major of the Airdrie Scots Pipes and Drums.

Kevin has learned how to dance a Highland Dance or two.  As a teen he was involved in Scottish Country Dancing.

Kevin has an interest in Highland Drumming.
