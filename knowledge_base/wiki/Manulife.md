===Advantage Bank Account===
All of an advisor's clients have a bank account.

 Banks use bank accounts to offer other services.

Hybrid Saving/Chequeing account.

1000 balance waives transaction fees.

No monthly admin fee.

Competitive interest rate.

 25 basis points to the advisor based on month's lowest balance.

There is also a Business Advantage account.

Easy linking to main banks to make fund transfers easy.

==Risk Proof your Practice==
While you might not be interested in your client's banking, the bigger banks are interested in your client's investments and insurance.

When a client applies for a mortgage, they provide investment statements, etc to the bank.

 If you can't beat the bank be the bank!
