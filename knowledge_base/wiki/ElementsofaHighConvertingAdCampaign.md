This covers SOME of the steps in the [[Customer Value Optimization]] process
==5 Elements of a High Converting Ad Campaign==
The Ad Campaign is designed for closing Cold Traffic.  However, you don't directly market the end product to the prospect.  You need to first, run and ad to a [[Lead Magnet]] for that traffic.

===The Offer===
You need to offer something that people want.  A beautiful campaign can not mask a bad offer.

*Give a reason
*Give something of value.  (Entry level bundle)

If the ad campaign is failing, check the offer.

 See [[Lead Magnet]]

===The Ad Copy===
Your offer should convey why the offer is something the market wants.

Think in terms of a before and after state.  How is the copy articulating the transition from the sad state from before the offer, to the happy state after they consume your offer.

 Articulate the shift from before to after.

{{:Traffic Conversion Hooks}}

Tips:
*Call out your audience out, but don't use things that are personal to them
**Do - Business owners, New Yorkers, etc
**Don't - Balding/Aging/...
*Use a question to poke at the pain point.
**What is causing their sad state
*Solution to pain point should be the offer
*Remember to include the call to action.
*If on social network, have personality.  People are used to seeing posts from friends and family.
*No need to yell.  This screams "AD, don't do this!"
*Use bullets, asterisks, emoji - makes ad stand out from newsfeed.

===Creative===
Image or video that is part of the ad.

*Should be a visual representation of your copy.  It is pictures (moving or stationary) rather than text.
*Should convey message and hook.
*Should tell story
*Should display product
*Should stand out, but don't be annoying (i.e. annoying bright colors etc)
*Be consistent with brand.
*Play off of emotions associated to that image.

Don't:
*Use an image that isn't relevant to the message

Search Google Images for your keywords from your ad copy to see what others are looking at.  Don't just grab them, use them to inspire your images.

===Ad and Landing Page Congruency===
Make sure the Ad and landing pages are congruent otherwise the traffic will be confused how they ended up there.

Use similar design and imagery between ad and landing page
*colors, images
*Offer
**Make sure the offer is present on the landing page.
*Copy
**Use the same or at least similar on the ad and landing page.

===Targeting===
The best ad campaign in the world in front of the wrong audience will not convert.
*Be specific
*Do your research (don't make assumptions)
*Focus on interests and not on demographics
