==Setting and managing priorities within the organization==

Decide on tasks to make best use of time of team

When setting priorities ask
which activities are critical for success and 

development
what urgent tasks require immediate attention
what tasks can be scheduled or eliminated

Root cause of problems
help focus on critical gtasks

Eisenhower principal - tasks that are urgent are seldom 

important and important tasks are seldom urgent
*Important and urgent
*important but not urgent
*urgent but not important
*not important and not urgent
Urgent tasks may seem critical but are often related to 

someone elses goals.
Important tasks impact long term success.  Also provide 

highest ROI on teams time and effort.

To determine importance of a task, evaluate against how 

it impacts the goals.

Focusing on important can help be proactive instead of 

reacting to crisis.


Pareto principal 
80% of tasks come from 20% of root causes
80% of profit generally come from 20% of products
#identify and list problems
*surveys
*talk ftf with customers
*check records
#identify root cause
*Write issue down.  Outline symptoms
**Gather info.  The more you know the better you can 

address it.
**Five whys.  
***Start with the end result and work backwards
****Ask why the problem is occuring.  If the answer 

doesn't provide the root cause, ask "Why" again.  repeat 

as many times as needed.
#group by root cause
#prioritize by root cause


Performance measures
*Effective measures focuses team on important tasks
*Improves decision making abilities
*reduces uncertainty about what is expected
*creates accountability
**measures if what is supposed to be done has been
*facilitates feedback 
*Helps pinpoint strengths and weaknesses

Properly measuring performance:
Measure right things
*Outcome instead of output
*Are outcomes acheiving goals?
Allow info to flow
*org units may act independantly and measure success 

differently
**Cross unit problems arise
**alignment is lower
simplify assessments
*You don't have to measure everything
*assign different measures for team members with 

different tasks
Focus on accomplishments not behaviours
*accomplishments can be observed in the absence of the 

employee.
*behaviour may have no impact on teams goal



Priorities are relevant to current situation
priorities may change as the situation change
some long term goals may be vital to long term but 

should not necessarily be sole focus

Plan tasks carefully.
Review plan frequently

research changing market trends to anticipate and 

prevent future problems


Priorities nbeed 3 characteristics
Clear and concise
*how, when, why, how much
flow from mission, vision, value statement
*Need to reflect reason organization exists
*values also affect workplace culture
Avoid confusing activity with progress
*many tasks involve urgent issues
*need to concentrate on planning so that urgency

Managing priorities help team to focus on tasks for goal
Can also lead to better working relationships
Failing to prioritize can demotivate team members
Can also impair productivity


Allocate time to deal with the longer term tasks that 

can be anticipated.
Ensure you provide time in the schedule to deal with 

unexpected tasks.  Also, which of these could have been 

anticipated so that they can be planned in the future

Ensure you allocate time for proper planning

Can you reschedule some activities?  (urgent but not 

important)  Can these be rejected?
You can also schedule time to deal with these.

not important and not urgent are the biggest time wastes 

and seldom have positive impact on goals.
*avoid where possible
*Sometimes best to say no politely and firmly.
