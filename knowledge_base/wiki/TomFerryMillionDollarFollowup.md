==Tom Ferry Million Dollar Follow-up==
 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1vCPbVkysvs
All the money is in tech-enabled, systematic, scheduled, relentless follow-up.
*0800-1000, 1600-1800
*Average agent follows up 1.4 times (real estate but insurance probably the same)
*Appointments set and bookings at 6-9 conversations.
*Average agent has no CRM.
*Tech enabled agents use lots of solutions.
**Fast
**Eficient
**Beautiful
*Average agent over-researches rather than call.
**Googling, and then dis-qualify rather than call.
===Foundation===
#Every lead put into CRM
#Every lead receives an immediate auto-responder.
##Video is the best auto-responder in existence.
#Every lead gets called in under 5 minutes.
##Get Reilly will followup any time of day
##Structurely is an AI bot that responds on your email/SMS leads.
##Research shows lead is gone within 5 minutes.
##The world we live in today wants instant gratification
#If its a lead in a place or community that matters to you, do the research WHILE you are phoning.

===Schedule===
 If it's not in your schedule, it doesn't exist.
*Day 1 lead is in
**Video auto-response
*Day 2 
**Call then text then email
*Day 3 through 30 
**Call text email every ___XXXXday___
*Day 31 on (until they buy, sell or die)
**Put into FB Advertising campaign (add to a group in FB)
***Targeted ads to them
***Once a month a Social Proof ad to everyone in the list.
**Once a month Everyone gets a Call, a text, an email.
