==What Is A Squeeze Page==
http://www.davidtan.org/what-is-a-squeeze-page/

In Internet and online advertising vernacular, a squeeze page is a Web page that contains information that would interest the marketer’s targeted readers. Squeeze pages are designed to obtain a reader’s name and e-mail address information by encouraging users to opt-in to an e-mail list to receive more information about the topic. Marketers will collect the permission-based e-mail and information to follow up at a later date.

Currently, there is still some debate over the actual definition of a squeeze page. Some consider landing pages that offer additional hyperlinks to information as well as e-mail opt-in list sign-up to be a squeeze page, while others in the industry believe only those pages containing just an e-mail sign-up are considered a squeeze page.

==Ideas for Squeezing Names into your List==
http://www.easy-smp.com/blog/squeeze-page-tactics

* Put an RSS feed on your squeeze page to keep folks current on the latest info you’re posting on your blog.
* Make your squeeze page look very similar to your members home page so people feel right at home when they get inside your membership area.
* Add your affiliate sign-up page link to your squeeze page, so affiliate marketers know you have an affiliate program right from the start.
* Use a video on your squeeze page and offer quality content in it.  This tactic proves to be a successful way to recruit more people onto your list.
* Put your sign-up form “above the fold” and make it obvious.  Remember the purpose of a squeeze page is to grow your list, not necessarily to make a sale.
* Offer a quality free gift to your visitors to encourage higher sign up rates.

==List Building Process==
http://hubpages.com/hub/freesqueezepages

The list building process is one of the most important aspects of your online business. With a big list you can send instant traffic to any website you want and make yourself a commission.

Also having an email list lets you keep in touch with your subscribers so that they can order your product when they are ready. Most people will need to be reminded of your product over and over until they are ready to order. Most people who visit your site will forget about it very shortly after they leave and if they do not remember your domain name they will forget you. Having an opt in form on your webpages lets you sign people up to your email list so you can remind them of your product until they are ready to order.

Let us say you create a squeeze page or in other words an email capture page where your customers can sign up for a free ebook or your newsletter. This will allow you to send them information on the topic they signed up for so you can send them valuable information to keep them happy like articles for example. Then at the bottom of your email you just put in your website address so your customers will be reminded of you and your products so that they can order when they are ready.

If you did not build an email list or at least put an opt in form on your website where customers can sign up they will probably leave your site and then never order and most likely will not remember your websites address so you will lose them when they leave and like I said 97-99 percent will not order on their first visit to your site.

Also keep in mind that when someone signs up for your newsletter you can email them for the rest of their lives or until they unsubscribe so this will allow you to make other offers to them for similar products. So if the person who signed up to your list is not interested in the initial product you offer them you can move on to offer them other products that they may be interested in.

So in this article we talked a little about whether or not you should build a list and the main reason you should build a list is the simple marketing fact that most people will not be ready to order your product on their first visit to your website. So if you choose not to build a list because you may think it is to difficult to add an email opt in form just remember this. It is too risky to not use opt in forms as you would be leaving a lot of money on the table. I can tell you from my own experience that adding an opt in form is not that difficult at all.

===The Smart Way to build the list===
====Tell a friend====
One of the smartest moves you can make with building your email list is to put a tell a friend script after the sign up page on your squeeze page. This will allow others to help you build your email list.

For example you can offer your customers a free ebook or your newsletter if they sign up on your opt in form. Then when they hit the submit button they are taken to a new webpage that says something like if you tell 3 of your friends about our free ebook we will give you an additional gift valued at say 47 dollars. This will motivate some to tell 3 of their friends and then those 3 could tell 3 friends and so on and so on.

Things can really take off sometimes when you add the tell a friend script to your sign up process. This is called viral marketing and it basically gets your message out there before many people all from the efforts of other people. You should seriously consider using this method of viral marketing in your list building efforts.
====One Time Offer====
Another smart way to build your list is to add the one time offer to the sign up process. I would recommend putting it in after the tell a friend script. The way it would work is first someone signs up for your free ebook or newsletter and then you hit them with the tell a friend script like above. Then they either tell their friends or they choose skip this option and then they go to your one time offer.

Your one time offer should be for a product or products related to your market. It should be a fair price. You would basically say on the top of the one time offer webpage to your customers to wait and check out this offer they will only be able to see here and now. Your customers will either take you up on your offer or they will scroll to the bottom of your webpage and choose the skip this and just give me my free ebook option.

This is one smart way to build your list as it can be built through the work of others. Also by having the one time offer there you can also make a few dollars while building your list. List building can be expensive when advertising so having the one time offer lets you basically offset the cost of the list building.
