==Social Media==
SM is a very hungry animal that needs to be fed every day.

Average person spends 50 minutes a day on various SM platforms.

Reading time is decreasing.  TV time is decreasing.

People are getting their news etc off of SM.

This is where you need to be on to target your custoemr.

OK to share other peoples posts, but need to create your own content otherwise you aren't really adding value, and people will start following the people you are sharing and you start to drop off their feed.
