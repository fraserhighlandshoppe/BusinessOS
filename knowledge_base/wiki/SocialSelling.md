===How to Fail on the Internet===

Every business speaks to a different audience.

You must run traffic on the platforms where YOUR market is “hanging out”.

If you craft the perfect marketing message but then fail to put it in front of the right audience, your entire campaign will fail. For example... Trying to sell Apple accessories using Google display ads on a blog about PC's would end in disaster.

You know... the proverbial, "You can't sell ice to an Eskimo."

But the strategy remains the same...

Conceptual traffic strategies such as “The Pixel Process” or Boomerang Retargeting are applicable no matter what market you’re in.

Those strategies are applicable across all traffic sources. To learn more about these universal laws of traffic, read these articles...

    The “Pixel Process”: How Digital Marketer Gets 81% More Leads and 38% More Sales From Paid Traffic
    How to Plug a Leaky Sales Funnel with Facebook Retargeting

But traffic is a commodity.  A click is a click. We view traffic like a water faucet.

 When we want some, we turn it on.

When we want some, we go to the “Traffic Store” and buy it.

But which "Traffic Store" is right for you?

You buy traffic when you have an offer that you feel is appealing to a certain market. You buy traffic where that market is hanging out so that YOU can get in front of them.

One of the biggest questions we get from members in Digital Marketer Lab is, “Where should I start running traffic?”

Great question!

I had the same question when I started running traffic. For most situations, I recommend starting with Facebook because...
*The ad platform is easy to use. (<< This is important, especially if you're a traffic newbie.)
*The targeting options are insane.  With the number of targeting options at your disposal on Facebook – you can find your target market easily.
*Facebook has 1.3 billion users; there is a lot of room for scale on the platform.

But Facebook isn't for everyone. Facebook has started cracking down and banning advertisers. Markets such as dating or weight loss just can’t advertise on Facebook.

So now I answer this question with a question... 
“Where is your market?”

If you’re looking to buy traffic, you want to do so from a source that will help you reach your market.

Unfortunately, there isn’t an easy answer to this. I haven’t found a tool that tells you which platform to use to reach a specific market.

It takes research. It takes time. It takes thought.

But, the research couldn’t be more important. Again, if you craft the perfect marketing message but then fail to put it in front of the right audience, your campaign will fail.

So how do you find your market?

First, you need to think about WHO your market is ...

What’s their age, gender, income?

Websites like Twitter, Pinterest, YouTube, etc. have aggregated millions of users --- but each platform has slightly different demographics. This broad demographic information will help you decide on a platform. More specific information such as...

    What are their hobbies?
    What TV shows do they watch?
    What authority figures do they follow?
    What books do they read?.

... can be used to target your audience once you’ve decided on a platform.

For example, here’s how to find your market on Facebook. We like to start with Alexa.  This tool will give you demographic data on websites around the web.

Pinterest is a wonderful platform to run traffic in the craft, clothing, make up, and anything e-commerce or female focused.

You get the gist! Research must be done to ensure your running traffic on the correct platform.

====Understand How Pinterest Works====

Pinterest is a visual search engine.

For example, if a Pinterest user types a query like 'survival skills' into the Pinterest search box, they would find 100's of image results returned from Pinterest.

One of these results (the #1 result for this query) is from our Survival Life property...

If the user clicks on that pin, they will see this enlarged version of the pin in Pinterest...

If they click on the pin again, they will be taken to the webiste where this content resises (e.g. the Survival Life blog)...

And that's it.

Can you see how Pinterest with millions of users --- can send you some serious traffic?

===Use YouTube===

Why use YouTube?

YouTube is an extension of the Google search engine.  It's a video search engine.  

Millions of people search YouTube every day for informative, entertaining and insipiring content.  

The leads generated from YouTube are searching YouTube for the content you are building on your YouTube channel.  

The YouTube strategy laid out in this section of the Execution Plan will show you how to build a long term YouTube strategy that delivers results for years to come.  

If you can create video content (even with a smart phone) you can execute this strategy.
====Step 1: Choose a Bracelet Term====

Answer this question...

"What is the biggest pain point of your ideal prospect?"

For example, if you are selling photography lessons or training, you might choose pain points like...

    My pictures are horrible

Then, write down a query that this customer might type into the YouTube search engine.

For example, a prospect that might want to improve their photographs might type something like...

    photography lessons

In this case, 'photography lessons' is the BRACELET term.  

Now, let's add some CHARMS.
====Step 2: Find Your Charms====

Now that you have your main "Bracelet" keyword, it's time to add some "Charm" keywords.

Go to YouTube and start typing in the "Bracelet" keyword.

As you type it in, YouTube will start to auto suggest a number of secondary keywords that are associated with your main "Bracelet" keyword,

These secondary keywords are your "Charm" keywords.

Record each of the "Charm" keywords that is relevant to your audience in a spreadsheet.

 
====Step 3: Create Short Videos For Each====

A major part of Google's YouTube algorithm (the math equation that decides where your videos will rank for a keyword) is WATCH TIME.  In other words, how many people that start the video --- finish the video.  How "deep" into the video do people watch?  

To have a high WATCH TIME on your videos you will need:

    Super, high-value content
    Laser focused
    Short

The first thing is to ensure that your videos are high quality.  That doesn't mean you need high-dollar production equipment.  

You can use your phone or a cheap video recorder.  Just ensure that the video is well lit and the audio is good.  

You might invest in a cheap lavalier microphone (one that pins to your shirt) especially if you are shooting from a distance.  

Secondly, make sure the video is laser focused on a single...

    Tip
    Trick
    Hack

Lastly, keep keep your "Charm" videos between 1 and 3 minutes.  

They need to be short and to the point.

Do a quick intro and get into the tip, trick or hack as quickly as possible.  Deliver the value and close with a call-to-action (CTA).  We'll get to the specifics on the CTA in a later step of this Execution Plan.  

If possible, shoot all these short "Charm" videos at once.
====Step 4: Add a Call-To-Action to Each Video====

When shooting your videos, you'll want to close each video with a call-to-action.  

When you upload the video to YouTube, you will use the YouTube Cards function to add the actual CTA button into the video.  

But, when you are shooting the close to your video, you will want to point in the direction of the call-to-action button --- that won't be there until later when you upload the video.

====Step 5: Upload and Optimize Your Videos====

Now that you have several charm videos created, it's time to get them into YouTube and add the finishing touches.  

Here's how you want to upload your video...

Step 1:  First, change the file name of every "Charm" video to the keyword you are targeting...

As you can see, most videos will have a number as the file name ---- you will want to change this file name...

For example, for the Basketball Training business, you might change the file name of the video to optimize the video for the "Charm" keyword "how to dribble better"...

Step 2:  When uploading your videos, you'll want to upload them as SCHEDULED instead of Public, Private or Unlisted.  This way all of your "Charm" videos will not go live at the same time on your channel.  

We'll talk a bit more about scheduling in a later step of this Execution Plan.  

For more information on how to upload a video with the "SCHEDULED" setting, read this from YouTube support.

Step 3:  Use the EXACT "Charm" keyword as the title of the video.

Step 4:  Add the "Charm" keyword to the video at least once in the description in the first 3 sentences.  The description needs to be in sentence form and should read correctly.  

Step 5:  Add the keyword as a TAG.  Then, add a couple of variations of the same keyword.  Lastly, add your name or the name of your company as a tag (particularly if you have a strong brand or a central person that people might be searching for)

Step 6: Add a CUSTOM THUMBNAIL image that has a text overlay that describes the contents of the video.  You can use a tool like Pixlr Express to lay text over the image that will be uploaded as the thumbnail.

====Step 8: Set a Schedule====

Use the scheduling function in YouTube (discussed in an earlier step) to "drip" your "Charm" videos out one at a time on a set schedule. 

You'll want to treat your YouTube channel the same way a TV network treats their channel.  You want the "Subscribers" you are getting to begin to anticipate your videos.

We usually publish videos 3 times per week but you could do more or less.  Just set a schedule and stick to it.  Stay consistent.  

This strategy, like all organic social strategies, takes time and consistency to pay off.

This video is longer and more in depth and targets the main "bracelet" keyword.

In our original example, that bracelet keyword would be "photography lessons."  

It is the views, likes, comments and long watch times on your "Charm" videos that will help you rank for a more competitive "Bracelet" keyword like "photography lessons."
