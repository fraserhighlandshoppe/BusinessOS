A High Performer is driven by passion (creates Excitement, Enthusiasm), excellence (being the best I can be) and a desire to serve (making a difference).  They have also figured out that it is how you are doing the work, not the amount of work you are doing.

==elements of high performance==
*productivity
*persuasion
*phsychology
*physiology
*purpose

===Psychology===
How you think about yourself and the world around you.  Your thoughts need to support your journey to success.
#Information - tons of info coming in every day.  The information coming in will impact your thoughts and intention.
#Interpretation - how you interpret that info changes how you act.  You will interpret info differently than another person hearing exactly the same thing.  Your interpretation (attaching meaning) of an event impacts how you use the info, your beliefs about it.
#Identification - was this info good or bad for me.  You are less likely to try something again if you labeled it a failure.  You also self label - attaching the label to yourself.  If you don't attach any meaning to an event, you won't do anything with it.
#Intention - Here is what you '''want''' based upon the info, your interpretations and beliefs.  
#initiative - Here is the acting on your ideas, beliefs.  Your psychology manifests as your behaviour.  Need to take massive and focused action.
#influence others - Bringing the social world into your life.  The amount you are able to influence others affects your psychology.  If you are unable to influence others, you become frustrated.

You may need to reprogram your mind to give you the support you need.  You ARE good enough.  You CAN and WILL succeed.

===physiology===

Your body supports your ability to acheive high levels of performance.  You need to feel your best so that you can accomplish the best work.

You need the stamina to make it through the day.  If you don't you won't have the energy to accomplish the work you want to.  This isn't about being skinny and cute - it is about having energy.

The person who has the most energy in your meeting will win.

There is also a mental component to firing up your energy.  Throughout the day, as yourself "What level am I right now in terms of vibrancy and presence in my body?"  When your mind realizes that you constantly feel tired, your mind will guilt you into improving things.  Your brain will activate your body to raise your presence and energy level.

Standing up, breathing deeply and moving around a little will energize you and activate your presence.

===productivity===
High performers accomplish a lot.  What is it that makes a high performer productive?
*Learn what a real deadline is (must have, not want deadline).
*Focus
*Get rid of distractions
**Check your email only once or twice a day, and not at the start of the day.
***Send emails to those you need to reach out to.  Then those that you are waiting on.  Only then do you respond to other people's emails!
*Clarity
*Not responding to everyone else's emergencies.
**Your inbox is a nice organizing system for other people's agendas.
*Be proactive not reactive
**Have the day planned out.  Don't depend on your inbox to determine what you will do.

Productivity is a mindset.  How much of the goals you had for the day have you accomplished?  How about for the year?
