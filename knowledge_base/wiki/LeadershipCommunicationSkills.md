Not everyone is a natural leader.  In todays environment people look to their leaders for more than their charisma.

You will need to inspire and motivate your subordinates.
==Establish your credentials==
Some people gravitate to leadership because pf their personality.  Some people have leadership thrust upon them, and some people seek it out as they think it is the way to carear advancement.

Leadership is not the same as management.  People follow managers direction due to fear, where as they will follow a leader.
===Communicate your leadership abilities===
People are looking to their leadership to install a feeling of
*confidence
*trust
*cooperation
*respect
*loyalty

People decide to trust you as their leader once they listen to what you say and observe what you do.

You need to convince your potential followers that you are worthy of being followed.  To do so you need to communicate
*who you are
*what you know
*what you do

Everything you say and do will impact the well being of your followers.

====Things you do====
A leader supports a team by:
*planning
*implement
*problem solving
*maintain good team morale

People need practical support from their leaders as well as help implementing them.  They need you to support them in the accomplishments of their goals.
====What you know====
You need to communicate your knowledge and competence:
*show knowledge of your job
*show knowledge of your subordinates job
*show knowledge of the organization
*coach team members
====Who you are====
People don't follow people who are self serving or unethical.

Ensure you convey your admirable traits such as
*honesty
*integrity
The slightest hint of dishonesty will lead to your subordinates losing respect for you.
===Respond to your team===
Your followers will sometimes express their view of how things should be done.  At times these will sound like complaints.

There are three steps to communicating with followers:
#consider options
##people want to feel that their opinions matter
##asking for other's suggestions help ensure your biases aren't overriding good decisions
#show a willingness to act
##If you act as if you don't care you will lose your followers.
#listen actively
##by active listening your follower will see that you are truly trying to understand.

Poor morale is highly contagious.  How you react to situations will greatly affect the morale.

When your team voices dissatisfaction, they need to be actively listened to.
*physically demonstrate through nodding and direct eye contact
*verbal demonstrate through asking direct questions to verify and clarify what they said

Don't react defensively when team members complain or criticize.  And don't be dismissive as your followers will not be impressed.  They will start to question why you are the leader, especially if you say there is nothing you can do.
===Develop Team Relationships===
Good leadership depends on relationships, which depends on communication.

Everything you do or say enhances or harms your relationship with the followers.

You need to ensure that all your communication displays:
*shows that you support your team
**leading by example
*working alongside the team members
**interaction with team
**advice, coaching, motivating
*displays that you have confidence in your team
**give praise
**provide feedback
**ask for their opinions
**delegate
*demonstrates that you are loyal to your team
**look out for their best interests.
**defend team members when team is under threat or when someone is being unduly criticized.

==Communicate the vision to your team==
You need to work closely with your followers to maximize their results.  You need to communicate the vision of a successful future.  Your followers want a strong vision.
===Participative Goal Setting and Planning===
Having team members help set their goals they are more involved.  It also leads to:
*them believing that the goals are achievable
*commit to the accomplishment of the goals
*become motivated to achieve the goals
*understand what achievement of the goals require

People often resent having goals forced upon them with a "failure is not acceptable".

Steps to reaching the vision
#Establish the goals
##while these are not measurable they are more specific than the vision
#agree to the objectives
##these must be defined so you can evaluate movement towards the goal.
##these need to be precise.
#determine tasks
##tasks are concrete and measurable
#set priorities and schedules.
##helps focus on getting something done

A team that is involved in the planning will be more enthusiastic in the execution of the plan.

If you let the team participate your role changes from setting their actions to guiding and coaching.  You can help them consider what their actions are by asking questions and sharing your experiences.
*Use open questions
You want to funnel the teams input.
*take previous ideas and use more specific questions.
*don't say "I don't agree".  Instead share experience where similar situations were difficult or impossible.
===Recognizing SMART objectives===
You need SMART objectives to allow the goal to be achieved.

Smart objectives help you to not set impossible objectives.
====Specific====
Specific outcomes or key results.  Who, What, When and Why
====Measurable====
How will you tell it was reached?  If you can't describe success through how much or how many, you need to describe how to tell it was achieved.

If there isn't a measurable description, the success or failure is subjective and will lead to arguments.
====Achievable====
You need to be able to control the objective.  It needs to be realistic, and you need to have access to and control over resources required.
====Relavent====
Objective needs to be relevant to achieving the goal.  Otherwise why are you doing it?
====Time Specific====
The timeframe or limit is required so that the tasks can be planned and scheduled.  Otherwise the actions will be deferred indefinately.
===Use your influence===
Those with the power to get things done are usually respected.

Your powers of influence are
*coerce
**based on fear.  You need to be in a position to intimidate or punish.
*reward
**based on offering incentive.  The incentive needs to be attractive to the person it is being offered to.
*legitimate
**based upon position.  It is an expectation that people will comply with legitimate requests.
*expert
**based on skills and knowledge.  Respect earned.
*referent
**personal qualities and traits people enjoy.

Not all powers are good for leadership.  They may cause obedience but not commitment.  Each of the powers of influence will have a different reaction from people.  Some forms of power are less potent (e.g. coercion).

You can always rely on referent or expert power, but the other forms have various strengths depending on what you have to offer/threat and the person being influenced.  They also lower commitment.

#Be Confident
#Be assertive
#don't be passive
#Do take responsibility.
#don't blame others for your emotions and reactions
#don't be aggresive in your communication.
#state facts.  Not your judgement of the facts
#be direct in asking for what you require.  Don't hint.
#ask assertively "I would like you there".  Don't ask passively "It would be good if you can make it".
==Support and Inspire your team==
Leadership is easier when times are good, but much more challenging during tough times.
===Delegate to empower===
Empowering people helps them develop their skills.  Try to create opportunities to allow people to make decisions on their own behalf as well as on behalf of the team and organization.

Delegation also communicates your confidence in their abilities.
#delegate appropriate tasks
#ensure they have the necessary skills
#ensure they have the needed authority

Delegation should not be used to avoid the jobs you hate.  It should only be used to allow someone to develop their skills.  But ensure that they can achieve success.  It should not be a training exercise.  They should be showing to everyone else and maybe themselves that they have the abilities.

People become more results orientated rather than task orientated when they are empowered.
*mentor
**have your follower coach or mentor a team member.  This helps them develop their skills.
*lead
**ask a follower to lead the project so that they get experience with the skills as well as the decision making.
*represent
**have someone else represent the team at meetings so they can build interpersonal skills.  
**also leads to networking
*solve
**ask a follower to find the solution.  They will become focused on the outcome rather than the process.
**also leads them to starting to make decisions on their own.
===Inspire achievement===
To inspire is to breath life into something.  To demonstrate this, you need to be
*passionate
**strong belief in the possibility of future success
**talk as if the objective has been acheived
**not if successful.  When!
*energetic
**resilience
**work tirelessly on behalf of the team
*a good role model
**take action to ensure success.
**good of team is paramount overshadowing personal needs.
People motivated by their loyalty to an inspirational leader will lose their inspiration if you no longer show enthusiasm for the objective.

Inspirational behavior also needs to be backed by inspirational communication
*don't complain about being tired or overworked
*need to sound energetic
*talk with certainty, conviction and commitment.
*always be positive.

Your followers need to feel that you believe they can succeed.  A person's belief directs their behavior.
===Provide Motivation===
Motivation is an individual thing.  What motivates one person doesn't necessarly motivate someone else.

Prime motivators are
*recognition
**like being recognized publicly
*responsibility
**need to be needed
**want to be trusted, 
**want to be able to achieve something meaningful
*remuneration
**tangible rewards for their efforts.  Prises, bonuses and other incentives.
**pay
*security
**Need to know their jobs are safe
*status
**focus on their position or title.
**promotion prospects
*work environment
**colleagues
**fair management
**challenging or exciting work

You need to determine which motivator is most important, for each of your followers.  One size fits all won't work.

People are attracted to jobs that will satisfy them.

Use language that appeals to your followers motivators.
