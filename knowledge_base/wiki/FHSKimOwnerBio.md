====Kimberly Henry Scottish Biography====
Kim has 30+ years participating in Scottish Culture. Through her childhood and youth, she was a Highland Dancer.  In her late teens she briefly took up playing the bagpipes.  In her adult years, she has participated in Scottish Country Dance.  She has also learned Highland Drumming.  She currently plays the snare in the Airdrie Scots Pipes and Drums.
