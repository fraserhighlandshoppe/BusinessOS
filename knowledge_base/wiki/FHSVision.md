====Vision====
 See [[Benefit to Company Vision and Purpose]]
=====Internal Vision=====
 This step is Where we are going.  Goals, growth of the business, number of employees, sales figures.

We want to be able to extract enough money out of the business on an annual basis to provide the basic lifestyle we want.  This is 25k for Kevin, XX for Kim.

This will require aggressive growth, leading to our needing employees.

At the end we want to be in a position to pass on the business to our heirs, though selling it to an outside party is possible if our heirs are not interested in being owners.  This will require creating a very solid business.
