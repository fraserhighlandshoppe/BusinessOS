==The Executive Summary==
[[Category: Executive Summary]]

We need to express our value proposition in a very concise way.  This is usually the executive summary.
#outline the storyline
##horizontal or vertical logic
##start with a hypothesis and challenge it with data points and facts
##focus on the key message
##make sure there is good flow
#develop a concise elevator pitch (max 7 points)
##we understand that...
##our proposition could...
##it essentially will transform...
##we are willing to demonstrate our commitment by...
##a useful start/next step would be...
#check the proposal against the executive summary
##adopt the proposal to the summary

Things to cover:
*Our understanding of the situation and challenges
*the required transformation
*business value to this approach
*model - based on performance based pricing
*deal finance structure
*risks involved and how we manage them
*commitment to client
*solution outline
**program management, 
**change management
**functionality
**implementation approach
*Why us.

The acid test
*is the necessary overall transformation visible
*do we impact the clients KPI
*have we outlined the key benefits and how we realize them?
*have we outlined the risks
*Is our commitment clearly visible
*have we translated jargon into CxO speak?
