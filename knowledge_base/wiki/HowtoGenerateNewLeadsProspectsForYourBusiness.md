==How to Generate New Leads & Prospects For Your Business==
===Inneficient Strategies===
*Cold market prospect
**Talk to people at malls.
**Build relationships.
*Buy leads/Cold call
**Only thing this helps with is getting good on phone. 
**Cold call.  People are grumpy and skeptical.
**Very low conversion rate.
*Post in random SM groups with link to products/business
**Not a lot of business comes from spammy marketing.

===Efficient===
Take marketing by the horns.
*Generate your own leads.
**Through FB
**Through blog
**Through your brand.

When your leads have seen your video/blog and they know what you look like, when they give you contact info they will be excited to hear from you.  This leads to a 10x increase in conversion
 Hey this is Tanya, you were on my FB/Blog and asked me to contact you...

When you put your contact info in, was there something you saw/liked/opportunity?
 This leads to 70% conversion for Aliza

*Build brand
*lead magnet
*distribute content with value
*followup to convert
*promote.  Where to place so you attract buyers.
