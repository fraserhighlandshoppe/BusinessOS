==What to Post On Social Media to Promote Your Business==
Don't spam your network!  Want to create curiosity.  Need to give value.

*Post story.  
**Facts tell, stories sell!
**People will buy off of testimonials
**Product story
**Success story
***Before and after
**Don't mention company name!  
***People google and will find only negative reviews!
***People google, like what they find but you don't have enough credibility so they find someone else...
**Value, curiosity based instead of pitch fest.
**Don't over promote.  Don't spam.  Quality people don't join this!
**Don't mention the name of your company.
**Don't leave a link
**PM to learn more!
===When to Post===
FB: 0700-0900, 1300-1600, 1700-2100
From those testing times, find your SWEET SPOTS of where you get the most engagement. Go with that!!!
===Social Media Posting Map===
[[File: Tanya Aliza Social Media Posting Map.pdf]]

#Make a plan
##Plan to post 3-5 times a day
#Answer and Engage
##Answer questions and engage with your audience
#Your Audience
##Not all audiences are the same. Pay attention and get a FEEL for what your audience loves and engages with most. (insight stats)
#Don't be Random
##Make sure to post content that is relevant to your audience's likes, wants, desires, and needs.
#80/20
##80% of your posts should offer content and 20% should be promotion.  Don't be that GUY... or GAL (spammer)

===Content===
====Pictures====
Everything from Selfies, Lifestyle, and Fun! Don't be afraid to let people into your world so they can build a relationship with you.
====Questions to drive engagement====
Example: Fill in the Blank. If I had all the time and money in the world, I would _________.
====Quotes====
Post quotes that are both Positive
and Inspirational.
====Resources====
Your blog posts, other blog posts,and YouTube videos
====Videos====
The best to share and upload are actual Facebook videos (Facebook favors and loves this most)
