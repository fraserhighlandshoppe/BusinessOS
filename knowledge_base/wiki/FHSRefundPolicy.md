Returns and Refunds Policy

All sales are final unless otherwise mentioned below.

We offer an exchange on some of our items.  Items must be returned within 14 calendar days of receipt.  If you are mailing the items being returned, please contact us in advance for a Return Authorization.  This way you don't mail something that can't be returned or exchanged.

We do not refund shipping charges.  You are responsible for the shipping cost of returning any items to us.  If the items are defective, we will pay the shipping back to you.  If the item was not defective, you will be charged for the shipping.
Costume Items

Next to skin items (kilts, shirts, hose, headdress) are not eligible to be returned.

Footwear (Ghillies, etc) are not returnable if there is signs of wear.

Sporrans and Kilt belts may be exchanged if defective.
Custom Orders

Custom Orders are not eligible for returns.
Piping and Drumming supplies

As long as the item isn't in the exclusion list below, it may be returned for an in store credit.

If the item is in its original (unopened) packaging, there will be a 20% restocking fee.

Opened items may be returned for an In-store Credit if it is in its original packaging and is sellable as new.  There will be a 30% restocking fee.

Items that are sellable, but in a used condition shall have a 50% restocking fee.

The refund price shall be the lower of the original price, or the current price available to the public (regular retail, or sale price if the item has an advertised sale price that is active).
Exclusion list

The following items are not eligible to be returned:

Reeds
Mouthpieces
Mouthpiece protectors
Pipe Bags

The following items are not eligible to be returned if they have been opened:

Hemp
Seasoning

If you are customer shipping from outside of Canada, please mark the outside of the parcel with “RETURNING CANADIAN GOODS ZERO (0) VALUE”. Failure to mark the parcel may result in customs charges which will then be passed onto you.
