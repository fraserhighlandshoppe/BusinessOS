==Approach and Contact==
We are all self employed.  We need to talk about the business or nobody will know we are in it.
*Get people's contact info.  They almost never call back if you give them a card.

When asked what do we do, people will shy away from "sell insurance" or "financial services" (people don't like to talk about money).  Instead, we educate people about how money works in Canada and assist them in preparing for the future!

It is only as difficult as you make it in your mind.

If you tell yourself you will be rejected, the contact will feel your uncertainty and almost certainly reject.  The moment you second guess yourself is when you fail.  The hardest step is the first - actually starting.  

Instead, be confident and excited, knowing in your mind that you WILL close (mindset).

You need to know the end GOAL, and have it firmly in mind:
*It is to educate and to understand.
*It is not to recruit nor sell! (these turn people off)
Know that we bring value to people.  Focus 100% on the value we bring people.

Keep it simple

You will have more success if you schedule (and keep the schedule) your phoning times.  This becomes a routine.  Expect it of yourself.

==Follow-up==
The followup is  where you collect information from the potential client in order to create a plan for them.

It is also where you pursue your prospect until they tell you to stop!  Consider, if someone upline of you had not followed up on their recruit who is up the chain from you, then you would not be in this position.  The entire chain below that 1 link would not exist, at least not as that chain.

When we call back when we promised to do so, we demonstrate professionalism, commitment, integrity.

If we get their voicemail during followup, leave a message saying "As I promised to followup, here is my call.  Please call me back".
