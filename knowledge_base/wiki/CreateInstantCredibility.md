==Create Instant Credibility==
 https://christianmickelsen.com/sa/ve-credibility/

===Speak Simply===
A study into what makes an expert witness credible for juries discovered it had nothing to do with letters behind their name, etc.  It came down to how clearly and simply they spoke.

 Easier to understand is more believable and credible.

===Speak the language of your clients===
Second key is to speak the language of your clients.
 Business owners are looking for Results!

If you can describe the problem they are experiencing better than they can, you gain credibility and expert recognition.
===Teach them something valuable===
If they feel they received great amounts of value for free, they will wonder what they will get if they buy from you.
