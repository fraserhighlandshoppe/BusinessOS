==What is a Brand==
We are exposed to different brands every day of our lives.  A Brand is an identity that stimulates a meaningful emotional
response  in a person about the qualities or values for which that person stands.  In a corporate context, it is the feelings and interactions that the customer has for the corporation.

Like it or not, others assign a brand.  Your brand is in evidence every time you interact with other people—internal
and external.  Development of a brand requires asking important questions about our company and how we are perceived.

Your Brand is a promise.
*a promise to deliver
*Must be Clear.  In your mind and in your customers'
*concise.  If it is wordy, it is forgettable.
*builds loyalty and trust

===Impacts of Brand===
We make decisions based upon the brand of our supplier.  We will buy something, or not buy, because of it.

With a strong, positive brand, this becomes equity.  While not quite money in the bank, it can be the next best thing when competing for our customers business and loyalty.
