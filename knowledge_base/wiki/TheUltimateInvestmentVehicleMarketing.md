==Ultimate Investment Vehicle==
*Dave Dubeau FTIC telecall with Shane Morgan
Targetted at serious business owners.  Self employed or business owners.  OK if doing a business on the side but putting effort into the business.

You can skip the first 7 minutes and 30 seconds as it is just a teaser intro.  In fact, most of the call is more in the nature of chit-chat than true information.

===What is Marketing===
Marketing is reducing the population of the world down to the subset of people with whom you want to do business.

===Marketing as an investment===
*For the cost of a piece of paper and a stamp, you have the opportunity to gain new clients.
*Direct response marketing can lead to a high number of deal opportunities.
*Direct response marketing has about a 1% response rate.
*If you see marketing as only an expense, and a PITA, then you are going about it wrong.

===Getting the word out===
*Having a good product/service is not enough. (Apple versus Microsoft, McDonalds vs other burger joints)
*Don't copy the herd, as chances are they are copying someone else.  No differentiation.
*You have to be not average to not end up with average results.
*Don't try to imitate a huge company.  
**Flashy brocures and fancy fun advertising is not measurable
**They have a brand name.
**Need to excite your customer about your product.  Do something to get their attention.
**Doing this is expensive - millions on an advertising campaign.
*Have to be in charge (oversee) your marketing.  Can't blindly delegate (i.e. to the newspaper ad rep).
**Ad rep doesn't know marketing - they know how to sell ads but not things in general.
**Graphics subdepartment of their ad department makes things pretty, but don't know what works for selling.
**An Advertising agency's goal is to get as much money out of you for as long as possible.  They are also after accolades and awards.  They are not concerned with the results their product produces.
***If you go to them saying the ad isn't making any money (no results) they will tell you that you need to run the ad longer.
*Accountable versus unaccountable advertising.
**Put an ad in the paper with a tracking code
***Coupon code
***Specific URL
**In general, a Radio or TV ad isn't accountable (unless has a specific URL or coupon code)
