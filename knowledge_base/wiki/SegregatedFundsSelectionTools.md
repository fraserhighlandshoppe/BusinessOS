===What tools are available to help me decide which segregated fund to purchase?===
 http://www.investingforme.com/classroom/investment-type/segregated-funds/faq?q=48
As with mutual funds, investors should make their investment choice based upon a number of investment criteria, not just a single reference point. Most investors simply rely upon the fund’s past performance, but as the liability disclaimers always state, “Past performance is not an indicator of future performance.”

Investors can obtain individual segregated mutual fund information by using a fund filter like those offered by Morningstar and Globefunds.

===How do I decide which segregated fund to buy?===
 http://www.investingforme.com/classroom/investment-type/segregated-funds/faq?q=49

Because segregated funds contain an insurance contract, only appropriately licensed financial planners and advisors can sell segregated funds.

Analyzing the investment component of a segregated fund is the same as for mutual funds. (See Mutual Funds Q&A http://www.investingforme.com/classroom/investment-type/mutual-funds/what-you-need-to-know?q=12)

Segregated funds contain a variety of features and details that can be confusing so they should be approached with the assistance of an advisor.
