===Recruiting for Financial Planning===
Reasons to recruit:
#The ripple effect on their lives
#Inline with the mission statement 
#Changes lives (ours and theirs).
#Because we CAN!

Everyone's life will be better by being recruited:
*Financial plan
*better environment
*leadership developmnent
*personal growth
*learn about money

 Is there a 100 squares equivalent for business growth at HUB?

By recruiting someone, you gain access to THEIR warm market.  (Hence the Network marketing).  Having access to a warm market increases your success and gets rid of the need to Cold Call.

Once you have worked out of your network, you will need to Prospect.  This works best if you [[Prospect without being Pushy]]!

The average 50 year old has $50,000 saved for retirement.  Running out of money or not being able to retire is their biggest fear. 
 This should be in a [[Customer Avatar Template|Customer Avatar]]


Getting there:
*Have a clear mental picture of being a leader of Thousands
*Have a written business plan for:
**Personal sales, training sales, new people
*Know how many of the following are needed:
**Prospects
**Interviews
**Sales
**Final Reviews
**Time and resources to manage all of these.  What needs to be minimized or eliminated?

Leverage:
*If you have people within a social circle that are sitting on the fence, but one of them starts you can use that for credibility and social proof to excite the others into starting as well - "So and So is starting, are you ready?  Let's be on the same team"

Before moving online, it is a good idea to be skilled offline
 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TlV4LESG1sk
*Contact
*Invite
*Present
*Followup (consistently)
*handling objections
*communicate effectively
*Belief about network marketing solid
It is a myth that there is old school/new school around the internet.  It is all about building relationships.

Necessary components:
*website/blog - sell you first, your products and company second.
**lead capture
*Be social on social media
*create content - posts, videos, status updates
*Connect with people (fun)
**People coming in want to know that there is someone there to answer the phone, answer questions, etc.

Find the methods that resonate with you.
*Warm market list, BNI, chamber of commerce, mall walking, lifestyle, shows and expos, ...
