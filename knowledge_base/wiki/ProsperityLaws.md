==Feel Free to Prosper==

Lots of people have negative conditioning to do with money.

There is physical marketing, but there is also mental marketing.

Prosperity thinking frees you.

When we become atuned/aligned with mental law, we deal with the conditioned (subconcious) mind.  The subconciouse mind is also the tap into the universal intelligence.

Our lives, businesses, health, prosperity is greatly influenced by the subconcious.  Once you open the pipeline to the universal intelligence, you will be granted many gifts.  This is a spiritual connection, though not necessarily religious.  The ultimate goal is to align with the universal intelligence and incorporate the prosperity laws.

Create a prosperity mindset.  Making space within the subconcious allows others to find you.  You will stand out.

There is unlimited abundance in the universe.  It is our mindset that creates the limits we experience.

Help take care of people in the way you are destined to, you will be taken care of.

Your business, sales, clients, employment, savings, retirement fund etc is NOT your source of income.  There is only 1 source, and that is the universal intelligence.  When you rely on this source, you will realize that the others listed earlier are channels from the source to you.  When you loosen up, other channels open up.

When you start to manifest, don't start blabbing about it until it is consumated.  Conserve the energy.  Basically a don't count your chickens before they hatch.

Mental activity is thousands of times more powerful than physical activity.  Look around and see the 99.9% of the population doing lots of physical activity but not necessarily progressing.  Inspired action always creates the results.

Most successes come when you outwardly do nothing.  Do the mental work, and it will guide you to the correct physical action that will lead to success.  Clients, chunks of money, etc can also appear out of the blue.

The subconcious mind will pass a message to the concious mind.  Once the concious mind agrees with the message, the success will start to manifest.  Affirmations are powerful, but you have to ensure you aren't causing an argument with the subconcious.  I.e. telling yourself that you are wealthy, if it says "yeah right you are lying" then things become worse because the mind will start to focus on the opposite of the affirmation.

To enable these agreements, you have to become ready for the change.  No negative statements.  No negative conversations.  Stay away from people who spend all the time in negative conversations.

CHANGE THE WORDS YOU USE.  This will have the immediate consequence of realigning your 2 minds.  If you don't want it, don't say it.  Don't use words that are opposite to what you want.  Words have power, and they seek into your subconcious.  

2 ways to influence the subconcious - repitition and faith.  You have faith that the sun will rise in the morning, or if you drop a rock over the edge of a cliff, it will fall.  When a parent says something like "you are stupid and will never amount to anything" to young kids, this sinks into the conciousness and will become "faith".  And then they will spend their lives wondering why they never succeed.
