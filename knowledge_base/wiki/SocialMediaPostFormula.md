==Post Formula==
===Hook===
A HOOK is a captivating first sentence.  Example: My friend’s Tanya and Cesar just wrapped up their 22nd vacation this year!

===Before and After story===
This is where you talk a little bit about what life was like before your product, service or business and then you share what life is now like after.
 DO NOT mention the name of your product, service, or company and don’t be too hypey.

I want to give you an idea of what it looks like to share a story vs. “BUY MY FREAKIN STUFF!”

Example: I remember when I first heard that Cesar and Tanya were wrapping up their 10th vacation for the year and I was like “WHAT!?’ I just had to find out what these guys were up to. So I hit the comment button on one of their posts and...

===The Take Away===
I like inserting a Take Away into my posts because it takes the salesy feeling completely away and it eliminates resistant.

Basically you want to add something like: Now I have no idea if 22 vacations and worldly experiences is something that excites you as much as me, but....

===The Call To Action===
Never insert a link to your company replicated website EVER. You only want to ask them to Private message you and connect. That’s it.

Example: Shoot me a private message if you’d like to see our vacation hack that we found out about. It’s pretty cool.
