==9 step create anything framework==
===Introduction===
*Who you are
*I help <audience> do/understand <what/topic> so that <results/benefits>
===Ask the audience===
Have you ever had these challenges?
*Want to show empathising
*Want to create reasonance with what you are talking about.
===Me Too===
Here's my story of struggle.
*Creates rapport
*Positions you as an expert.
*People don't want to hear you brag about your success until after the struggle.

===Story of finding the solution===
Transition from the struggle to the search for the solution.  What was the journey.

===Results===
Describe the results you've gotten or that you've helped others get.

At this point, you should be seen as a credible expert without having had to brag.

===Old world - new world - myths and trends===
Tell how in the new world things have changed.  What has changed.  What assumptions and myths have you found to no longer be true.
*This solidifies you as a needed expert in their life because they need your help changing.

===The solution framework===
Here is where you teach the content.  Everything that led to this set the stage.

Need to create the framework/process that the subscribers can repeat.

This is what they think they are paying for.

===Most common mistakes===
Describe the most common mistakes so that you can teach the dos and don'ts for implementing the framework.

===The first step===
How do they get started.  Due to lots of info, they are confused or overwhelmed.  Tell them what their first step is, and then you end the program.

==Product Development==
*list customer's biggest fears and frustrations (top 10)
*put each at the top of a page.  Write down everything you know about that fear that is relevant.  
*You will probably find that you have 10 ideas per fear.  If you can write a page for each idea, this gives you 100 pages for a book, as well as a product that you can now create courses, etc for.


===2 ways to create a product - my view and other's view===

#My view - OUTLINE and BRAINSTORM.  
#External view - find what other people are teaching, and look at the areas where you have the same or similar knowledge.

What is it people pay for in all this knowledge?  Take that and transform into something that people will pay for.

===3 Reasons People Buy information products===
When people buy information, they are buying for 3 reasons:
#Buying the expert perspective (experience and knowledge distilled to relevant)
#a plan (how to now use the relevant perspective)
#Progress - shorten the learning curve.

Create the framework where it takes the person from where they are today to where they want to go.  Take your knowledge, and set it up in a set of 5-7 steps.

The outline for creating each of these are different, as is the marketing.

Expert story positioning
*What do you have
*why is it valueable
*why they should trust you

===Strategy===
People need to know the What.  If they know that, they often can figure out the how.
===Synthesis===
What is everyone else doing.  What are the other experts, competitors doing?
*Gather all of that other expert info into one place so that the customers don't have to do the research themselves.
===Structure===
How often have you sat in a presentation and not been able to figure out where they are going?  How often did they not get to the content fast enough?

What to say, when.

===Simplicity===
Our lives are complex enough.  If you can make it simple, you will be the one they come back to.

===Step by Step===
People want a step by step system so that they know they will be able to sit down and follow it, and succeed.

===Levels you can teach===
#Tactical (How to do something) (Method)
#Transformational (How to be/become) (Motivation)
#Transcend (Have them reach a higher purpose) (Bigger interaction with the community in service to the community)

===Market your products===
Need to look beyond just the next campaign.
*Draw 12 boxes on a page, each box represents 1 month.
*Think about what you are going to do to deepen your relationship, create rapport each month with your clients.

Value Value Value Sale Upsale Downsale Deliver Value
*Need to emails a month minimum to your list.
*Make sure your delivery is mind blowing.
*After the sale ensure you are still providing value to the rest of the list so that you aren't seen as just a sales person.  This keeps them enrolled.
====Customer====
*Ensure that the customer is at the front of your mind every time you contact them.
*How are you helping them with their struggles towards their dreams
====Distinction====
*How are your products different.
*How is your approach distinctive?
====Greater Value====
Ask them questions.  Interact with your customers.
====Products and Promos====
Need to be strategic about what product you will release when.  What are the value products leading up to the sales.  Most people release a product the moment it is ready rather than adding value up to the point the sale makes sense for the customer.
====Partners====
Who is going to promote you.  Getting partners is the biggest un-kept secret.  You need fans, followers, partners etc that are promoting you.  You will hit a ceiling as to how much you can market yourself.
====Sales Psychology====
What is it you must say and not say to make a real difference with your customers so that they get excited about what you offer.

===10 Most Frequently Asked Questions===
====What do I teach====
Teach what you are passionate about doing, learning, serving, making.

If you aren't interested in learning about this topic, you will have a harder time with the synthesis.

====What if I have no results====
Go get them.  Doesn't matter if you are just starting out - we are all at that point at some time.

Go out and learn the thing, or go and create it.

Get the results for yourself, and then for family, friends, clients so that you can report the success.

====What if I have no list====
So what.  This is on your todo

You are already doing what you need to.  You are a creator.  So create the good content, put it out there.  

====What if I have no partners====
4 different types of partners
#affiliate partners - other experts in the area
#non profit partners
#corporate partners
#media partners

Have your brand up (website up, content available).  They need to be able to check you out.

If you don't have a good product available, people won't have anything to promote.

====What if I don't know how to market====
Education is marketing.  Put out quality information.  Your job isn't to market, it is to produce content.  The marketing is important, but do the marketing by educating, inspiring.

====What if I am not teaching get rich quick stuff====
This is not about get rich quick.  This works for everything.  Transformation and Transcendence is about better life for the clients, not about getting rich.

====Why just info products====
Doesn't make sense to focus only on info products.  Your expertise may also include physical products.  Don't limit yourself.

====Bringing the product to market====
#research.  Make sure it is unique and the product doesn't have an equivalent.  Space, price point, where to place the product.
#Figure out your path.  Manufacture on your own or License it to a manufacturer.  Licensing is easier, and moves the risk to someone else.  It also takes care of the marketing, distribution, etc.

====What if I am unknown====
Create the product, and then campaign it.

==Making a sale==
You need 7 web pages to make a sale.
*optin page
*2-3 value pages to deliver on what you promised
*sale page
*checkout page
*thank you page
*product delivery page

when creating your free product, teach both theory and technique.  Describe how you discovered it.  This gives the people a bit of a plan, and helps them progress.

Teach people a mindset.  Teach them how to think about something.  Try to change the way they think about something.  Debunk myths.  Then you need to motivate them to use this new knowledge and methods.

Mindset + Method + motivation moves you from trainer to leader.  People will follow you if you are leading.

opt in pages for a teleseminar is different than a webinar, etc, etc.
