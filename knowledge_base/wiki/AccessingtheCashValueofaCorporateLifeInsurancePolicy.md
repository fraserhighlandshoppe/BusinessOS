==Accessing the Cash Value of a Corporate Life Insurance Policy==
Reasons to access the CV:
*Supplement retirement income
*Unexpected life events
It is an alternative to income from personal or corporate assets.

===Cash Withdrawal or partial surrender===
*Appealing to clients not comfortable with leverage
*No financial underwriting
*No lingering interest charges
*Access to the CSV is guaranteed and relatively fast.
However
*Reduces the cash value left within the polcy
**Affects future growth of the cash value
**Reduces the death benefit payable.
*Taxation
*Withdrawals are not automatic
**Need to submit a form for each withdrawal.
*For UL policies surrender charges may apply.
====Taxation====
Every dollar removed is taxable in the same proportion as the total CSV would be taxed as if the entire policy was surrendered.

Assume CSV is 100K and ACB is 20k
*Partial surrender 30k
*Prorated ACB 6k
*Policy Gain is 24k (100% taxable as passive income)
*Remaining CSV 70k
*New ACB 14k
===Policy Loan===
Loan against the policy.  Insurer advances secured loan against the CSV.
*Interest charged
*Policy continues to grow uninterrupted
*On death, death benefit reduced by outstanding policy loan
Pro
*Access to CSV is guaranteed under contract
*No financial underwriting
*Policy growth is unaffected
Considerations
*Interest rates are typically higher than rates at commercial lenders
*Taxation
*Interest if not paid is added to the debt.  When the debt equals the CSV the policy terminates
*Not automatic
**Need to submit a form.
====Taxation====
Policy loan advances reduces the ACB
*Once the ACB is 0 every dollar of the policy loan thereafter is taxable
**Repayment of loan may become a tax credit
*CDA is affected

Assume CSV is 100K and ACB is 20k
*Partial surrender 30k
**Year 1 interest paid
**Year 2 repaid.
*Year 1 
**Policy Loan - ACB equals 10k taxable



===Collateral Loan===
Assign the policy to the bank as collateral
*Lender conducts underwriting
Up to 100% loan to CSV ratio
*interest paid monthly or annually.  In some cases may be capitalized (rolled into loan)
Death benefit pays off the loan.

Pro
*Interest typically lower than policy loan
*Several insurance lenders across canada
*Net death benefit typically illustrates higher than cash withdrawals AND policy loans
*Generally favourable tax treatment
Considerations
*Borrows must underwrite the borrower
*Interest rate flucutation
*Risk and place the policy assigned as security to the risk
*Obtaining and renewing is not guaranteed
*Tax laws and lending practices change
*Complex tax issues when loan is to shareholder directly (as opposed to flow through corporation).
====Taxation====
*As the collateral loan doesn't affect the policy there is no tax consequences to the owner.
*Interest on the loan is not deductible unless the loan is used for business purposes
*CDA is not affected

====Shareholder Borrowing Taxation====
*Some lenders will lend to shareholder
**Taxable shareholder benefit during life of insured.  Typically addressed with paying a guarantee fee to the corporation based upon the amount of the loan.
*Taxable benefits at death - must ensure a policy which prevents the Insurance from (directly) paying off the shareholders loan
*No interest deductibility to the shareholder unless used for business or investment purpose
*Does not reduce the death benefit nor the share value

===Tax Considerations===
Options are usually tax distributions unless the corporation is repaying a loan from the shareholder.
*Dividends
**Non eligible dividends - 45-48% depending on province and may generate a tax refund to the corporation
**Eligible - taxable at slightly lower  rate than non eligible.  may generate a tax refund to the corporation
*Bonus
**Taxed as regular income
**May be a deduction to the corporation

===Partial or Total Surrender===
 This is a contractual provision
  Some policies allow partial, some only allow total surrender.
 per CRA this is a disposition
Whole life you can't put the money back in.

Universal life you may be able to put the money back in.

#Corporate makes premium payment
#Life insurance policy
##No tax on growth
#Corporation withdrawals.
##Taxable income being reported (ACB)
###Lowest taxable in early years due to ACB
###At the end, 100% taxable.
##Insurance company issues full amount.  Issues a T5 at tax times (so set aside tax owing)

Remaining policy amount continues to grow after partial surrender.  However it will be stunted due to the reduced amount.
===Corporate Policy Loan===
#Corporate makes premium payment
#Life insurance policy
##No tax on growth
#Corporation Policy Loan
##Loan up to ACB is tax free.  Anything above is taxable.
##Loan is advanced by the insurer.
###Insurance company issues full amount.  Issues a T5 at tax times (so set aside tax owing)
###Lowest taxable in early years due to ACB
###At the end, 100% taxable.
##Considered disposition
##Do not require credit approval
#Pays salary or dividend
##Reduced death benefit
##Taxable income being reported
##Shareholder receives cash., pays tax
#At death - corporate receives death benefit payout
##Credit to Capital Dividend Account
#Pay Shareholder
##No tax on CDA (Capital Dividends)

If the policy loan doesn't exceed the ACB, then not taxable - ACB is reduced.  

Loan can be repaid at any time.  If loan is repaid, ACB goes back up.

Interest on the policy loan can be capitalized.

Interest payments not required so long as loan to CSV ratio is not exceeded.

Full value of policy can continue to grow.

Taxable income is passive, highest rate, and included in AAII.

Credit to CDA is reduced by outstanding loan balance at insured death.

===Collateral Loan===
#Corporate makes premium payment
#Life insurance policy
##No tax on growth
#Corporation requests Loan from a 3rd party financial institute.
##Can usually access 50-95% of CSV
###Will depend on if WL or UL.  UL have investments so greater variability...
##Loan is tax free.
##Interest can be paid each year or added to the loan.
###Margin call might occur if loan balance exceeds CSV ratio
#Pays salary or dividend
##Shareholder receives cash., pays tax
#At death - LOAN is repaid.  Then corporate receives remaining death benefit payout
##Credit to Capital Dividend Account
#Pay Shareholder
##No tax on CDA (Capital Dividends)

Interest are deductible IF bank requires payment.

Policy owner is responsible for loan repayment.

May tie up policy owners access to other credit.
