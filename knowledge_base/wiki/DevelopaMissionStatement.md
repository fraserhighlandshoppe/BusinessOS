===Team Mission Statement===
Having a Team Mission Statement will help your current team AND your prospects.

Think of this as having something that you all stand for that’s deeper and bigger than just your products of services that you sell.

What do you want to do together as a team that’s bigger and has a deeper meaning in the world?

Our mission is to help <specific number> of <ideal customer type> to accomplish <their pain point solution> in <timeframe/date> by <action step>
