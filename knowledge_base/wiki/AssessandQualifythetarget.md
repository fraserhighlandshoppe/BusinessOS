==Assessing and Qualifying a client==
There are 3 dimensions to evaluate a client with.

#Does the client have a compelling reason to act.
#Do we have a compelling proposition? (storyline)
#Do we have access to the decision makers?
##if we can't get to the right people we can't tell our story.

If a client doesn't pass all three criteria, it is unlikely you will gain any business.

We should not waste time on clients to whom we can not sell.
