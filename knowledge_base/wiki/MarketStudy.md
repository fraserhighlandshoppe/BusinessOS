====Market Study====

Your purpose is to understand and describe in detail the nature of your competition and customers.  Research and analysis of the market for your proposed product or service should be of sufficient depth to allow you to justify for yourself and any investors that:

    *A viable market for your product exists.
    *Entry to the market can be successfully undertaken within the time planned.
    *Your product is sufficiently distinctive to attract the sales you anticipate.

If there is not a real or perceived need for a product or service it will not sell no matter how great the producer thinks it is.  In order to be successful, the product or service must do one or more of the following:

    *Fit in an unfilled market niche.
    *Offer a lower price.
    *Have obvious or perceived superior quality.
    *Have little competition or augment scarce supplies.
    *Require highly specialized experience.

Your research will help you confirm the need for the proposed product or service. You can use a combination of primary sources; people you contact while networking, and secondary sources such as online and published information.

View the [[Media:MarketingResearchChecklists.pdf| Marketing Research Checklists]] for questions you can use to guide your research and interview your networking contacts.

The Market Study Should Include:

 *Your Product and Customers: Define and list the different types of products or services you will offer. Provide as much detail as possible. 
 *[[Media:BusinessDescription.pdf|Business Description]] to define the customers for each product or service (where applicable). 
 **Type of market (consumer or commercial; wholesale or retail; industrial, institutional, governmental).
 **Demographics of customer (age, income, education level, occupation), if consumer.
 **Size and industry niche of customer, if commercial.
 Using the resulting working definitions, learn as much as possible about the characteristics of your customers. For the primary research such as networking, you will need a self-introduction that helps your contacts understand your information needs. Review the [[Media:30SecondCommercial.pdf| 30-Second Commercial]] to draft your self-introduction.
 *Market Size:  Provide a description of the existing market size in units and dollars.
 *Market Share:  This is the percentage share of the market held by each of your competitors.  Also include your rationale for the percentage share you intend to capture or the rationale for your claim to 100% of a new market.
 This section is the most critical element of your business plan as this information allows to you develop a monthly sales forecast.  This will help you arrive at realistic projections.  Also review the [[Media:SalesPlan.pdf|Sales Plan]] for tips on developing the monthly sales forecast.  
 *Market Trends/Growth Rate: Describe new and potential markets, potential technological and regulatory changes or issues, and changing customer needs.
 *Competition:  Develop an analysis of who they are, how they operate, and their strengths and weaknesses.  Investigate and analyze competitors' market strategies, market niches, means of market differentiation, product/service development strategies, and life cycles and market types (industrial, commercial, residential, franchise, government) served and under consideration.  View the [[Media:CompetitiveAnalysis.pdf|Competitive Analysis]] to help you structure and complete this part of your marketing plan.
 *Sales Strategies and Cycle:  Analyze the sales strategies of your competition and the purchasing behaviour of your potential customers.  Include methods of selling the services, distribution channels, market segmentation and promotion strategies, pricing trends, and sales cycle.
