==Dealing with negativity in prospects==
 [[File: Tanya Aliza Negativity Response One Liners.pdf]]
why do we even get negativity from some prospects?

Most of the time it’s THEIR mindset and they need a little mindset shift. Sometimes you can help them with this and sometimes you can’t.  If you can help them with this, its important to understand why they’re negative in the first place and it could have something to do with how we’re approaching them. 

I know this sounds crazy, but take a minute and ask yourself, “Is there anything that I did to cause the negative reaction?”, ”Was I presenting in a salesy way?” and “Do I have my prospects best interest at heart?”

When you’re getting negativity from your prospects, re-evaluate the situation and ask yourself if there was anything you did to cause them to be negative.

*Sometimes it’s a simple tweak that we can make in our process.
*Sometimes it’s not us and there’s nothing that we could have done differently.

People who experience negative or stressful emotions are less healthy and live shorter lives…

And if you get a negative prospect, whether its justified or not, please please please make sure that you’re always standing above the negativity and you’re not stooping to their level. This is going to make you look more professional and it’s also going to make them THINK!

When your prospects are negative, they are expecting a REACTION from you. Don’t give it to them. Diffuse the situation with a ‘Take away’ one-liner and don’t give them anyhow to deal with negative people negativity back.

Keep your posture, and stay positive. Give our industry and the brand you’re representing a good name.

If you show that you’re a professional and you’re not NEEDY of their business, they will be more attracted to you!

Don’t let someone steal your dreams and your motivation… just let the negative people go and keep moving forward to find the people that are as positive and awesome as you.

===Be an educator not a sales person===
People love to connect with those that add value into their lives.

Professional Network Marketers help people buy.
*They ask questions
*Find out what their prospects desires are.
===Be Tool Based Not Skill Reliant===
Don't have to be an extrover to build your community
===Prove your duplication ability===
Use your tools.  Start showing people your ability.

People look more than they listen.

They observe how passionate and committed you are.

People need to know that if they jump into your team there are systems that are duplicate-able

===Common Interests and Common Enemies===
Most recruiters share a common interest.  Top recruiters share a common enemy.

People get passionate about both positive and negative things
===Leverage Social Media===
Need to have a plan to use social media.

Need to show that you are a real person.  People connect to people.  Most products people can buy on Amazon.

Share your message to generate leads.

Needs to speak to people.  Needs to spark interest.
===Tap into training===
Leaders are Readers.  They are students.  Sharpen the saw.
===Have an accountability Coach===
To have success you need to consistently work a system.

===Teach to Teach===
If you don't teach your team how to teach the next generation, you become the choke hold.  And that prevents you from owning your time.
