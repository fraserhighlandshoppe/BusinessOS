==Referrals==
Leverage your clients individually or in groups.
*Personal Introductions
**Ask for introductions
***Would you be willing to send an email connecting us
*Events

If you are helpful to your client, your clients will gladly refer you out
*They are helping their friends and people like to be helpful.
===Ensure clients know your Ideal Client===
Can your clients articulate who you work with?
*Who you work with (key attributes)
*What you do
*what you help them achieve
===Build a Social Relationship with your clients===
*Had a social lunch 65% referred
*Did something socially 59% referred
*Worked alongside advisor in charitable efforts 59% referred
*Engaged in hobbies or activities with advisor 56% referred
*Were invited to advisor's home for dinner 52% referred
*Met advisor's spouse 45% referred
*Share a common interest or hobby with advisor 44% referred
*Went to a social event hosted by advisor 42% referred

===Post Ideas===
#What is the #1 question you get asked by prospects? Clients?
#How has your approach changed since you started as an advisor?
#How does one of your hobbies or interests relate to what you do?
#What should your ideal prospect be asking you but is NOT asking yet?
#What’s a personal trait that differentiates you from competitors?
#What are the top 3 things that are preventing prospects from hiring you?
#What is a statistic that emphasizes the importance of how you help your clients?
#Who is a COI you could highlight? 
#Why did you decide to become a financial advisor?
#Why do you love being a financial advisor?
===Conversation Starters===
#Common connection: I noticed we’re both connected to [name]. I used to work with her at [past company]. How do you know her?
#Alumni affiliation: Great to connect with another [university] alumni. Did you live on campus?
#Content posted: I wanted to reach out because I read your post on [topic] – great stuff. Curious, what do you think about [question about topic]?
#Job commonality: I noticed you used to work at [past company]. I used to work there as well. Random question… do you happen to know [name]?
#Sports Fan: I saw you’re a fan of [sports team]. Go [team name]! Did you catch the game last Sunday?
#Location: I have a good friend in [location], how long have you lived there? Do you remember that [popular landmark]?
#Career Intrigue: I noticed your work involves [specialization]. I’ve always had an interest in [specialization], even took some classes back in college. How did you get into that field?
#Website Research: Thanks for connecting. I was on your website today and noticed [discovery]. I’m curious, [question about discovery].
#Company News: I was reading an article today on [website or publication] and I noticed that your company is currently [discovery]. I’d love to hear your take on it. Do you think [question about discovery]?
#Comment Recognition: I noticed your comment on [name of poster] and I couldn’t agree more. I’d love to know your take on [insert related question]?
