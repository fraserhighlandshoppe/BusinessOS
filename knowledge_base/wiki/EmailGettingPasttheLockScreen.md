===Get Past the Lock Screen===
 ideas from https://www.xaprb.com/blog/effective-sales-outreach/
When a new email pops into someone's inbox, there is a very good chance they will see it on the Lock Screen of their phone first.  Don't waste that opportunity:
*Don't waste the first few lines with crud
*Have a subject line that is Short, Concise, on topic.
*Have the email body short, concise, on topic
*Don't use gimmicks
*do your research, 
*add value to the reader, 
*make a single specific “ask,” and 
*let it go if the stars aren’t aligned.

The best sales outreach emails optimize for simplicity and directness. They make every word count, especially in the subject or first sentence. They are easy to scan from the preview of a lock-screen on a phone and decide whether to delete or not. They are optimized for not messing up a potential engagement, and they waste no effort on trying to win in a losing situation.
