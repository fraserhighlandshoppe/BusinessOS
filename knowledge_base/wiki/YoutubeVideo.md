Make sure your video has a voice over with your keyword naturally placed through, if you know your related keywords insert them as well.

Plan out your video to make it relevant and unique, ask for ratings, give a question for comments and encourage social interaction.

Build backlinks and send views instantly as the video has been released.

Always leave a reason to check your site (the missing piece of the puzzle should be there)

Build a quality youtube channel and in the long run you'll have a lot more success in converting and ranking (as opposed to creating automated videos one after another with ****ty voiceovers and horrible graphics).

Quality is paramount though, and it seems the focus overall online is shifting towards quality ... so make a channel worth watching and keep interacting with your target community.

One thing I know for sure though is that once your video gets ranked or noticed you should see steady gains... I haven't touched my channels in months but if I look at the analytics I can see a very steady gain in visitors from all sources.

Which in my view translates into something such as 'gaining authority'.

Which is the main benefit of creating a quality channel ... everything you release will be given instant authority at which point video marketing becomes a breeze 


Keep videos between 3-5 minutes long....look for popular videos related to yours and copy their tags....this will give you a great chance of popping up on the main search page or on the related videos page.
