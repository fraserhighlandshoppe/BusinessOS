===Headline===
You'll want your HEADLINE to speak to your differentiators and call out your target audience. 
*What makes you different or unique? 
*Why should somebody do business with you over a competitor?

Think of your headline as the tagline for your brand. It follows you around on LinkedIn and you want it to draw your prospects in and educate them on why they should work with you.

Here's a good format you can use for your headline: [Title] at [Company Name] | Helping [Avatar] [Benefit]
===Summary===
*Mention the types of business you do business with
*Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
*Highlight what makes you different
*Include a Call to Action (how can they contact you)

You'll also want to write a SUMMARY that speaks to your prospects.

Start with a paragraph with a story that gets to the heart of your prospects pain points. You really want to get what’s in their head out on paper to sell them on needing a service like this.

Second, explain to them how you’re the solution to all of these problems. I would also call out your target audience here as well. Who do you specialize in working with?

Then go into detail about your services and what makes you unique. Get across why they must have you!

Lastly, provide a call-to-action and give them a way to contact you.
