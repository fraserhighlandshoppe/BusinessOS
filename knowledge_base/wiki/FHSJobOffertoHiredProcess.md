==Job Offer to Hired Process==
This process assumes that a job offer has been presented and accepted.

Needed info:
*Info to Add and Employee (OrangeHRM)

**Name, DOB
**Address and Phone Details
**Emergency Contact details
**Dependants
**Position (Job) details
**Salary Components
***Direct Deposit details is an option
**Org Chart details
**Qualifications
**Memberships
