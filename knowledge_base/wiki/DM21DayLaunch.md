==3X Method==
[[Media: 3X Worksheet.pdf]]

The normal steps of business:
#Generate Traffic
#Traffic -> Leads
#Leads -> Customers
#Customers --> Money
#Reinvest Money -> Generate Traffic (Grow Business)

The trick is to get more from what you have - more leads to become customers, and more money from the customers.

An email subscription box (by itself) is NOT a lead magnet.

#Product Splintering
#Self Liquidating Offers (Tripwire)
#Ultra Specific Lead Magnets
#Two Step Opt-In
#Speed and Automation Upsell
#Lead with Proof
#"Are you Still" followup

===process===
Lead magnet leads to the (splintered) tripwire which leads to the core product.

Lead Magnet is a very specific thing.  Needs to be interesting, tactical, tangible.  Not large boring reports (can still be a report).


After Opting In, send them to a page where you acknowledge the step they took (send the report to their email) but start PAGE 2 (tripwire) in the sales funnel.  
*Lead with Proof.
*Standard sales letter.

If they buy on tripwire, then offer an upsell (core product).


===Conversion Hacks===
If you have list burnout (less and less purchasing), you can take your $47 dollar product, bundle it with a bonus report, sell it for $7.  Research shows that if someone buys this bonus they are 10X more likely to buy your main product.  This is because of commitment and consistency - people who start down a path tend to continue down it.

====Product Splintering====
Most products and services have smaller chunks that make up the main product/service.

Take some of these chunks out and sell them separately.  If someone buys the splinter, they are 10X more likely to buy the main one.

Each month, take one of these splinters and show it to your customers.

By changing your upfront product, you keep the marketing fresh and this prevents list burnout.

You don't try selling your core product to people who aren't already buyers.

With these splinters you give your leads more opportunity to interact with you.

====Tripwire====
Low end offers at break even with the goal of generating a customer while covering your marketing cost.  Easiest to use splinter products from your core offer.  They are the key to perpetual traffic.


====Two Step Opt-In====
Lowers shields because not asking for email address on main lead page.

Microcommitement - they have clicked a button, so now they are likely to opt-in.

===Perpetual Traffic===
You need to make more money from the traffic you get than the amount you spend on acquiring them.

DM spends 50c/click on facebook.  Unskilled copywriters (and unknown businesses) get about 0.5% conversion rate from cold traffic.

So a 500$ budget with 1000 resulting clicks, .5% conversion => 5 sales.  So a 50$ product is loosing money.

====What DM does====
#50c/click -> Landing page to capture lead.
#Send lead to a [[tripwire]] (7$ offer)
#Send buyers to core offer (50$)
#Send core buyers to Profit Maximizer (Favourite/Best is "speed and automation" upsell).  "Want some help"...  Focus on desired end result of buyer!  Why did they buy your product in the first place?  A large chunk of your buyers will upsell if they can get the results faster with less work.  It's common to double the average customer value just by adding a Profit Maximizer to the offer.
##Physical product sellers can offer expedited delivery, assembly, advanced training on use of product.
##Info sellers can offer done for you services, software, consulting.
##Software sellers can offer installation or migration services, higher levels of customer support.
##Consultants/advisors can offer more hands on experiences.
#Followup with those that didn't buy.  Send an "Are you still" "Have you yet" followup campaign. (No one likes to feel stuck/trapped.  Are you still XXX something we want to no longer do, it is like being hit in the gut and we go to avoid those)

500$ budget -> 1000 clicks => Lead Magnet with 40% opt in rate is 400 leads -> tripwire with about 10% conversion => 40 purchases of tripwire ($280) -> 40 sent to core offer with 10% conversion rate (4) => 4x50 = $200 => $480.

DM usually sees better than 20% conversion rate on core offer.

Plan on 40/10/10 ratio (Opt in/tripwire/core).  If you can get this, you have perpetual traffic.

==Inverted Funnel==
This is an architecture process.

Begin with the end in mind.  We want our customers to get to our Core Offer.

When we plan starting with the end in mind, the flow through the funnel is much more smooth and seamless to the customer.

Planning Sheet:
*Lead Magnet: _______________ $FREE
*Tripwire: __________________ $XXX (impulse buy)
*Core Offer: ________________ $YYY (mid priced) (this is not an impulse buy)(should be priced so they don't need to get approval)
*Profit Maximizer: __________ $ZZZ (higher priced)
===Core Offer===
This is the thing you are most proud of and you want all visitors and customers to get this/experience this.  

*Flagship product
*WOW factor
*mid priced.  $50-200 for b2c
*this is not an impulse buy
*should be priced so they don't need to get approval
*membership product or software or...

You can have more than 1 core offer

====Creating Products====
Options include
*Manual Labor (i.e. Consulting)
**This helps you ensure people are interested in the end result
**If people won't buy it as a service, they won't buy as a product
*Productize an action/activity
*Affiliate Offer (someone else's product)
**Pays you to do market research
**If you can't sell someone else's (similar) product you won't sell your own.
===Tripwire===
(Splinter of Core Offer)

You can have more than one tripwire, just like you can have more than one core offer in your business!

Irresistible super low ticket offer that exists solely to convert a Lead into a Customer.

 Example: Columbia House Records offer.

A tripwire is NOT a coupon!

Coupons are a marketing message where they are trying to sell their core offer.

[[Image: Coupon vs Tripwire.png]]

As a general rule, the tripwire should be less than $20.  HOWEVER, it should be an impulse buy.  There should be immediate obvious value.
====Types of Tripwires====
*Physical Premium (pure want not need)
*Small but critical (need but not necessarily want)(candle wick, guitar pick)
*Book or Report
*Paid Webinar/TeleSeminar (actual teaching should not be free)
*Software/Plugins
*A la Carte service  
*Small Service (Look at Fiverr.com for ideas)

====Discover your tripwire====
Questions to ask yourself:

#What is the one thing everyone wants but doesn't necessarily need?
#What is the one thing everyone needs but doesn't necceserily want?
#What is a valuable service you can perform quickly and inexpensively that will deliver results in advance and get your foot in the door?

====Branding====
The less navigation the better.  However some networks (i.e. google adwords) wants some navigation.

DM experience is unbranded outperforms branded landing page.  However, this is changing.
*Logos used to supress conversion
*Consumers want to do business with reputable brands because of fear of being scammed.

New method is to book-end your page - put your logo at the top of the page, and your face on the bottom of the page.

[[Image: Landing Page Bookend.png]]

If using a Video Sales Letter on your thank-you page, the face on the bottom of the Landing Page is important so the customer isn't asking who is the named face-of-the-company on the VSL.

On thank you page, you once again bookend the page.

====Reverse Squeeze====
With a Reverse Squeeze page, you are giving them value in advance of them giving you their email address.

Pure conversion standpoint, a traditional page converts better.

The downside to the reverse squeeze page is that the lead needs to consume the value first.

A traditional squeeze page should be 40% conversion (if the lead magnet is valuable).

The Reverse Squeeze page usually has 20-30% conversion.

HOWEVER, the reverse squeeze will have better qualified leads.

Some traffic sources may not allow you to drive to a traditional squeeze page.

You should use/test both.

Steps:
#Headline, useful but incomplete proof (show what (not how) you did, result) video, opt-in box
##Start with a bold promise (I want to show you ...)
##Show proof within the first 90 seconds, sooner is better.
##Useful but incomplete content
#Hint at part 2 requiring an opt-in.
##In the next video I will show you exactly how to do this...
#More Proof.
#Call for Action - Opt-in
Keep video to less than 5 minutes.  If it is longer, you better be showing a ton of proof.  Otherwise you are probably sharing/teaching too much

Video 2 - 
#Show them HOW.  Do not go straight into a sales pitch or you WILL LOSE THEM.
#Reveal the offer.
#Sales message

The Reverse Squeeze page can be hidden within a blog post.  People will share blog posts, but they won't share a squeeze page.

====Types of Thank You Page====

Download Now:

[[Image: Thank You Page Download Now.png]]

This fails you because your customer just took an action, and is trusting you, etc.  They are at the pinnacle of taking the next step.

Check your email:

[[Image: Thank You Page Check Email.png]]

While waiting for your email, please do the following....

This method ensures you get an accurate email address.  If they gave you a junk email address they will go back and give you a real one.

This also doesn't produce a speed bump in the process.  If you offer the download/access link right here on the page they start consuming the Lead Magnet and never watch the sales message.  This hurts your conversion rates.  However, this will annoy some people.

A video sales letter typically converts best.

Hybrid offer page:

[[Image: Thank You Page Hybrid.png]]

On this one you deliver on the lead magnet, but also makes an offer.

This is the most up and up method, but it lowers your conversion and also leads to more bogus emails.

Smarter Hybrid - deliver on the Lead Magnet as part of the sales message.

===Double Opt In===
Confirmed opt in (they have to click a link in the email) adds hoops to the customer's process to get your content.  Some email providers require it, as does some countries and/or companies.

Remember if you do this, your thank you page needs to make mention of it, right at the top.

[[Image: Double Opt In.png]]

Steps to have on the thank you:
#Step One - check your email
#Step Two - click on the link to confirm you want our content
#Step Three - Click on this link for a bonus video
