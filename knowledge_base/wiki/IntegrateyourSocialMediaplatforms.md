====Integrate your social sites====
Create a presence in each major social media site where they spend time.  This builds trust faster and makes a stronger relationship.  They see your great content everywhere.

Content is king in social media.  Make sure you are everywhere your clients are so you are always in their mind.

Aim for 100 social videos.  These are information, not sales pitches.  At the end of the video, offer more videos, tips, etc.  Ensure squeeze page has a reminder about the videos, arrow pointing to opt in box.

4 different platforms:
*Youtube - put 40 (40%) here, as rapidly as you can on one channel.
*Twitter - everytime you post a new one, promote it on blog and twitter even if not available unless they opt into Facebook or Youtube channels.  
*Facebook - create a page (not profile).  Post 20 additional videos (different from Youtube).  Different so that people follow you here.  Each has an offer as well.  Want 10 more, like us on facebook.  Create a section on the page which is visible only to those who like you on facebook.  These 10 have the offer to go to the blog for the extra 20 mentioned on Youtube/ blog squeeze.
*Your blog - everytime you post a new one, promote it on blog and twitter even if not available unless they opt into Facebook or Youtube channels.  

Members only section of your blog.  Give them immediate access to 10 videos that are unique to the membership site.  Autoresponder series to send a new video each day for 20 days.  Don't sell anything in these emails.  Direct to a video only.

Email (Day) 15 - include an offer to buy something from you.
Once all 20 emails are done, send another offer to buy something.
Day 31 - upsell to a paid membership site - advanced or related info on the #1 niche topic.
