==Center of Influence Talk==
From a young age I knew that it is important to have money, to save it, and to use it wisely. 

I started at World Financial Group which piqued my interest in the Financial Services.  I moved to Hub Financial because it lets me better serve my clients through a larger range of products.

*Are you familiar with people working in Financial Services?  What was your experience?

With most financial services products, most people recognize they should do something, but it is only after it is too late that they try to take care of it!  After all, how many of your peers will tell you that their kids and family are the absolute most important thing in their lives?  But then their money isn't first being allocated to protect those.  Everyone has good intentions; they just aren't provided the opportunity to truly take care of it.

I work with individuals and families to align their finances with their goals so that what matters most gets taken care of!  I will ask questions, and most people will squirm.  But this is a good thing, because it means they will think about the answers.  And many will be motivated to do something about the questions and the answers.  

I start with a 15 minute introductory meeting where we have a simple conversation.  We will determine if there are any areas of interest where I may help them with.  This is entirely about them, not about me.  The focus is on the areas they might want to discuss.

There are 4 common results from this simple conversation:
#We have a good connection, and we start working together
#We have a good connection but now isn't the right time, so we stay in touch.
#The person is well taken care of
#There are areas we could work on but there wasn't a good connection.

I do meet people who aren't ready to do anything, and that is OK.  But when they are ready, they will know how to contact me!

I do not want to be a salesperson.  I want to be a resource.  If the people I meet aren't ready, I am not going to hound them.  I only want to introduce the services I may provide, and then they can decide if they want a relationship with me to pursue those services.

Since I am solely paid by commission, I lose my commission if my clients change their minds.  So this means I only benefit by doing what is absolutely right for the client.

I provide my clients a "financial compass".  Everyone is extremely busy being busy.  We are distracted by internal and external noise.  We spend a lot of time trying to keep up with the latest and best of everything.  This is where the financial compass comes in.  By helping clients understand and document their goals, and establish that which is truly important to them, we can then look at how they spend their money and determine a comfortable amount that can be set aside to eliminate their biggest concerns and facilitate their dreams.

For those that don't have an interest at this time, I will ask if they want me to touch base at a later time, and if there is anyone to whom they would like to introduce me; Another family for whom a simple conversation might be of interest. 

I am looking to meet someone similar to you.  Are there anyone you could introduce me to?
===background===

I want to help people.  I also like to teach, and this is one opportunity to help instruct people on money and finances.  And of course there is a possibility of income if I help someone out.

Everyone learns something different about money as they grow up.  Even within the same family what we see and learn is different.  If you look at how I and my 2 sisters handle and treat money,
you can see that we didn't learn the same thing.  This is natural, as the kids grow up, their lives evolved within the family.

My philoshipy is that there should be mutual benefit.  And that can only happen through ethical and integral actions and behaviors.

One reason to deal with me rather than someone in a corporate office is that I don't have a boss breathing down my neck to make quotas.  I can take the time to really get to know my clients, their wants and needs, and ensure that they are properly taken care of.

I may not have a direct team to lean on, but I do have the support of the team in the office through the MGA I place my business as well as the insurance carriers with whom I place the policies.

When working with clients, I am not going for the quick sale.  I want the life long client.  This means we are going to be thorough and complete.  I also don't expect that everyone will work with me.

I am paid by the insurance carrier that I place business with.  While I am paid commission, this is entirely client focused.  If the policy is cancelled, I am penalized, but then so is the carrier.  So we want to ensure the right product for the right client.

My goal here is to get introductions.  Once introduced, I will followup with the other person within 48 hours to line up a time to sit down with them, and have a very similar discussion.  We will mutually decide if there is areas for the two of us to pursue together.
