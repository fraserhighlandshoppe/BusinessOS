==Signs of a Stuck Deal==
 Brad Zingle, HUB
#Low conversion from lead to opportunity or opportunity to customer.
#Lots of deals stuck at 2nd meeting
#Average sales cycle that keeps getting longer.
#many prospects "rolled" over to next renewal (they procrastinate - lets talk then.)
 Inertial can be the toughest competitor.  Some prospects simply choose to do nothing.
==Get Unstuck==
#Create a sense of urgency
#Send a pamphlet directly related to their situation. 
##Mark up the pamphlet with comments, highlights, circles.  Point out how it directly relates,
==What non feature issues might your prospect be worrying about?==
*migrating to new markets with new procedures
*Not trusting the service
*Not getting the support they need
*Having to re-train staff.
===In Person===
Structure questions to generate urgency with SPIN
*Situation
*Problem
**What problem(s) does the solution solve?
**Ask questions about these problems.
**Tell me about your "XXX" plan currently in place.
**Are you looking for ways to XYZ on ZZZ
*Implication
**Does that lead to...
**What affect does that have on...
**Has that caused...
**Does that create...
**Does that result in...
*Needs - Payoff

 *Would you like to be able to pass assets to a beneficiary?
 *Do you have a corporate life insurance need?
 *Do you have taxable passive investment assets?
 *Do you own corporate investments with a deferred capital gain?
 *do you want a certain amount of estate value guaranteed?
