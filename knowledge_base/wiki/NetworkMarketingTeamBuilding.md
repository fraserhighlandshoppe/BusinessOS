==Team==
 https://onlinemlmcommunity.com/top-10-team-building-tips-for-network-marketers/
People join people, not organizations.
===Have a Team Name===
*Helps with identity
===Hold Weekly Meetings===
*Builds Excitement
*Provides support
*Builds sense of belonging
*Creates unofficial accountability
*Share struggles and success
*Ensures information flows
===Hold Events===
*Social Aspect
*Share ideas
*Fellowship
===Newsletter===
*Tips of the month
*Recommended book
*Product info
*Quote of the month
*Recognition
===Contests===
*Ramps up the competitive spirit
*Creates excitement
*Creates momentum
*Provides an opportunity for recognition
===Recognition===
People crave recognition.  Provide recognition for even the smallest thing

Celebrate the wins, even minor victories.
===Train your team===
===Look for the good in people===
Everyone has good qualities.  Identify these and help people build these up.
===Have Empathy===
It is difficult to put yourself into someone else's shoes.  But it is what leads to long term relationships and success.
===Have Fun===
*If it isn't fun, people won't show up.
