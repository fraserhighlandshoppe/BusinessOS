==The 5 minute pitch==

[[Category: Marketing]]
[[Category: Fasttracks Superconference 2014]]
[[Category: Fasttracks Superconference]]
[[Category: Fasttracks]]

The 5 minute pitch would be used at something like a wine and cheese party, where you are being introduced to people that would be interested investors.

The purpose of the pitch is to tell them a tiny bit about what you do, what you are looking for, and to create enough interest that they would be interested in sitting down with you later to discuss further the opportunity.

===Tips===
#Don't mention negatives if you can avoid it.
#If including negative information, make sure it is done for full transparency.
#Make sure you have a call to action (future meeting)
#Little story telling.  Lots of facts.
#Ensure your end goal is included in the pitch.
#What problem are you solving.
#Avoid sounding like a sales pitch.  People don't like being sales pitched.
#W.A.I.T. - Why am I talking.   Stay on track.
##Ensure your end goal for the pitch (5 minute mini meeting) is clearly included in the pitch.  The listener should not walk away wondering why you just told them all that.
#What problem are you solving.  
#Answer the 5Ws.
##Who are you.  Who are your team.
##What are you doing?
##When are you doing this (especially important for future products)
##Where are you doing this.
##Why are you doing this.  This will cause an emotional reaction.
#Upfront contract.
##It's OK to tell me no
##You will probably have some questions like X, Y, Z
##I will have some questions for you to ensure this can be a fit.
