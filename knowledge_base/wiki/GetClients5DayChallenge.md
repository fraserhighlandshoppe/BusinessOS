==Get Clients 5 Day Challenge==
 Kathryn Calhoun email series
===Day 1 - Low-Hanging Fruit=== 
Do you sometimes overcomplicate things?

I am all about keeping things simple and I want to help you do the same. (Did I tell you I'm also a stay-at-home mom? I have zero time for complicated.)

That's why, when it comes to this challenge of getting actual paying clients before January is through, we have to focus. We have to be smart and strategic.

We have to go after the low-hanging fruit...

There are TWO types of low-hanging fruit and I want you to embrace both:

====Your Target Clients====

We are not going to cast your net far and wide. There is a time and a place for that but it is NOT during this challenge...

Instead, we are going to focus on who are your easiest potential prospects.

You don't want to worry about who may or may not convert from prospect to client. You want to go after the MOST LIKELY prospects.

(Set an alarm on your phone for 11am PT/2pm ET to go over to my free Facebook Group, the Techie Stuff Clubhouse, where I'll be doing a live video training on how to identify your easiest prospects.)

====Your Offer====

If your creative entrepreneur brain is anything like mine, sometimes you get all excited about what you could sell and start coming up with all kinds of new courses or trainings you could put together...

Nonononono Kevin!

Remember, we have to keep this simple.

We only have this month and I am very, deadly serious about helping you get real clients before January is through.

Keeping it simple means going after the low-hanging fruit...

And low-hanging fruit means picking and choosing from the offerings you already have.

I am willing to bet you already have a plethora of content you could sell easily...

So:

Your mission, should you choose to accept it Kevin, is to pinpoint your lowest-hanging fruit both in terms of prospective clients AND in terms of prospective offerings.

===Day 2:  Quick 'n' Dirty Copywriting Shortcuts What to Actually Say to Attract Perfect Prospects===
Day 1 was all about figuring out your lowest-hanging fruit, both in terms of whom to target and what to sell...

We're going after low-hanging fruit because we do NOT have time in this short challenge to overcomplicate things.

In Day 2, we're also going to keep things uber-simple in terms of marketing...

Now, there are people who teach whole big long expensive courses on marketing copy who would totally disagree with me that I can teach you everything you need to know about copywriting in under an hour...

What you may not know about me is that I actually got my entrepreneurial start as a marketing copywriter and that's how I supported myself and my family for close to a decade...

So, I'm blowing the lid off this whole copywriting thing:

It does NOT have to be hard.

To get you going, I'd love for you to do a bit of brainstorming first on these 2 questions:

#Who is the ONE PERSON (real or imagined) who represents my perfect prospects to a 'T'? Name, likes, dislikes, hobbies, family makeup, fears, passions, habits, everything. 
#When it comes to your niche, what is frustrating the heck out of your people? What's keeping them up at night?
The more completely you can answer these questions, the easier it will be for you Kevin.

We don't have endless amounts of time to get this done... In fact, too often I see entrepreneurs and biz owners NOT get out there enough with their stuff because they feel like they haven't quite perfected their marketing messages yet.

That makes me so sad! You'll neeeeevvveeeeer make money sitting behind your computer tweaking your marketing copy...

So let's just get it done. TODAY. Okay? I'm going to show you:
*A simple fill-in-the-blank template to get your copy done in minutes
*How to get your words right, quickly (because this is a 5-day challenge, not a 5,555-day challenge! We're spending the minimum amount of time, to do nothing more than get the words needed for you to get out there and find. those. clients. now.)
*Tricks to make sure you never have to worry about what to say to attract your perfect prospects ever again.

As I mentioned yesterday, I am super-serious about helping you find real, paying human clients before January is through...

That means 2 things: (1) keeping it simple and (2) taking action.

===DAY 4: Find leads the easy way===
You've figured out what you want to sell, whom you want to sell it to, and how you're going to price it and get paid.

Awesome!

Now what?

Well, as you know, you need people to make offers to! If you don't have a big email list, large social media following, or roster of past leads and clients, that can be tougher.

Today we're going to be talking about joint venture partnerships and affiliate relationships:

In a nutshell, this is how to leverage the goodwill other people have with their own audiences to sell your stuff...

And. It's. Awesome.

We'll be covering how you can create these amazing collaborations, even if you don't have much of a following yet.

To get ready for today's live, brainstorm a list (if you didn't already do it on Day 1) of people who are in front of your people already. Doesn't matter how "big" or "small" they are. Just think of anyone you can, close to you or not, who has the ears of your perfect prospects.
