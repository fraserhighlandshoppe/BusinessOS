==Perceptions and Perspectives: Future Proof your Practice==
===5 Disruptors===
*Focus on Fees (Regulators introduced CRM2 and transparancy)
**Actual dollar costs now must be disclosed
**Competitors declared war on Fees (Questrade) - claim to provide up to 30% more wealth (by discounting value of advice to 0)
*Innovation and Tech
**Robo Advisors - designed to replace advisors
***Canadians are willing to use robo advisors.
**Trading Platforms
**Customer Relationship Management - allow more efficiency
***People can do certain things better than tech
*Increased Client Expectations
**Have increased to the point where they are now unrealistic.
***Amazon has led this - fast access to discounted high quality product.
***Consumers want more for less.  They want Ritz Carlton experience at Holiday Inn Express prices.
*Shifting Demographics
**Aging population
***Inter-genrational wealth transfer (1T$) over next 10 years.
***First transfer will be to women (spouses) and then children
****80% of women will fire their advisor after the death of their husband
*****Not meeting with/returning calls of advisors
*****Cashing out wealth
*****Not taking the advise
*****Don't have the relationship with the advisor that their husband did
***67% of millenials want robo advisor.
****Perception of high fees
****Perception of High Minimums
*Regulation and Compliance
**CRM2
**KYC
**KYP
**New regulations on 20191231
***Pushing advisors into backoffice (admin) instead of out front building better experience/value
===Perception===
Perception is reality

Everyone's perception of value is different.  Think brand name versus generice/no-name.

Perception of value is not fixed; Can be influenced.

Since 2008, the perception of value of advice has decreased compared to cost (value gap)

[[Image: Value Perception gap.png|500px]]

Problem is tangible services can be commoditized and replaced.

Most advisors are Left brained and focus on Tangible; Need to focus on what can't be commoditized.

[[Image: Coffee Latte Art experiment.png|500px]] 

 Most people thought the "art" version had higher value, and online respondees said
 they'd pay 10% more.  In person, respondees thought the art version tasted better
 and paid 13% more.

Human advisor can increase perceived value!!

A small event can have a big impact down the road. (Butterfly effect)

===Minimize Impact===
Future proof by providing intangible (and un-commoditizable) value.

*Address disruptors directly before they affect your business
**Address Questrade claims!
***Say it ain't so - countering your competitor's claim (Bruno De Pace webinar)
***CI Sound Financial Advice Campaign
*Create Stickier clients (fully committed to you)
**Advocate will give all of their money to their advisor
**Advocates will refer their friends and family
**Advocates have to be created.  Advisor has to deliver more and more value.

***Behavioural Coaching

***Can control risk, time horizon, behaviour
