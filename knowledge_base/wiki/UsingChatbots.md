==Build a Marketing Chatbot==
A chatbot is software that interacts with people within chat applications.
===How to get sales===
*Email - 24% open rate and <3% clickrate according to mailchimp.
**Annoying
**email address typos
**secondary email addresses just for lists.
**Typos in name
**inboxes with 10,000 unread
**people are spending less time in email and more in messaging.

Using a bot, no forms to fill out, no typos.
*Users press 1 button and they get the alert + the lead magnet + message + ...
*Open rate 93%
*Click rate 80%

Many more options than landing pages for subscriber caption.
*can have comments on FB trigger a bot message
*Manual click on "send to messenger" button
*...

Message can include download link for lead magnet, but also ask if they want it emailed too.  This gets the email address as well...

===Convert Website Visitors into Customers===
Hello Bar - helps intro visitors to your brand
*modal
*slide-in
*page takeover

96% of visitors aren't ready to buy yet.
*People check reviews
*People check around

A/B test showing multiple versions of the same offer.

98% leave your site without having taken an action.
*You haven't given them a reason to stay.
*Need an exciting and enticing offer
**Join my newsletter doesn't work
*Wrong message + wrong page
*Too many CTAs.

Money is in creating a relationship with your visitors.

====Irresistible offer====
Purpose of free offer is to get customer into funnel to go down the customer journey.

Make sure the offer leads into the funnel you want.
#something interactive
#quick win

switch things up
*instead of give us your email to get your free offer ->
**Do you want 10% off your entire order?  use coupon code XYZ...
**Collect email then.
*Yes/No questions
**Think about users.  What are they seeking.
**Don't have condescending answers.

Tip
*yes/no gets 20-30% more leads
*Journeys and courses convert than ebooks by 16%
**ebook makes people feel like they have to do something - read 100 pages...
*Offer lead magnet throughout the site (homepage, blog, header, exit pop-up)
**hello bar and chatbots can help here.
