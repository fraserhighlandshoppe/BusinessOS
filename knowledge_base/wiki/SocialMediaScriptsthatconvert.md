==Social Media Scripts that Convert==
Make sure to make these your own!  That means use words that you usually do.  Be authentic.  Adopt the layout, format etc but make sure the message sounds like you!

[[File: Social Media Scripts that Convert.pdf]]

These will work for your Warm, Luke Warm and Cold markets!

Do NOT ever:
*Send business info or links in a message
*Send links, EVER!

You are a professional in your business, and no big deal EVER completes via social media.  That happens in person or over the phone.

===3 Message Formula===
#Learn something about the person you are going to message
##Look at their ABOUT section
###Do they look like someone you want as a customer or partner
###What do they do for work
###Where do they live

====Message #1====
The purpose of this message is to start building the relationship.

 “Hey John, what’s up? I noticed we’re friends here on Facebook
 and we haven’t chatted for a while. I noticed that your profile says
 that you’re living up in New York. Is that correct? You up in NY?”

If I were talking to a friend that I know a little more, I might ask them what’s
been keeping them busy these days to start the conversation.
Now you wait until they respond back.

====Message #2====
DO NOT send until they have responded to message #1.

 “John’s that’s awesome, I visited New York for the first time last
 September and I loved it. I was actually hoping you said you were
 still there, because I have a business project that I’m expanding and
 I’m doing some Networking in your neck of the woods. Would you
 at all be open to connecting more regarding a side income project,
 if it didn’t interfere with what you’re already doing?”

====Message #3====
If they say ‘No I’m Not Open’ kindly thank them for their time and leave
on a good note. You now have their attention and they might come back
around and connect with you later. Leave a lasting GOOD impression.

 ‘That’s great. Shoot me a few times that you’re free this week for about
 15 mins and can have access to the internet. Tuesday and Thursday are
 the best for me if you can make something work either of those days’


 ‘That’s great. Shoot me a few times that you’re free this week for about
 15 mins and can have access to the internet. Tuesday and Thursday are
 the best for me if you can make something work either of those days’

After they shoot you a few times:

 ‘Okay let’s schedule Tuesday 4pm EST. Here’s my number so you know
 who’s calling 555-555-5555. What’s the best number to reach you on?’

After they send their digits:
 ‘Great. I’ve got you in my calendar for Tuesday at 4pm EST. Pop me in
 your phone and set a reminder. I got a busy day that day, so I’ll assume
 you’re punctual with your appointments! Looking forward to connecting
 more. Chat Tuesday!’

What if they ask what is it? I never get into too many details EVER. You loose
control over the process if you deliver too much in a message.

So if they ask what it is, I’ll say something like this:
 ‘I’d love to get into details over chat here, but it would be like giving a
 haircut over chat, I wish I could, but it’s impossible. I like to keep
 business a little more professional than a Social Media chat. Do you have
 a couple days that would work to connect more this week?”

Super Simple Right?! I promise you, that if you follow this message flow when
reaching out and connecting with people on Social Media, you’re going to have
a fully filled appointment book with people that are interested and excited
about what you have to offer.

You’ll also look way more professional!
