==How to make money with webinars==
===Webinar promotion manager===
What is it?  You find the product owners and put Deals together to promote their stuff via webinars.
*No products to create
*No customer support
*No merchant account
*Don't need to be on the webinar.
*Can be the promotional manager for multiple clients
*Almost all product owners need your services
*Most of the time, in your JV you get 50% of revenues.

===Your own product===
*Add value to the product to increase the price.  Ensure you add REAL value though.

===Affiliate Marketing===
*Find high profit affiliate/CPA partners

===Network marketing===
*Set up auto recruitment funnels
*You are targetting those that are already interested.

===Local Business Webinars===
*Business owners pay you to provide ongoing webinars for them
**Chirpractors
**Orthodontists
**Insurance Agents
**...
*These are education based leads.
*You can automate everything
*No limit on the number of clients you can have
*500-1000/month

==Conversion Rates==
High Ticket items have about a 1% conversion.

Can expect about 50% of people that sign up actually show up for the webinar.

Conversion rates are about the same for a low ticket item compared to a high ticket item.
*Sweet spot is between $497 and $1997.
==Webinar Sales Formula==
Quality Product + Quality Content + targetted traffic + webinar + offer == High earning, repeat buyers, large list, long term business.

===Quality Product===
If you wouldn't buy it, you shouldn't promote it.

If it sells in any other form of direct marketing it will sell on a webinar
*traditional seminars
*radio
*tv
*direct mail
*sale page
*...

===Qualty Content===
*Over deliver
*Don't keep the secret sauce

Many people are visual learners.
*create eye catching slides
*picture the person speaking
*Use wide screen
Avoid live demos

Use a count down screen
*include callin number
*Include instructions to un-mute
