==Value Proposition==
[[Category: Value Proposition]]
Value is subjective. (Talking Frog joke)

Value in a business context
*shareholder value
**Revenue growth rate
***New market entry
***new products
***Globalize the business
***Customer Loyalty program
***Pricing advantage with new distribution outlets
***focused advertising based on differentiation
**Operating Margin
***modern work practices
***cut costs
***restructuring
***consolidate/centralize
***Business/IT alignment
**Working Capital to Sales
***working capital reviews
***Improve debtor management
***SCM and JIT systems/methods
**Capital Expenditure
***utilization reviews
***capital appraisal
***lease versus buy
***treasury system
***risk Management system
**Tax Rate
***international holding structures
***exploit IP and brands
***minimize withholding taxes
**Cost of Capital (WACC)
***management understanding of WACC
***share buy-back and de-merger of non core business
**Competitive Advantage
***predictable financial performance
***return to core competencies
***link executive rewards to share price
***include all employees in company ownership (shares)
***Strong risk management procedures

All of this becomes the input into an executive level storyline/value proposition.  This can then become an informal or a formal presentation.
*Don't put blinders on.  Have the proposition wide open
*Position the storyline for the highest level for confirmation or refinement.
**our understanding
**required client transformation
**business value of our approach
**Our commitment to their success
**next steps
