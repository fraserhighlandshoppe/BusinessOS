Ensure that before you hop into a niche, others are making money in that niche.

Start with one or two niches and one or two websites.

==Sytems are key==
Systems let you stay focused.  They are strategic.  Tactics come and go (i.e. changing colors to stand out).

Without a strategy you will have a really hard time to succeed.

===What to do first===
Need to come up with a game plan that works for yourself.

Drive the traffic.  Targeted traffic is money in your pocket.  Free traffic from search engines, and build a list.

===Leverage yourself===
Online income = leverage.

Become an expert in your niche market.
====Expert====
Someone who demonstrates something and does it well.

Untied shoes - walk into a room and people are tripping over their selves.  With their untied shoes, you demonstrate how to tie them.

Facebook - The early adopters become the experts.  Example is when facebook finally allowed advertising - within days there were ebooks etc coming out.

Researching and reporting is lucrative.



===Leverage your business===
Focus on getting traffic.  Utilize SEO tactics.

Leverage your traffic and list to earn from affiliates.

Find Matt Cutts videos on Youtube - he works at Google explains what Google wants.
===Requirements===
*Visitors - people that keep coming back over time.  Seasonal items have limited potential.  Hobbies etc allow constant communications.
*Product or service to sell
*Search volume to support high earnings potential
*Create high quality unique content
*Authoritative site, massive traffic.

===Niche Keywords===
Learning
*learn
*learning
*how to
*howto
*tutorial
*course
*book
Research what the market is searching for.

Choose 15 or so keywords that have a high search volume.

Use a wordpress site.
*Use 3-8 SEO pages
*Use 3-8 categories
Focus on a site with awesome content

Have the pages named with the keywords separated by a dash

People love videos, as does google.

Embed the video improves your SE ranking

Easy method for creating high quality unique content that people love (Rank and Pillage)
*Leverage YouTube.
**Find an actual video where someone talks about an expertise that they have.  Most of these videos are 7-8 minutes.
**Watch the video, take notes, and make bullet points.
**Make a 100 word description from the notes/bullet points from the video.  Embed the video and attach the notes.
*Offer value
*Make it easy to use

This leads to rankings, leverage, authority

Give people video.  By adding a video sitemap, this will add you into Google's results.

Add content to your website on a regular basis

Add links.
