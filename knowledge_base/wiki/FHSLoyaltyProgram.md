===Loyalty Program posted===
Members of our Loyalty Program receive 10% off of virtually all products (Registered Users price book).

Members of our Loyalty Program can receive additional discounts (Non Refundable Instore Credits) through our [[FHS Referral Program]].

Members of our Loyalty Program will receive special offers not available to the general public (offers in newsletter).

Members of our Loyalty Program will receive advanced notice of sales and promotions (newsletter).

 Loyalty program payments will be recorded as Discounts Given (4510 - Other Revenue/Discounts Given).
