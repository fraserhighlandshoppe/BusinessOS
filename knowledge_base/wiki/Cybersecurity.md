Cybersecurity Protection focuses on information and assets that are of business value.

Cyber Security involves establishing security controls (a.k.a. safeguards) to protect those assets from UA, modification, destruction, deletion, disruption or loss.

====IT Asset Management (ITAM)====
Creating an asset inventory helps track which IT assets you have, what they are connected to, who has access to them, which devices (phones/tables) and peripherals (printers, scanners, etc) may also have access and to what degree they are protected.

Knowing this helps prevent:
*Creating covert pathways for cyber criminals
*Providing UA to your systems
*Unmanaged vulnerabilities in your systems.

====Indications of a problem====
*If you get a change your password notification in Chrome (google searching exploit lists)
*Alerts from legitimate security tools (e.g. virus checker)
*You have posts on your SM that you didn't make (someone else has your login info)
*Messages you didn't send (could be email spoofing, could be a virus, could be someone has your credentials)
*Unknown login locations (Google will alert you to their services being logged in from somewhere unknown...)
*MFA prompts that you didn't trigger (the service wouldn't have sent the email/sms if the user/pass wasn't correct)
