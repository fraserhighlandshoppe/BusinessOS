 Sales processes are the steps a company's sales representative takes to find prospects 
 in the marketplace and make them customers. The definition of the sales process uses 
 varied terminology, but regularly includes identification of sales prospects, delivery 
 of company information to qualified prospects, assessment of prospect needs, presentation 
 of appropriate company products, negotiation of product sale, then finally closing the 
 sale. Some sales processes may also include steps for client followup to maintain 
 customer satisfaction and to generate client referrals.

==Customer Satisfaction Followup==
After XX days from the delivery of an item we will followup to ensure that everything is going well.  We will include
*A thank you for purchasing item XYZ
**A query whether the item is meeting their needs.  If not a note that we want to hear from them on how we can help make it meet their needs.
**A link to the product testimonial for product XYZ
*A link to our Customer Satisfaction survey
*A request for a testimonial.  If the customer had said something previously, we can pre-draft the testimonial and ask their permission to use it with their name.
