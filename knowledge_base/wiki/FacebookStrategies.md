Facebook is broadly used on mobile and desktop, at work and at home. It really depends on the audience as far as who and how they use Facebook.
==Facebook Strategies==
===Posts===
*make sure anything you repost is true.  Lots of fake news now on FB.
*Provide engaging content that fans want to share.
*most popular Social Media platform for info, photos
*it is easy to manage
*reaches large audience

Facebook has a half life of 90 minutes [https://blog.hootsuite.com/best-time-to-post-on-facebook-twitter-instagram/].  Facebook reaches 75% of its potential audience within 5 hours.

The best time to post on Facebook is 3:00 p.m. on Wednesday. Other optimal times include 12:00–1:00 p.m. on Saturdays and Sundays and 1:00–4:00 p.m. on Thursdays and Fridays.

Engagement rates are 18% higher on Thursdays and Fridays, and weekdays from 1:00–4:00 p.m. tend to see the highest clickthrough rates. On Fridays, Facebook use spikes by 10%. Since people tend to be happier on Fridays, Neil Patel suggests posting funny or upbeat content to match your audience's mood.

The worst times to post on Facebook are weekends before 8:00 a.m. and after 8:00 p.m, according to SurePayroll's research.

===Go Live===
Facebook wants you to go live.  By going live regularly you appear in more of your followers feeds.

Going Live gives you a virtual stage to sell from.

Make sure you have a Call To Action in the session.

===Direct Message===
DON'T SPAM.  No - "hey girl, how's your business?  Want to join my call?"

You need to build relationships.

Direct message people who show interest.  These are the people that like your posts or better yet comment on them.  Also reach out to anyone that attends your live sessions.

===Profile===
Feature your logo in your profile
Use a "gorgeous destination" photo (change once a month)
Complete all info boxes on page
Connect Call to Action to your website, not phone number.

30-45 second videos do really well
Share interesting articles, deals.  Remind why fans should deal with you.


Use great photo/vids and short copy
share customer pics (with permission) or ask them to tag your FB page in their posts
share supplier posts of interest to your clients
Keep page active.  Post at least 3 times a week.
Make sure your page represents your store.
Call to action.

Don't share dark photos or videos
Dont post more than 30 words with the photo.
make sure you have proper grammer, spelling

don't post about things that don't relate to what you sell
don't leave your potential audience hanging without a good site to click over from your page.

===Insights tab===
*learn about your fans demographics, geographics, interests
*lets you split test
*tells you when it is most active.
**preschedule posts to be there just before the normal busy times so that they are seeing it in their feeds.
