====Creating Effective Videos====
Most effective videos are the ones that answer the questions that people in your niche are asking. Identify what they want and then give them those answers.

Videos don't need to be long, and they don't need to take a long time to film. Quick and short - 1-2 minutes are best, but 3-8 is OK too. Can be screen captured power point slides. Don't have to be live action.

First 8 - 18 seconds of exposure is when people decide to continue or not. Intro tells the viewer what it is about, and allows them to decide if they are going to watch.

Steps to make the video:
*State the question. Short and sweet as an intro.
*Answer the question. Keep it simple. If it is complex break it up into multiple videos, provide URLs to blog posts, etc.
*Provide an offer. Make it cool, make it free. Learn more, get more, download more, etc. All they need to do is give them your email address (opt in).

How you state the offer is very important. Carefully craft the offer, film it separately and then add it onto the end of the topic video. This way you don't have to focus on it during the filming of the topic video, and you don't have to re-record it for multiple videos.

Why do this?
*Gets people to follow you
*Builds the opt-in list
*Sells more stuff.
*Gets you higher page rank
