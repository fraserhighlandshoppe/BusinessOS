===Distribution===
Also consider Website/Blogs/Podcast.  
*You could produce content for someone else's site.

On the SM, they are free to easy to distribute.

On someone elses site, you need to see what they are looking for.
*You need to become the person they want to collaborate with.
**You need to be a Subject Matter Expert so you have value to provide.
Eventually people will reach out to you once you have your brand developed.

===Produce Content===
What perception do you want people to have?

What value will you provide to your audience?

People aren't going to buy from people who they don't see as influencing.

Your content should be solving a problem.  It should be adding value
*Short
*Insightful
*An Action
*Has a purpose.
*Relevant
If they can just look it up online, you are less valuable.

If there are a lot of books, you could read the book and summarize.

Attend a conference, break it down into "snack-ables".

Most people aren't going to do the work (read the books/attend the seminars).

 It's unlikely you are trying to teach content that doesn't already exist.

Facilitate the content of the experts (books/seminars/etc).

People have excuses for their problems, and then don't take the action necessary to solve the problem.

You don't need to be miles ahead of someone to give them advice, you only need to be one chapter ahead.
*Summarize someone else's knowledge (make sure to credit them)
**Short videos
**Short articles
Once you've done enough of these, you will have the confidence to do your own.

The average person is consuming content on SM for about an hour a day.
*Allocate 5-10 minutes a day out of that to create, and watch the results!
