===Content===
*A Page About YOU. 
**what sets you and your company apart. What differentiates you as better than the next guy?
**What drives you? 
**Why do you XXX? 
**What do you love? 
**What are your interests? 
**What inspires you?
*A Professional Photo of You. 
**Potential clients want to see what you look like and what your style is. 
**They are much more likely to call you if they connect with you and your website. 
*High Quality Images. 
*A Blog. 
**A blog gives you a connection to your visitors. 
**tell them what they don’t know about your company 
**give them tips on how to use your services, 
**tell them about future deals, products, and new services. 
*Clear and Easy to Find Contact Information. 
**your phone number and email 
***Even if you have a contact form, list your email, business address, phone number and social media contact information on your site.
*Client Reviews or Testimonials. 
**There is no better marketing than word of mouth marketing and referrals. 
**Customer reviews and testimonials on your website offer proof that you have done this before (or done this many times) and have satisfied customers. 
**If  yelp.com, google.com or cityvoter.com have good reviews for your business, place a link to those sites on your own website.
*A focused site.  People should be able to tell quickly what it is about!
*A clear roadmap/next steps.  
**If they are filling out a form tell them how the info will be used and what happens next.
*Know your audience.  
**If you are an expert in the field but talk about stuff your audience doesn't care about, they won't connect.
**Customer avatars
**Write for your audience.  They are selfish.  If its about you and not them they will quit reading.
*Focus on a niche
**Trying to be everything to everyone means you do poorly for everyone.
*keyword-rich posts with persuasive, compelling titles that will be read, linked to and shared on social media sites.
*Good writing
**Spelling
**Grammer
**concise
**Easy to read.  Easy to understand
*don't spend all your time talking products.  Solve a problem.
*Engage and interact with your customers.
**Comment section on posts
***respond to comments.
