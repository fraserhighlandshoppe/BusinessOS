==Managing Conflicts of Interest==
*Identify Conflicts
*How can they occur
*Management and Best practices
*reference materials

Conflict of Interest is a conflict between the private interests and the official or professional responsibilities of a person in a position of trust.
*perception or reality of putting your interests before the interest of the client.
*When the advisor acts in a  way that influences the clients decision in the advisor's interest rather than the client's
*when the impartiality of the advisor is impacted
Examples
*not disclosing compensation structure
*creating an inducement to buy insurance
**e.g. a gift for purchasing a policy
*Becoming a POA, Executor or trustee for a client
*Entering into a debtor/creditor relationship with a client
*Acting in a way that would influence a client's decision
*Another occupation a.k.a. Outside Business Activity

===3 principals for managing===
#The interest of the consumer must be placed ahead of the advisor
#Actual and potential conflicts must be disclosed
#Recommended product must be suitable for the needs.
===Q's to ask yourself===
#Would your advice or product offered have been different if the situation incentive giving rise to potential conflict of interest did not exist?
#Would it appear to a reasonable  informed third party looking at all the facts that you acted in the best interest of your client?
===Examples of Outside Activities===
*Pastor/Elder
*Running for Office
*MF sales person
*School board trustee
*sales director in MLM
*AAA coach

CCIR expects all brokers to:
*take reasonable steps to identify and avoid or manage conflicts of interest.  Communicate these through appropriate policies and procedures
*Ensure customer understands.  Don't leave it up to the customer to determine if a conflict exists.
**Make sure you have a signature that they were advised and understand.
*avoid potential and actual conflicts of interest to ensure fair treatment of customer.

===Divorced/Divorcing Clients===
*Have each spouse sign a separate CoI form
*Have a commitment to privacy notice.

===Family as client and being their Executor===
*Can't sell products to estate (i.e. seg fund).
*Don't take actions that benefit executor.
*CAN sell to beneficiaries after estate is settled (i.e. sell seg funds to them once estate is closed).
