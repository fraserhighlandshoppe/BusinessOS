===Goal Setting===
Most people fail to achieve their goals because they are not setting [[SMART GOALS]].
*Specific
*Measurable
*Action (plan)
*Reason (emotional why)
*Timeframe
The goal is the intention behind scaling your brand.
===Systems and Structures===
Need to have the goal underlying the achievement.

A bigger goal will have a bigger set of systems.

Once you know your goal, you can start to identify the systems, organizations and structure to achieve it.

As you start out you end up doing everything, but that doesn't mean you should.

What are your highest income/impact skillsets?  

Create a system with a checklist so that others can do the other tasks.
===Professional Sales and Marketing===
Businesses that are struggling to scale have a problem with their marketing and sales.

You want a sub organization with their system (checklists) to reach and convert leads.
===Networking===
When doing things by yourself it can be lonely (lack of connection feeling).

Identify people that can help you AFTER they see how you can help them.

Bank example - make a deposit before you make a withdrawal.
===Delegate and Outsource===
It is important you don't wear all of the hats in your business.
*There isn't enough time for a scaled business
*You are a Jack of All Trades, not a specialist.
Delegate and outsource any tasks that you aren't a specialist at.

Can be as simple as having a friend do something.  Or maybe someone from your network.
