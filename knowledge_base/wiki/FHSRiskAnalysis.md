===Risk Analysis===

Risks need to be evaluated to ascertain their probability and severity. The risks may be enormously damaging with very high cost implications that may plunge the business into financial constraints. Similarly, an event may be low in cost liability but the rate at which it occurs may be too high that the long term cost implications is detrimental to the financial obligation of the business. Depending on the probability of uncertainties’ occurrence and the severity of the effects of risk, expressed in monetary terms defines the model of strategy to be applied.

Risk management strategies are avoidance, reduction, sharing and retention.

    Avoidance (eliminate, withdraw from or not become involved)
    Reduction (optimize – mitigate)
    Sharing (transfer – outsource or insure)
    Retention (accept and budget

====Not enough demand for products from customers to carry stock====
  
As we are still in the process of building a customer base, this risk manifests on all of our products.  We have some of almost every product we brought in during year 1.

For this risk, we have to retain the risk.  Otherwise we don't hold any product.

As we sell items, we can better judge the turn-over rate and therefore only bring in replacement product to ensure our turn-over is covered.

====difficulty acquiring product (suppliers out of stock or slow to replenish)====
Even after three years, this is a real issue.  Suppliers don't always carry product in stock, and only make them when there is known demand.

Reduction strategy is to order product with lots of lead time.  

Avoidance Strategy means looking for alternative suppliers for equivalent products.

====Difficuly acquiring accounts with suppliers.====
Many suppliers are slow to respond to initial contact.

A reduction/sharing strategy would be to be referred by one of thier existing contacts.

Avoidance would be to not seek further suppliers.

Reduction would be to leave lots of lead time, and continue to follow up.

====Competition====
See our Compeition analysis at [[FHS Competition]].  We have identified areas where we have a weakness, as well as some opportunities.

We can avoid competition by having a very strong USP.

We can reduce competition through pricing, customer service, USP.

We want to avoid retaining competition.

We want to minimize sharing with the competition.

NOTE - our competition doesn't appear to have the technical where-with-all so we could out-tech and out-Social Media them with sufficient effort.  However, is that the best use of our time?
