==Living Benefits on a Budget==
 If loss of income for 4 or 6 months would devestate your family, what would happen if it lasted for 2 years, or until age 65?

===Top Up Benefits===
*Company group LTD has a $3000 non evidence maximum
*often consider only high income earners (100k+)
what about the other employees?

Average employee:
*35 yrs old
*60k income
*how much DI does he qualify for?
**Current - 3000
**Proposed - 3500 + Future Income Option (FIO) Rider
**Premium - 16$/month

FIO
*opportunity to increase benefits as income rises
*Opportunity to replace LTD (ie change in carear)
*Locks in insurability and occupation class
*Opp to increase benefit while on claim
*Built in reason to review yearly, and add revenue with each option exercised.
