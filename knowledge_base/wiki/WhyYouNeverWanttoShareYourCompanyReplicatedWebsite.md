==Why You Never Want to Share Your Company Replicated Website==
Company replicated websites never work.  Here's what to do instead!

Amateurs post on SM with the CTA sending them to the company site.
*Every agents' site is identical with minor changes (e.g. picture).
**What differentiates you as better than the next guy?
**company site is sales site (list of products and how to join)
***people then google, find negative...
*no way to tell who clicked
**how do you then followup?
***what grabbed their attention
***what did they like
*People join people, not companies.  So going to company site removes you out of the equation, so people don't join!
*Need Lead Magnet (opt in form)
**People clicked on your CTA because they are interested, but without the lead magnet, then what?  Fortune is in the followup!
