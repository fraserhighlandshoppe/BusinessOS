==FAQ++
===Can anyone become expert===
Yes.  Go out, get results, and publicize them.  They don't have to be super spectacular, just better than the people seeking info.

===What are the fundamentals that need to be done?===
Different for each business focus/
===Why would someone buy from me===
You are unique.  People  buy from people they believe and trust.  Get in front of them, and they may buy from you.
They buy because of free content that is valuable to them.  

===What if I don't have a product or knowledge===
If you don't have it - create it.  If you don't know it - learn it.  It isn't the resources available but the resourcefulness of the businessman.

===What is the sales psychology?===
Sales Psychology - add lots of content and value.  Then later offer something for sale.

Pain marketing (Kennedy) is great for the bootcamp trainers from the 80s.  Might do well for a short while (get attention) but won't build a brand.

What is your audience's true ambitions.  Help them along that struggle.  Help them move closer to the dream.

Challenge your audience.  Encourage ambition.


===Which product should I release first?===
There is no "right" way.  Choose the one for you that will engage you.  The one that excites you.  However, master this one before moving onto the next one.  Focus for 18 months on this.

===What actions do I need to take?===
*Blog weekly.  
*Continous campaigning.
*Regular releases of products

==The 10 essentials==
===Subject matter===
what are you already an expert in, or what can you become one in?

What do you love to do?  What are your passions?  What do you like to excel at.  What do you love to preach (soapboxing values to others etc).

What are you going to OWN online?  What niche will you become known for.

What do you really like to make an income from?

===Stories===
this is what differentiates one brand from another.  We all have (access to) the same tools and technologies.  Your thoughtfullness in developing stories is what will make you stand out.

You need a story that explains your credibility.  Results story - what results have you acheived.  Researcher/Reporter story - go out and interview the experts in the space.  Even better is to combine the two.

Role model story - the person that did the research and then got results.  

Another story you need is your connection to your audience.  They need to see that you have seen the same struggles and challenges or ones similar enough that you can understand and empasize.
