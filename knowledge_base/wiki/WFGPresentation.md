If you aren't asking for referrals you are losing money!


==Preframe==
#Think about current mindset about referrals.
#Consider what I just told myself is incorrect.
#What if this is wrong - what if I told myself a story, and it is impeding my business growth and hurting team too.

5-4-3-2-1

Important to preframe presentation so that you don't surprise client.  It also prepares them to generate the list.

Share mission statement "No Family Left Behind".  Means we need to see more people, and to do that we need referrals.

Hey, Mr X, you wouldn't want to break the tradition of this...right?

Before we get started I'd like to thank the both of you.  This meeting will provide great training for __TRAINEE__.
We are excited to share these strategies with you.
100% of people who see our presentation will either get value out of it, or know friehnds or familty who will.
At the end of the meeting we will be asking for a list of 10-15 people who need to see this information.  The referrals you share with us is the reason we are making a HUGE impact.  Everyone does the best they can with sharing with their friends and family.  Does this sound fair?  <nod and smile> - they will say yes.

Once people trust you, they will willingly give over their referrals.

"This is a long time relationship, not a one time transaction.  I will be here for you forever, as long as I am in business, and that is going to be a very long time".

Do you think most people have access to multi-million dollar fund management firms?  <wait for answer, acknowledge for what they say>

Do you see how 91% of canadians need to see this?

If you haven't seen this before, do you think your firends and family know this material?
This is why referrals are important.

What 3 areas of these 5 are your favourite?  Do you think your friends and family are using them?

Where do you think they are going for financial help or education?
*google is a "search" engine not a research engine.

==ask==
the moment you chicken out is when you need to go 5-4-3-2-1 and then ask.

Hey, wasn't that great information?  Now, I can assume you saw value in it?  Great, can you jot down 10-15 people that could use seeing it as well?

<hand over paper and pen with 1-15 written>
*They need to hold the pen and paper to take control and responsibility.
*let them write, and take time.
*don't use tentative language.  
**(i.e. if you could)
**I need you to jot down 10-15 people
**who do you know (not do you know)
***who do you know that is in sales
***has business background
