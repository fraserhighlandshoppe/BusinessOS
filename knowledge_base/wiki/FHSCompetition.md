The Scottish Shoppe is an established businesses.

They operate Tuesday to Saturday, 10-17.

They have a website.  Their online store only has practice swords.

They have a facebook page.  They have a twitter account.  They have a number of newsletters.  Owner has 2 linkedin pages.

Word on the street is that they are a high priced supplier.

There may be an opportunity here to compete on price, product, availability.

They offer Band Kit Lockers.

They offer discounted drycleaning to band.

They offer kilt/outfit rentals.

The Scottish Shoppe is an established businesses (40 years).  They bring in Scottish, Irish and English import items.

They have a website and facebook page.

Open 10-5.

Free delivery on items purchased online within Calgary city limits.

The Scottish Shoppe supplies mainly the clothing side and British imports.

They do not have a large piping supplies section.

As we bring in more novelty items, we are starting to compete more with them.

The Celtic Pipe Line is an established business.

They have an established customer base.

They are a part time business run out of their home.

They have long term ties into the piping community (Father well known).

They are not going to games that are farther away.

They have piping and drumming supplies, but not dance, and not a lot of novelty.

They don't keep a lot of things in stock.

They appear to have a better Social Media (Facebook at least) presence then we do.

The Highland Shoppe is lacking in their customer service.  Multiple customers have made note of this to us.

Bill and Jeanie are very well known within the piping community.  They are grade 1 pipers.

They don't keep piping supplies in stock.  Their main business appears to be rentals and novelties.

They don't appear to attend games anymore.  The Used trade is an opportunity they have given up here.

Apparently Scott's has a bit of a reputation for screwing over their (pipe band) business partners through book and CD publishing in the past.

Scotts is not only our competitor but they are also our supplier.

Their product inventory level strategy is more along the lines of a retailer than wholesaler - they don't do well keeping things in stock.

Their pricing is wholesale for items they produce (Piper's Choice) but halfway for anything they are a middleman for.

We have established contacts with many of their suppliers.  We could do something similar.  Is there a way to become listed as official distributors with these manufacturers?
