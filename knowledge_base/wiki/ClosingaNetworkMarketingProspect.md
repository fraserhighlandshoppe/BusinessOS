==How To Close Your Prospects by Eric Worre==
 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCh9p4BL1MU

At the end of the presentation, if you are just pitching, giving only facts and not asking questions, you will have trouble.

Ask the following questions:
#If you were to get involved on a part time basis, how much would you need to earn per month to make it worth while?
##Don't oversell or undersell by setting your number rather than theirs.
#How many hours per week are you willing to dedicate to build this business in order to earn this amount per month?
#How many months are you willing to put in these hours to get to this income?
##If they are willing to only put in 2 hours a week for 2 months and then expect 10k/month, is this reasonable?  If not you need to reset their expectations (I don't know we can get you there in that time frame with those hours).
#If I can show you how to make that X/month working Y hours a week over the next Z months, is there anything else you need to know to get started?

People might surprise you; you don't know the conversations they've been having lately about their situation and the need for more income.
