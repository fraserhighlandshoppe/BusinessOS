==How is life calling me to serve==

Life is calling, but you have to be living in the inquiry to recognize the calling.  Need to say YES when opportunities arise.

Don't have to help everyone; There will be a specific group with a specific way to help them.

The universe knows who you are.

==Pain Body==
You are probably already getting the calling, but your pain body and ego are in the way.  They will be telling you to not change, that your comfort space is comfortable whereas meeting the calling will require growth.


Your pain body is conditioning you've absorbed over time.  

The pain body:
*Fear 
*Regret
*Doubt
*Worry
*Greed for power

Need to jump in, even if only part time at the start.

Need to add value to people's lives.  Do so by continually learning and evolving, and sharing with your tribe.

Things change, people's needs change.  How can I add value to people's lives?

Discern what the monetary value of your service is, and you will be able to set your rates and therefore maximize your income.  By charging good value, you aren't doing it only for you.  When the client writes the cheque, it encourages them to use the material/knowledge and spend the time to make the investment worthwhile.  So don't be the cheapest rate.

==Leadership==
Become an expert in inspiring your tribe.

==Inner Purpose==
The same with everyone on the purpose - to awaken and continue to grow.  Dissolve the pain body to harness the energy of the universe.
==Outer Purpose==
Unique to every purpose and you need to discover it.  Often starts with a flash of inspiration.  Your outer purpose will change over time - as you grow, your target audience and how they need help will evolve.

Meditation is what allows you to grow through the pain body.

Enthusiasm:  Joy combined with a goal.
*Make sure your goals are dynamic.  Point towards an activity through which you are connected to others and the whole.  See how you can enrich other's lives.
