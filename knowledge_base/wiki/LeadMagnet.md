The Lead Magnet, which you are giving away to your viewer in exchange for their email, should be FREEMIUM as opposed to free and cheap crap.  They will remember their FIRST and their MOST RECENT interactions with you, so if the gift is crap, that is the tone of the relationship.  OTOH, if it is valuable...

===Lead Magnet===
 The lead magnet is about offering what is in demand, not what is convenient.  For ideas, look through:
 *FAQs.
 *Testimonials from your customers
 *Fun/asked for activities.
 *What are the most talk about among your clients about your program and would love to replicate?

(Chunk of Tripwire)

A Lead Magnet is a small chunk of value that solves a specific problem for a specific market that is offered in exchange for an opt-in.

The Lead Magnet must have value, as you are exchanging value.  They are giving you their time and attention.

====Lead Magnet Checklist====
 To be effective, your lead magnet needs to be targeted at a specific [[Customer Avatar Template|Customer Avatar]]
*ultra specific solution to ultra-specific market
**a 14 day course or 300 page e-book won't be specific
*promises one big thing as opposed to many little ones
*speaks to a known desired end result
*offers immediate gratification (no newsletters, podcasts)
**a 14 day course or 300 page e-book isn't immediate
*moves prospects down a continuum of belief 
**what happened, not how it happened (how would be the tripwire)
**what shows proof of end result happening
*Shifts the relationship (pre-frame so they are open to do business)
*High percieved value
*high actual value (an aha moment)
*can be easily consumed (5 minutes or less)
**a 14 day course or 300 page e-book won't be specific
*Immediate Gratification (an answer or solution right now)

====Types of Lead Magnets====
*case study (tell the story, demonstrate proof, show the what not the how).
*toolkit or resource list (people love lists)
*Answer their #1 question (less is more - 6 or so paragraphs.  easy to consume)
*reports or guides (over used, so become under valued)
*free software download/trial
*software trial/plugin
*assessment/test (learn the #1 reason you are not...)
*quiz/survey 
*cheat sheet/handout
**1 page.  Ultra specific.  Checklist/mindmap/blueprint
**If using an image of the cheatsheet, blur out the text to create curiosity
*templates
*ebook
*video training
**Useful for demonstrating a skillset.
*discount/free shipping
*Blind/sales material
**This is where the most desired piece is pricing or product description
*Step one of order form

====Specificity====
Lead Magnets need to not be boring, boiled chicken.

Because Lead Magnets are the entry point, it needs to grab the prospect's attention.

[[Image: Specificity in Lead Magnets.png]]

Names and phrases matter.  Speak to a specific desire.

The same book, republished with a new title!

[[Image: Naming Lead Magnets.png]]

Also, offer 1 report, not 6.  Your opt-ins will go way up.
