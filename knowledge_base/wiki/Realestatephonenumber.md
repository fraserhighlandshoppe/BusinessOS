You will probably want a dedicated phone number for your Real Estate business.  There are any number of reasons:
*You can treat it as a priority number (i.e. check its messages first)
*You will know why it is ringing.
*You aren't sharing the number with all other caller's reasons

Having the dedicated number, you want to ensure EVERYONE that answers the number knows how to respond to calls on it.  For example, children and babysitters make poor sales people.

If you can't answer the phone, make sure there is an answering machine on it.

You can get a DID number from voip.ms for as low as $1.99/month + 1.5c/minute in Canada. (Dec 2013).  It is a SIP or IAX connection so you will need some VOIP gear.  Otherwise you need a phone line from another reseller like Telus/Rogers at their $30/month...
