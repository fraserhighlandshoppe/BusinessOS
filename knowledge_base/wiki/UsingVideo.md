==Using Video to capture traffic==
*Resolution: 1280x720
*Codecs: h264 mp4
*Video Bitrate: 1500kb/s video
*Audio Bitrate: 128kb/s audio

If self hosting, 750kb + 64kb

To distribute your video to multiple sites at once, consider traffic geyser.

==Creating the video==
The video doesn't need to be complicated.  It can be as simple as a series of slides, with a good voice over and an exciting soundtrack.  

===Must have distinction===
Your signal must be distinctive.

===Connection===
Buddy rule - all about 1 on 1 engagement.
Talk to your "buddy"
*He is in pain
*Wants the most pleasure to replace the pain
*Is sceptical, so wants to know why

Ownership experience - this is what you show, tell in the marketing
*How does this reduce my pain, increase my pleasure
*How does this replace my scepicism
*Show results - show their life improved.  Not just the greater pleasure.
Tell a story of progression of how life keeps getting better through use.

authenticity is about beauty scars.  The more flaws, the lower the trepidation.  Vacuum cleaning commercial - do you prefer "Hello I am so and so and lets talk about suction loss" or a more personal approach with the ums, ahs, oops, etc.

===Story telling===
*Tell a story about an adventure.  Think reality tv (MTV Jershey shore, etc).
*Most people don't know what success is.  Does your message define what success is, show the viewer?

The only thing that matters during the journey is the fight.  We don't want the movie to end if we are experiencing the journey.  It has nothing to do with the win, only the fight.  We want to go through the experience.  It is almost as fulfilling to watch someone else go through the experience than us,
*quickstart guides - takes the user on a quick journey.

Eliminate the questions, trepidation, by taking people on the journey, then the sale is more likely.


===Voice Over===
To improve the voice over, you need to position the mic correctly.  The positioning of the mic is more important than the cost of the mic.  This will take some trial and error.  Generally you want the mic to be in line with your jaw - it is shaped similar to a tuning fork and resonates.  You may find that having the mic pointing above your mouth produces a better sound.  

===Exciting Soundtrack===
When trying to find the right soundtrack, you will probably need to "audition" many.  There are many places you can purchase royalty free audio.  When choosing the soundtrack, ensure that you are looking at the video that you will be matching the audio to.  Only when the audio makes your hairs rise when watching the video and listening to that audio, you have found the right audio track.  Trust your gut.
