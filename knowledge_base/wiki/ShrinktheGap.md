===Nobody Knows you===
Your financial success is proportional to how many people know, like and trust you!
*1000 know
*100 like
*10 trust
**1% give you money (buy).

To build trust takes a long time.

To get people to know you, all you have to do is run an ad.

Getting more people to know you increases the number of people who like, and then trust you.

Get know by the right people, for the right things.

Mere Exposure Effect
*People tend to develop a preference for things they are familiar with

Dunbar Top 150 Theory
*We can only remember 150 people / brands / products / places / ...

If your product or service helps people and solves a problem, you get the sale.
