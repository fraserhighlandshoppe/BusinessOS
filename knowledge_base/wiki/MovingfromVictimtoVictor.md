As an individual and small business owner, you can choose to remain where you are and be dissatisfied with your current marketing efforts or you can choose to intentionally create the results you want.

You can choose to dwell on your previous poor decisions that have led you to where you are now today in your business.

You can choose to worry about present and future events and how you think you have no influence or control over them….

…or you can choose now today to reclaim your personal power and choose to create the future marketing results you want to experience.

Unfortunately, many people are dissatisfied with the results of their marketing. All they can do is remind themselves of their lack of leads, signups, sales and referrals, and how empty their pipeline is and are worried how they can cover the overheads and make progress.

They’re not happy. They have fleeting moments of wanting to change, but don’t.

They remain the same – day in, day out. Miserable because they're not practicing "Quantum Marketing" principles in the promotion of their products and services.

I want to share a new “take” on marketing, a new paradigm if you will, and it starts in wanting you to take control of your future. It starts with the realisation that you must choose whether you want to move from a “victim” mentality to “victor” mentality and accepting responsibility for your future and your destiny in becoming selfless towards focussing on others instead of yourself.

Once the transition is made away from victim consciousness toward the acknowledgement of personal power through alignment with the originating expansive flow of creative energies, the thirst for greater understanding will be aroused, and the engine of desire will be ignited with a passion for wanting to share one’s skills, talents and expertise with others….

…and to benefit greatly from the returns in such a change of focus.

It’s the small quantum changes that can have the greatest impact creating permanent breakthroughs.

Unfortunately for the majority of the populous, they see the fake tans, the artificially whitened teeth of others living a lifestyle that they themselves are not, and they feel that there is an in-adequacy in themselves and that they too could do better to enhance their current personal, professional or financial situation.

What exactly, they don’t know for they remain in a situation where they feel under-valued or un-appreciated and can’t see a way out or an alternative to their current situation in order to generate the results they want.

Many yearn and search for answers in what to do or how they could better themselves and quickly turn to their smart phone, tablet of pc for answers. They recognise that they’re not happy with their current situation and feel they want to change, but none of the celebrity gossip websites, linkedin groups or facebook updates that they subscribe to, are giving them the answers they want.

The misunderstanding of looking outward for answers is deeply ingrained in society as we have been shaped and moulded by the media, the government even our parents and peers as we have followed the crowd instead of breaking free from the “rat race” and instead of following and copying everyone else – we must choose to go inward and identify the skills, talents, experience and expertise we already have and who we can share these with, in return for the value that they put on them. In other words, it’s not just your immediate upline manager or boss that could benefit from what you have to offer – is it?

It’s time for change. Instead of wondering how much you can get, what extras you can receive, what allowances, perks and benefits you can get from your existing employer or government. It’s time to stop and think and consider what it is you have to offer the world at large, and more specifically - a hungry, target audience.

It’s time to choose to become a victor instead of a victim.

It’s time to choose to reclaim your personal power and live the life and lifestyle you want by changing your approach to your marketing.

Making a decision to change is one thing.Acting on that decision is a completely different thing all together:

    recognise where you are in your life today and that Quantum Marketing beckons

    relate the principles of “Quantum Marketing” to your own circumstances

    assimilate what I share in the forthcoming pages.

    apply what I have to share too.

In order to lead a more productive and results driven approach to your marketing, the focus and emphasis needs to change from yourself to that of others. It’s not just about your appearance, your status or deciding what your next car will be. It needs to a more holistic approach to finding and creating a solution to better your current situation by helping others with the knowledge, skills, talent and solutions you already have.

It’s time to choose to change your focus from you to others.

Unlike many others, It’s time to choose to start being selfless instead of selfish.

Quantum Marketing is all about giving up your victim status and pursuing your dreams with the intention of manifesting them into reality by sharing your skills, talents and expertise.

Quantum Marketing is a personal decision, and not necessarily a choice for everyone.

Quantum Marketing is not an easy process, for there will be issues, challenges and obstacles to overcome.

Quantum Marketing is about personal growth and how we learn to respond instead of reacting to personal, professional and commercial situations – responsibly.

Quantum Marketing is not just about generating the marketing results you want. It's also about identifying what's holding you back and preventing you from achieving your marketing goals and objectives.

Quantum Marketingis about small business owners becoming what it is they really want to be and growing personally, professionally and commercially.

Quantum Marketing is about helping small business owners living their life purpose in abundance, but not necessarily – luxury.

Quantum Marketingdoes not mean that each must become a millionaire to prove they have accomplished this.

Small Business Owners tend to find that abundance is measured by the inner feelings of self-approval and confidence that precludes the necessity of impressive material demonstrations for ego aggrandizement.

In other words, what you do with regard to self and others is more important than what you have.

To choose to become, is the goal of all Quantum Marketers. Remaining as is, is not an option.

Choose to become what you really want.

Each Small Business Owners choices are different as the journey with Quantum Marketing is different and unique; experienced differently; for each individual as their pursue their marketing goals and objectives.

The issues, challenges and obstacles are different as are the way the individual chooses to respond to each.

Help is available for each individual for those who want to stay focussed, on their path and to develop their Quantum Marketing skills, attitudes and talent.

The choices each individual makes are their own.

There are no mistakes other than to remain closed to seemingly new concepts and continue to react to and repeat the current experience instead of choosing to respond by intentionally creating the next step in the Quantum Marketing journey, even if that means accepting help from unexpected sources.

Knowledge + Experience = Wisdom.

Quite often we have to experience the current experience so we can learn from it, and apply the learning to ensure we don’t repeat the same lesson in our future marketing campaigns.

This develops wisdom and it is wisdom which is one of the major tools in the tool box of the Quantum marketer. Remember, wisdom comes from making choices and experiencing the results of those choices.

Do you want to remain where you are, reminded by the emotions you are experiencing on a daily basis and by the closed perceptions and opinions of those around you?

Do you want to apply what you’ve already learned with the wisdom you’ve already acquired and choose to take the first steps towards Quantum Marketing and start living the life and lifestyle you really want to create?

It’s your business. You’re in control. You make the decisions. No-one else.

    Choose today to take responsibility for the life and lifestyle you want to create.
    Choose today to become a victor and not a victim.
    Choose today to help others with the knowledge, skills & solutions you already have.
    Choose today to start living an abundant life and lifestyle by moving from being selfish to selfless…

…with your marketing.
