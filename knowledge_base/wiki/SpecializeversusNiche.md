==Specialist vs niche==
Anyone can take a course on something and become a specialist.
A niche is more along your passion.

Better to get into the niche.  If trying to sell the world you get
tired/frustrated because trying to do too much.

You can develop your niche because of your experience and education.  If
starting on own you can pick your clients and tightly focus.  But it is
your passion that makes you good at the selling.

You don't have to be part of the niche, but you have to appreciate/like
the group.  An anti-wedding person will have difficulty selling
destination wedding packages.

Part of the sales job is getting to know well the client so you can meet
their needs

What connections do we have through our suppliers that can get bonuses
or perks for the customers?

Evolve the niche - travel club/sock of the day/...  clients can bring in
the new idea that you can make happen and then expand.

Search the hashtags for the niche you want to hit.  Then start
networking to the people that are commenting/liking/posting with these tags

Posting on SM will help you gain authority as an expert.  And it will
start the conversation when someone contacts you about what you posted.

Instangram is the largest growing SM, especially now owned by facebook.
