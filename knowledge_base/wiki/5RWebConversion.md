Daniel Levis 5R Conversion System

Each subscriber you turn into a customer is worth hundreds of dollars

The average website collects 5-10% of visitors

1000 visitors -> 50/100 opt-ins -> 1-2 sales.

==Right person==
You need to be perceived as being the right person doing the communicating.  Don’t want to look like a corporate brochure because that gives the appearance of a faceless entity.

*Use images of yourself strategically on the page
*Dressed for the target market
*Positioned to the left of the target text
*Clear identification (address, phone, etc)
*Personable communication
*Focus on the needs and desires of your audience
What’s really dribing you to do this.  If you don’t understand your mission beyond money, you are just a pretender chasing the real contenders.

Selling is about leadership

People buy from people they feel they know, like and trust.

If everyone agrees with you you obviously stand for nothing and your destiny is ignored.  Formulate a core story that tells people who you are and why you’re here.

==Right message==
#Simple, easilty understood ultra-compelling promise
#Prove that your product will fulfill the promise
#Call to action
Promise is the move away from pain towards pleasure.  Describe where things were, feelings etc.  What are they going to get from you.  Proof will include things like guarantee, etc.  Big request needs big proof.  You can have too much proof.

One page, one purpose.  If people have lots of choices they tend to do nothing.  Is your opt in box competing with a lot of content?

Keep the page down to 3-5 well researched points.  Keep opt in above the fold.

Need to test promise, pain, offer, etc.  Easy to test on shorter pages.


==Right Audience==
Process of gathering customer intelligence and converting it into your marketing.
Need to understand the past buying behaviour of your target audience
*Who am I really communicating with?
**To verify these people are the right audience for the product
**Having verified working in the promising pond, discover the right bait.
Collect feedback on your opt in form.
*Pain points – problem they are trying to solve
*Aspirational statements – what they want to be
*questions and objections
Capture the language used by your market
Categorize the statements
Quantify the prevalence of the problem, aspiration or question really is.
⇒	Quality Sentiment.  Passion and emotionally charged interest.
Dominant emotions and beliefs
Awareness and sophistication
Objections likely to be

Exit surveys – offer a freebie to discover why they aren’t buying

Looking for answers to the 17 key questions, after which everything falls into place.
#what questions does the audience have about products like yours
#What are their top three immediate goals (near term) which the product can help
#what are their dreams (long term) for the future
#what objections to purchasing
#what other options do they have
…
Take the answers to these questions and move to the right message step.

==Right Time==
Example path:
Banner ad -> sales page -> shopping cart.
Define what the thought, feeling and action is for each of these steps.

Banner ->optin->followup->sales page-> shopping cart

IS EACH BOX NEEDED?  Is the page confusing the issue?
*stimulate curiosity
*arouse interest
*inspire belief
Will your target audience accept all of the proof statements, answers etc?

What do the prospects believe right now about products like mine?

What do they need to believe about the products like mine in order to buy now?

Are the facts, arguments, benefits etc in the right order?  Can I eliminate redundant steps
*Am I convincing the audience to click?

==Right Way==
Sales Killers
*Boredom
*Confusion
*Disbelief

Let me tell you a story – captivates the audience’s attention.  Wrap the facts etc in the emotions in a story.

===Hero’s Journey===
#1st person story.  You the seller want something.  
#You have difficulty getting it.  
#Conflicting forces stand in your way.  
#You stumble on a magic sword.  
#You vanquish the enemy to obtain your desire.  
#You share the secret of your success with the world.

The viewer needs to be able to put themselves into your story.  The more they bond with your character in the story, the more sales you will make.

==Tweaks to your business==
#Begin with the end in mind
##A single product does not make a business
##Dumb marketers make the initial sale to make profit;  Smart marketers make the initial sale to build a client list. (Sales Funnel)
###Add surveys to determine what your customers need/desire
##Source those items directly or through affiliates
#Create an aggresive follow-up system
##How many people buy on the first visit? 1%?
##How many of these initial sales become repeat business?
##Tackle one sales objective ONLY on a page
###Collect contact info on one page, sell items on another.
##If you send valuable info/freebies, you can continue to sell to your contacts for years
#Jazz up that offer
##Show the prospect that the money is far more valuable by giving it to you rather than them keeping it.
###Magnify perceived value
###Add a bunch of free stuff.
###Do this after you reveal the price, not before.
###Establish the price for the core product
###Present the free stuff (regularly X dollars, for 0)
###Buy one, get one free outsells 2 for the price of 1.
##The fear of loss trumps the prospect of gain
###Limited time offer
###Only so many slots/products
##Guarantees - guarantee that the customer will be happy
###Use the guarantee as proof that it works, or you would go broke
###Reverse the risk - double your money back etc.
##After sale product enhancements
###Offer a complimentary product in the sale confirmation email
###The best time to make the next sale is immediately after the first sale.
#Prepondence of Proof
##Build more proof elements into your offer.
##Giving away a sample.
##Plain talk sounds like the truth.  Short words, short sentences.
##Establish how to buy before presenting the product
###Make them feel they are being educated rather than sold
##Likeable is more credible.
###Show having the same beliefs, goals, etc
##Authoritative quotes
###Cite expert opinions.  Expert credibility rubs off.
#Close strong with pride.  Make the close more explicit and emphatic.
##Few people want to have the stigma of asking people to buy
##If you believe in your product and are honestly portraying its attributes, close strong.
##Summarize the benefits
##Outline the cost of inaction
##Tell them what to do next (Take out your credit card)
