==RESP Refresher==
*50,000 lifetime max contribution per subscriber
Growth is tax sheltered (deferred).
*Idea is the beneficiary is low income and pays the taxes on the growth.  
*May be beneficial to lump sum the 50k at birth rather than limit to 2500/year to get the CESG.
There are some restrictions in the last couple years of RESP (i.e. ages 16-17 )

Catch up room
===RESP Account Types===
*Individual RESP
**Only 1 beneficiary (e.g. neice/nephew/god child/neighbour)
**Anyone can be the owner
*Family RESP
**Beneficiaries are siblings
**owner is parent or grandparent
All RESPs require permission of the primary caregiver.  (So a Grandparent can't open one as a surprise).

The person who receives the CCB is designated as the Primary Caregiver according to the Government.

RESPs can be transferred.  They can also be converted from an Individual Plan to a Family.

You can add and remove subscriber (beneficiary) to the Family plans.
===Ownership===
*Individual Owner
*Joint Owner

===Family RESP===
Family RESP is a pooled plan.  Some of the grant money (CESG), instead of being lost, can still be used for one of the siblings if one of the beneficiaries doesn't go to school.

==Canadian Education Savings Grant==
*Gov't matches 20%.
**Government does the matching on the investment from the end of the month, so match is deposited next month.
*BC, QC has additional grants.
**AB and SK used to have a grant as well.
*30% or 40% matching for low income families. (Cutoffs change yearly)
*Canada Learning Bond for low income families.
*Lifetime maximum grant is $7200.
**Max $500/year.
No grant paid in the year the child turns 18.

At 16 and 17, won't receive grant unless
*100 was contributed in four prior years, OR
*At least 1000 was contributed in prior year.

===Notes===
*Beneficiary must be enrolled in post secondary education.  Trade school in Canada, or University in Canada or abroad.
*Capital returns to the Subscriber.  Can be tricky if other people contributed.

If you get the Grant and Growth out first, the parents can withdraw the capital at any time without taxes, proof of enrollment, etc.  So some benefit to get the Grant and Growth out first.

===Doesn't go to school===
Grants go back to government.
*Other growth can go into RRSP of parent.  OR
*Growth becomes taxable income to owner.  10% Penalty tax on top.

Grant and Growth in a family plan can be used for the other children, but there is a max of $7200 grant that can be withdrawn per child.
