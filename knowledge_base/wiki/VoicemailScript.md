 Before calling, you should have a goal for the call.  If it goes to voicemail, you still need to act towards that goal!

 For the call, you should be standing to increase your energy.

 As you are dialing, smile.  Changes your tone and energy.

 *Practice the script.  Try to stick to it.  When you go off script you talk yourself out of the meeting.
 *You only get the one chance to leave a good first impression.  Words, Tone and Body Language make up most of our communication.
 **The words themselves almost don't matter - you can't say the wrong thing to the absolute right person
 *
==Voicemail==
 Don't leave the script on voicemail.  Ask for them to call you back.
*On average, cold-calls result in a voicemail over 90% of the time.
*Often, your first contact with a prospect will come via voicemail or email. 
**Make sure to remain upbeat in your messaging, 
*keep it short, sweet, and to the point. 
*you need to capture the prospect’s interest in the voicemail
**the goal isn’t to sell in your message, but to pique their curiosity enough to call you back.

"As I promised to followup, here is my call. Please call me back"

===How to Leave Voicemail That Gets Your Prospect’s Attention===
#Decide what you want your prospect to know after they hear your message. 
##Is it that you’ve been recommended by someone they know? 
##That you can help them solve a problem you know they face? 
##Or maybe you just want them to know who you are. 
#Choose just one message to deliver to avoid leaving rambling, long voicemails. 
#Write your scripts. 
##You should have a few scripts ready, each of which communicates a different, specific message. 
###“I come highly recommended by somebody you trust.”
###“I understand the challenges you face, and I believe I can help.”
###“I have solved the problems you face for others, and I believe I can do it for you.”
###“I have something urgent to speak with you about”
#Leave your messages.
##DO keep it under 20 seconds.
##DO use an upbeat, friendly, and energetic tone of voice.
##DO insert some urgency.
##DO use your prospect’s name and your name.
##DO NOT try to sell in the voicemail.
##DO NOT sound desperate by telling them about a deal, special offer, or discount.
##DO NOT leave a voicemail only once.
#Track which scripts work best. Do some testing and continue to improve them!
