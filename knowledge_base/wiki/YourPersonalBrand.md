===Your Personal Brand===
We are exposed to different brands every day of our lives.  A Personal Brand is a personal identity that stimulates a meaningful emotional response in another person or audience about the qualities or values for which we stand.
*gut feeling (first impression and/or long term)
*interactions + feelings

Like it or not, others assign us with a personal brand.  Your brand is in evidence every time you interact with other people—internal
and external.  Development of a personal brand requires asking important questions about ourselves and how others perceive us.  And why do they perceive us like that?
*Competence + Appearance/Personal Presence + Communication => Personal Brand
**Competence
***Knowledge
***Critical Thinking
**Appearance
***Dress
***Posture
***Gestures
***Facial Expressions
**Communication
***Voice Projection
***Accent
***Vocabulary
***Grammer and Sentence Structure.

====Reputation is a pyramid====
Positive interactions + consistency leads to Credibility.  Credibility and Trust lead to loyalty.
====Promote yourself not the product====
*look at what you are posting.  Don't artificially limit yourself.
**you want to be able to attract a larger audience.  If you only post 1 product/line then that is all you will be called about.
*continue to foster existing relationships via Socail Media
*Your SM channels (business) is about the ideal client, not about your business or product.

Remember that your clients aren't dealing with your store; they are dealing with you.  
*They will be loyal with you if you keep your brand promise
*your client trusts and values you
*Your biggest asset is YOU.  You are priceless.

The special role we play is not the (commodity/product/service) transaction.  It is the interaction with the client.  It is the network we have in the background with which we can bring benefits to the client.  We need to maximize the VALUE we bring to the customer.  We get paid for the Value; Our customers exchange value with us - they give us what they value our product/service with in cash which we can then use to purchase value from others.
