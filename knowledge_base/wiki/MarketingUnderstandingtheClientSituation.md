==Our Understanding of the Client Situation==
#read through recent documents
##analyst reports
##market/industry reports
##company website
##company news (Factiva)
##client presentations
#Build hypothesis for potential storyline
##what benefits does the client want to realize
##look for conflicting and supporting data
##outline potential benefits
#prepare CxO level storyline
##what solutions and capabilities are needed to realize the identified benefits
##outline dependencies
##translate into CxO language
##test it internally
This is not to explain their business to the client.  It is to show that we understand.

Ensure your understanding is watertight, as it is the basis for your storyline.  If the understanding is poor, your pitch will be as well.
