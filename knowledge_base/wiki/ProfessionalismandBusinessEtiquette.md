==Accountability==
[[Category: Accountability]]
[[Category: Responsibility]]

You need to accept personal accountability for your work and for furthering the goals of the business.

It isn't unusual for someone to have good intentions but fail to follow through.

Taking ownership of your actions can have negative consequences for everyone
*low morale
*more errors
*sloppy work

You need to have a definite direction and be highly involved.
*you have to know and believe in the organization's goal
**helps define what you are accountable for
**gives you a direction
**influences your decisions and actions
**if you don't know the goals you can't help achieve them
**need to develop and maintain adequate communication channels with your peers
**your actions will affect those around you.
**you need to keep your commitments (e.g. scheduled meetings, information)

===Clear Goal===
#What you want
#what are your priorities and passions
#align the goal with these priorities

Once you know your goal you will be more committed to achieving the goal.

If you don't align your goals to priorities, you won't take action.

==SMART goals==
[[Category: SMART goals]]
*Specific
**Vague or general goals are difficult to follow through on.
**Specific goals give you a clear understanding of what you need to achieve.
*Measurable
**How will you know when you have reached the goal?
*Acheivable
**Do you have the power to attain the goal?  If you set a goal that you can't achieve, by definition you have set yourself up for failure.
*Realistic
**How likely is your goal to be met.  If it isn't likely you won't work on it.  You may be able to reframe the goal to something that takes you towards the end goal so that the end goal will become realistic.
*Time Framed
**A time constraint helps you stay focused.  Without the due date it is easier to keep putting the goal off.

==Managing Priorities and Energy==
[[Category: Managing Priorities]]

If you don't take action, having the goal and plan are meaningless.  Only through action are you taking ownership of the goals.

We use different amounts of time and energy for various tasks throughout the day.  You want to save and focus your time and energy where they matter most.

===Types of Activities===
#Maintenance Activities
##routine tasks that much be completed to ensure normal functioning.  An example is answering email messages.
#People Activities
##Those that involve others.  Negotiations, meetings, presentations, networking, training, interviewing, dealing with complaints, performance reviews
#Creative and Analytical Activities
##Anything creative, planning, solving problems, assessing problems, brainstorming

===Schedule Activities===
#How much time do you have for each
#How much energy will each take

Creative and analytical activities take longer to complete.  Therefore slot them into bigger time spots.

Maintenance activities generally take the least amount time because they are routine.  They also generally use little energy.

People tasks may be short or long depending on what is involved.  Remember to add a little extra for preparation and follow through.  These can also be draining as emotional energy is consumed.  

Once you figure out when you have the most energy in the day, that is when you schedule the tasks that take a large amount of energy.  Energy levels drop after a couple hours of concentrated work.
