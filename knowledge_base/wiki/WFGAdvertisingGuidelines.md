==WFG Advertising Guidelines==
===Guidelines for Creating Advertising Materials===
Please consider the following points when creating advertising materials:
*Overall visual impact of material
*Nature and tone of the subject matter
*Clarity and context of the material
*Is it fair and balanced?
*Could someone consider the material confusing or misleading?
*Use of the current approved company logos
*Balanced discussion of information, i.e. include features and benefits as well as risks and drawbacks
*Appropriate disclosure(s) and tagline(s) in 8 point font size or larger font size
*References to materials that are not of your own creation may require that permission be attained and “Used with permission.” should be disclosed in the material
*Clarity when creating recruiting advertisements. Be sure to represent the business as an opportunity.
*“For Internal Use Only” is required on all pieces that are for Associates’ eyes only, such as internal sales contests and internal training materials.
*Sources and dates for facts or statistics used in the brochure; one must be able to easily find the information. The source of the information must be dated three (3) years or less.
*In order to abide by copyright laws one must ensure that they have permission to use or reproduce any copyrighted text, photos, graphics, video or other material owned by others (e.g. McDonalds, iPad logos or photos, hotel logos and photos, etc.).
*Be sure to include the phrase “no obligation” when referencing any life insurance or any fixed annuity products.
*Correct placement of company logos, taglines, contact information, etc. See WFG Brand Guidelines

===Rule of 72 Disclosure===

The following disclosure must be on the same page of your piece when the Rule of 72 is mentioned.

All figures are for illustrative purposes only and do not reflect an actual investment in any product. Additionally, they do not reflect the many types of investment risks, expenses or charges associated with any actual investment. Past performance is not an indication of future performance. The Rule of 72 is a mathematical concept that approximates the number of years it will take to double the principal at a constant rate of return. The performance of investments fluctuates over time and, as a result, the actual time it will take an investment to double in value cannot be predicted with any certainty. Additionally, there are no guarantees that any investment or savings program can outpace inflation.

===Sales Contest Specific Instructions===

Both WFG Associates and TFG representatives must submit all Sales Contests to WFG Advertising for approval. The sponsor of the sales contest must receive approval from the WFG Advertising Department prior to conducting the contest. Important: If the contest sponsor intends to use any flyer, poster, brochure or other material related to the sales contest, he/she must submit these materials along with the WFG Advertising Submission Form signed by the SMD to WFG Advertising for review and approval.
General Sales Contest Guidelines:
*Sales contests may not be structured so as to favor a particular product or provider.
*All products offered through WFG affiliated companies must be equally weighted.
*It is highly recommended that contests be based on WFG Net Points.
*Sales contests cannot be based primarily on recruiting. a. Recruiting may be a component of the sales contest, but the contest must be primarily based on production. b. Contest based primarily on recruiting may violate state statutes or industry regulations.
*Product providers may not contribute to the prizes or awards offered through the contest.

Sales contest flyers or other material that you want to use to promote the Sales Contest; it must adhere to the following guidelines:
*Sale contest flyers or other material cannot include the Transamerica logo since points generated to win the contest are through WFG-affiliated companies
*It needs to include the term: For Internal Use Only
*It must include both of the two contest disclaimers listed below:

====SALES CONTEST - DISCLAIMERS:====

*FOR INTERNAL USE ONLY; and
*This sales contest is being sponsored by (Sponsor Name) an Independent Contractor of World Financial Group, Inc. (WFG). All points are generated through the sale of products and services offered through WFG-affiliated companies.

==Cold Calls==
===Caller Identification===
Every WFG associate must, upon making contact with the solicited person, identify himself or herself by the following:
*First and last name
*The caller’s title
*The purpose of the call

Under no circumstances should a WFG associate misrepresent his/her title, position, capacity or qualification for the purpose of gaining access to the solicited person, for increasing the probability of making a sale, or for any other reason.

A caller should disclose the purpose of the call. Under no circumstances should a caller provide erroneous information, such as stating that the call is “not a solicitation” or that it concerns an “emergency” or “critical” situation or that it is of a “personal” nature.

Automated Dialing / Message Systems: WFG prohibits the use of automated telephone dialing or automated facsimile dialing machines or message for any purpose.

===Calls Made to Prospect’s Home/Do Not Call List===

A caller is not permitted to call a prospect’s home if the telephone number is on the “Do Not Call” list. Therefore, all telephone numbers must first be checked against the “Do Not Call” list.

====Social Networking Best Practices====

As financial services professional physically meeting with families and recruits, a WFG associate is a direct reflection upon the brand image WFG. The same is true when a WFG associate virtually communicates with the general public using social networking sites. Take for example sending a twitter message, known as a “tweet” being posted on the internet for the general public consumption. When the general public reads the tweet they believe the WFG associate who posted the tweet is also expressing the opinions and beliefs of the WFG home office. In as far as the general public is concerned, the WFG associate is WFG. Therefore, please be vigilant regarding the reputation management of WFG. In other words, what a person says, what a person does, and how a person conducts them matters and reflects both the person who posted the and the entire WFG organization. Reputational management is a 24-hour a day responsibility. WFG encourages a person to have fun with their posts, but a person must always remember to conduct themselves with the utmost professionalism and decorum, and with the highest of ethics. A million positive accomplishments can be discredited by one foolish act or post/tweet. A WFG associate is the keeper of the WFG reputation. In short, a WFG Associate is the brand.

The following is a list of helpful hints:

*Think before tweeting. Twitter is a public forum, and anyone can read it: an Upline, a team, a colleague, a regulator, a client, a friend or a family member.
*Be very diligent before posting on a social networking site. Use caution before posting on the internet. Ensure that what is typed is exactly what was intended to be said.
*Make sure that what is being tweeted, it's relevant and substantive. Don't tweet for the sake of tweeting. Make sure that a tweet is meaningful and of interest to the reader.
*Don't use tweets discuss products, product specifics, commissions, payouts, or anything related to these topics.
*Don’t feel obligated to respond to someone else tweets. This is especially true if the person is a stranger. Evaluate Direct Messages (DMs) and determine how or if DM needs a response. If the contact is derogatory or inflammatory, especially about the company, then please do not respond.
*If a tweet was inadvertently posted on Twitter don’t panic. Consider, and then try to remove it. If that doesn’t work, then take a step back and evaluate other options. Take solace in the fact that each tweet can only have a maximum of 140 characters; therefore, the actual may not be as problematic as one would think. If the tweet is too problematic, then posted another tweet to mitigate the circumstances.
*Follow Angela Maier’s, an educational consultant, 70-20-10 rules on tweeting: a. 70% of tweets should share resources – links, articles, opinions and tools. b. 20% of tweets should engage in conversations and connections. c. 10% of tweets should “chirp” or chat about you, your life and your thoughts.
*Don’t stretch one comment across multiple tweets. If you can’t say it in 140 characters, you’re saying too much, and it defeats the purpose of Twitter. If you have an extended message, use e-mail instead.
*Do you tweet a lot, and what is considered “a lot?” This is a common concern of many who use Twitter. Here’s what Corvida Raven, a recognized authority on blogs and social networking, writes on SheGeeks.net: “As a new Twitter user, it’s hard to find a balanced way to build up your stream. Unless you have a reason to tweet a lot, don’t! This is the quickest way to be classified as a spammer. As you’re following/followers builds up, your twittering habits will naturally follow suit. Sending out 10-20 messages a day on Twitter is fine. It’s even perfect for some of your followers.” Still others complain that people who tweet excessively push other messages off their Twitter page. The bottom line: Only you can determine what’s right for you and the message you want to get out to your followers. 

If you have something important to share, go forth and tweet.
*Don't discuss AEGON.
*Apply the old adage, "Don't discuss politics and religion at the dinner table" to your tweets. We have clients and agents of all political persuasions and religious organizations.
*Watch your language. Casual language is fine. The laid-back environment of Twitter encourages it, but offensive talk is off limits. If you wouldn’t say it to your client or teammate face to face, then don’t post it on Twitter.
*Rude behavior is as unacceptable on Twitter as it is in life. Whether overtly or covertly, it is never appropriate to post mean-spirited or disrespectful tweets.
*Don’t link your Twitter account or any other social networking site to worldfinancialgroup.com or any of its other public websites as this is a regulatory infraction.
