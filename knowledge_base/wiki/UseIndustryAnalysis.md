==Industry Analysis==
[[Category: Industry Analysis]]
===Strategic Control Map===
[[Category: Strategic Control Map]]
A chart with the following:
*X axis - Market to book (Price to book) ratio of each company within the industry
*Y axis - Book value of common shares (total shareholder equity divided by outstanding common shares)
The position on the chart helps indicate the future of these companies.
*High Y value is a High Value Specialist.  Justify the value. (Q4)
**Usually small firms (low market cap) and could be a buyout target.
**Already performing very well.
*High X and High Y is an industry shaper that needs to stay ahead. (Q3)
**Have done everything right, but need to keep pace to stay ahead.
**Market leaders, so hard to improve (low odds of selling them something).
*High X value is an integrator and needs to be more attractive. (Q2)
**These companies will usually be looking to increase value players.
*Low X and Low Y - laggards at risk.  They need to take action. (Q1)
**Need to increase market value since the odds of increasing book value are low.
**could be a buyout target.
How do you determine High vs low?  Find the centre point (heat map style) and divide the chart into 4 quadrants.

This helps you target clients.  It also gives you talking points.  But you do not want to show this diagram to your clients.

You can make this chart more powerful if you compare the same companies for 5-7 years previous.  
*Drifters
*Value Players (Increasing Y axis)
*Volume Players (Increasing X axis)
A company that is both a Value and Volume player is in the ideal position.
