Effective Communication Skills
==Meeting Customer Needs==
Meeting the needs of a customer can pose a challenge.  Meeting the needs generates customer loyalty, customer happiness and word of mouth advertising.  These lead to increased profits.

Customers want a polite knowledgeable customer service representative.

Emotional need + business Need = whole person.

Important areas when dealing with the customer:
*friendliness
*effective communications with the customer
**address each point raised
*effectively listening to customer
**express empathy
===Different Customers Needs===
A customer wants:
#Effective service
#polite and knowledgeable rep
#want to know that their problem is most important to you
#want to know that you can and will fix the problem.
You need to acknowledge their frustrations and empathize before dealing with technical problems.  You need to deal with their psychological needs.

The ultimate goal is customer satisfaction.
*Customers are not always aware of their needs, but they always know when their needs are not met.
**reassurance and positive feedback.
**empathetic statements
**personalized service

Customers will seldom contact you with an unimportant problem.  By the time they contact you they have worked on it for a while.

You may not be able to meet their business needs 100% of the time but you should strive to meet their psych needs always.  

Dissatisfied customers generally don't complain.  They just stopm using your service.  They also tell 10-20 of their friends about the bad service.

Satisfied customers generally come back.  They also tell less than 5 people of their good experience.

You also need to ensure that the solution is acceptable to the customer.

You want to listen for unspoken service requests.

===Communication Styles===
You can't treat everyone exactly the same way since each person is an individual.

There are 4 different communication styles:
#director
#analyzer
#collaborator
#influencer

Once you have identified their style, you should adapt your communications somewhat.
====director====
Seek action and results.  Speaks in a clear cut way and cut to the chase.

These are change agents known for getting it done.  They don't spend a lot of time on niceties and small talk.

It is easy to misinterpret their direct approach as demanding, assertive and highly abrasive.

#Speak with confidence
#focus on deliverables
#talk in specifics rather than broad terms
#tell them what you will do by when
#avoid talking out loud
====analyzer====
Want to do it right.  Centers on clarifying, correcting and analyzing accuracy.

Focuses on specifics down to every detail.  Mya grow impatient if you avoid their questions or respond with general statements.

Tend to be successful at QA.  Able to identify gaps and flaws in a plan.

Can be misinterpreted by others as being over critical.  Can also be seen as picky and over controlling.

#have facts in order.  don't try to wing it as you will lose their respect.
#try to appreciate the value they bring in identifying gaps
====collaborator====
Want concensus.  Strive to create agreement and commonality to gain cooperation from others.

Peacemaker.  Avoids contentious communications.

Willing to go above and beyond to acheive the goal.

Misinterpreted as wishy-washy, indecisive or easily swayed.

#win win approach
#build options and choices
#avoid either or statements
#may need to draw them out as they will stand back letting others express theirselves.
====influencer====
Want to be like.  Usually start with niceties and spend a lot of the time on social talk before getting down to business.

Have a strong sense of diplomacy and tact.

Easy to misinterpret as frivolous or unfocused.

#address social needs
#responds to positive non verbal cues eg smiles
#may need to set clear deadlines
#may need to supervise frequently checking on progress

==Writing Styles==
Main goal is clear writing

Want to present relevant facts and details concisely.

Two element that help:
*objectivity
*completeness

Benefits of clear communications:
*Contents will be easy to understand
*Easier to get to core issue
*Can determine what was the intention
**what is the recipient to do?
===Documenting Incidents===
Consider the following:
#Write with the reader is mind, and not just for yourself.
#What does the recipient want
#how your response affects the reader
#why you are writing

When doing the writing
*Use precise defensible language (not vague)
*Use impact words

Make sure you edit post documentation before saving:
*check for grammatical errors
**wordiness
**negative words
**choppy sentences
**sexist language
*read from readers point of view.  Even have someone else look at it.
**goal is to inform or to instruct.

Focus on big picture first, and then on details paragraph by paragraph and sentence by sentence.

==Asking Questions and Building Rapport==
Asking questions and building rapport help address the customers needs.

When emotions become involved, communication can break down.  You need to be able to understand their emotional responses.
===Open ended and Closed ended Questions===
Use open ended questions to get customer to tell what they want.  They can also clarify facts, uncover wants, improve understanding, reveal possible alternatives and choices, remove roadblocks enabling progress toward solving the problem.

Open ended questions typically start with:
*what
*when
*how
*why

Use closed ended questions to get specific details.  THey tend to have short responses.  They can be useful to obtain facts, halt a rambling customer, bring a call to a close and control the direction of a call.

Closed ended questions typically start with:
*do
*did
*may
*are
*should
*were


===Understanding Customer Emotions===
Help you to recover from communication break downs.

Anger
*generally feel they have suffered harm
**policies
**level of service
**actions of frontline personnel

Frustration
*can not resolve an issue or accomplish a task/goal
**having to call more than once are very likely to be frustrated

Impatient
*when service is slow
**hurry
**have a lot of work to do
**want to get things done
**Want things to go smoothly
**want someone who will listen
**want fast service

Hostile
*argumentative or nasty
**bad day
**unhappy in job/life
**have irritable personalities

Emotions can not be ignored.  You can use the following techniques:
*empathy
*caring
*patience
*sincerity
*responsiveness
===Building Rapport===
Building rapport strengthens the customer relationship.  People only buy from others they know and trust.  Without rapport there isn't trust.
*compliment customers
*find common ground
**can be on personal level or business to business
*build concensus/focus on agreement
**agreement creates a bond.

These only work if you are sincere.  Rapport is build on sincere desire to help customer.

Compliments are different from flattery.  They must be sincere.

Establishing rapport depends on a first impression.  First impressions are formed within the first 3 minutes.

==Build Value==
Nothing can be self serving in the sales process.  Customers are turned off if it is blatant that you are getting the better part of the deal.  If you say anything that isn't in the best interest of the client, it will destroy the rapport and trust.
