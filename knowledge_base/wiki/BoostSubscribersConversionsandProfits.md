===You are only one good conversion system from success.===
If you can make more money per visitor than your competition, you win.

The website that makes the most per visitor has a lager budget for advertising, can easilty outsource more work

Simple truth:  You don't have a traffic problem; You have a converison problem.

===Unlimited traffic technique===
If you can generate more income per visitor, you can dominate AND choose the source of advertising you want - free or paid traffic.  Pay your affiliates more than they can earn off of the traffic themselves, and they will feed you traffic.

You don't need to be a writer to convert visitors into income.  The less you write, the more you make.  
====TweetGrid====
Your prospects will hand you the right words on a silver platter - TweetGrid.  TweetGrid lets you create a twitter search dashboard that updates in real time.
*Start with your 2 most important keywords
*Create a 2x1 grid
*grab the short ULR
*monitor daily
*Capture striking language, benefits, problems shared
Take the tweets, take the interesting words and problems expressed, and use that as what you are addressing.  Use their language, terms.  This should resonate with people better than doing it blind.

====Youtube Viral Video====
*Search Youtube for your two most important keywords
*Note the most frequently viewed relevant videos
*Capture their titles
*Craft and use a tested and proven headline based on these viral video titles.
DO NOT WATCH THE VIDEOS when engaging in this technique.  Don't use the language in the videos, because they haven't been tested.  Just the titles.

===Marketing===
Marketing has changed.  But human nature doesn't.  How we communicate does change.  Those that stick to direct marketing and sales techniques designed decades ago are failing.

Internet is rewiring our brains. 
*Harder to concentrate for long periods of time
*You've gotten much quicker at scanning
*Feel a lot more rushed even with all the time saving tools.

Need short crips benefit statements with high info density.

==Success Formula==
#Desperate problem
#Unique promise
#Overwhelming proof
#irresistible offer
#reason to act now

Does your product/service solve a truly desperate problem (like the desire to get away from a hungry bear)?

Can you reposition the product/ service to solve a problem?

People weigh pain vs payment.  They more desperately want to hold onto their money rather than give it to you.
*The more your product is a magic pill solution, the better
*Are people currently paying money to solve this problem?

==On the first screen==
Needs to be above the fold:
*Is it obvious what problem you solve?
*Does your perfect customer raise their hand and say this sounds like me
*Do you have a story that illustrates the problem and identifies with your audience?
Design is about on parity with the headline for influencing purchasing.  The site needs to feel comfortable.
Need to give the impression that you will transport the audience from their pain to the comfirtable solution above the fold.

Is the unique promise on the first screen?
*Have prospects shown this is a unique point of difference benefit?  
*You don't want them saying "I've heard this before".

Prospects are gone in 5 seconds.  Headline must stop them in their tracks within 5 second.

Expand on Results, how it works, unique stories.

==Irresistible offer==
8Is your offer worth more than the asking price
*Will it be more painful to ignore your offer than exchange money for it?
*Is it totally risk free to them - and an amazing value

===7 steps to winning offers===
*what is the goal of your offer
**prove concept
**generate revenue
**sell something more expensive later
*What will the customer receive
*what bonuses will you provide
**Needs to be releveant to the product/benefit.
*what is the right price
**Front end product price that tends to succeed is 150x the PPC cost.  Folloup (upsell) pruodcut then needs to be 5-15x of the frontend cost.  So if $1 per click, your product should be $150, and the upsell is $750-$1500.  If you don't have the resources to put this together, this will not be very profitable.  (Economics of the offer have to support the cost of buying the traffic)
*how do you remove risk
*why should they take action now?
*what should they do now?

2 bumps - start at a price 2 levels below then you want to be at.  i.e. target 49.  Start at 29, then 39.
Announce the price increase, and see people getting in to save the money.  They will feel they ogot a special, and you got the revenue.
