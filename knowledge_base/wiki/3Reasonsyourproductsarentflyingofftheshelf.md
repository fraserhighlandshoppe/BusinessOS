==3 Reasons your products aren't flying off the shelf==
Need to attract Five Star clients.
*Need passion for your venture
*Need a BIG vision
*Need a plan.
Build it and they will come is a fantasy from that Costner movie.

You need to lose the mindset that you need to do more to get more.  Make fun and pleasure a priority.

All of us has an internal regulator that stops us from making more money than this level.  We need to continously adjust this set point!
*Once you up the setpoint, 
**it feels easier
***everything you do is with intention
**the actions you take change
***less hoops
**the fun level goes up (more satisfaction)

===Don't have a five star business===
*Designed for your pleasure.
*Designed to attract 5 star client (most ideal client/customer)
**Botique versus discount warehouse (e.g. Walmart)
**You don't blend in - you express your uniqueness
**Your clients don't care about your price - they care about the value you deliver
***mindset and marketing call forth the ideal client.
***you are solving the problem they are searching high and low for.
*create an offer your clients can't resist.
===Don't have a lead magnet attracting your 5 star prospect===
*valuable
*designed for 5 star client.
*solves a top of mind issue
*client will appreciate the hell out of it
*want to talk/write about free gift in a way that is compelling.
**need to be highly targeted.  Not generic.
===Don't have a followup system===
*need to get the client to consume the free gift.
*also encourages them to take the next step with you.
**can't be weird nor pushy.
**should feel like next logical step.
**need same tone from free gift through followup.
*Build Trust
*gets the client excited to continue.
===Haven't launched offer(s) in 5 star way===
Need to speak to the (potential) clients highest deepest desire!

Don't prolongue their suffering by keeping things under wraps.

Get people to experience your brand and expertise through a series of experience.
*multiple touch points.
*tribe recognizes you as expert.
*online launch lets more people to show up

Your 5 star offer is typically your big money maker.  However, the excited client tends to buy more than just that one item.

====Elevate Inner Game====
*Need to get to the large money mindset.  Get past the just enough mindset to get to the more than enough.  Going for more isn't harder.
====Take bite sized chunks====
*Most people want to rush it all to get it done.  This leads to rushed work, stress
====Include the Fun Factor====
*if it isn't fun the vibe will be bad

Most people are busy in a process designed for struggle.  They are throwing out one-off attempts/products/services/...
*Launches need to be designed.  With intention.

*streamlined

*grab client's full attention.

*build curiousity
Articulate power of offer without feeling like a pitch
*videso

*conversation rather than 1 way pitch

====Credible Biography====
====Social Proof====

====Maximize revenue====

==Systems==
Need to capture leads from people when they are most excited about you.
*when you speak in front of them
*one-one conversation
Leads are then followed up with.

System needs to be
*simple
*exciting to design
*fun before, during and after

You get to sequence the events in your launch to showcase your genius.
*webinar
*meet and greet
*facebook live
*online Q&A

It is how you tie it all together that is important (that and the underlying mindset).

It is through the process of the launch that you take yourself on.  
*Makes you to remove the things that are getting in your way.  
*Message gets communicated with clarity and confidence.  
*You streamline your business
