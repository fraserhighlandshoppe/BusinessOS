===Invoicing the Shipment===
You can get here from the Purchase tab or after clicking Process Receive Items

From the screen after clicking Process Receive Items:
#Click Entry purchase invoice for this receival 

[[Image: FA Supplier Invoice.png]]

On the next screen
#Choose Supplier from Dropdown Menu
#Type in a Supplier Reference Number (Supplier Invoice Number or Today's Date if unavailble)
#Ensure the dates selected are reasonable (accrual accounting at EOM?)
#Confirm quantities to be invoiced and prices (matching to supplier invoice)
#update the shipping cost (if an invoice item) to be $0.01
#Add a GL Entry for Shipping (account 5100) and GST Payable on Shipping (account 2310)
#Click the ADD button at the end of the row for each item as you verify the line items OR click Add All Items Button at top IF you have verified the entire invoice.
##While it may be tempting to add everything in one step, if there are multiple applicable invoices this just makes the accounting audit more difficult.
#Click Enter Invoice

On the next page
#Click View This Invoice Link on the following page (a pop-up window will appear)
#At the bottom of the Pop-up screen, click the PRINT link and print 2 copies (ensure bookkeeper gets a copy)

If needed, the next step would be to Entry supplier payment for this invoice.  See [[FHS FA Paying a Supplier]]
