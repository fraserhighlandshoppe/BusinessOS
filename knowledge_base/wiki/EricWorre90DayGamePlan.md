==Pre-Launch==
This is the most powerful stage of the 90 day game plan.  During this time you can be influential.  You can grow your team.

Need to have a detailed description of what you are going to do on the Launch date.

Now you need to recruit as many people as possible into your Campaign.

The longer the better.  Get people behind your campaign.

 "This is not launched yet, but let me tell you about it.  I'm putting together my inner circle.  I'm looking for people who are serious, excited and engaged."

Recruiting will go through the roof if you talk about something you will do rather than something you are doing.
*A detailed description of what the campaign will be will be very powerful.
Have a checklist of what people need to do to get into the inner circle.
*e.g. They need to create their team

Tell a story.  Fresh mindset.  New product?

==Launch==
Start on Day 1 (e.g. March 1).  End in 90 days (e.g. ~June 1) and hold a BIG EVENT.  The event is the glue that keeps everyone focused.
*The event should have a goal such as 100 people in the room.
*Needs to be compelling and exciting.
Then we reverse engineer the goal to determine how we are going to do it.

Plan out what has to happen to make this happen:
