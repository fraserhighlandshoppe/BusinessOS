==Hub Training Branding==
FINTECH - Website/etc promoting the 2 click and get your quote.
*They brag about lower fees and prices.
We combat this by building our brand.

===How Branding Works===
#Awareness
#Affinity (if likeable and relatable)
#Decision Threshold (Value we bring.  Why they want to engage)

There are about 45000 insurance agents and 90000 financial advisors in Canada.

#Be distinct
#Think value

===Where do you Brand===
*Everywhere.  
**Online
**In Person
***Clothes, haircut, products, etc.
*Forever
Every choice you make affects your brand.
===3:30^2===
*3 seconds to catch attention.
**Impactful
**Brief
**Focus on what them working with you will do for them.
*Followup with 30 second brand statement driving exchange of contact data OR continue conversation
*3 Minute extended branding statement designed to get prospect to give you time on their calendar so you can do the initial interview.
**Goal for them to commit to a meeting.
====3 Second====
 The word insurance is an automatic shutoff.
[[Image: gtw40.png]]
 I help my clients increase their wealth
 I build generational wealth
 I create effective succession plans
 I maximize long term estate value.
 I create liquidity in long term savings

====30 second====
 My team and I help clients realize their goals by
 maximizing access to capital, make financial plan more
 efficient and reducing taxes.
 We're not right for everyone.  Clients I've partnered with
 experience savings gains, lower taxes and generational wealth.
 It all comes down to how we help them structure their strategic plan.

An advisor can understand the clients wants and goals.  We can help them
find the right products to ensure they are going in the right direction.
Them clicking a link on a website will not get them there.  Products are complicated enough.
=====3 Minute=====
It is a mini story
*Follows a pattern
*puts the prospect in the story
*goal to get a meeting.

The set of needs a client has had (retire by X, buy a boat, etc).
*Conversations held
*What we did to help them to do that.

 My team and I worked with a client who approached savings the traditional way
 Save for 18-24 months to save a down payment for a house.
 Then they bought the house.
 Then they started putting spare cash into RRSP.  This did give them tax savings
 but fluctuations in the market meant their savings was flat.
 Then the market tanked and their house was worth less than they paid.  They couldn't
 liquidate their asset because they were upside down, and their long term goals weren't
 well in progress.
 This is where we started work together. 
 Then we designed their plan to
  use a life insurance policy that allows access to cash
 Client is seeing significant gains.
 Also will allow them to pay off the mortgage.
 This will then also allow them to shift payment into other savings.
 This will allow them to travel as much as they want to once they finish working.
