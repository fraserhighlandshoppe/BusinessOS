==Sales Prospecting Checklist==
 Make sure to review [[Facebook_marketing#ineffective_and_useless_strategies]] before Actioning the Prospecting
#Understand your Product or Service and how it is used today
#Define your product's specialty
#Analyze your current customers and why they buy
#Describe your ideal customer in as much detail as possible
#Analyze your competitors advantage and why their customers buy from them
#Define how your product or service is different
#List customer segments that most benefit from your specialization
#Anticipate weakness your customers may find with your product
#Which markets are not buying your product?
#Identify what your prospects must believe to buy from you
#Evaluate your prospect's GOOD characteristics
#Evaluate your prospect's BAD characteristics
#Focus your energy on your best prospects

Good sales people ask themselves "Where is my next sale coming from.  Is what I am doing leading to that next sale?"

What is the plan for developing excellent prospects?
