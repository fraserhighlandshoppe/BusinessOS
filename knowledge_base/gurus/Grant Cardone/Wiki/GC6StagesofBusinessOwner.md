=====Dreamer=====
*Millions of ideas
*no system
*No coach
*No accountability
*Never gets started
*Can't get motivated
Write down your dream in present tense so you can get past daydreaming.
=====Startup=====
*No personal coach
*No ongoing mentor
*No system
*No money
*No Marketing
*No Traffic
*One Man Show
=====Flatline=====
*No Coach/Mentor
*No Systems
*Screwed up Priorities
*Marketing problem
*Growth without scaling
*Exhausted and frustrated
*Working harder than ever
*Constant fear
=====Comfort=====
*No Next level mentor/advisor
**Smartest person in room
**Thinks he knows it all
*Successful compared to peers
*Some money saved
**Don't know how to grow this
*False sense of success
*Can't find drive anymore
*One crash can set you back
=====Scale=====
*coach and mentor
*Systems
*Highly motivated
*Cash flow management
*Networking is a priority
*Fractional Revenue Consultants.
=====Go Public (Sale/merge/IPO)=====
*Consultants not coaches
*Sales systems in place
*Financial systems in place
*KPI measurements
*Hiring and Training Protocols
*Expanding through acquisition
*Networking with equals/above equals.
