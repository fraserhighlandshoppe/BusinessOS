===10 Truths of Conversions (Sales)===
*Always agree with prospect
**Doesn't mean they are right, you are just telling them they are
**The customer is most often not right.
*Be transparent
**What is your intention
***Confidence
***Certainty
***Get them to acceptance the problem will be solved
*Clear Intention
*No one thing will blow a sale.
**Don't need to walk on egg shells selling
*100% write up
**proposal
**contracts
*People shop price.  They don't buy price.
*Use hard questions
**Always get an answer to the hard questions
***do you have the money
***Are you the decision maker
***1-10 are you ready to go
***Is there any reason you couldn't complete this deal
***Are you in a position to purchase this
***Have you ever spent this much money before
*Decision Phenomenon
**Have you seen enough to make a decision.
**Buyer makes the decision to purchase before you ask them.
**The right client will be ready to purchase when they walk in.
*2nd sale is easier to make then the first sale
**Always go back to a previous buyer
**Don't get a new customer - go back to someone that saw the demonstration but didn't buy
*Always use 3rd party touch
**Why didn't you buy?
