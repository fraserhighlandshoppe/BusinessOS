===Biggest Threat===
Biggest threat to gaining wealth is 
 Disengagement.

You need people that are
*Curious
*engaged
If you aren't fully engaged, you settle.

*On any given day, what is your engagement level?
*On a normal day, what is the engagement level of the people around you?
**They are probably lower than you.
*What is the engagement level of the people you take advice from?
**Parents, friends, etc
**Influencers
**Mentors
 Take a look at the great people (e.g. Elon) - they are trying to figure out how to do more in their time.
 Contrast Ferriss (4 hour work week) who was trying to do less.
 If you can get rich in 4 hours a week, how much can you do in 100?

People will not change their behavior until the payoff is big enough.
*What payoff is so big that you would walk on glass to achieve that....

Their problem is your problem
*64% of small business have no employees
*67% of small business are losing money or breaking even.
*Businesses with employees have difficulty getting excited employees.
**Would the way people are paid affect how engaged they are?
