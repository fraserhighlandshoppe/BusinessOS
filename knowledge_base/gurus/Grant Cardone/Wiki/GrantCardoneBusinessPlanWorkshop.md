===7 Secrets for Fast Results===
====Accelerator 1: Get a fast win and expand====
You need a victory.

Somebody needs what you are selling.

Your first sale will almost certainly be within your network
*Friends and family
*People you spend money at

Money attracts money.
====Accelerator 2: Maximize Income per Buyer====
Its no harder to sell high margin products than low margin product.

Happier clients, less work with higher priced items.
====Accelerator 3: Recurring revenue====
Subscriptions
*Predictability
*Confidence

Make it easy for people.
====Accelerator 4: Copy what works====
You don't need to be creative.  Copy and tweak.
====Accelerator 5: Amplify your personal Social Media====
Be everywhere.  Omnipresent.

Doesn't have to be your own content.

Collaborate with others wanting the same thing.
====Accelerator 6: Use Arbitrage to create money====
Get Paid $$$ and outsource the delivery for $$.  You pocket $.

Get paid in advance.  Out of pocket cost is 0.
====Accelerator 7: Always Systemize everything====
Checklists.  Flowcharts.

Systems take away the guesswork.

No confusion, no doubt.

Your customer lacks system.
