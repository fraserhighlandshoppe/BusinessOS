===Pat Quinn===
Communication is the skill that allows you to grow.

Pay attention to how your audience hears, learns, pays attention.

Watch your audience during a presentation.  You will see where they pay attention (what's engaging) and where they aren't.

What is the problem that you are solving (as opposed to the solution).
*This is the difference between focused on the audience and focused on self.
**Focused on audience is problem
**Focused on advisor is solution
***FA often say problem is "Great Retirement" whereas this is the solution/result
*What words do your audience use to describe the problem?
**If you get in their head and use the same words they do, they unconsciously nod along...

Break out of your brochure.

Serve groups you already belong to.  
*Easiest to get traction
*You already speak their language
*you already know their problems.

Signature Talk:
*Appeal to 
**Heart,  
***tell the story of when you had this problem
***connect with the audience
***50% of audience decides in first 5 minutes whether they will do something with you.
***This must be your own story.
***Don't read us your resume.  Nobody cares your last 3 jobs, nobody cares why you started the business.
***Sharing the most painful day of your life is not the right story to start with.
***This story is the one you have in common with the maximum number of people in the audience.
**Head, 
***teach the audience something
***This can be someone else's story.
**Hands, 
***What action they should take
**Heart (finish where you started)
***Leave them with a story that shows them what they need to change.
***Story that inspires them to take action.
What are the struggles.  How are they hurting.


 During content section, refer to yourself by your first name
 *ask yourself a question (hey Kevin, how do you ...)
 This makes sure the audience knows your name.
 This also helps get attention.

 When you have something important to say, don't pound the podium they assume you are angry.
 Instead, scooby-do them:
 Look Left, Look Right, drop your head
 Lean in a bit, the audience will too

A difference between a lead or a customer is a customer will call you by your first name.

To improve your CTA - only have ONE CTA.  
*What is it you want your audience to do.
*Don't give them options.  They won't do anything as they consider the options.

Things popping into your head when nervous:
*I will forget what to say
*I might fall on the stairs
*I might ....

Getting rid of stage fright:
*What is the problem you solve (Clarity)
**I don't have to be the smartest person in the room.  I'm here to solve 1 problem.
*Stage Fright is selfish.  Quit thinking/talking "I"
