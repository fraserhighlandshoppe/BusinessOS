==Key Takeaways from the Bootcamp==
===Commitment===
If you can't commit to the 3 days, how can you commit to the rest of your life?

You need to commit, and recommit daily, to what you are trying to accomplish.

Until you commit nothing great is going to happen.

Commit First.  Then figure it out.
*If you have to figure it all out first, you will never commit.

The faster you commit the faster you make big money.

Write your goals EVERY morning and night.
===Invest in your people===
Too easy to get into a Scarcity mindset.

If you invest in your people, they will trust you more.
*Higher loyalty
*Higher Commitment
*Less turnover
===Country Club Rich is worst place to be===
You are just enough to be good, but not far enough along to generate generational wealth.
*Wasting time
*Wasting Money
*Wasting Potential
===Knowledge===
What you currently know is a bigger obstacle than what you don't know.
*Thinking you already know something blocks you from learning something further about it.
===Time===

===Quit Playing too small===
If you play small you stay small

64% of small business in USA break even or lose money.

Most solo-preneurs make less money than they would working for someone.

Go Big or Go Bigger!

Quit thinking about your little problems!

===Entrepreneur Phallacy===

====Rules====
#Always agree with customer.  If you disagree you start an argument and they dig in.
##Of course it is expensive
##You're right
#Be transparent in your sales process.
#Have a clear intention
##I intend to sell (you) XYZ.
#There is never 1 thing that can derail a sale.
#Write it up.  Take full responsibility for the sale cycle.
#People shop price.  They never buy price.
##On price objection, agree, and then show higher priced package with more value.
#Ask relevant questions.  Ask hard questions.  Determine what they value
##How much did you pay last time
##Are you the decision maker
##Who else is a decision maker (for this decision).
##How would you rate your current XYZ
##When is the last time you bought XYZ
##Why are you looking today/now/.  Why not yesterday or next week?
##How much do you believe/trust what I am saying.
##Is there any reason you would ever change your mind?
##Have you seen enough to make a decision
#Always get an answer to the questions.  Don't let them dance.
##Whoever controls the process gets the money
##Listen.
##Stay present.  Don't create deal fatigue.
#If the communication stops, the sale stops.
#Decision Phenomenon.  You can see when they have made a decision.
#Second sale is always easier.
#Always use a 3rd party touch.
#Customer questions are a sign of uncertainty.
#To close requires the following:
##The decision maker
##Money
##Urgency
##Trust in product or service
##Believe this product or service will solve their problem.
