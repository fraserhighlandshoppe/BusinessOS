===Ed Mylett===
You weren't born to be average; You weren't born to have a mediocre existence.  When you were born a race began.

Every Day has to be the pursuit for the best possible you.  To grow, stretch.  You are chasing the destiny version of you.

Every action (or inaction), decision, call, relationship is either moving you towards or away from your best you.  Keep asking yourself "does this put me closer to the ultimate version of me?"

Maxing out 10X requires energy.  People respond more to what they feel than what they hear/see.
*What do you make people feel?
The more you become concious of what you make people feel makes all the difference in the world whether you will influence their lives.

Typically in business, Highest Energy wins!  You have to be intense about your life and business.  If you are going to play the game, play to win.

The difference between being a manager and a leader in a company is being able to see the greatness in people.  Leaders are evangelical about their message.  They are spreading the (positive) word about the cause/company.

When Ed runs a competition for Money versus for the Cause, the money contests have a lower performance.

The right people are drawn to meaningful work (cause).  If you can build a cult like evanelism you will end up rich.

What you become obsessed with is what you end up with.  So if you are obsessed with your goals, you will achieve them.  If you are obsessed with your problems, that is what you will end up with.

All great leaders are visionaries.  You enroll people in your vision and their skepticism goes away.

Entrepreneurism is a self improvement package with a massive compensation package.
*You need to be focused on growing you instead of growing your company.
*Being an entrepreneur will help you identify your weakness, what you don't know, your resilliancy, your toughness.
*Your business will never exceed your vision and identity.
*If your business gets ahead of you, you start making unconcious decisions to shrink it.
*The leader is out in front.  Your #1 job is "let me tell you what I can see".  Being in front you are blocking their vision.
*If the company catches your vision/identity, the company is dying.

Your will to win can be bought.  With enough failure and rejection, you can be bought (sell their dream).  The difference between the winners and losers are their resiliancy.  The thing to ask yourself IN ADVANCE is what price are you willing to pay?  Ed - As long as it is legal, ethical he will never stop.

In business it is a continuous cycle of forward and back.  Each time it falls apart a bit you feel it is less worth it, as you are exhausting yourself.  Like swimming in the ocean, there is an undertow taking you back out.  But each time it kicks in you are closer to your goal.  Is each decision you are making taking you closer to or farther from your goals?

Stop acting like a poor person.  Poor people look at the price tags (what does it cost)(position of scarcity).  Successful and wealthy people don't look at price, they look at worth.  Is doing this/owning this worth it?
*Stop negotiating the price.  It's not about the price, it's about the worth!
*When you totally commit to something it is freeing.

You should be continuing to grow.  A 7 year old isn't the same as a 6 year old.  A 16 year old does not resemble the 6 year old.  At what point did your growth stop?  (i.e. does your 40 year old life look like your 30 year old life?)

The last superbowl was won by ''1'' play.  Keep calling the plays, because there ''will'' be that 1 meeting/relationship that will make all the difference.  Every time you hit a setback you start asking about the price/cost.  Resist this poor man thinking.  Stop letting the world push you around.  Push back!

Great leaders care, believe, love people.

Every person is born with a gift.  Your job as a leader is to identify the gift in people and point it out to them.  Then you become one of the few people that sees their gift.  Once you have that connection with them, you can hold them accountable.  

If my life ended right now, am I happy with it?  Is the book of my life complete?  Is there chapters yet to come?  The leading character in your book of life is you, and the people you love.  Far too many people are obsessed with what other people think about what you are doing.  The thing that will kill your dream is your addiction to their approval.  They aren't showing up for any of the important chapters of your life.  As the leading character you can choose your script.  Don't be constrained by the narrative that your boss or parents or someone else gave you.

At the end of your life, don't let the person you could have become be a perfect stranger.  Be the identical twin to who you were born to be.  Max Out your life.
