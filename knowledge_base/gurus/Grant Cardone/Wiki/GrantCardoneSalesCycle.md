==Sales Cycle==
You want to shorten the cycle as much as possible.
===Idea===
This is where you come up with your product.  But it must be a solution to someone's problem.
===Attention===
Advertise.  Marketing.
 Generate LEADS

===Intention===
Know in your mind what your intention is.  It is to get your client to purchase your product.
===Present===
Many sales people can get to the presentation, but never actually propose the client do anything.

Some personalities want deep details but most don't.

Only talk long enough to sell the client.  Don't talk yourself out of the sale.
===Propose===
You MUST propose the solution.  The client won't just hand you cash.

 See [[Grant Cardone Proposals]]

===Close===
Objection handling.  Practice during training time so that you are able to handle in the close.  DON'T PRACTICE DURING THE CLOSE!!
 You shouldn't be surprised by an objection.  
The objections you hear will be the ones you walk in prepared to hear.
 In most industries money isn't really a problem.
There are really only 2 objections 
#They aren't the decision maker.
#They don't think it will work.
