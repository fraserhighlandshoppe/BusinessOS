Attention requires action.

Generating leads:
*Attention will get you mindshare but leads are what gets you customers.
*Leads are always the results of an offer.
**Attention with NO offer will NOT MONETIZE
**This is not the product or service.  This is the offer that creates the opportunity to sell the product/service.
Types of Offers:
*Straight to product offer
**Lowest conversion offer.
**Think pizza place with sign guy on corner.
*Offer of additional information
*Newsletter
**Value based info
*Free Webinar
**Adding value to them
*Ebooks
*Free Consult
**Strategy call
**Assessment
**Estimate


3 Ways People take up your offer
*DIY
*DI With You (DWY)
*Done For You (DFY)
People frequently move from DIY to DWY to DFY

Offer Checklist
*Higher Value perceived than what they are giving up (time, email/phone number/...)
*Low Cost or Free (low barrier to entry)
*Low Friction
**Easy for them to do

Come up with 5 offers to move someone from attention to a lead (aka Lead Magnet).
*If you can't come up with any ideas, mystery shop your competitors.
**What are they doing?  How are they doing it?

Have an upsell for the offer.  Probably won't work for brand new prospects, but people that are familiar may upgrade.

Nurturing Leads
*98% will not convert on your first offer
*80% of transactions occur after the 8th interaction

Think about why people don't buy
*They aren't qualified right now.
**Doesn't mean they won't be in the future.  This is why lead nurturing is important.
*Didn't have enough information about the product
**Or they don't know about you
***Send testimonials
***Research / Case Studies
*Wasn't able to build enough trust that you can solve the problem
*Don't think it will work for them.
*Unanswered questions about price.
**Payment plans?
*They want to shop around
**Show comparisons of your product vs someone else's
***Here's how we are better, cheaper, more valuable
*They aren't the decision maker
*They were distracted by something so didn't finish the purchase.

 Nurture - care and encourage growth or development

==10X Sales System==
Predictable Sales System:
*Idea
**What you are going to do to make money
**A Target is an idea.  
*Attention
**Pre-sell
***Market will tell you if its a good idea
*Leads
*Nurture leads
*Sales Conversion
*Sales Management
*Scale
**Repeating the process over and over and over.

You have to close the distance between you and the target.  The closer to the target you are the easier it is to hit.
*You need to wrap the target with other ideas/activities to get closer.

Harder than selling tickets to an event is coming up with an idea that is interesting enough to keep you engaged.
*You have to stay interested in the idea when it isn't working out. (You're discouraged)
*Have to continue taking action when no-one is looking.

 Show me your liability and I will show you your assets -- Grant Cardone responding to a guy complaining he is bed ridden so can't take action.

The idea will be
*something you don't have
*maintain something you already have
**really difficult to do

Your target should change your life significantly.
*It should create NEW problems (and therefore opportunities).

In your sales system, you have to treat everyone like a buyer.  Even if they don't buy right now, they could buy later.

Commit First.  Figure it out later.

 When someone comes forward to exchange value with you and you don't accept,
 you have invalidated the relationship and it will cost you more than just the lost sale.

When you want more money, you go back to the people that already bought for you
*Easier than building a new list
*Their second purchase reinforces that the first was a good choice
*Giving referrals validates the decision.

Reason why people don't by things:
#They don't trust themselves
#Because we didn't ask them to purchase (No CTA)
#not sure the problem will be solved



Sales process
#Greet
#Qualify
##Have you had experience with this
##How much have you spent on this in the past
##Are there anyone else you want involved in this?
###get decision makers involved.
#Presentation
#Cost
#Offer
#

To close you need:
*Urgency
*Decision Maker
*The cash (ability to purchase)
**Check writer is not always the decision maker.

When you have a buyer asking "When can you start" you have urgency

Most people going after large companies go to the wrong person (decision maker) and by the time they get to the right people that person has moved on, and you have to restart the cycle.

To scale:
*The product
*The process
*The Team
*The followup
**If you can text someone (details) while you are talking to them, the chances of closing goes way up.

 Become the manager of your future.
