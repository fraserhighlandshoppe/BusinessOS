===Never Spend it Until You Get It===
Until the money is in the bank, you haven't received it, so the deal could fall through.  Never spend money you don't have.

This also applies in business.  Helps cash flow - don't count money in until it is in.

Until the money has come in, the deal isn't done.

Don't spend tomorrow's money.

===40% Rule===
100% of your income becomes:
*40% goes to tax man
*40% pay yourself!
**If you will pay the taxman 40% shouldn't you pay yourself the same?
**In a business this is marketing and advertising.
***Feed the beast!
*20% for living.
You don't change your lifestyle until the 20% changes!

You've figured out how to pay the tax man his 40%.  Figure out how to pay yourself 40%.
*Not figuring this out shows you aren't disciplined when it comes to money.
*If you don't take care of your money, your money won't take care of you.
*If you ignore your money it will ignore you.

You need to be disciplined with money.  That is why the taxman gets paid first - they don't trust you to manage your money.

If you can't live on the 20%, you need to either:
*Lower your standard of living
*control your standard of living
*increase the 100%

===If you can't write it off, don't buy it===
And the converse if you can write it off, it may be worth buying.

===Rent and Lease.  Don't Own===

===Stabilize and Grow your first flow before you work on a second===
Why are you working on a side hustle when you were in your main hustle for 8 hours.
===47 Rule===
If you are putting in 40 hours at work, spend at LEAST 7 hours a week working on yourself.
*1 hour a day self improvement
**Read
**Mentors and coaches.


Don't complain about your hours.  There is going to be a day you can't go to work.

Successful businessmen talk about 100+ hours working.

===Always Stay Broke===
Cash is trash.

It is worthless until it is put to work.  It is useless until it is used.  It is a piece of paper.

If you give your money to a bank THEY will put it to work by lending it out 9 times (fractional reserve system).

===Never Lose Money===
Leaving money in the bank is losing money (inflation, negative rates, etc)

Can't compound a fraction of a percent.

We are headed for negative interest rates.  This is already happening in some countries in Europe and Asia.

===You want Cashflow not Cash===
===Never quit until you achieve your goals===
Never give up on your financial goals until you achieve them.

Though experiment - if you attend a conference, would you accept that someone in the crowd could put together a $1B deal?
*means you accept that the money is available.
Do you accept that if you had the $1B deal, you would do good things with that money?
*You can know this about yourself, but you can't about the guy next to you.
So this means that you lack the faith in your ability to execute your vision, not in the vision itself.
