===Secrets of the Wealthy===
#Pay yourself first
#Live on 20% of your income
#Don't lose money
#Invest in assets that cash flow
#Have more passive income than earned income.

The most important is to be patient and invest for the long term.

If there is immediate gratification, it is most likely an expense rather than an investment.

Investments take time to bear fruit.
