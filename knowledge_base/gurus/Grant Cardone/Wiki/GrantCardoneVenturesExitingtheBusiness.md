{{:Grant Cardone Pages}}


Most people don't think about selling until they are under stress and pressure.  And because of this they are desparate and can't get the true value of their business.

2 types of exit:
#planned
#forced

2/3 of businesses go out of business every 5 years.

Your business will never be worth anything (to a buyer) if you can't exit it.

If you don't have an exit plan, and you are forced to exit (e.g. die) and the business isn't structured correctly, all of the people who came along with you will also be forced to exit!

==Intentional Exit==
If you can't exit the first job within your business you won't be able to exit your business.

You need to be thinking about your exit right from day 1, and every day.

If you don't run the business as if you don't need to be there every day, you HAVE to be there every day.

Thinking about running the business without you there forces you to consider:
*how to hire
*how to delegate

If you are building your business for no reason other than it is what you love it isn't a business it is a hobby.
*Your business needs to be bigger than just yourself.

Having the exit plan is a commitment to:
*your customers
*your employees
*Your capital groups
*your family
===Growing your business===
*model
*mimic
*master
*multiply

If you are serious about your business you will pay it some attention.
===Staffing===
Building a business is not doing the work.  It is building the team:
*attracting
*finding (selecting)
*aligning
*developing
*retain
===Exiting===
You need to transfer the knowledge of the job to your "replacement" to allow you to exit.  Then you run parallel, and then once they are competent you can disengage from that role and move onto another.
 If you don't have an exit plan, you have a hobby not a business.
==Planning the Exit==
 How many of "whatever" do I need to have sold/control of in order to be able to Exit (retire) forever (and live off of passive income).

What you look for is what you get.  If you don't know to look for something, you won't pick it up.

You can't create freedom one transaction at a time (unless it is a HUGE transaction).

Follow those that have been through this process before.

Set targets.  Work the target.  Your potential is only limited by your confidence.
