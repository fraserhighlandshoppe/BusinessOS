cultivate a positive attitude on a daily basis. 
*maintain optimism, resilience, and a growth mindset to overcome challenges and drive sales success.

A Product of Your Environment: the influence of the sales environment on attitude and performance. 

surround yourself with positive influences and create a supportive atmosphere to foster a great attitude.

Tips to Have a Great Attitude: 
*practicing gratitude, 
*visualization, and 
*self motivation.

Your Attitude Defines You.  
*a salesperson’s attitude is a reflection of their character and professionalism. 
*consistently project a positive image that inspires trust and confidence in customers.

What You See & Say Creates Attitude: 
*perception and verbal expressions shape your attitude. 
*focus on positive aspects, 
*avoid negative self-talk, and 
*use empowering language to cultivate a winning attitude
