===Debt===
#Myth: All Debt is bad debt
#Debt is your distribution channel
#Debt is your ability to go in the market and produce more income
#Never use debt for consumption
#Use debt to build income
#Use debt when it gets you more money, when you get a write-off of the interest you are paying.
#Use it when it allows you to expand your empire.
