===Build your target audience===
A majority of people think they know their target audience but they are in fact missing it.
*People placate the audience they already have rather than work on the audience they don't have.
If you make it easy for your customers they will give your contact info out.

Connect with your audience. 
*Collect contact info.

Build a relationship.  This leads to retention.

===Define your Marketing Goals===
Without goals all of the ideas and tactics are pointless.

===Understand your ROI===
If you are spending money/time/effort you need to get an ROI.

To know if you have an ROI you need to measure activities.

If there is no ROI, you need to redirect those dollars/hours/effort.

===Get Personal===
You should have 150 data points about your customers.
*Favorite tv show
*favorite food
*What do they do on their holidays.
*Age
*Gender
*Marital status
*Kids
*Location (down to the zip code).
*Interests
*Likes
*Dislikes
*Values
*Culture
*Hobbies

Deals are a lot easier when you have something in common.

You don't need to know all data right up front.  However, run surveys over time to gather the data.
