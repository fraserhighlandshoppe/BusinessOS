===Core Values===
*Inspiration
*Discipline
**Lack of discipline means random things happen
**Need to inspire with the discipline
*Accountability
**Without accountability there can't be discipline.
*Transparency
**Need to explain to the staff where you are at, where you are going, why, etc.
**If staff knows where things are vs goals they can adjust their own work to help pull
*Alignment
**If staff isn't aligned then they won't be working towards the same things.
**No one will be serious about your goals if you aren't serious about theirs.
*Results
**What is the expectations (target)?
**Who owns the success?  
**Who is ensuring the targets are being worked towards?
