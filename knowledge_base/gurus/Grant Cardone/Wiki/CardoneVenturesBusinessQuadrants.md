==Biggest Problem==
Everyone says its Marketing, then Ops, then People, then Financing.

However, it is really a Person Problem.

What is the top 3 attributes of your ideal customer?
*If there is a pause, where you/your employee has to think about it, you don't know.
*If you don't know, how can you attract them?
*If you can't attract them, how can you sell them?
So if your people don't know who/what you are doing, there is the biggest problem.
===Grow the Business===
People take their foot off of the accelerator when the business is growing.

They are so worried about what ''might'' happen that they get distracted from the good that is happening.

===Boredom===
Most people get bored of doing something just before they break through.

Those that do make it through then re-discover the fun, because they are now bringing in people to help handle the business.

===Customer Segmentation===
Take your top 30% of your clients.  What do they look like (attributes).  Then go find more of them.  Trim off the bottom 30% to make room for the new.
