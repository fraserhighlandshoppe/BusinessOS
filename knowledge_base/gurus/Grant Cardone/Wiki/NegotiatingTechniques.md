==Negotiating Techniques==
Negotiation is the discussion towards an agreement.

You don't get what you deserve, you get what you negotiated.

You will negotiate for EVERY transaction you do.

You want to give them options A, B, and C.  C is ground zero.

You need to not have emotions during the negotiation.

Deal occurs when you feel you are getting more than you are giving up.  You want what the other side has more than you want what you have.

Deals are done when problems are solved.  Gaps can be filled.  Need to find the sticking point.

Any time you negotiate, always write it down.
*People don't believe what they hear, but they believe what they read.

Even the best negotiators in the world won't close every deal.  Therefore your FOLLOWUP also needs to be over the top!
*97% of deals aren't closed on the first negotiation.
===Remain Seated===
 https://cardoneuniversity.com/5-rules-to-hack-your-sales-game/
Missed by every seasoned prof, you’ll hardly ever close someone on your feet, negotiate terms on your seat. When you’re attempting agreement and closure, you also want your prospect seated. Even if your prospect stands up mid negotiation, you should remain seated, suggesting that you’re not done just yet. Don’t react. Stay seated. Staying seated shows your confidence and seriousness. If you stand up, it shows that something has changed. This may make your prospect leave and end the negotiations.
===White Flag===
#First step is to surrender.
##Give them what they ask
##Have them take care of items you need done (roof, kitchen, AC, etc... in real estate deal)
*A - 410 and you do it.
*B - 410 less the 110 cost and I do it
*C - no deal/sale

On deal A improve their situation - pay extra, change closing date to their benefit.
===Back to the Future===
Take the guy into the future.  

 Let's assume the sticking point has been handled. 
 What does it look like when I take your item and you take my offer?  
 What problem has been resolved.

Nobody sells anything unless they have a problem with it.

===Inquisitions (Hard Questions)===
The more sophisticated the negotiator the easier this is.
*Have you seen enough to make a decision.
*When is the last time you invested this much money
*Why was the last time you invested this much money
*What is the worst thing that can happen if this deal falls through
*What happens if you don't do this
*Why did you let me do this presentation
*What is the worst decisions you made in your life.
*When is the last time you made a bad decision
*When is the last time you made a great decision

Other side will have confidence in you if you ask these questions.

Give the buyer a bigger problem so you/they can make sense of the current one.
*How would you justify ...

If you were going to make the deal...
*how much money do you have you could spend



===The One Thing===
There is no one thing that will blow a deal.

However there is never more than 1 thing that prevents the deal.

All negotiations come down to 4 things
#Product
#Time
#Stall (I need to wait for...)
#Money
##Price
##Terms
##Contracts
##Obligation
##Rates

Need to keep pulling on the string.  They won't tell you the real thing the first time.

"What is the one thing holding you up.  What if I fixed it right now..."

===Selfish Child===
Your kid gets everything they want because they can't see the other person's side.

There is only 1 logic that matters when negotiating.  
*Never make sense of their logic.  
*Never support their case.
*Only make sense of ''MY'' story

I agree that is a good idea (white flag) but it doesn't benefit me so I'm not paying for it.  You want/need it and benefit from it, you pay for it (selfish).

===Time===
Time either benefits you, or the other side.

If the time benefits you, you are in control.

The buyer has 100% control of the deal in the last moments of the deal.

===Bring Buyer to Zero===
Don't chase what they are leaving.  They are going to ZERO.

Ground Zero is always choice C in the negotiating.

Ground Zero is where you get nothing, and I get nothing.
====Bad Deal====
Never stay with a bad deal.  If it was a good deal, but has changed over time, you need to leave or renegotiate!

If the deal is bad for you, it is probably bad for them, and if not now, will be when you go under!

Most people stay in a bad deal because they were raised to be honest and ethical.  Since this is emotional, most people avoid bringing it up and to avoid the conflict they don't renegotiate.

*We made a good deal
*I want to keep doing it
*Here is how it changed...
**Why I can't continue.
**what they aren't doing they agreed to...
===Role Reversal===
Once you have hit ground zero, you need to role reversal (become the other person).

What will happen to the other person if the deal falls through?  What goes through the other person's mind...
*stress
*problems
*pain
*headaches
*fears
*concerns

Make sense what is happening to him.  This gives you leverage.

===First Base (Pregnancy)===
If your partners don't win, you lose.

Have to get your partners to first base before you can get to second.

Need to make a proposal.  Use what-if...

 Ghengis was brutal.  If you aren't aware while being the seller, you are very vulnerable.

==How to Persuade Anyone==
 https://cardoneuniversity.com/persuade-anyone/

You could argue that “persuasion” is another word for selling. It definitely is a large portion of it. To get another person to do business with you will take some level of convincing. Being uncertain of this skill will, in turn, make you uncertain of your selling ability and income. In this article, you will learn how to persuade anyone— without any underhanded tricks.

===See Things Their Way FIRST===
The first thing you need to do to be able to persuade anyone, and the first rule of selling itself, is you have to agree with the other person. You can’t get other people to do what you want if you make them feel wrong about how they feel. They will become defensive and more solid in their viewpoint. 

Agreeing with the other person is the most senior rule in selling and persuading others. 

This is not the same thing as the old saying, “The customer is always right.” However, you have to agree with them before you start to shift their viewpoint. And to actually change their perspective, you have to do the next step I’m going to share with you… 
===Show Them, Don’t Tell Them ===
People believe what they see, not what they hear. This is true now more than ever. No one will take your word no matter how honest a person you are. You shouldn’t take this personally, but you will need to handle it if you want to persuade someone. Their distrust of you is not their problem. It’s yours. 

Third-party information is extremely valuable here. Remember, you agree with them first. Use the line, “I agree. I thought the same thing until….”, and present information that supports your price, viewpoint, etc. Doing so builds trust, credibility, and value. 

Follow these two steps, and you’ll be able to persuade anyone. No one makes it in business or in life without the support from others. You need to have the skills to present your ideas and products to the world to be successful.

===Treat them like they can===
 https://cardoneuniversity.com/5-rules-to-hack-your-sales-game/
When you treat your prospect as though they have money, they will act as though they have it. They’ll even act in regards to money they don’t even have. This is simple buyer psychology; if you treat someone in a certain manner, they will respond accordingly. Every behavior, word, and facial expression from you must communicate that you’re treating the prospect in front of you as though they are someone who will say yes and go along with your proposal.

===Always agree with the buyer===
 https://cardoneuniversity.com/5-rules-to-hack-your-sales-game/

Always, always, always agree with your buyer and NEVER disagree, no matter what they say. Now, this is completely different from the saying, “the customer is always right.” That’s absolutely not true, the customer isn’t always right. Whether they’re right or wrong isn’t the issue. If you want to resolve any sort of disagreement, it only requires you to agree with the other person’s point of view so you can actually handle the disagreement. Once you do this, you’ve created some commonality now. Disagreement only results in not closing a deal. Pro tip: you gotta be willing to lose the battle, to win the war (and win the sale).
