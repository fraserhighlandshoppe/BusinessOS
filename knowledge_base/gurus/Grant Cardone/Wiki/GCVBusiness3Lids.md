===3 Lids===
====Beliefs====
Your actions follow your beliefs.  

As you don't hit targets/thrive you lower your belief about what you can accomplish.
*This results in lowering the beliefs of those around you (employees).

You can't attract people better than yourself.  You are the highest example.

To attract high impact people, you have to have the highest belief about what is possible

85% of people can't believe something until they've seen it.

Successful entrepreneurs can believe something is possible before they can see it.
 Low belief is low activity
 Low activity is low results

====Operations====
The more effective you get, the higher results you get.

====Leadership====
If you don't have strong leadership skills you wont have success.

Leadership isn't telling people what to do.  It is to make other people's success easy.
*Create conditions so that they can succeed.
*Generate confidence about what is possible.

Leadership needs to be contagious.  
*Starts with ME leadership, becomes 
*We leadership

Your people need to help amplify your impact.

You need to coach people to get the results.

By the time you get to 8M you need a leadership team of 3
*The visionairy
*The operator
*The builder.

Once you get to 25M, it becomes US leadership


*The more people you throw at a problem the faster it gets done.
*Price is only an issue in the absence of value

Leader's responsibility to set the tone within the organization.
*Be as clear and concise as you can so there is no confusion

When you advertise for staff, are you advising what will bring life to them, or are you just advertising the work you don't like?
