==Grant Cardone Objection Handling==
===Reasons People Object===
All these "objections":
*“I need to think about it.” 
*“Let me talk to my spouse about it.”
*I don't have the money
*Time

They’re decisions the customer made for one simple reason.

They lacked trust.
*Trust in you, 
*trust in the product or service, or 
*trust that was lost somewhere during the sales process.

And when they don’t trust, they don’t buy. It’s as simple as that.

===GC Objective Handling===
Practice during training time so that you are able to handle in the close.  DON'T PRACTICE DURING THE CLOSE!!
 You shouldn't be surprised by an objection.  
The objections you hear will be the ones you walk in prepared to hear.
 In most industries money isn't really a problem.
There are really only 2 objections 
#They aren't the decision maker.
#They don't think it will work.

====Handling the Objections====
#Agree with them!
#Move into negotiation/close
##Just because you agreed with them doesn't mean they are right.  Agreement means no friction.


 “Your price is too high.”  
You’re right, and I get it. In fact, I’ve heard the same thing from a lot of my customers. Now let me show you why and why this deal is a no-brainer.

 “I can’t make such a rash decision.”  
I agree. In fact, I never make rash decisions, and I wouldn’t expect you to, either. In fact, let me show you my competitor's offers so you can see why this would be a solid decision for you right now.
