===Fixing their problems so they aren't your problem===
Have a bonus structure where your employees can earn extra, while at the office (not on their own time).  This way they are engaged.

If they are working side hustles, they are dis-engaged and worried about the other hustles.

People that are in poverty or Middle Class can't realistically help others
*Their budgets are based upon them
*They don't account for inflation
*They don't have the time freedom to consider others.
