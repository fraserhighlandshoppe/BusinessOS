==Prospecting Tips==
These are only five of my tips for world-class prospecting that blew up my 15+ businesses and I’m confident they can do the same for you.
===Know your prospects===
One of the biggest mistakes people make in prospecting is that they try to treat every prospect the same when they’re not. There’s not a one size-fits-all formula for every call you make on a potential customer. You need to meet your prospect where they’re at in the buying relationship, and over time, help them move closer to a buying decision. While every prospect is unique, all of them will fall into one of these four major categories:

====Cold leads====
These are people who don’t know you, and you don’t know them. You have their name and contact information, but you may not even know the name of their business or if they could even use your product or service. Contacting these leads is the worst strategy for your prospecting as it’s least likely to generate any promising prospects. These are the kinds of leads that feed massive call-center telemarketers and give prospecting a bad name. So do yourself a favor, don’t even bother with these.
====Cold targeted leads====
These are people who don’t know you, but you know that they fit your ideal customer profile and could benefit from your services. Maybe you’ve never contacted them, or maybe you have and it’s just been a while. But certainly, these leads wouldn’t be existing customers. This area is where all-star sales people shine. If you can take cold-targeted leads and warm them up to the point they become buyers, that demonstrates mastery of sales and a nearly limitless earning potential.
====Warm leads====
These are people who know you and are familiar with your business. They could be 
*past for current customers, 
*referrals, 
*inbound leads, or 
*customers who come to you to service a product… even if they didn’t buy from you. (Like a mechanic who fixes cars that he didn’t sell.) 
====Hot leads====
these are the prospects you’re well acquainted with, or at least, they’re very well acquainted with you and your business. They know about your product and know they want to buy. All they need is someone to close them.

Ensure that you and your sales team have a unique script for each lead type and proper strategies to handle the needs of each. This is the only effective way to prospect. Pay special attention to your targeted cold leads as these will quickly become the lifeblood of your outbound lead generation (you’ll quickly run through your Warm and Hot leads). So to truly master the prospecting game, you and your team need to perfect your cold outreach strategy.
===Newsflash: prospecting is not sales===
Prospecting is brutal enough without letting unreasonable expectations make it even worse. Many times, people who are trying to generate leads for your business think they’re trying to generate an immediate sale. They’re hoping to end the call with a “yes” with ink on a contract. So when they go through hundreds of calls without closing a single deal, they convince themselves that “prospecting is dead,” or something else equally ridiculous. That’s just wrong, they’re convincing themselves they’re failing because they’ve got the wrong mindset about what prospecting is.

Prospecting isn’t about “getting a yes” or closing a deal, it’s about 
*getting out of obscurity, 
*rekindling a relationship with a lead, and 
*nurturing that relationship. 

Most often, the desired result of prospecting is merely gathering more information about the lead and scheduling the next contact. That’s a win in prospecting. You’ve warmed that lead up again, you’ve become top of mind again, and that’s mission accomplished. 

If you go into prospecting hoping to close deals, you’ll be sorely disappointed and probably quit. Get your mindset right first, then follow it up with a prospecting attitude.
===Adopt a world-class prospecting attitude===
I can simply define this as an attitude of community, giving, and service. You’ve likely heard me say that most of selling is “give, give, give.” In fact, I’d guess 80% of the content I provide people is free, the other 20% is where the money is. That’s because the 80% that’s free is about getting people close to me. It’s about generating valuable leads and nurturing them until they’re ready to join the top 20% who want to get the most out of their relationship with me.

Whenever you or your team members get on a prospecting call, it should be from the position of providing some service to them. 

Maybe it’s 
*free advice, 
*a free trial of a product, 
*a free webinar, 
*“just checking in to see if there’s anything I can do for you,” 
*or reminding them about an important date coming up that even they forgot about. 

Heck, I’ve even called on businesses and offered to do a free seminar on how to get the most out of one of my competitor’s products. I’m telling you, it’s about giving and service.

Secondly, you need to have an attitude of resilience and indifference to upsets. Because the reality is, when you’re prospecting you’ll get a lot of rejection. Lots of negativity… even hate. You need to be able to let it roll off your back. Take it with a smile. And the best way to do that is…
===Do the math===
With the right attitude, every call, no matter how bad — is a victory. In my office, we have “hang up high fives.” People get celebrated when they go through a particularly brutal shutdown. They make a game of it, which is the only healthy way to do this, by the way. And the only reason they can do it is because they know that every call they make, good or bad, is worth money. It’s one call closer to a deal.

But how can they know that? It’s all because they’ve done the math. They’ve traced the path of success and crunched some simple numbers that give them confidence and certainty of their efforts. See for yourself what I’m talking about. Run these numbers for your own business:
*How many prospecting calls does it take to set an appointment with a lead? 
*How many appointments does it take to sign them up for a demo?
*How many sign ups do you need to get for someone to actually show up for a demo?
*How many of those do you need for somebody to agree to a contract?
*How many contracts need to go out before you close a deal?
 
You must go through the whole process from start to finish and figure out your numbers. In this way, you can reverse engineer the science behind generating a single deal in your company. Once you know that, you’ve got the key to limitless prospecting confidence, because now you can turn prospecting into a game. Do the math like I’ve described and you and your people know exactly how many calls they need to make to get a deal. It’s like walking into a tree-felling competition and you’re the only one who knows exactly how many swings required to bring the tree down. That’ll bring confidence, right?

If you don’t do this, you’ll be walking into battle blind folded, completely unprepared for what’s ahead with no way to justify the massive amount of rejection and “failure” you’ll encounter.
===Prospect daily===
As I’ve made very clear from the beginning, prospecting is the life and salvation of your business’ future. Your inflow is proportional to your outflow. If you don’t give enough, you’ll never get enough. It’s like an infallible law of the universe.

You and your team need to set aside a certain amount of time every single day that is only for prospecting. 

 This time should be sacred, and preferably, the first hours of the day. 

Prospecting is difficult, which means that most people are afraid of it and avoid it. Around the office it’s known as “call avoidance,” and it’s a deadly poison in your business’ sales process. The antidote for fear is action. So set a period of time every day to relentlessly and mercilessly crank out your prospecting calls to keep filling your pipeline to overflowing.
