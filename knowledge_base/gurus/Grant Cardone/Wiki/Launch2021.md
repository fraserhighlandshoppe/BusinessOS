 Use the System!

 Make the System work for you!
Collect 3 statistics for every role within the company/organization.
*calls made
*leads handled
*Demos performed
*...

You need to compare today to yesterday; this week to last week.  This is the only way to know if you are up or down.

You need to be able to influence/control the stat for it to have any meaning.  This is especially true for employees in the roles.  If they can't affect the stat they won't care.  So if all of their stats are not within their control they are going to be demotivated and not work towards the goal.
