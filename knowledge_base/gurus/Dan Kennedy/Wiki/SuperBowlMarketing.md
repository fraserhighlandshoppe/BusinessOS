==Market yourself every single day==
Dan says, "he does at least one thing each day to prod, poke and rustle up business every day.  What can you do each day to move business your way?  This doesn't have to necessarily take a lot of time."

For example, you could make phone calls to customers you haven't heard from in awhile to find out how you can help them.  Forward an article to a prospect about something you think would help them.  Spend fifteen minutes a day engaging in conversations on social media with your customers and prospects.  Or use one of your GKIC marketing systems such as sending out the "Giorgio Letters" that Dan teaches about in Magnetic Marketing.

==Do more and different stuff==
Dan says, "If you want a good New Year's resolution, it'd be to do more stuff, constantly, to kick up such a storm around you that you start feeling in peril of not being able to handle it all."  What can you add to your Marketing this year that will help you consistently kick up new clients?

Pick something new to add to your Marketing this year that you aren't already doing.  Test it and give it a fair try.  For example, if your business is all online, consider incorporating direct mail pieces to your customers this year.  Start attracting new customers by booking some speaking engagements in front on your ideal prospects. Or send out press releases to get stories in media whose focus is your target audience.
   
==Put everything you do in a "financial frame."==
In order to make your financial goals, look at what your Marketing goal is with each piece and then package it accordingly.

A great way to Market your business and raise your profile in your niche is to write a book.  If you plan to write a book, before you print your book, think about how you want to use it.  Do you plan to put your book in a "shock and awe" package?  Then your book should have a hard cover.  Are you looking to get it into a bookstore? Soft cover is the route you'd take.  Or do you plan on selling it from the front of the room at speaking engagements? In that case, you'll want a big book format.  Same product, but different formats will work better depending on your audience.  Take the time to think about what you want each campaign to accomplish and what format will work harder for you.  Details like this pay off.
   
Take a few minutes today to look at your 2012 Marketing Plan (if you don't have one yet and if you're serious about
achieving your goals this year, you need to put one together ASAP.)

Add to your plan to do at least one thing, no matter how small, each day to Market your business.  Make sure you aren't
using "Superbowl Marketing thinking" and putting too much of your Marketing resources into one event, month or season.
Find at least one new Marketing technique to add to your business this year.  And make a note to review each campaign
to make sure the finer details match up with your goals and target audience.

When you do, you'll be surprised at how much these can change your business to create not only more income, but a more
consistent stream of income.
