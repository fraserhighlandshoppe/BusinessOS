==Recession Survival Guide==
From a newsletter:

The pre-recession we are in now features runaway inflation fueled by Biden Administration policies and Fed raised interest rates in combat with each other, big debt bubbles and default risks thus shaky banks, credit and cash liquidity drying, and loads of uncertainty. 

 

This one wasn’t necessary, but here we are. We might see a classic recession or we might see long-term stagflation and/or we might see sector crashes; commercial real estate likeliest first. The specifics may not matter that much to you, and you don’t have to be an economist to identify and implement smart, proactive measures for surviving whatever flavor of recession we get.

 

Bears fatten themselves up and hibernate, just waiting it all out, emerging in Spring. If you are quite wealthy, you might try that, but if you have a going business you put it at high risk of stalling out, its slumber permanent.
