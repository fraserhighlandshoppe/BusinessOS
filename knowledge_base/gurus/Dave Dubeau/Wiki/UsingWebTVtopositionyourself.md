Using online video to position yourself above your competition.


==Why Video==
*KLT (Know, Like and Trust)
**People are more likely to buy from you if they know, like and trust you.
**You won't find investors if people don't know, like and trust you.
**Total strangers who have been watching your shows "know" you.
*Can build relationships with video
*Extends the lifetime of your relationship with clients.
*1 to many relationships
*Easy, free
*Little to no investment
*SEO - get found
**Appear higher up in the search results, especially the competitors aren't posting video yet
**If it is the only thumbnail (from video) on the page, you get more clicks
**Google loves video because the searchers love video
*Drive Sales
**reduces the buying cycle.
*Makes you a leading authority
*Can help you build a massive list
*help you create products for your business
*Gives you a celebrity status.
*Increased satisfaction
**reduces refunds


Video provides you an unfair advantage :-)

===Zappos.com experience===
"We noticed that the people who saw the videos purchased more and returned less.  This implies that they are making better decisions and when they got the products they were happier" - Laurie Williams - Zappos.com.  
*They also found that real people outperform models in terms of response to the video.  Perfection appears too slick and therefore a sales job!
*Same with realness versus production quality videos.  Real videos work better.
*Sweet spot is 60 seconds for a video.
*Zappos is now creating over 400 videos a day.  (Zappos sells shoes).

==Youtube==
*2 Billion videos.  24 hours of videos being generated every minute.
*Available 24 hours a day
*Is #2 search engine on the internet.
**This will drive customers to your store/business.
*Strike when the iron is HOT.
**Impossible to re-enact the perfect testimonial.
**Ensure you have cameras around so you can record.
*Ensure you have your own business channel.
**Never let someone else host your videos on their channel
*Build friends and subscribers
*Can put the videos into your blogs, website, emails.  The more people watch them the better chance they appear at the top of the youtube search result.
*When setting up the channel, look under the videos and playlist tab - set the "autoplay featured video" setting.  Ensures more views.

TVs are being made now with youtube channels.
