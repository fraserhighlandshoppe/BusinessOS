===4 pillars===
====Income Statement and Balance Sheet====
Fundamental Analysis
*Personal
*Corporate
*Sovereign
Gives you an idea of WHAT is going to happen
====Stock Charts====
Technical Analysis
*Patterns
*Indicators
Gives you an idea of WHEN it is going to happen
====Cash Flow====
*Entry, Target, Exit(s)
*Reward to Risk Ratio (ensure greater than 2)
**Once we hit the target, we adjust the Entry, Target and Exit.
====Risk====
Amateurs use diversification hoping the winners outnumber the losers.
Pros use cash flow strategies
They also have Exit Strategies
Properly using OPTIONS acts as insurance.

Types
*non-systemic (i.e. British Petroleum)
**one company has problems
*systemic (i.e. sub-prime mortgage meltdown)
**S&P goes down hard

Warren Buffet says diversification makes no sense.
*value investing - unjustifiably low values compared to intrinsic value.
*fundamental analysis gives you the intrinsic value.

George Soros tries to catch the trends early, and then catch the trend reversals.
*look at trends - SMA(20).  Trend reversals happen as price crosses the 20SMA
**Be in the market/stock when it is above the line, and out when below.
