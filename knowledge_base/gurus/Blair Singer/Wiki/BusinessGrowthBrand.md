==Complicated Brand Problem==
Example:  Insurance companies say that if you are breathing, you can be a client.
*This is trying to be everything to everyone.

===Complication===
Key too failure is trying to please everyone.
*Who is your target market.
*What do those customers want?
===Remove the complication===
Pick a Niche that you can actually do something with.
*You understand their lingo and jargon
*You can understand their problems
*You can help them solve the problem.

Once you get this down well, you can probably replicate across industries.

==Marketing Funnel==
If you only have 1 product to offer, it is an all or nothing.  Real tough to sell.

You want to have a funnel - huge opening at the top, small opening at the bottom.

Lower the risk in time/money at the opening end of the funnel
*Free products or reports or ...
*These free items becomes a lead generator.
*Make them easy, so that the customer gets some experience with you
Then as the people work into the funnel, they are further qualified.
*More offerings to the people who made it this far.  Can be free, or low cost
Then further up-sell items, etc.
*More expensive items, more details or information.
Try before you buy, with the customer trying more expensive items along the way.
*This allows you to determine what level of items your customers want.
*If you ask the general public if they want all your products, they will say yes.
**Reality is most people won't want your deepest in the funnel product.

Remember that just because you are ready to sell they are not ready to buy.

Keep adding value, some day they will take up your offer and become a life long customer.

Another analogy - don't ask your date to marry you on the first date.  You shouldn't be trying to get to the honeymoon before the first date is over.

==5 Steps (Calls) to selling==
#1st call - make the appointment
#Appointment - meet and schedule the demo
#Demo - show the product and then schedule the proposal
#Proposal - deliver the proposal.
#Final Closing

Each of these steps should be adding value and then providing the opportunity to move to the next step.

Have an audit method to discover the customers that are falling off the path and provide the ability to get back on track.

===Team building to deliver brand promise===
How do you get everyone on the same vision?

*Articulate the business
**What does it offer the customers
**What does it offer those that are in it.
*Create the mission
*What are the rules of the team (Code of Honour)
**Be on time
**If you have a problem, go direct
**Everyone has to sell
**Do whatever it takes to win as long as ethical, legal
**Integrity
**Honesty
**Family values and support
*What is the brand promise
**Only a certain type of person can be on the team.  Has to be willing to live to the code of honour, and the Brand Promise.
**Internal brand promise - what is being delivered to the team
***What is the employees receiving in order to motivate them to sell your product Brand Promise.

===Stay in contact through the sales cycle===
How do you become an invited guest rather than an annoying pest?

*Build Trust
**Be good to your word
**Making agreements and keeping them.  
***These can be simple agreements, small agreements.  Idea is to show trustworthyness.
****i.e. your client shows an interest in golf.  You happen to have interesting articles about golf.
****Offer to deliver a copy of the article a couple of days later
****When delivering, they will probably mention something else that you can deliver on
****When it becomes time to bid on a product RFP, you have now built trust and demonstrated value.
*Offer to do something that creates a reason to followup and get back to them.
*Serve first.
**Seen as positive if you are adding value.
*The longer/more frequent your contact with the client, the higher the probability of a sale.

Business is a marathon, not a sprint.  It is for the long haul that is important.  You need to continue to test your offers, segment your market.  But you need to look past the next couple of weeks and look into the long term sustainability of your Brand Promise.

===What programs work well for small/medium businesses===
Try before you buy.  Give the clients the experience of who you are.

Affiliation is one of the best marketing approaches.  You can ride the coattails of your partners where they have already earned the trust of the potential clients.

Attend events to generate exposure to your business.

Joint Ventures.  With other businesses that have the same customer base but non competing products, you can be value-add for each other in your marketing.

===Getting a Startup Started===
How do I sell the startup to investors and clients?

Startups measure your passion for the idea and business.
*Get your first testimonial.
**Maybe you do your first presentation or sale for free in exchange for the testimonial.
**Create a prototype.
**Test the prototype
**Sell people on the vision
***What is the market
***How will this business weather the market
***How will this business weather the competition
**Have a sales partner assist with the first sales
***You often need an outside catalyst to give you that burst of energy to get the first stage done
**Practice delivering your sales pitch.  Deliver it often, to different people.
***You will become congruent with your message
***The more congruent and passionate you are about the product, the more attracted to it your clients will become
**Raise client's energy on the product and they become attracted to it.
*Originate.  Create something that isn't already there.
