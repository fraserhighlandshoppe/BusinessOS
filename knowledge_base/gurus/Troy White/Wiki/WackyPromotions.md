Fewer people are mailing now adays.

Social media is starting to take over.  Personality is one of the keys to marketing.  With personality being the focus of your marketing, you start to stand out in the clutter.

Once you have the right message, with a few minor tweaks allow you to change the target of your marketing.  Targeting niches will grow your sales faster than generic messages.

Every time you "touch" the consumer it is marketing.  This includes blog posts, articles, etc.

Most everyone is sending the same type of message.  Using a wacky message, your customers will start looking forward to opening your message.  By using whacky ways, you become the one your customer wants to deal with.

Keep a file of every day events of things that can be used for blogs, postings, email messages, etc.
*Look in the news
**Find what people are glued to i.e. celebs
***Charlie Sheen and Mental Health
***Spoof Bieber's hair being auctioned after a hair cut

==Amish Furniture sales==
Picture of a beautiful 30ish girl in skirt, long boots.  Headline contains back pain, Amish, furniture, etc.
*Picture catches the eye
*The Amish being in the headline intrigues
*Details then attract and sink in.

==Steps==
*List out what is thought about your business.  Both truth and perception.
*List out what the characteristics of the product
*Find the opposites of the perceptions, etc.
**2 extreme opposites plus supporting image can't fail to get attention (i.e. Amish and mini-skirt)
**A really striking graphic that talks to the product is a win win.

Tie in unusual events to your business.

The media is always looking for interesting stories.  i.e 9th day, 9th month, 2009 - becomes 999 campaign.

==90 day marketing campaign==
*Commit to the campaign.
*What kind of campaign will you do.
*How often
*Random thoughts and pictures tracked - tickler/ideas file
*Plan out the campaign
**Write the article
**Get a web page up
**Ensure timing is right
**List what actions on what day/date
*get over the analysis paralysis.
**Commit to writing X minutes per day.  Write something.
*Share the calendar with your staff and accountability partners
*75% content to 25% promotion
**Ensure it is valuable content. Otherwise clients unsubscribe.
*Test the reaction.
**Not everyone will like what you are doing.  You will get complaints
**Dan Kennedy says if you haven't annoyed someone by noon you aren't doing your job.
**The ones that complain the most are those that purchased the least.
*collect
*take action
