==Word of Mouth Worthy==

===Your story can help you connect with more people and build your tribe===
*How to stand out - too much clutter
**Everyone has a newsletter, site, blog, facebook, twitter
*How do you engage them?
*How do you keep them on your list?
*How to start word of mouth - get your list to talk about you with others

Study: non profit tested a story versus the fact.  118% more donations for story than facts.  Combination was 25% more than facts but 39% less than story.

Hard to find good stories in the business world but easy to create

Low cost = big opportunity for small businesses

Internet marketing - traffic is King

to drive traffic- write a story about the people you like to follow.
*What makes them great
*Make sure they know about it.  Chances are they will link to it.
*Fans find the article, join your newsletter etc

Create an inventory sheet of your stories
*Take an hour or two undisturbed
*Grab pen and paper - studies show you are more creative with pen and paper than computer
*write about your past, pets, jobs, education, things you love, things you hate, your successes, your failures
**Very few people share their failures - they try to hide them.  So by sharing, you stand out.
*Don't act like a big company. 
*Bring a spokesperson to the forefront.
**Face of the company - Me and You approach
**People want a deeper connection, especially with repeat sales/relationships

Most businesses don't know how to get a customer back.  
*Groupon is now training the public to think everything should be 80-90% off
*Businesses think its great to have new customers, but they generally lose money on the transaction and don't get repeat sales
*Get the contact details in exchange for the price break
**Once per month, send a marketing story
**Tell stories about someone/something in the company.  DON'T send a picture of a product with a price.  Stand out, and get a smile.  This will entice them to read, and keep coming back.

Limiting belief: I'm just a solopreneur

===Success Stories===
Superstore ads: Galen Weston (CEO) is in all ads.  The women in Canada is starting to get a crush on him.  It used to be about the store and brand.  But now it is a relationship with someone in the business.

Office supply store - their ad talks about the "store" dog Roxy in an article Roxy's Rant talks about the activities of the dog - stuff it gets into, etc.
===What is story selling===
===Create your own story===
Using these stories, the clients/subscribers feel they know you.  They have that comfort feeling of you, so that translates over to the business. (Credibility, Trust)  This really breaks down barriers.

If you collect the events of your day, this will lead to stories that you can expand upon.  Whenever you have some wait time, that is when you can expand the stories until they are ready for use.

Easiest way to create a marketing breakthrough is to look at a different industry, find something that works, and bring it over to yours.

 Monthly themes to your marketing.
 i.e. March 9th 
 *Ash wednesday
 *Cabin Fever (spring is coming)

Media loves a good story.  The more you get used to telling a good story the more opportunities you will have to get in front of them.  Especially if the story isn't self promotional.

Great stories mixed into your packaging (Moleskine, Britton, Apple)

You, the character, the story is (Chris V, Kinjos, Hugging Butcher

Your product has a story built in (J Peterman)

You build a great story into your product (i.e. Freding's wine drinking cows (selling beef))

You use your product in a way that creates a story (Blendtec (blenders))
