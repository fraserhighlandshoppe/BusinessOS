Debbie:

Toxic emotions are the emotions that we’ve made wrong or the people
around us have made wrong that we don’t allow healthy expressions
for. So, hurt, we all get hurt, but most people can’t just say, “Wow, that
just hurt me.” So, they suppress it. They deny it. They eat over it. They

hope that you feel some sadness. If you lose something you love, it’s
healthy to experience some sadness. If somebody violates your
boundary or somebody else’s boundary or your child’s boundary,
should you get angry? That is a healthy emotion, but if we don’t find
healthy ways to express them, then they become toxic, and then they
can explode.
I use the example – I like to write in metaphor because it really helps
me. A volcano, they happen because there’s no steam. The lava is
beneath the surface. If there’s a place for that steam to come out, we
don’t have to have a volcano. It’s when there isn’t enough places for
that energy to come out, that all of a sudden it explodes. I’m not a
scientist. I’m sure I didn’t do that well, but you get the point.
