the hassle. The reson why is because air travel to St. Barth is severely

forced to stand in line at the airport, drag your luggage behind you while you

your flight. Meanwhile, the rich people with the mony for the chartered
flights have an agent to take their luggage and passports through customs
while they drink leisurely in a lounge and wait for their air-conditioned
vehicle to take them straight to their plane.

down, and was able to build his company up in just seven years from idea to
amazing. Thirty percent of St. Barth’s travel is now done through him – and
it’s only growing from there.
