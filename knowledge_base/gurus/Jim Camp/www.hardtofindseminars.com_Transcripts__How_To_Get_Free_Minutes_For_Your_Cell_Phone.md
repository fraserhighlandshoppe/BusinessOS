How To Get Free Minutes For Your Cell Phone
Would you love to listen to Michael Senoff’s HardToFindSeminars audio interviews on your cell phone? It would be
perfect for not tying up your land-line home phone or for enjoying audio interviews while driving, exercising, standing in
line or at night time in bed before you go to sleep.
If you already have a cell phone plan that has unlimited long distance minutes, you’re ready to go! Go to page two for
dial in number and access code. Of for a full listing go to http://www.hardtofindseminars.com/on_the_go.htm
However, if you do not have unlimited long distance minutes, there may be a way for you to get special free long
distance plans already offered by your cell phone carrier.
Here are plans offered by the major cell phone carriers that you can sign up that will allow you to get unlimited long
distance minutes for selected phone numbers.
When inquiring to your current cell phone carrier, ask how their unlimited calling to selected numbers works with the
plan that you already have (Single Line Plan or Shared/Family Line Plan).
Verizon – The Friends and Family Plan
If you are a Verizon customer and have a Single Line Plan with a certain number of Anytime Minutes, you can
select up to 5 telephone numbers for unlimited calling. Certain rules may apply.
Call Verizon Customer Support to see if you already quality for the Friends and Family Plan.

T-Mobile – The My Faves Plan
The customer can get an Individual My Faves plan and can call up to 5 telephone numbers for unlimited
calling. Certain rules may apply.
Prices depend on the number of Whenever minutes the customer has signed up for.
Call T-Mobile Customer Support to see if you already quality for their My Faves Plan.

AT&T – The A-List Plan (available 9/20/09)
If you are an AT&T customer and have a Single Line Plan a certain number of minutes, you can select up to 5
telephone numbers for unlimited calling. Certain rules may apply.
Call AT&T Customer Support to see if you already quality for their A-List Plan.

Sprint – The Simply Everything Plan
This should not be confused with Sprint’s new “Any Mobile, Any Time” Plan because it only applies to calling
other cell phones. You need the ability for unlimited long distance calling to a land line.
The Simply Everything Plan applies to calling specific land lines as well as cell phones. It has “unlimited
everything” for one price. Certain rules may apply.
Call Sprint Customer Support to see if you already quality for their Simply Everything Plan.
