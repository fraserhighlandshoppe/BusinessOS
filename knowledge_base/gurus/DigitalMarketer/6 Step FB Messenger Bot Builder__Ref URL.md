Ref URL
The Ref URL growth tool creates a unique link that, when clicked, opens a conversation inside Facebook Messenger.
It’s best used to generate Messenger conversations from another source, like:
Email
A webinar or Facebook Live event
Your website
Business cards
Speaking on stage
Other traffic platforms
Here’s an example of how it’s used at DigitalMarketer to move people from their email list to their Messenger list:
If you clicked that link in the email, it would automatically open up a Messenger conversation with our chatbot.
To set this up inside of ManyChat, just choose the Messenger Ref URL under Growth Tools:
You’ll see a screen that looks like this:
All you have to specify is (1) the opt-in message and (2) the sequence you’d like to subscribe them to.
Keep in mind, when using this growth tool the user will first see a Get Started button when they click on your link. So they’ll have to click on that to be subscribed to your list.
Once that’s done, just click Setup to get your Ref URL:
And that’s it! Just copy that that URL and use it anywhere you want to be able to send people straight to a Messenger conversation.
