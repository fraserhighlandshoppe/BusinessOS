Video Script­ish
Objective 1: Give Them Something To Look For
1. “Congratulations on your purchase of <<PRODUCT>>”
a. Pro Tip: Don’t fake this. Be genuinely excited and thankful for them as a client.
2. “My name is <<NAME>> and I’m the <<TITLE>> here at <<COMPANY>>”
a. Pro Tip: Don’t be boring, but don’t be fake either. You’re job is to keep their
attention so you have to be engaging.
3. “Our team is getting to work packaging up your order right now.”
a. Pro Tip: Shoot the video in the place where the orders are actually packaged and
show a team member.
4. “I want to do 2 things: First, say thanks for being a client, and second, show you
what you’re about to get so you recognize it when it shows up...”
a. Pro Tip: You’re selling them on the idea that seeing the package before it arrives
will be more valuable to them. Don’t be boring here.
5. “Your <<PRODUCT>> is going to come in a box that looks like this…”
a. Pro Tip: Show the box and turn it around and over so they can see it
b. Pro Tip 2: Highlight and unique markings that will help them recognize it
c. Pro Tip 3: This is more effective if you have a cool box/envelope

Objective 2: Have Them Visualize Using What They’re About To Get
1. “Now the fun really starts when you open it up…”
a. Pro Tip: Remember, your job is to be engaging (but not cheesy)
2. “Here’s what you’re going to find inside…”
a. Pro Tip: Take pieces out one at a time and take your time explaining them
b. Pro Tip 2: Consider taking the first item out but setting it aside and telling them
you’ll talk about it later (keeps them on the video)
c. Pro Tip 3: With each item that you take out, describe what it’s for, what it will do
for them, and then a way that another client has benefited from it.

Objective 3: Get Them Anticipating The Bonus
1. “So, that’s almost everything that comes in the box, except at the last minute I
decided to throw in an extra bonus…”
a. Pro Tip: Describe what it is and why they should be excited about it (in other
words, what it will do for them).
b. Pro Tip 2: Don’t tell them what it is. Just tell them what it will do for them.
