Understand the Objective
In this Execution Plan (EP), you’ll learn how to build a Facebook Messenger marketing chatbot to...
Connect with your audience
Acquire leads for your business, and
Convert them into new customers
Messenger marketing is the marketing channel of the future. It’s the best way out there to connect with your audience in a way they’re comfortable with: using short, conversational messages that are delivered instantly… and all using a platform (Facebook Messenger) they’re already familiar with.
But while Messenger marketing has a TON of potential, it’s so new that many marketers still haven’t figured out how to use it as an effective marketing channel. One common mistake I see is companies treating Messenger as if it were the same thing as email… which it definitely is NOT.
But don’t worry because I’ve got you covered in this EP. Just follow the steps below, and by the time you’re finished, you’ll have everything you need to create an effective Facebook Messenger acquisition bot.
A Quick Note on Messenger Marketing Platforms
There are several different Messenger marketing platforms available like ManyChat and ChatFuel. You are, of course, free to use any tool you want. I really love and recommend ManyChat, so all the examples I show in this EP will be inside of the ManyChat dashboard.
