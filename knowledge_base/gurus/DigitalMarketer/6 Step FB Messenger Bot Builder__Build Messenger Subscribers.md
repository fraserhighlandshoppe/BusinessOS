Build Messenger Subscribers
One goal of Messenger campaigns is to build your list of subscribers.
Similar to email marketing, in Messenger marketing you have a list of subscribers—people who have opted in to receive messages from you.
And the bigger your list of subscribers, the better! Because you’ll be able to reach more people and generate more sales.
Actually, let me add 1 caveat to that statement:
The bigger your list of relevant, interested subscribers, the better!
Just like with email marketing, you want your list to be targeted. So, if you sell products for yoga instructors, it’s better to have a small list made entirely of yoga instructors… instead of a huge list where only 1% of people are yoga instructors.
Keep your targeting tight and focused to build a list of highly engaged prospects. This will work out to your advantage because you will be able to send focused messages that will really resonate with your subscribers’ problems and desires.
You’ll also have clean data because your analytics won’t be muddied up with a lot of non-interested subscribers who are less likely to open or click on your messages.
And finally, here’s 1 last thing to keep in mind:
Any Messenger marketing campaign that you promote to cold traffic (people who have never heard of your brand) will help build subscribers (even if it’s not the primary goal of that campaign).
It doesn’t take much to add somebody to your Messenger list. All you have to do is get them to interact with your chatbot by sending you a message or even just clicking 1 of your pre-written quick replies.
Just 1 tap—that’s all it takes.
And that’s something that will happen naturally over the course of many Messenger marketing campaigns, even if building subscribers isn’t the primary goal of that campaign.
