==Web Sales Secrets==
Your website works for you 24x7.  It is a powerful sales force.

The task of putting it together can seem overwhelming.

Your COPY is one of the most effective way to connect with your ideal client.

You need to connect with your client.  People are more skeptical, don't like to be "yelled at" or spoken down to by the marketing.

If your site is a passive/pretty placeholder it won't get much action.  If your site is ugly and useless, chances are it is cluttered with too much information.


===5 Elements your site must have to build relationships and get more sales===
Your website is never done.  Needs to be regularly reviewed.  
*Ideal client may change.
*products and services may change.


*Website and copy needs to ENGAGE.
**information is clearly stated with compelling copy.
**headline catches attention
**asking people to do something
**speaks to ideal client (target market)
**Free offers
**quizes
**games

*Your website must INFORM.  
**how will they benefit from your products or services
**how are you solving their problems.
***testimonials
***showcase your products
**Never assume people will connect the dots
***Lay out here is what I do, here is what you get
*Develop Reltationships
**free taste
***report/audio/...
**welcome mat to business - like giving them a cup of tea.
*Site must flow well
**logical and strategic steps.
**lead the viewer to go to the next step.
**overall site strategy.
**each page has a strategy.
**ask people to take a step on EVERY page.
***now that you've downloaded the report, would you like to purchase the tools?
***now that you've purchased the tools would you like a free consult?
**each page in a sequence of steps needs to be related.
**people are busy.  
***They need to see your message 7 times before they take action.  
***You need invitations everywhere.
***people won't browse your offerings.  They will search for solutions to their current burning problems.
*Must promote action
**invite visitor to do something that engages, informs and creates the relationship.
**why do they need the product
**why do they need the product NOW
**how can you incenticize the purchase

===2 key reasons owners fail to make profitable sites===
*site isn't connecting with ideal clients
*no strategy in place
Your website is the hub of the wheel.  It is the least expensive way to improve your conversion.

===When to listen and when not to listen to your web designer===
You want the sites to be beautiful but the marketing must come first.

====Pages needed====
*Home
**First words customer reads - needs to POP
**Headlines (takes 50-250 to get the right 1)
**testimonials
**emotional power words
**People decide to purchase based upon emotion (needs to feel excited/relief - solve problems)
**People rationalize their purchase with logic.
*About
**Terms and Conditions
**Rates (sometimes)
**testimonials
*Events
*Blog
*privacy policy
*contact info - address, email, phone, copyright
