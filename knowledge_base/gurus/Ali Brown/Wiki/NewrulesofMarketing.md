==Relationship rings==
*Email subscriber list
**Personal or company blog
***Micro blogs (twitter)
****Networking sites (Facebook)
Email is the most engaged, Facebook is the least engaged.

The point of social media is to get your friends/followers lower into the rings.

These relationships being built should not be an accident.  Like a dating relationship, you need to continue to do things, plan it out, work on it, etc.

==Process==
Email + CRM
*If your customer is being offered multiple things, and chooses one of them, it can quit sending the other offer too.
*Layer smart automation on top of the offers.
*Multi-media
*Auto thank you letters
*Email + CRM + eCommerce + Affiliate modules

==Roadblocks==
*Multiple system madness - your systems don't talk to each other
*Follow-up failure - people will buy when they want to, not when you want them to.
**Manual Process.  Takes effort.  Therefore people let this drop
