The recession "officially" ended in June 2010.  But most people feel that their business is stuck.  It is time to take some action and get things going again.

You can't keep doing the same thing.  You need to do something radical.  If it isn't working, why do more of the same thing?

==Strategies to revitalize a business==
*[[Nine ways to network|Networking]]
*Driving your business.  Your feelings act as the fuel.
**A lot of people started feeling a stagnant energy starting this past spring.
**People were feeling that they were working a lot but not accomplishing a lot.
*Change comes with progress.  Things that do not advance your goals need to be cleared through.
**It's like pruning a tree to allow it to flourish.
*Do not just keep doing what you have been doing.  That will result in you continuing getting the same results.

===What have you been resisting===
*Nature has ebbs and flows.  So do we
*Time to make a massive change.
**Changes come with a component of "the unknown"
**The unknown is scary
**You need to make room for the change
**Most of us want our lives to stay the same but add on this new great thing.
**You will find yourself struggling with yourself.  Self doubt and buyer remorse just before the change.
*With light comes shadows
*Make decisions from where you are going, not from where you are at.

===Do something radical===
*When is the last time you've done something outrageous or radical?  For both you or your business?
**Doubling your rates
**Dropping that client driving you crazy
**Leaving a job and starting a business
**New forms of advertising
**Change the product you are offering.
*Choose one major thing that scares you a bit.
*Break your pattern.  Do something new.
===Change your environment===
*Your environment affects you.
*New environments influences you more than you know.
*A new environment will usually boost your energy
*A new environment may provide new ideas
*the Turning Point for the major successful people was a change in environment.
===Meet New People===
*The people around you affect you.  You are very close to the "average" of the people you are surrounded by.
*Meet people who will inspire you
**People who are doing things you want to do
**People that have goals
*Centers of Influence (6 degrees of separation)
**People who would be your target market
**People who have access to your target market
**Joint venture partners.
*You need to get up out of your chair/house and show up to things.  There is nothing as successful as meeting people in realspace as opposed to cyberspace.
**The internet is a place where you can help maintain real relationships, but if you haven't met the person it isn't a real relationship.

===Invest in yourself===
*Business owners do what is necessary.  They invest in their businesses and they invest in their selves.
**The formula is Attraction + Action.  You have to show up to take the opportunity!  It isn't going to happen on FB/Twitter.
*The portal through which you invest in your business is the same portal through which profits come back to you.
**Money is a proxy for energy.
**Money needs to flow.  If you don't spend it, it won't make its way back around to you.

==5-step formula for making FAST business decisions==
You want a YES answer to the all 5 of the following questions.
*Is it something you want with your heart; Does it feel good to you?
*Is it something you want something with your head;  Does it logically make sense.
*Does it align with your long and short term goals.
*Does this excite you even if it is a little scary.
*Faith and not fear.  Inspiration and not desperation.
Notice there is no HOW question above.  Decide you are going to do something, the fear will begin to fade away and you will start to see the things that need to be done to make it happen.
==More==
*Always be marketing
**You don't need to spend more money - you just need to put more force behind the marketing.
**When people show an interest in something, produce a product for that.  Physical goods, courses, books, CDs.
*Always be poised for change
**Be quick on your feet.
**Change your business/offering as needed.
*Look up from your desk.  Look to where things are going.
*Multiple streams of income
**Ebbs and flows.  Different streams will peak and trough at different times.  Having multiple streams mean the peaks and troughs average out to a consistent income stream.
***One on One
***Information products (CDs, books, etc)
**Coach around your products and services
**Sell directly, but also leverage by selling larger quantities to distributors and affiliates.
*Stand out from your competition.
*Leverage
**Instead of marketing 1-1, how do you market to 1000's at a time?  INTERNET!
***Linked In
***Twitter
***Facebook/Myspace
***Websites
***Email
***WebTV show
***Affiliates - you only pay for results.
**Strategy
**Tools
*You need a proven system.
**Get rich quick methods don't work by themselves.  They are only a part of the puzzle.
**Need to bring in clients
**Grow your leads
**Grow your sales
**Need to get the 5 critical components lined up
**If you don't have the system, you are the system.
*Want to thrive, not just survive.
