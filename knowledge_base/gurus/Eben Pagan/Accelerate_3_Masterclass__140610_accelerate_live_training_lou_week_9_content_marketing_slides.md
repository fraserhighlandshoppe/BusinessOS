Fast Start Coaching - with Lou D’Alo

Content Marketing
★ Refers to the process of creating, distributing

and promoting content to attract visitors to your
blog and/or landing pages.
★ Ideal scenario: you create the content once, then

outsource the rest (repurposing, distribution and
promotion)

Content Marketing
★ Good news: volume of publishing is not as

important as promoting your published content
★ All things being equal, 10 pieces of content that

are distributed and promoted will outperform
100 pieces of content that are not promoted

Content Marketing
★ The implication of that is: you can create less

content, with more value, and get better results.
★ Takes away some of the “endless content

creation” fears some may have

Content Marketing
★ Repurposing, distribution, promotion is a “define

once, execute forever” process that is easy to
outsource
★ You focus on creating your expert content - set

’n’ forget the rest.

Content Marketing Principles
★ There’s no place like home
★
★
★
★

Your blog/website is home - publish your original core content
there
Use content on 3rd party channels to direct visitors to your
website/blog (including youtube, facebook, g+, amazon, etc)
You don’t control these channels - they can shut down, or your
account can be closed without warning or your consent
As channels come and go, your website is always there

Content Marketing Principles
★ Create once, publish/syndicate everywhere
★ Use a media that makes outsourced

repurposing easy (e.g. video)
★ Use tools, technologies and services to get

your content to as many places as possible
(where your audience ‘hangs out’)

Content Marketing Principles
★ Create “Core Content” of 500-1500 words or 15-30

minutes (more if you have it in you).
★ Have a call to action in Core Content (to an optin

page)
★ Create “Teaser Content” of 3-5 minutes that entices

people to visit the Core Content or Optin page.

e.g. Content Marketing Process
1. Create teaser content on youtube (hangouts or
other). Include a call to action on your video to
bring people to your core content or optin page.
2. Post the video to your blog with a transcript or
other text description that is SEO optimized.
3. Create an audio (MP3) of the video and publish to
your blog and any podcast channels you have.
4. Announce the video on G+ and FB and point
people to your blog post.
5. Advertise on FB to get more viewers and point
them to your blog post. Always ask for likes,
comments and shares.
6. Find the influencers in your market and comment,
like and share their posts on G+/FB.

7. If your video/blog post would be of interest to one
of your influencers (or if you specifically design it to
be), tag the influencer so they see the content on
their timeline and hopefully post to it.
8. As you develop a relationship with the influencer,
invite them to be interviewed, or for you to be a
guest on their platform. Build a relationship from
there.
9. Put out at least one new video/blog post each
week (more if you can) but give yourself time in
between content releases to promote the content
to all your channels.
10.Promote using paid advertising too, if you have a
budget for it.

Action Steps
★ Create an editorial / publishing calendar
★ At least once weekly, create a main content piece and some

teaser content for it - with CTA to landing page.
★ Follow the steps for repurposing, distributing and publishing the

content
★ Track visitors and optins
★ Follow up actively with your email subscribers
