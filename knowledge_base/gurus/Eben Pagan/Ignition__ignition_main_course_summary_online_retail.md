The Keys to Becoming a
Successful Internet Retailer
How to Decide What to Sell, Where to Get It &
How to Sell It
with Colette Marshall
Key Points:
1. Learn:
• Why online retail?
• The “getting started” steps
• How to decide what to sell
• Deciding where to get your products
• Deciding how and where to sell your products
2. Online retail is growing, in 2008 it grew 13% and in 2009, it’s still growing. Many
people are saving time and gas, and shopping online because they can find great
deals and do it 24/7. Also they can compare prices and select a variety of products
online.
3. Getting Started Step 1: Set up a legitimate business
• Business name
• Federal EIN
• State Sales Tax ID
4. Step 2: Decide what you want to sell
• FIND A PROBLEM AND SOLVE IT!
• 3 Core Principles: High demand, low competition, plenty of supply (renewable)
• Use keyword research to get a sense of online demand
• Research the online competition
5. Step 3: Find a Source for the Product
• Product sourcing is the process of researching and locating REAL manufacturers
and Authorized Wholesale Distributors
• Work directly with an authorized wholesaler
• Light bulk wholesale in small, affordable orders ($500 or less)
6. Step 4: Decide How & Where to Sell It
