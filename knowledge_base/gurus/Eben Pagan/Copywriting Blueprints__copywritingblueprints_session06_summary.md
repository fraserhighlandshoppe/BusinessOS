Session 6: Introducing Your
Product Or Service And Selling It
In Session 6, you’ll learn the important distinction between features, advantage, and benefits and how to use
them like an experienced sales person. You’ll get a summary with some of Eben’s all time favorite
copywriting tips & tricks. You’ll also be doing two powerful exercises to put what you’ve learned into action
immediately.

• In this phase your prospect is asking a few more questions:
‣

What is the product or service exactly?

‣

What are the specific results, benefits, outcomes, or relief that it will give me?

• There is an old distinction is sales between features (the thing itself), advantages (what it does) benefits
(the result that you get from it).

• Inexperienced sales people tend to only talk about features, but customers buy benefits.
• You must translate your product’s features into your customer’s benefits.
• Actually introduce your product AS a benefit and a solution.
• As you really get into helping your prospect to buy your product, you now need to talk more about the
results it’s going to deliver.

• Instead of complex ideas use simple, easy to understand, bite-size ideas.
• When you’re writing copy it’s important to use short words, short sentences, and short paragraphs. Use
as many 1 and 2 syllable words as possible.

• Marketing bullets: Each bullet is a short summary of a benefit - almost in a “mini-headline” form. Then
list them one after the other. Bullets can be very simple, or longer & more complex.
