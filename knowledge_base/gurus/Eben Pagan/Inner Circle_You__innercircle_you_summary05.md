YOU: Session 5
Secrets To Ultra Health
Ever wonder what I put in those crazy shakes I talk about? You'll learn it in this session, plus my top
tips on eating for super energy, productivity, and a long and healthy life.

• The past several years I've dialed into a nutrition plan that really works for me. It's simple - I
basically eat the same meals each day - but powerful in the level of nutrition and energy it gives
me. If you would have told me this is how you are going to be eating 3 years from now, I would
have said you're smoking crack. Because I love variety. But once I got into it, and optimized the
tastes and convenience and nutrition, now you couldn't get me to stop. And it's all SUPER
nutritious for me.

• I now think of food in terms of the eﬀects it has in three time frames: Now, The Short Term, and
The Long Term. The Now time frame is the enjoyment of eating the food itself - the taste. This has
a certain value. Next is the Short Term: how you'll feel 3-5 hours after the meal. Are you going to
be in "the zone"? Will you have high energy and high mental clarity? Or will you be sluggish and
foggy, ready for a nap? Then there's the Long Term: the eﬀect the food will have on you from 5
hours after you eat it until the end of your life. Will it make your body more healthy, more vital? Or
will it drain you of longevity?

• Each of these time frames can be rated 1-10. Most people are looking for a 10 in the Now, and the
Short and Long Term eﬀects they really don't care about before hand. What I want is 10's for the
Short and Long Term, and The Now score isn't nearly as important. My meals these would score
about a 6 or a 7 in The Now. But that's okay. I feel GREAT eating them, knowing they'll give me
energy, mental clarity, and a long life of fewer health problems.

• One of the cornerstones of my diet is to eat as much organic and close-to-raw food as possible.
There's no "empty carbs" anywhere. No white flour, no white rice.

• My first meal of the day is a blueberry shake with super reds, super greens, rice protein, one table
spoon of flax seed oil, etc. My second meal of the day is another shake, this time with oat bran,
hemp seeds, ground up flax seed sprouts, etc. My third meal of the day is an organic protein bar.
And my fourth meal of the day is a huge salad with every superfood I can find: kale, spinach,
chard, avocados, etc. I make a flaxseed oil dressing with garlic and onion. I add beans, quinoa,
tempeh, garlic. That lasts for several days. I also try to eat half a grapefruit before each meal.
Research shows it helps to burn fat.

• It's great knowing that when I'm traveling, what and where I'm going to eat is taken care of. Yes
it's a little weird traveling with an extra suitcase just for food. And yes it takes about 15 minutes
when I first get to a hotel room to set up the blender and all the food. But after that, I'm golden. It
takes about 7 minutes to make the shakes or meals, clean up, etc. That's a lot less time than it
takes to decide where to eat, drive there, order, wait for the food, wait for the check, etc. This is
streamlined. There's no decisions. It's the same foods every day.
