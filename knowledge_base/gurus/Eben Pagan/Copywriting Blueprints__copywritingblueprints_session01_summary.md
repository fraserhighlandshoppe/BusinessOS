Session 1: 10 Mindsets For
Achieving Copywriting Success
In this program you’re going to learn how to write marketing copy. You’ll learn how to use words to sell your
products & services, and how to actually get your products & services to “sell themselves.” In Session 1,
you’ll get 10 mindsets for copywriting success and you’ll also be doing an exercise that helps you take action.

• Copywriting is about learning to use words in a way that grabs attention, triggers curiosity & interest,
and then gets people to take action.

• Copywriting gives you the confidence to sell your product or service again and again in any
environment.

• Mindset #1: Selling to individuals is key to business success.
‣

Selling is a very individual process, taking one person and walking them through the emotional and
psychological steps to make a buying decision.

‣

Products are bought, not sold. Selling is about helping a person buy, not pushing them toward
something that they don’t want to do.

‣

Professional selling is about ethically helping people make a decision to fulfill a need.

‣

Each prospect, from their perspective, is alone. They want to be talked to as if they and their needs
are special and unique.

• Mindset #2: Marketing is selling on a mass scale.
‣

If selling is a one-to-one process, then marketing is selling on a mass scale.

‣

Marketing is communicating to a large number of individuals where the product/service solves their
problem or meets their needs.

• Mindset #3: Copywriting is the key element of marketing in print, audio, and video.
‣

Copywriting is the words that we are using – it’s “word-smithing”.

‣

The words are what carry most of the message in the story.

‣

When you learn how to word-smith, your copywriting gets your prospect emotionally excited to
take action and get the results that you’re promising.

• Mindset #4: Words are powerful weapons.
‣

Words can be dangerous to your business if you don’t know how to write copy successfully.

• Mindset #5: Use the highest impact words & phrases possible.
‣

Find words & phrases that really trigger emotion and get people motivated to take action.

• Mindset #6: Use proven marketing and copywriting techniques.
‣

Start by using proven techniques, then when those work add your own touch to the copy.

• Mindset #7: Market for action and response.
‣

Write copy and market so people make decisions and take action, not just to entertain people.

‣

If you use these techniques ethically & intentionally, to specifically get someone to take action, and
buy from you, it will change your mindset and your copy.

• Mindset #8: Write copy the way you talk.
‣

You want to communicate that you’re comfortable and that the person reading is a friend, someone
you want to have a relationship with, and someone you want as your customer.

‣

Write in a conversational tone to make people relax and feel comfortable. This build rapport with
your customer.

• Mindset #9: Build relationship with copy.
‣

We’re going to sell and market for action & response. The way to do this is to build relationship with
your copy.

‣

Tell a story about yourself that gets your prospect to relate to you.

‣

Use words, sayings, and phrases that your prospect would use. You speak your prospect’s language.

• Mindset #10: Great copy takes knowledge + practice.
‣

Writing great copy takes learning the techniques of the masters and then takes daily practice.

‣

Practice daily by writing an email newsletter, blog, video, podcast, etc.

EXERCISE:
‣

Choose one of the mindsets that really spoke to you. Choose the one that you feel you’ll really need
to grasp or take with you to take you success to the next level. Put a star next to it.
