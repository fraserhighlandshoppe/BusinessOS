# 10_Master_Attention_Communication.pdf

```markdown
The 10 Master Attention-Grabbing
Communication Formulas

Key Points:
1.

In this session you’ll review your “Ultimate Influencer” Exercise, as audience members
share their results.
2. You’re right. What you fear most is about to happen. And the counter-intuitive things
you will do to solve the problem will actually get you into further trouble.
3. In the “10 Master Attention-Grabbing Communication Formulas” exercise, you’ll learn
10 headlines formulas that reach through the noise and get your customer’s attention.
4. Now try and adapt each of the formulas to your marketing by starting with the “Fear &
Frustration” or “Want & Aspiration” that has been run through the 7-Layer Refinement
Process. You’’ll take one of the powerful motivators that your customer has, and run it
through these filters.
5. A headline is the first word or words that a prospect sees in your advertising. You have
to communicate that you understand their problem and you have a solution - to grab
attention. If you get the headline right, the rest of the content will follow.
6. 3-stepBenefit Headline Formula: If you don’t know what to say in your headlines, start
with:
• “How to...”
• Get what you want or avoid what you don’t want.
• Conveniently, quickly, or without risk.
7. 3-step Pain Headline Formula:
• “How to...”
• Eliminate specific pain.
• Without more pain.
8. Talk about their needs (not your product), in specific, concrete terms.
9. “The Marketing Stage”: have a spotlight that can only focus on one thing (you, your
product, your company, or YOUR CUSTOMER). Focus on their needs.
10. Find your prospect’s strongest emotional motivators, then consider the benefits your
product offers. Use the “Power Headline Formulas” to re-write at least 10 variations of a
headline that will draw your prospect in instantly.
11. When you re-state the motivator using one of these formulas - voila! - you’ll have very
powerful headlines.
```
