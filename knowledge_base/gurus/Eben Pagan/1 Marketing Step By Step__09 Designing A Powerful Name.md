Designing A Powerful Name
The name that you give your product or service is the highest-leverage Marketing you can do. Choose a name that’s
appealing to the ear - not to the eye - because that’s how brains remember. Focus on repetitive sounds, rhythmic syllables
and powerful associations.

What are the words that most specifically and powerfully describe
the Result, Benefit or Solution you provide to your customer?

Can you combine words in a way that creates a repetitive sound
either alliteration or rhyme?

Can you combine words in a way that has a rhythm that keeps the
name bouncing around in the mind for that extra few seconds
required to commit it to long-term memory?
