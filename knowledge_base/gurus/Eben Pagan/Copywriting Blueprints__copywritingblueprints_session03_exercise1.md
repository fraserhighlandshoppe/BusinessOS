Abstract Ideas vs. Tangible
Benefits & Results
Humans live in two worlds: The first is a world of concrete experiences in “reality” - the other is a world
of interpretations, feelings and abstractions. When creating marketing, it’s critical that you meet people
in the “common space” of the concrete, measurable, tangible and experiential. To do this, we must
switch off our abstraction mechanisms, and switch on our concrete experience mechanisms. Don’t talk
about conceptual, abstract ideas like “weight” or “currency” or “signs of aging.” Do talk about concrete,
tangible things like “one pound of fat” or “$5,000 cash” or “wrinkles around your eyes.”
