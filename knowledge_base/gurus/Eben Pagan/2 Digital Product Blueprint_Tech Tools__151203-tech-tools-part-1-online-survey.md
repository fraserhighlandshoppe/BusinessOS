Survey Design - Introduction
• The tech part of surveys is relatively simple, and

lots of available tools
• The success of your survey will depend more on

the survey design and strategy
• A well thought-out survey, optimized for ease of

participation is worth planning

Survey Design - Introduction
• For purposes of understanding the needs of our audience, the

survey format doesn’t need to be complex or sophisticated — you
don’t have to have a degree in research methodology, don’t worry!
• This presentation: the basics, plus a few enhancements you may

want to use.
• Goal: a good enough understanding to design and deploy your

surveys asap, and get good information about the needs of your
customers and/or prospects

Survey Design - Best Practices
• Clarify goals/outcomes
• Single objective for survey - ask only questions that

support that objective
• When gathering information, more open ended

questions, or “other” fields
• Once information gathered, fewer open ended questions

and more multiple choice, ratings, etc., to prioritize

8

Survey Design - Best Practices
• Ask strategically
• The right audience is key (confidence interval)
• Time the invitation for maximum access /

participation
• Articulate WIIFM
• Roll a snowball

9

Survey Design - Best Practices
• Short, simple, fast ’n’ easy
• Generally - use ratings, scores, multiple choice instead of

open-ended essay style (Exception: “most burning
question” survey)
• Mandatory questions? Include everyone!
• Avoid bias or steering
• Don’t cram 2+ things into a question

10

Survey Design - Best Practices
• Attention comes at a premium, so:
• Fewer questions = more responses
• Ask only for info that you need
• Suggest 1-3 questions; 1-5 ok; but < 10 for sure
• Exception: application and intake forms, etc
• Consider breaking up longer surveys into sections/pages

11

Survey Design - Best Practices
• To incentivize or not?
• Better to have people participate out of organic

benefit and interest
• If you do incentivize, use an incentive that would

be of value/interest to your ideal prospect

12

Survey Design - Best Practices
• A note on “hyper responsives”
• People who are your target audience who WANT

to and DO participate and give long or detailed
input
• These often are your best “avatars”
• Invite / facilitate follow-up

13

Survey Design - Sample Size
• Everyone’s biggest question: how many

responses do I need for results to be significant?

14

Survey Design - Sample Size
• For market research/interviews: any amount is

better than none and: you don’t need it all at once
• Best practice: build it into your marketing, not just

a one-time event
• Start today!
