How to Market Information
Products Online
“How to Market Information Products Online:
eBooks, Audio and Video”
with Eben Pagan
Key Points:
1. If you are looking for a way to have a business that you can create from home with
low risk, low overhead, very few serious technical skills that you have to learn, high
profit margins and you can help people too…then creating and selling information
products could be a great business for you.
2. Information products are the best opportunity to start and grow a business that I know
of:
• Low risk
• Low start-up costs
• High profit margins
• Rewarding (you're helping people solve a problem)
3. “Marketing” is meeting a customer’s selfish need. Figuring out what it is that they
want and creating and framing it so they say, “Oh…this is what I want.” It’s not trying
to talk people in to wanting things, or buying things they don’t want.
4. “Information” is data that has been organized for USE. Wikipedia is a great
example.
5. “Information Marketing” = organizing data that meets a customer's selfish need.
6. A more profitable way to think about “Information Products”
• Most people focus on “telling” – not “teaching”
• Customers don’t really care about “information” – they only care about the
RESULT
• Increase the value of your information dramatically by offering NEW + BETTER +
RESULT
7. The Three Mega-Niches
• Money and business
• Health and fitness
• Dating and relationship
8. How to create content (your advice or information) FAST

• Start with a list of “Fears & Frustrations” + “Wants & Aspirations”
• Best to start by talking to REAL people – “check writers”
9. Name Your Product for Value
• Avoid naming your book or product something “cute” or something “general”
• Focus on specific, measurable, and tangible benefits that your prospect WANTS
• Discipline yourself to focus on EMOTION + TANGIBLE
10. Creating an eBook with ommon PC or Mac Software
• Use simple, professional design
• 1-2 fonts, 1-2 colors, 1-2 sizes, 1-2 alignments
• Make your life easy long-term by using “Styles”
• Insert an instant Table of Contents
11. Build a List-Building System…For ONE Person
• 10x factor when you build a list
• In order to create a big list, you must not think of it as a “list”
• Each person is an individual – “Individual Think”
• Intentionally design your experience for your ideal customer – your Customer
Avatar. Combine all of your prospects in your head in to one prospect and build
the system for them
12. How to Write Email Newsletters that Sell Information Products
• Use email to build relationship
• Imagine how you’d build an in-person relationship
• Script your email chain just like you’d build a friendship
13. The Long-Term Formula for Successfully Launching & Dominating a Niche
• Research
• Write daily
• Coach 1-1 Daily
• Create your first introductory product
• Do small group live training
• Create an interview program for your continuity product
• Create a high-end product
• Create a high end continuity product (like individual coaching)
• FOLLOW YOUR SUCCESS + NARROW YOUR FOCUS
14.BE TRUTHFUL: only claim what you can deliver on, what you can prove, and what
you have information to back up.
