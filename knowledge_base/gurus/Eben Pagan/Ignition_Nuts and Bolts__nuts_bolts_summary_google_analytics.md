Nuts & Bolts:

Google Analytics
Key Points:
1. Google Analytics is a free program that will tell you:
• How many visitors your site is getting... and if that number is going up or down
• What keywords are bringing those visitors to your site
• What other sites are referring visitors to your site
• What country your visitors are from
• How much search engine traffic you're getting vs. pay per click traffic
• and more!
2. Knowing what keywords visitors use to get to your site can help you... because you
can then better optimize the pages they are "landing" on for the keywords that are
bringing them there... and get more free traffic.
• Example: you discover that one of your pages is getting search engine traffic
from the keyword "weight loss belt." But that keyword is not in the title of the
page, and it's hardly mentioned anywhere. So you put it in the title and sprinkle
it through the page... and soon the amount of traffic you get from this keyword
DOUBLES.
3. Knowing what sites are referring visitors to you can help you... because you can call
them up and work on ways where they can refer even MORE visitors to your site.
To install Google Analytics on either your blog or static pages, first:
1. Set up an account at adwords.google.com (it's free)
2. Click on “Reporting”
3. Click on “Google Analytics”
4. Scroll down and click on “Add Website Profile”
5. Enter your website name
6. Uncheck “Cost Data”
7. Hit “Enter”
8. It will tell you "Tracking Not Installed" - that's ok
Next, to install on your static pages:
1. Take the code and paste it, following the instructions from Google
2. This code needs to be on every static page
