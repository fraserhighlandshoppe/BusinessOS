Designing Simple Ads And
Marketing That Get RESPONSE
Remember, the purpose of your design is not to impress... it’s to amplify your message, make the promise clear, and get
your Prospect to take ACTION. Design for RESPONSE. Use these “rules of thumb” to keep your designs simple and effective:

๏ Use a dominant visual element and visual hierarchy
๏ Use “Reading Gravity” - design copy to be read
๏ Keep your designs ultra-simple
๏ Use a maximum of 1-2 Alignments, Fonts, Font Sizes, Colors
๏ Use a big, bold headline to call attention to your main message
๏ If you’re using a video to Market, “translate” these concepts - by
putting your “dominant message” at the beginning, keeping your
video simple and straight-forward, create a simple “set” that
involves few elements, etc.
