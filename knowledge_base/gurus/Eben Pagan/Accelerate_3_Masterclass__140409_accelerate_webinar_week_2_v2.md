★ A question to begin asking yourself every day is…
★ “What is value?”
★ And then: “What is value to my customer?”
★ Remember: Value is a subjective experience that is
mainly emotional - customers receive value when
they feel relief, hope, joy, superiority, validation, etc.

★ A man who is socially awkward and insecure and who wants

entrepreneur who wants more customers for their business
★ To the man who is insecure, value might be the relief he
feels when he says hello and doesn’t get rejected - or the
excitement he experiences from getting her contact info
★ To the entrepreneur, value might be the feeling of security
that comes with seeing more customers buying every day
