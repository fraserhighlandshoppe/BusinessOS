Taking The Risk Away From
Your Prospect
In a business transaction, the buyer typically feels like they have a lot at risk. They are risking their time,
their money - and possibly even looking and feeling stupid. Use this exercise to identify the risks that
your prospective customer is facing, then create ways to take those risks away. For “extra credit” see if
you can turn the risk around - so that your offer is “better than risk-free.”

What are the biggest risks that your prospective customer faces if they buy what
you are offering?

How can you remove the risk from the transaction with a guarantee or other
addition to the offer?
