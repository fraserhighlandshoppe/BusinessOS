Accelerate Video 2: Profitable Productivity
★ Everyone can become incredibly productive, powerful, organized, and creative MOST of the
time in our businesses - and do the high-value work that leads to rapid growth, more profit,
and higher income.
★ Everyone can be organized, creative, effective, and productive – we just need to find our
optimal environment, mindsets, and habits.
★ Most of the things you can do in your business don’t accelerate growth - they just
“maintain” at best.
★ Time management and productivity is a different game for an entrepreneur CEO that runs
their own business than it is for everyone else.
★ As business owners, you and I are responsible for EVERYTHING that happens inside our
businesses.
★ We’re about twice as motivated to avoid loss as we are to achieve success and gain, so we
have to work much harder to focus on what we want to create, and not get distracted by fear
and anxiety.
★ The “trick” to productivity is deciding what you’re going to work on at some point other
than in the moment, and then to practice that high-value work over and over until it’s
natural, habitual and automatic.
★ Your body-mind is like a high-tech airplane that has a very sophisticated auto-pilot
system that’s engaged anytime you’re not consciously “at the wheel.”
★ Focus on what you want to create, not what you want to avoid
★ Focus on what produces the most value, not on what produces the most “busyness”
★ Focus time in chunks of 90-120 minutes, minimum, not multi-tasking, and no
interruptions.
★ Focus on what will work in the future, not what worked in the past.
★ Steven Covey: “Time management is a misnomer, the challenge is to manage ourselves.”
★ It takes about 30 days to imprint a new habit, so it becomes automatic and natural - and
you must overcome Habit Gravity and achieve Escape Velocity by practicing every day until
it’s “in.”
★ Make big commitments personally, socially, in business and financially to get yourself into
action, and to make sure you complete the important money-making projects in your
business.
