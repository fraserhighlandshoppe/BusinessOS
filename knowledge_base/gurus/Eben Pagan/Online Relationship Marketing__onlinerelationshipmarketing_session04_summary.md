Session 4: Move The Free Line
In Session 4, you’re going to learn how giving away your best ideas can attract high quality prospects
and build strong relationships with your new and current customers. You’ll also be doing an exercise to
take what you’ve learned and put it into action.

•

Free is one of the most powerful words in marketing.

•

“Moving The Free Line” is putting your best knowledge and insights into a product that you can
GIVE AWAY to potential customers.

•

Because of the advances in technology it’s a tiny fraction of the cost it used to be to give products
away.

•

An important component of moving the free line is to give away your very best ideas.

•

When creating free content follow the rule of thumb of making something worth at least $100.

•

Once you’ve created this free-line gift, oﬀer it in exchange for an opt-in to your e-mail list. This is
where your “opt-in landing page” comes in.

•

An opt-in landing page is a simple webpage that has a video or graphic that says “get this free gift
from me, just enter your name & email address.”

•

Moving the free line is a great way to get you and your product in front of high quality prospects.
