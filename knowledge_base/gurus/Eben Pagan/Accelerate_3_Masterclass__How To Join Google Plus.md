How To Join Google Plus
Create an account

Create Your Google Account

Visit https://accounts.google.com/signup to create a google(plus) account. Fill in the form and click
"Next Step" to continue.
Notice that it IS possible to create a Google Account using your existing email address by clicking
the "I prefer to use my current email address" link under the "Choose Your Username" field on this
form.
How To Join Google Plus - 1

Choose your profile image

Click "Add a photo" to add your profile picture from your computer, or use your web camera to take
a picture.

How To Join Google Plus - 2

How To Join Google Plus - 3

Set the profile photo

Use the guides to choose the part of the photo you want to use, then click "Set as profile photo" to
continue.

How To Join Google Plus - 4

Confirm or edit your profile photo selection

If you are happy with your profile photo, click on the "Next Step" button, otherwise, click "Edit
Photo" to go back and adjust.

How To Join Google Plus - 5

Welcome - you're all done!

Your account is now set up - click "Get Started" to enter the Google Plus environment.
Notice your profile image at the top right of the screen. Clicking on that image presents links for
you to manage your account, your privacy settings and your profile information. I suggest you
browse through those links and configure your account to act in accordance with your personal
preferences. Pay particular attention to the privacy and notification settings.
