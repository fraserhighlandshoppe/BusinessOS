Make A Picture Of It Finished
★ What will your business look like when it is “finished” and running exactly the way you want it to?
★ What is the most important difference between the way
it runs now and the picture of it finished and running
perfectly?
★ What do you need to learn to help you bridge the gap?

Your Biggest Challenge / Frustration
★ What is the biggest challenge or frustration
that you experience in your business right now?
★ What would you like to get at Accelerate to help
you move beyond it?

Your Biggest Growth Opportunity
★ What is the biggest opportunity that you have right
now to grow your business?
★ What would it be worth to your business - and to
you personally - to be able to take advantage of it?
★ What would you like to get from the Accelerate
Summit to help you take advantage of it?

What Do You Need To “Figure Out”?
★ Success as an Entrepreneur is typically a series of
things that you “figure out” on the way to a
thriving, highly profitable business
★ What are the next puzzles that you need to solve,
things you need to learn, solutions you need to find,
that will help you build the business that you want?

Who You Want To Meet
★ Who would you like to meet at the Accelerate
Summit?
★ What could you do now to prepare to meet these
people, so you can offer them something valuable
when you are networking with them?

Your Goals For The Event
★ What was your biggest motivation to attend the
Accelerate Training?
★ What do you want to leave the Accelerate event
with, specifically?
★ Are there any goals that you have that you’d like to
achieve at the Accelerate Summit?

Unplug As Much As You Can
★ The Summit is an opportunity to get outside of your usual
environment, and look at things from a higher perspective
★ Use the next several days to organize your business and life
so you have minimal obligations during the Summit
★ The more you can unplug from “business as usual” the
more you will be able to immerse yourself in the new
mindsets and methods - and the more you’ll take home

Sit With People You Don’t Know
★ If you sit within “eyeshot” of people that you know, a
large percentage of your mental and emotional cycles
are dedicated to them, and not your own learning
★ By sitting next to people you don’t know, you put
yourself outside of your comfort zone, and make it
much more likely that you’ll make new connections

Sit In Different Parts Of The Room
★ Again, you’ll get more from the program if you’re
outside of your comfort zone
★ People often set up their “territory” in a training
program like this one, and sit in their zone throughout
the entire event
★ Different parts of the room give you different
perspectives, so move around the Summit!
