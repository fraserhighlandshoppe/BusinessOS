Accelerate Your
Product

The “Myth Of Great Marketing”
★ Most people that are running a business online think that
the name of the game is marketing
★ I have invested almost 20 years studying marketing, and
doing very well with it - and I now believe that a great
product is more important than great marketing
★ Why? Because the game has changed, and if you have a bad
product but great marketing, the word just spreads faster

A Great Product Is A Game-Changer
★ A great product is the “secret sauce” of a great business
★ If you pay attention to the cutting-edge of product
development, which I believe is what’s happening in Silicon
Valley, you’ll see that their focus is on the PRODUCT
★ If you look at companies that take off and grow rapidly, they
typically came up with a great product - and THEN figured
out how to market and distribute it

We Learned About “Productivity”
★ The root of productivity is “produce” - and it also
includes the word “product”
★ Just like productivity isn’t about just being busy… but
instead it’s about producing, it’s important to see your
product as something that produces a benefit for
your customer - and not as the “end” in itself
★ This shift to seeing your product as “a means” is key

Reality Of A Great Product Creator
★ Great product developers live in a reality that is continually
generating new “problems” - and new opportunities to
solve them
★ Great product creators know that the product they have
today is the foundation for the great product of tomorrow not the great product of tomorrow itself
★ Product creators see fears and desires not as “vices” or
negative things, but as opportunities to add value

Self-Image Of Product
Developers
★ Great product creators and developers see themselves
as the ones who are responsible for helping others
get the results they want in their lives
★ The greatest product developers often see themselves
as artists (Steve Jobs, Richard Branson)
★ Steve Jobs answer to “what have you learned?”

Beliefs Of Great Product Creators
★ The more clearly I understand the “use case” of my
ideal customer, the better my product will be
★ The study and practice of design isn’t just for website
makers, it’s for ME - and it’s a key to my success
★ The reason most products fail is because they were
created without input and collaboration with
customers

Mindsets For Product Creation
★ My product is not only a means to the outcome that
my customer wants, but it’s also an obstacle
★ I’m going for Version 3.0 before I decide if I have a
“winning product”
★ A great product makes sales and marketing easy
★ Ultimately: My product can become my marketing

Values Of Great Product Creators
★ Great product creators value what customer say they
want and what they actually buy approximately
equally
★ Great product creators value innovation over what
worked in the past (innovation = new + better)
★ Great product creators value understanding
customer needs over “expressing themselves”

Begin With Emotional Needs
★ We’ve learned the importance of focusing on the emotional
needs of customers - and nowhere is this more important
than when creating your product
★ If your product doesn’t lead directly to relief, delight,
satisfaction, inspiration, etc. - then it’s not likely to succeed
★ This is one of the reasons why we’re always asking our
customers about their biggest challenges and desires!

The “Minimum Viable Product”
★ In Silicon Valley, the concept of the “Minimum Viable
Product” or “MVP” has emerged
★ A Minimum Viable Product is a product that has the
minimum features to be able to give to customers to
actually use as a solution
★ The MVP is a powerful mindset shift, as it puts the focus on
getting the product into the hands of the customer,
rather than getting it “perfect”

The First Product I Sold Online…
★ …was a book of dating advice - that took me about 3
weeks to write
★ The book was full of typos and mistakes, but it proved to
me that there was a market for this information
★ I was able to go back later and fix some of the minor issues
(and the few refunds I got that said “I want my money back
because you have typos were good entertainment!)

How Could You Test Your Next
Product Idea Rapidly?
★ In my business, I can test a new business idea by doing a
live webinar, or offering a seminar and seeing if it converts
★ New marketplaces like Kickstarter are allowing you to test a
product with only a prototype available
★ If possible, talk to real, live customers and see if you can
get them to buy individually - and if you can’t, then this is a
sign that you might need a new direction

If I Had To Test A Product Idea In 24
Hours, I’d Do A Live Webinar Or Hangout
★ One way to get yourself “off the fence” and out of “paralysis
of analysis” is to write to your list, and say “I’m going a live
broadcast tomorrow for people who are interested in “X”
★ Then see who shows up, and tell them your idea - and
listen to what they say
★ It costs you almost nothing, and it gives you immediate
feedback, which is priceless (feedback is key)

The Power Of Positioning
★ As Al Ries and Jack Trout teach: “Positioning isn’t
something you do to the product, it’s something
you do to the mind.”
★ You’re not just looking to create a new product, you’re
looking to create a new category with your product
★ The category isn’t in your mind, it’s in the mind of
your customers

We Can Only Remember 5-10
Things In Any Particular Category
★ Think about how many companies you can name in
the following categories:
★ Hotels
★ Rental Cars
★ Search Engines

Instead Of Competing For “Mind
Share” With Established Products…
★ …create your own category that you can be first in
★ It’s not only challenging on a financial level, but also on
a psychological level, to compete with established
brands, companies and products
★ Instead, create a new category, and enjoy the benefit
of being the first one into the mind

New Categories Are Worth Creating
★ Every time a new technology or other innovation
creates the opportunity, there are new categories that
emerge - and new WINNERS that emerge
★ From calculators to computers, from records to tapes
to disks to downloads - in each new category, there
are new winners
★ If you can create the category, you are perceived as the
original and the leader

Examples Of Category Evolution
★ Computers > Personal Computers > Laptop
Computers
★ Self-Help > Relationships > Dating Advice > Dating
Advice For Men = Double Your Dating
★ Business Advice > Business Growth > Information
Product Business = GURU MasterMind

More New Product Category Examples
★ Metal money > paper money
★ Mainframe computer > personal computer
★ Horse & buggy > automobile
★ Drinks > energy drinks
★ Books > videos > webinars
★ Health > weight loss > fat loss

Look Where Things Are Going…
★ Take a minute right now, and ask yourself what new
products and categories will emerge in your industry
★ All products and categories follow the pattern of
evolution: They evolve, diverge, combine and refine
over time
★ By asking yourself where things are going “anyway” you can often find ideas for new products and
categories that will win

Products That Are Coming To The
Mainstream “For Sure”…
★ 3D Video Conferencing
★ Cars that drive themselves
★ Computers that communicate with you just like one
human communicates with another
★ What’s coming in YOUR industry?

Your Product As A Category
★ When I’m creating a new product, I first ask “what
new category will this product be creating?”
★ Again, it has to be a new category from the
customer’s perspective - not just from mine
★ When you think of your product AS a new category, it
makes it easier to create both the product and the
category with it

Naming Your Category
★ Naming your category can be as important as naming
your product
★ When you name your category, you’re creating a new “brain
bucket” for someone to carry around in their heads - to fill
with 5-10 new product names
★ Sometimes your category can be obvious, and sometimes
it’s not - it took me a year+ to come up with the category
“Dating advice for men”

Even If You Don’t Name The Category…
★ Still use this strategy of thinking of each new product as
creating its own category
★ As I built my dating advice business, I thought of every new
product as creating a new category (Approaching
Women, Meeting Women Online, Deep Inner Game, Cocky
Comedy, etc.)
★ Even though I didn’t pursue developing each category much
further, the products were still very successful individually and sell to this day 10+ years later
