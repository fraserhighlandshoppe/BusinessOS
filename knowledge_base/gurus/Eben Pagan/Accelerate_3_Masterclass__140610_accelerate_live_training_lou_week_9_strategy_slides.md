Fast Start Coaching - with Lou D’Alo

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Based on a "Trusted Advisor" process, and

consistent with Eben's strong encouragement.
★ This is an especially good method to bootstrap a

coaching business, but applies also to getting
your new business or brand off the ground.

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ A great way to keep in touch with your audience,

understand their FF/ND, and make sales in a
high-value, high-touch (and high-ticket)
manner.
★ Can be free or low-cost, typically from 15-45

minutes

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Give it a name that implies what you'll be doing

on the call, or the outcome/value your guest can
look forward to.
★ e.g. "Revenue Review", "Sales Doubler", "Client

Generator", etc.

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Can use with "cold traffic" from ads or SEO, but

best results usually with "warm" leads that
know, like and trust you somewhat. Test both.
★ Consultative selling approach: “Sit on their side

of the table”, understand what they REALLY
wants. Make bona fide recommendation to help.

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ You provide real value to your guest, you learn a

lot about your avatar FF/ND, you find "ideal
clients" that you WANT to work with, and you
make some sales in the process.

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Invite to apply
★ Review and make appointment
★ During the Meeting:
★ Discover, Reflect, Offer and close

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Invite to Apply
★ Set up a landing page sales letter for the call
★ Drive visitors to this landing page from your virtual

events, e-mail broadcasts, content marketing and
paid ads
★ Describe clearly and fully what the call is about
★ Require an application to see if the fit is right

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Review and make appointment
★ Review the application and send invitation or decline
★ Most efficient way to do this: online calendar
★ Block out certain times in the week dedicated to this
★ Use an appointment calendar system that sends

reminders
★ For paid meetings, Have a cancellation policy

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Discover
★ Welcome, establish rapport
★ Set expectations
★ Ask questions, probe FF/ND
★ Ask about impact value of resolving versus not resolving
★ Brainstorm solutions
★ Take detailed notes

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Reflect
★ Review problems and solutions discussed - use your notes!
★ Reflect back in their words
★ Summarize the FF/ND, impact, The solution, The value/

benefit
★ Review the pain of not moving forward versus the benefit/
value doing so
★ Confirm or correct

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Offer
★ Ask if they're ready and committed to take action
★ If yes, recommend how you can best help them
★ Answer questions (objections) that helps and start being

accountable to results right now, overcome fears that have been
holding them back
★ Don't push, just support them to move beyond their fears and
uncertainties
★ Have especially high integrity here

The "Strategy Session" Process
★ Close
★ If they accept your offer, get them into your system right then and there if

possible, or ASAP if not
★ Give then action steps, set up next follow-up, or explain how automated
follow-up Will work.
★ If they decline your offer, thank them for the time, encourage them to use
the ideas discussed, invite them to subscribe to your list, and leave the
door open
★ Congratulate them and thank them
★ Close the call warmly

Action Steps
★ Create and name your free/low-cost session
★ Set up a landing page, application and online appointment

calendar
★ Start to invite people from everywhere it makes sense (core

content, email marketing, webinars and hangouts, paid ads, etc)
★ Set a goal to do your first 10 conversations, at least one before

the end of the Accelerate Coaching Fast-Start coaching program
