Writing Copy That Sells
Answer these questions in simple, plain language that stays focused on Results, Benefits and Relief that your Prospect will
experience. Try turning on a recorder and answering these questions the way you’d answer them if someone asked you in
person - then transcribe what you say - and use it as the basis for your copy.

What benefit will your Prospect get from just going through your
message?

What are the fears and frustrations your Prospect is going
through that you solve?

What Results, Benefits, Relief will your Prospect will get?

How did you discover that the Product works?

How can they know that it will work?

Who is this for, and who is it not for?
