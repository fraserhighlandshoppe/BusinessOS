Session 4:
Communicating With Yourself
In session 4 you’ll learn why the most important communication is the communication with yourself.
You’ll learn how to speak to yourself to boost self-esteem and how to create affirmations in order to
communicate better with yourself.
• The most important communication you can have is with yourself. A high percentage of the
communication we have with ourselves is conflict and criticism.
• We burn the little bit of will power that we get when we’re in internal conflict.
• Carl Jung said that conflict only really exists in the mind and can only be solved in the mind. The
way to resolve conflict is through symbols. Example: the “yin & yang” symbol.
• Our self-image, or the story that we created about ourselves, really drives what’s possible in our
lives.
• Dr. Nathaniel Branden defines self-esteem is felling that you’re competent to handle the challenges
of life and that you deserve to be happy. When you bring consciousness, awareness, and selfdirection to your life, this improves your self-esteem.
• Since we’re not operating in the world as who we are, but as who we think we are, it pays to go
to work on who we think we are. Don’t live out of your past, but out of the possibilities of who you
can become and what you want to create.
• Social status and where you are in the human tribe has a fundamental impact on your self-image.
Until you view yourself as a high-status person it’s likely there will be a lot of internal conflict. This
is not ego. True high-status is when you are helping others in positive ways, and others are
looking to you for learning.
• Self-criticism can be very damaging if it becomes a habit. It’s also about the tone in which you
speak to yourself. If you go to work on the self-criticism it will free up energy to focus on the truly
important values in life.
• EXERCISE:
• Take your 3 goals and say them to yourself in a loving and encouraging tone. Painting
pictures of success is one of the most powerful tools to accomplishing your goals.

• Rapport is about being in sync with another person. Another type of rapport is the rapport that
you have with yourself. Get your physical, emotional, and conceptual parts lined up.
• EXERCISE:
• The Physical Brain is the “ME” brain or selfish brain, and the Emotional Brain is the “WE”
brain or social brain. These are in constant battle with each other. The way to solve this is to
use the logical brain to communicate and get the physical and emotional in sync.
• Use the logical brain to put awareness on your whole body. Start with your feet, hands, arms,
shoulders, torso, face, and head. Where do you notice tension in your body? We hold
tension unconsciously, so we consciously have to let go and relax.
• Next, notice your emotional state. Name it (happy, sad, frustrated, intimidated, etc.) and then
notice if your emotional state is related to the external expectations of others.
• Ask yourself how can my physical body and actions work with my emotions and my social
relationships, to help make each other work better together.
• These are the first steps to get into self-rapport.
• Creating affirmations is another very important tool that makes a profound impact in selfcommunication. Example: Take your goals and physically write them down everyday.
• Visualization is another powerful form of self-communication. Example: Each time you write your
goals you visualize them as if they were real right now.
• Your rituals, habits, and who you choose as your social influences are some of the most impacting
things in your self-communication and self-image
• By putting rituals in place, you are self-communicating that you are in control of your life.
• Your social influences are sometimes in more control of your self-image that your selfcommunication. The highest leverage in communication is the people that your surround yourself
with.
• EXERCISE:
• Take your 3 clarified goals and tell yourself that you can achieve them by positive tone and
encouragement.
• Then, create an affirmation that you can do it.
• What ritual can you put in place so each one of your goals becomes real? Do 1 per month
for each goal. Trying more than one is overwhelming and takes too much will power.
• Who can you add as a social influence that has achieved that goal so they influence you on
an unconscious level?
