YOUR MARKET: Session 6
Going Deep With Your Customers
In this session, you'll learn how to shift the perceptions of large groups of people, how to cut through
the noise to grab the attention of your prospects, and a powerful 4 level model that gives you a
roadmap to creating a deeper and more meaningful bond with your customers than your
competitors could ever imagine.

• One of the things we're talking about is shifting the perceptions of large groups of people. This is
sticky territory, because it butts up against the concept of manipulation. But when you're trying
to influence masses of people, it's important to understand how our perceptions work.

• For over 30 years now, experts have been describing ours as the first "over communicated"
society. It's very diﬃcult to cut through the noise and reach your prospect and get their attention.
In order to do this, what we're talking about is creating your category, the name of your business,
your unique selling proposition, the names of your products, etc., in a way that cuts straight
through all that noise.

• So, when you get that moment with your prospect, the very first word they hear... and the
second...and the third... they cut like a laser beam because they're so relevant to their situation.
You're tuned in to their frequency, so they in turn tune into you. We don't live in a time any more
where we can hope to have a sustainable business by simply making a good product. We have to
create advantages, and these advantages are psychological in nature. Advantages of perception.

• One way to gain an advantage in perception is by using a model from NLP called Logical Levels.
• The model has 4 levels, from highest to lowest: Reality, Identity, Beliefs, and Capabilities. This is
one way of looking at how we humans operate and perceive the world. Most people aren't
conscious of these levels at all, but if you understand these levels, you can things that will seem
like magic to others.

• Most people who set out to develop themselves - to become more powerful as an individual they focus on the lower levels, starting with capabilities. They think the capabilities will lead to all
the success they want. If someone wants to be rich and successful and happy, they think what
they need to do is learn how to make money. That's the natural path to follow.

• Newbies always want techniques. I started there too. But if you move up and work on identity and
reality more, then capabilities and beliefs largely take care of themselves. I found that by hanging
out with people who were already who I wanted to become, so to speak, that the lower levels just
fall into place. It becomes natural.

• So now when I want a new capability, I don't go and read some things about how to do it... I ask
myself, "Who is the expert who knows how to do this better than anyone, who is living the life I
want to live or has the reality I want to be in?" and then I reach out and connect with them and
begin making friends with them and get to know them. And by hanging out with them, their
reality, identity, and beliefs trickle into my brain. And of course I pick up some of the capabilities

• What we're doing here is we're trying to work in these areas with our prospects and customers.
