Session 9:
Leadership Communication
In session 9 you’ll learn what leadership is and how communication supports it. You’ll also learn the
key aspects of becoming a great leader as well as an important exercise to practice your leadership
skills.
• What is leadership? A lot of people think that its telling people what to do, or being the decision
maker. Effective leaders are actually helping people getting in touch with their own power. They
are facilitators and help guide people on to where they want to go.
• Leaders have a power that most people don’t have. They can go out into the future in their own
mind and figure out how to make that vision come true.
• Visioning - Leaders can assess where things are, how they can be different in the future, and
determine what needs to be done to make that happen.
• As a leader, the most important thing you can do for others, is to see a bigger future than they can
see for themselves. Then, communicate that future to them in a way that is compelling.
• Communication helps support leadership.
• Who you’re being is more important than what you are saying. Steven Covey says, “You can’t
talk yourself out of problems that you’ve behaved yourself into”. The most powerful communicators
are doing and being what they are communicating.
• A good rule of thumb is the concept of “you go first”. If you want to lead people in a direction
then you must go first.
• Becoming a great leader:
• Find your unique talent and develop it. Whatever it is that you’re naturally good at, is what
you should be doing.
• Find a mentor and mastermind group of people that are naturally talented that have
developed skills and mastery in this specific area.
• Take your talent and make yourself very successful. Those that are making the biggest
difference in the world have first made themselves very successful.
• Learn how to collaborate with others.
• Then, come back and give your greatest gifts. Make a difference in the world.

• EXERCISE:
• Close your eyes and go 20 years out into the future. Imagine how the world can be better in
a way that’s really meaningful to you.
• Notice some detail of that future that really speaks to you.
• Come back to now and remember that detail from the future. See how you becoming
successful will help create that future.
• Now, reflect on that future everyday and clarify it. Use that future to pull you into it.
