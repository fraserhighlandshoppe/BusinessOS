The List-Building Game Has Changed
★ It’s important to understand how much the internet
has changed list-building
★ We now need to go much further “upstream” when
building our lists
★ If you approach building a list with an out-dated
mindset, it will limit your sales and growth
dramatically

The Past Reality
★ Before the internet, it was relatively expensive and
time-consuming to build a list of prospective
customers
★ It was also very expensive to follow up, as each step
involved either mailing, calling or some other
“manual” work
★ The internet has completely changed the game…

The New List-Building Reality
★ Now you can literally build a list without spending any
money on marketing (and you can also use many different
paid channels as well, of course)
★ And you can follow up with your prospective customers
dozens - or even hundreds - of times, for almost nothing
★ This changes the dynamics of how to build a list and a
business online

Considerations For Prospecting
★ Prospects want information, they don’t want to be sold
something quite yet
★ The invention of the search engine has given prospects a
lot more power - and they use it
★ One of the reasons I like search marketing so much is
that you learn a lot about what information people are
searching for before they buy - which is highly valuable

The “Price” For An Email Or
Follow Is Going Up
★ As we become busier in our lives, the “payment” we must
get for giving out our contact information goes up
★ This is one of the reasons why we must offer highlyvaluable free content in exchange for opt-ins and follows
★ Also, when someone is a suspect, this also means that they
“suspect” YOU - they don’t trust you yet

“Move The Free Line”
★ The fastest way to build a list, in my experience, is to
offer highly valuable information about how to get a
big benefit - and to give it away for free
★ This can be in the form of free reports, videos,
audios, webinars, hangouts, software, an
analysis, etc.
★ The key to moving the free line is that you must give
away knowledge that’s incredibly valuable

Think Of This As A “Starter Product”
★ I like to think of my “Free Line Magnet” as another
product that I’ve added to my Product Path - only
this one is free
★ If you add this free product to the front-end, you get
a lot more people to “upsell” later
★ Since this piece is, in a sense, a product, we want to
make it fantastic so that it’s easy to market later

What’s The Most Valuable Thing
You’ve Ever Learned?
★ When it comes to your product or service - and more
specifically the result it delivers - what’s the most
valuable insight you’ve ever had about it?
★ Even more specifically, what’s the key insight that will
allow someone else to go from “in the dark” to
“empowered” and confident… immediately?

Examples
★ Attraction isn’t a choice
★ Make sure it’s obvious what your website is about
★ Identify the highest value work you do, and do it first
★ An ad I’ve used to hire someone
★ Add an opt-in as the front page of your site
★ Move The Free Line

Communicate With Impact
★ Jay Abraham says “Communicate with impact, or don’t
communicate at all.”
★ This has never been more important than when you’re
prospecting and following up online
★ The key point to remember is that a low-value
communication doesn’t just waste time, it also programs
your audience that what you say isn’t valuable

Creating Your “Free Line” Piece
★ Start by identifying the specific, tangible result or outcome
that your customer wants - and then create an educational
piece to help them get there
★ Start with highly emotional desires, and not “logical” or
“rational” objectives
★ A good place to start is by listing the mistakes that are
important to avoid when buying your product or service
★ Finally, turn it into a “magnet” by putting it into a report, etc.

A “Mistakes” Report Is A Great Start

Don’t “Hoard” Your Expertise
★ It’s easy to get trapped in a “scarcity” mindset when
creating Free Line content
★ Instead of thinking that giving away your best ideas
for free leaves people not needing more, realize that it
actually makes good prospects want more
★ My experience has been that the more valuable my
free content, the more sales in the long-run

You’ll Still Have To “Market” Your
Free Educational Piece Later
★ Even if you have the most valuable piece in the world, you
still have to convince people to come and get it
★ The $100 bill / $20 bill problem
★ Stay focused on the specific fears and frustrations - and
the specific outcomes that your prospective customer will
get by getting your Free Line piece
★ This is another reason why you have to make it GREAT
