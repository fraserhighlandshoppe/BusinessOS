Accelerate Your Productivity
Shifting Focus To Achievement
What is most motivating to you in life? What do you naturally seek to achieve? Are you
more motivated by love and approval, power and influence, or by achievement of specific
goals?
To maximize the productivity in your business, develop your achievement motivation
TIP: Create a “vision board” - and use goal visualization & affirmation in order to motivate
yourself to take specific actions in order to create the value you’ll need to build your business

Designing Your Productivity Dojo
What is the ideal physical space for you to practice your productivity rituals?
What needs to be removed from your current space in order to maximize your productivity?
What needs to be added to your current work space to maximize your productivity?

Designing Your Energy Cycle
What is the best way to center and prepare yourself to do a focused 60-120 minutes of
concentrated value-producing work?
What is the highest value work you do in your business, that pays off big in 1-2-hour
chunks?
What is the best way to do a focused 30-minutes of relaxation and rejuvenation during a
work day?

Incentivize Yourself
What are the strongest incentives in your day-to-day life - the things that motivate you to
either take action or avoid situations - that you could use to motivate yourself to action?
How can you layer multiple incentives to make sure you do whatever it takes to get the
results you want?
Use pictures, affirmations and visualization daily!
