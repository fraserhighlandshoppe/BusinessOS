YOU: Session 6
On Causation
In this session you'll learn the limitations of cause and eﬀect thinking, the freedom of Teleological
Causation, the danger of pursuing things other people want for you (but that you don't really want
yourself )

• According to Wyatt, the biggest problem of the world is sacrificing long term gain for short term
profit. One solution to this is to begin thinking Ecologically. Ecology is the study of consequences,
but it's diﬃcult to trace out consequences because it's a complex thing to do and takes a big
eﬀort. Few people do it. If you want to have massive success, begin thinking Ecologically.

• Many of us are searching for something that we got programmed to search for during childhood.
We need to ask ourselves: is this thing that I want, in reality, just what my parents wanted me to
want? Is it something my friends wanted? My culture? Is it something I STILL want? People spend
most of their lives on a certain path, only to realize much later that it was the wrong road all
along: the fulfillment they thought would be there at the end, isn't. And at that point it takes a lot
of integrity and eﬀort to turn around, find the right road, and start heading down the right road
instead.

• There are two approaches to doing things. One is to focus on results, the other is to focus on the
process. If we focus on results, we win or lose based on the result. But if we focus on the process
and enjoy the journey, and let go of results, we may find we get a lot more satisfaction out of life.
We become less compulsive, less driven. That can be beneficial. Enjoying the journey makes life a
lot easier than if life is only about getting to a destination. Carlos Castaneda taught that all paths
lead nowhere, but some paths have heart. Follow a path with no heart and it may turn around
and kill you. Follow a path with heart, and every day you can have joy. There's only one true goal follow a path with heart, and traverse it for its full length.

• The ultimate philosophical question is... does the universe pull, or does it push? Should we push
toward goals, or should we create goals and allow them to pull us toward them? Teleological
Causation is the idea that the universe pulls... that the future can restructure the now to create
itself. Create a vivid picture of what you want in your mind, and this in itself has some power to
put into motion things needed to make it a reality. When we live this way, we can free ourselves
from the constraint of thinking we can only use what currently exists to get us to our goals, and
instead dive into a realm of possibility that's free of the constraints of where we're at now. But for
most people, change is about identifying what causes now would create the eﬀect they want in
the future. This is limiting because we must start from where we are, and there are only a finite
number of actions that will get us from here to where we want to go.

• Another problem with traditional cause and eﬀect thinking is unintended consequences. Most
linear change requires backing up 5 or 6 steps to get started. But when we back up, it causes
consequences we didn't foresee, and now we have to deal with these problems as well. This
creates even more steps to get to our goal...

• How you represent your desired future makes a diﬀerence. If you can describe a picture of a world
better than what we have now, that picture will have power to help make it real. Great leaders
know this. They are able to create a picture of a better reality and communicate it with enough
feeling that it become infectious. Whatever you want in your own life, you'll have a better chance
of getting it if you first create a powerful vision of it. When you do, it can literally pull you toward
it.

• There's a type of thinking called Solution After Next Thinking. It acknowledges that whatever we
do to solve a problem will create additional problems. The point is to plan for the solution to
THOSE problems, the ones your immediate solution created, so you're always thinking 2 steps
ahead. That'll help you avoid major obstacles most people can't predict.
