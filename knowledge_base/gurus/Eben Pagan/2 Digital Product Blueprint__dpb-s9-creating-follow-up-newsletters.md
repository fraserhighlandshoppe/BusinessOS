Creating Follow-Up Newsletters & Videos
Once someone has opted-in to your mailing list, it’s important to follow up and build a relationship while at the same time connecting and offering your products and services. The formats that I like best
are the “Tip” newsletter, the “Q&A” newsletter, and the “Mailbag” newsletter. All work well, and the Q&A +
Mailbag formats have the added benefit of having others helping to create your content.

List The Top 3-5 “Hot Button” Emotional Topics To Write About

Create 5-10 “Serialized Variations” For Each Topic

Start Creating Daily Newsletters Or Videos With Tips For Each Topic
