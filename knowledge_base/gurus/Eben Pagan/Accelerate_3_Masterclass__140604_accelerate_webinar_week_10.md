The Rhythms
Of Success

A Key To Learning & Growth: Feedback
★ “Things that are measured improve. Things that are
measured and reported improve exponentially.”
★ In order to take your business to the next level, it’s
important to put systems in place that create a culture
of reporting and feedback
★ The higher quality the feedback, the faster your
improvement, growth and evolution

“Operating Rhythms”
★ It’s very easy to get distracted in your day-to-day
work, and forget what’s important
★ It’s even easier for your team to get distracted, as
they don’t have the perspective that you have
★ To maximize your alignment and productivity, add
operating rhythms to your business - and watch it
become a learning machine

3 Key Operating Rhythms
★ Daily Huddles
★ Daily Updates
★ Daily Dashboards

The Danger Of Validation Bias
★ One of the most fascinating “cognitive biases” is what’s
called validation bias
★ We humans walk through the world looking for evidence
that what we believe is true - and we tend to avoid
evidence that what believe is false
★ This leads us to waste time creating products and
marketing that customers don’t buy, and to do activities
that don’t result in money being made

The Daily Huddle
★ This is a short, 10-minute or so meeting that you do
ideally at the beginning of every day
★ It’s a place for everyone on the team to meet, align,
connect and report in their progress
★ The format is simple: Each of your project or team
“leads” gives a 60-second update about where they
are at, and what they need

Benefits Of A Daily Huddle
★ Everyone knows that they’ll be attending the huddle,
so they start thinking in terms of having to report
progress on a daily basis
★ Everyone knows that they can find everyone else there,
so it reduces the need to interrupt during the day
★ It gives you the chance to keep your finger on the
pulse of your business and your team

Daily Updates
★ When you have a team, it’s easy to confuse activity with
productivity - and to “see a person doing a lot,” but miss
that they aren’t getting results
★ By installing a daily update system, it gives you another
way to look at the progress and results of each of the
people on your team
★ The daily update also helps you to identify when
someone new isn’t getting the job done early on

A Simple Daily Update Outline
★ What I did today & results I got:
★ Challenges and obstacles:
★ Questions I have:
★ My current priorities:

Daily Update Tips
★ Have each team member write their daily update by
email - and submit it to you
★ Tell them that this should take a total of 5 minutes or so
- any longer, and they’re “doing something wrong”
★ Ask them to do their daily update last-thing each day,
which will give you the most insight into how people
behave and report on what they do
