Marketing Time Management Plan
In my experience, the “first domino” that causes all of the others to fall is DAILY MARKETING
EXECUTION. The key elements of your daily marketing execution are: 1) Marketing design and
writing, 2) Marketing testing and tweaking, 3) Building relationships with partners and advertising
channel contacts. I recommend that you invest a MINIMUM of 25‐30% of your day on these activities.

Ritualize Your Marketing Design And Writing (60 Minutes Daily)
How will you need to set up your environment, schedule, business to ensure that you invest at least 60
minutes UNINTERRUPTED per day designing and writing your marketing?

Ritualize Your Marketing Testing And Tweaking (60 Minutes Daily)
How will you need to set up your environment, schedule, business to ensure that you invest at least 60
minutes UNINTERRUPTED per day testing and improving your marketing?

Ritualize Your Relationship Building (60 Minutes Daily)
How will you need to set up your environment, schedule, business to ensure that you invest at least 60
minutes UNINTERRUPTED per day building relationships with your marketing partners?
