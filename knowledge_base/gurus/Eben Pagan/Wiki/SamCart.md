#1 - Pick A Gorgeous Custom Template
As soon as you login to your SamCart account, the first thing you do is pick from one of our pre-tested page templates.

We have a ton of them to choose from, and you can customize any of them so they look exactly how you want them to.
Every single template we create has each of the essential elements you need if you want to turn visitors into paying customers.

Here's just a few things each page has built-in, and why each of them will send your income soaring through the roof...

    An area for your company's logo so your visitors know who's behind the product...
    Your company's contact or customer support info so your visitors know there's a real business behind the scenes...
    A section for customer testimonials so your visitors know this product has helped other people in the past...
    A spot for a sales video (if you have one) so your visitors can see the product in action...
    A section for displaying your product's guarantee so your visitors know there is no risk if they purchase from you...

    An image of the product so your visitors can see what they're getting...
    A short description of the product so you can tell your visitors why they should buy...
    A section for bullet points that can outline the benefits of the product so your visitors know how they will personally benefit after they buy...
    The ability to accept credit cards or PayPal so your visitors can pay however they want to...
    "Trust Seal" graphics that prove it's a secure page

#3 - Put Your Checkout Page Anywhere & Watch The Sales Roll In
After your page is ready, the next step is simple: put it everywhere you can.

You can embed it on your blog, your landing pages, or send and email to your list that takes people directly to it.

You can add it to your YouTube videos, your Facebook & Instagram posts, or send people there from Facebook/Instagram/Adwords/YouTube Ads.

Just get people to your "1 page"...and your product will sell.

Plus, starting in early September, you'll have a simple "Buy Button" and "Pop-Up" that you can embed anywhere you want, which will make it EVEN EASIER to sell your stuff anywhere on the internet.
After that, just sit back, and watch sales start to roll in.
