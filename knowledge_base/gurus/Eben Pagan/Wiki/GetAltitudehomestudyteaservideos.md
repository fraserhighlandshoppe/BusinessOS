==From Altitude Home Study==

Humans make emotional decisions.  As an entrepreneur you need to zoom in on this process.  We need to translate our products into values, and it is systemic.

#Need to see the world through the eyes of our customer.  However we need to put aside our own perspective, and this is extremely difficult.  Keates (poet) had the concept the negative capability - shutting down your own process so that you can feel what your target does.  You will then feel the emotions the other person will.  They want delivery from pain or delivery to enjoyment.  This want is a form of currency.  If you translate what they want into a currency you can use, you can then get them to give you their money (an alternate currency).

===Creating your customer avatar===
"be them"

It's impossible to overestimate the extent of civilization is built upon a renunciation of instinct, human emotions, communication.

It's impossible to overestimate the extent that business is built upon a renunciation of instinct, human emotions, communication.

The aim of marketing is to know and understand the customer so well that product or service fits them and sells themselves - Peter Drecker

When you know your customer, you can create products that speaks to them and fits their needs.

Take your complete set of your customers.  Project a persona that describes the common attributes that all of them have.  This provides you with a customer avatar, with which you can talk about them intelligently.

Then take everything your business has created (products, services, marketing) and project that out into your business's avatar.

Then create a conversation between your customer avatar and business avatar.  You want this dialog to talk to the "sweet spot" within the Zenn diagram of your customer persona.  (Sweet spot is where each of the attributes overlap).  If you talk to a different attribute, you will loose a good portion of the audience because it doesn't care about that attribute.

===Stars===

Non stars see themselves at effect - they feel things happen to them.  They don't feel they have the power to change their future.  They have the "victim" mindset.

===Building the business===
When it comes to building a business - it is better to find people who are already intrinsically motivated - already have the passion, already pre-wired to accomplish what they can.

===Central Planning===
Philisophical approach to running an economy - governments thought that if they controlled all of the resources than things will run efficiently.  When you try to control things too much, it doesn't work.  There is a resistance to perform as expected when so controlled.

People from big business want to plan the success.  They want to know how much sales will we have in the next year so that they can allocate resources, etc.

When things don't work out, the blame is always put on having not stuck to the plan.

Need to balance.  Try to put as few conditions onto the plan.  See how people and the system  behave all by itself/themselves.  If you are too busy trying to plan the success, you won't be able to see what is happening and reacting to it.

Need to test the plans, marketing campaign, etc.  Things will probably not work big if they don't work small.  So start with a small test for the marketing campaign.  If no one calls, don't spend more money scaling up a losing campaign.

Analagy - put down buildings but don't put down the sidewalks.  Watch where the paths are going, and that is where you build the sidewalks.  Put the conditions in place (buidings) and run the system.  See where things are going, and then start to solidify the plan, process, etc.

===Becoming more effective and efficient===
Money making - happens on the edges or even outside of your business.  These are the things that get the money to come in.  There are typically few of them.  Examples would be a page on one of our partner's websites.  This is when the customer does something, and interacts with some system you set up.  Money making is not a function of something done within the business.

Strike the term employee from your business.  Call them team mates.  Employees have the mindset "I'm the one who does all the work;  I'm the one who makes all the money."

The purpose of the business is to support, protect and optimize the moneymaking.  The business is your order takers, fulfillment system (warehouse, shipping, etc).  

Focus your energy on the money making and delegate the business processes and systems.

===Staffing===
New hires - give them a project that they own.  See if they take charge and get it done.

Reviews - ask your team mates what they think of the different team members.  You will find out if there is an individual that is a bad fit.  Bad fits decrease motivation and therefore performance of the team as a whole.  However, people won't come to you unless the person is a real problem.  But asking about it allows them to mention the friction.

You need one of each on any team that you start to make it go well.

If you put 4 organizers on a team - they will spend a lot of time making a plan.  Once they get a few steps in, they see that things aren't going to plan, and they stop to replan.

Put 4 creatives together, they will spend months making graphs, figuring out the theory of the universe, nerf guns, etc, but they won't make any progress.

So if you aren't seeing results from the team, it means you don't have the right mix of organizers and creatives.

===Capacity and Demand===
Once you hit the age of 30 you max out at capacity.  Your body has built as much muscle mass it will without intervention.

If we don't have enough physical energy, we can't bring it all to the table.  
#nutrition (what, when, how much)
#fitness (how fast do you recover)
#sleep
#rest and renewal (mid day breaks)

emotional energy
#quality.  To perform at your best you have to feel a certain way.
##High positive zone.
Any time you aren't elated, engaged, focused, you are sub-optimal as a performer and leader.  You need to learn how to train this.

Mental energy - capacity to focus your attention (thinking in an absorbed way for a sustained time thinking about 1 thing only).

Stress is the only way we expand capacity.  Stress is necessary.  When you stop growing you start dying.

You need to continue using things you want/need available in the future.  You need to keep using it past your comfort zone.  However, not continously.

Recovery - This is the relaxation after the demand is over.  If you don't get the relaxation (stress doesn't stop) then you get spasms, pain.

Need to build a systemic relationship in your life between expenditure (stress/demand) and recovery.

You will notice that demand continues to grow.  However, the capacity will peak, and then decrease over time.

===Inevitable thinking===
In order to make a result inevitable, it is important to use the best actualization tools.  The key element is the conditions that are put into place.

Best way to actualize:
Most people are better at problem solving than setting goals.
#Get the list of problems out of the way
#Then get the list of objectives and goals we want to create.

Instead of going right to action steps, you need to ask yourself what are the conditions that need to exist for the desired outcome to happen automatically.

This also gets your mind away from thinking that you (yourself) will do it.  You need to get things to happen automatically.

THEN set up your action steps to create the conditions so that the goal occurs.

Until recently there was one main way of achieving things - goal setting.

Bob Beile - book "Stop setting goals if you want to solve problems"
 
80% of people are motivated by solving problems whereas 20% are motivated by the goal.

You need to be able to specify "Here's the goal, here is the problems".

You need a methodology that activates the mind that guarantees getting there.

If you start asking what are the conditions for success, this is a "problem" that can be solved.

===Use Case thinking===
Use the tools that the [[Customer Avatar Template|Customer Avatar]] use.  Start thinking in the use case of how the customer will use the tool (or product/service).

Business people like to idealize our customer.  "They are just like us - smart, attractive, successful, etc".

You want to talk to your customer from an idealized perspective, but don't idealize the customer themselves.

By definition the average customer (american/western) has an IQ of 100.  They are also 30 pounds overweight, moderate to low self esteem, a level of anxiety or depression, and have .75 best friends.  We don't have a lot of people to connect with, we don't identify with a lot of people.

Your customer is probably a regular person.  You need to be in touch with your customer so that you can speak their language to fix their problems.

===Failure and success===
Take the mindset that failure is lessons, rather than the common failure is a reason to stop.  Keep failing to lead to the insight.  Don't allow the failures to affect your optimism, confidence, etc.

Spectacular failures can be cheap lessons.  Unsuccessful people use failures as a reason to not try again.

Set up small things to fail at, so that you gradually learn with little loss.  Once you work through a bunch of small failures, you will have learned the things that will then succeed which will then pay for all of the failures.

You need to set up your business so that the employees stand up and acknowledge that they screwed up.  You do this by not punishing them for it.  This drives out the fear.  It keeps them from hiding the failure and their efforts to fix it.  It also gets rid of the defensiveness and looking for scapegoats if they are discovered.
