===The Million Dollar Multiplier===
Presentation summary and worksheet: [[Media:MillionDollarMultiplier.pdf]]

#Attract better quality client
#Work less
#Quit competing on price

If you aren't the trusted authority in your market, you are a commodity.  
*This is bad news because you look like all of your competitors.
*This means you are competing on price.
Competing on price is the worst one you can follow.  Your prices will have downward pressure.

====Positioning====
Example - song writer.

Used to be "I write songs in the style of Elton John and Billy Joel".  Became "I write love songs that make women fall in love".

The formula:
#What I do is:
#What this means to you is:  (Think from the client's perspective.  What is the benefit to them from buying from you)
#What this really means to you is:
Example:
*I sell Insurance
*I help you id risks that you should protect against.
*I help insure a happy and healthy future for you and those you love by protecting the things you value the most.
====Becoming the trusted authority====
It is '''not''' about:
#being the best known
#being the best educated
#being the expert
##knowledge
##experience
##credentials

People don't buy what you know, they buy what you do for them.  They don't care about what you do;  They care about what you deliver to them.
*Talk about what you deliver.
**NOT: I am a coach/therapist/dentist/...
**I remove pain from XXX
**The brain is asking '''What's in it for me'''
Your client doesn't want the product or service - they want the solution to a problem that you provide.

'''Expert''' - has knowledge.  '''Authority''' - has influence and power

#You have to have a core competence in your market.  You only have to be able to help your customer solve their problem.
#You have to be committed to helping as many people as you possibly can.
The only person questioning if you are an authority or not is YOU.  No one will award you the title - you have to claim it.  Someone will be the authority, might as well be you.

Most people trying to become the authority are doing this because they are trying to make a quick buck.  However the market is reasonably smart so these people aren't rewarded.

You don't become the trusted authority by being the best - you become the best by being the trusted authority.
