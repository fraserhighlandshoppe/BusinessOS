==Opportunity 5 day challenge==
===Day 1===
There is a big window of opportunity when the collective feeling is negative (like during Covid-19)

Notice how virtual events are taking off.  Notice how the way of doing things has really shifted.

Research shows that you remember best what was happening to you when you are having the strongest emotions.  What you do now will imprint on the collective memory.  Now is the time for leadership.

Do things to help others.  Contribute.  If you do things that benefit mainly yourself the community WILL remember (Karma).

Parado principal applied - spend 80% of your time in the positive.  Only spend 20% of your time in negative (worry, planning for the worst).

We will be exposed to an ever increasing number of smaller opportunities.  Conversely we will have less large opportunities.

The window for an opportunity is also going to become smaller.

===Day 2===
Beware of the danger, but recognize (we aware of) the opportunity.

#In the future the value of things will change faster and faster.
#Opportunity will come from unexpected places.
#creativity will become more and more important to opportunity.

Need to find the new, unexpected way to put things together.  There are new ways because new capabilities are always appearing.

When in fear, people fall back on old habits/patterns. 

Notice people are saying "when things get back to normal..."  Everyone is making plans on the idea
that we are going back in time, rewiding to December 2019 and starting over again.

 Creativity is taking what we know from one domain and apply it into another domain!

Opportunity is what you bring to reality, not what reality brings to you.  You are responsible for the amount of opportunity in your life.  Creativity is important to opportunity.

===Day 3===
Beware of the danger but recognize the opportunity!

You need imagination to create opportunities.
*Logic and science will actually block your creativity.

Metaphor: The universe is full of opportunities.  Your mind needs to be fertile ground for the opportunity to take root.

What are you naturally good at, and is valuable to other people?
*Because we find it easy we find it hard to believe other people don't and that they will pay us for it.

Also look at everything you are doing and how it could be transition to online
*Poets and musicians are doing drop in performances on Zoom parties.

Also look at things that you experience in life that should be obsolete.

What are peoples Fears and Frustrations?  What are their desires and aspirations?
