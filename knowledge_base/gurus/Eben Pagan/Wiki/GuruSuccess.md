==Branding==
Brands that are named well do much better than those that aren't.
===Naming Products===
The phonological loop is the processing path for sound entering the ear.  It bounces around for about 5 seconds as we are determining if it is important or not. 

To make the sounds bounce around longer, and therefore get processed into short and long term memory:
*use repetitive sounds (Coca Cola, Dixie Chicks, M and Ms, Eminem) (Marilyn Monroe, Marilyn Manson.  Ozie Ozzbourne.  Blackberry.  Bed Bath and Beyond)
*Rhyme (Junk in the Trunk,  Cutie with a Booty, Double your Dating.  You Snooze You Lose)
*Rhythm (David DeAngelo, Da-vid De-An-Ge-Lo Dou-ble Your Da-ting)


We tend to like things we are familiar with.

==Productivity==
You need to focus on the highest value activities to drive value for yourself.
===Removing Friction===
Friction is anything that slows you down.  Catches on you, snags, slows you down.

Try to pro-actively remove any friction from your processes.
====Stop setting goals if you want to solve problems====
80% of people are problem solvers rather than goal setters.

Instead of saying "what is the goal we are setting", ask "what are some of the problems we need to solve right now".

Asking about the problems fulfills your team's needs first.

====Brainstorming====
Be careful when brainstorming in front of your team.  The things you say sounds like an order rather than just an idea.

Lead with "Lets brainstorm.  Lets not do any of these just yet".

====Share your thinking process====
Laying out the plan after you have made all the decisions does not empower people.  Since they weren't involved and it isn't their idea, they aren't as invested.

Instead, have them participate.  Ask them what problems to solve.  Walk them through how you reason out the steps, ideas, etc.  Ask them what they think.

====Not Letting Go====
This is huge friction.

 "What you stick with you get stuck with"

Don't get too attached to anything.  Not a process, car, home, etc.  If you are, there isn't a space for anything else.

If you are doing chores, you need to determine your why.  If you are doing your own shopping (low value) because of "work ethic" etc you are decreasing your productivity.  If you are doing your own cooking because you enjoy it, then it has higher value.

==Mindset==
===Everything is a test===
Should be in a constant state of testing, refining, editing.

You want to get everything into action as soon as possible so you can be testing the results and improving on them.
===Product Plus Marketing Equals Success===
Identify opportunities.  Where do your customers have wants, desires, fears.  Package the knowledge up so it has a high perceived value.

Most of your time effort and energy should be spent in creating the product and creating it's marketing.  Don't waste time on setting up your desk, or worrying about the color of your logo.

Great products and great marketing lead to customers which lead to sales.

The knowledge in your head can be valueable seeds (Jack and the Beanstock).  Nurture them correctly and you will have success.

===Narrow Your Focus===
Most people open their focus because they want to reach more people.  They then make more generic products.  Instead, determine who is most of your customers.  What are their traits, and a specific need that you want to zoom in on.  This way you are speaking to a specific customer rather than the world at large.  They hear you speaking to them and their need rather than to the world in general.
===Irrational Need===
Humans are more irrational than rational.  Our natural wisdom is from the irrational part.

Marketers fail when they speak in abstract ways to the rational mind.  This doesn't help people make changes, and doesn't goad them to buy products from you.

Some people think it lacks dignity to talk to the animal in another person.  This is true if you talk down to them.  Instead honor their irrational need.  Speak to the biological part in a persuasive way.  This will help you connect with others.  Once you have the bond they will invest in you.
