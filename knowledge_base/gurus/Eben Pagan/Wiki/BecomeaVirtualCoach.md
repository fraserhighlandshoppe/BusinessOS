The key is to do work you love
*Survey - only 13% like going to work.

primary reasons:
*Virtual coach gets to make a real difference in peoples lives
*Can be very profitable

Coaching is about connecting with people and helping them get to the next level.  It is one of the few jobs that is relatively future safe as computers replace a lot of work.

===#1 skill of high paid coaches===
Average coaches help their client do something.

High paid coaches focus on supporting their clients through transitions and transformations.


===Getting more clients===
*Targeting people looking for a coach (tip of the iceberg)
*target people who are looking for a solution, but not yet looking for a coach (99% of people - base of iceberg)
**give away your best ideas for free
*use google adwords to identify the solutions people are searching for
**generate the client magnets
*use your new client magnets on facebook
===Become a coach faster===
*Need to discover the profitable niche
**Google keywords
**use planner to see if likely profitable
***is enough people searching
***are they motivated
***are there lots of competing (against you) solutions
*Need a methodology that is universal
*need a certification
