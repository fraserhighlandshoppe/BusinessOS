If you have a product, make a related, much more expensive one.  It will double your business.

Success comes in as a series of small breakthroughs one at a time.

For physical products, you should have a digital one too.  How-to/manual/tips/...

With 3B people inline, There are people searching for knowledge who 
don't want to spend the time to learn it the hard way - willing 
to pay 20/... Just has to be easy to consume/learn/use.
