==The Psycology of Conversion==

Conversion is the convincing the prospect to take an action.

*Decisions are Hard.
**They are immobolizing for most people.
*Every one of your communications must have a sales/marketing component.
*Content and Marketing are becoming 1 thing.
*Everything we do is a test!
**We want to test as fast as we can
**Most things don't work
**We are looking for the things that do so we can collect, organize, focus them into a funnel.
*Long copy sells better than sales copy
**this is counter-intuitive to people's attention span being shorter
**Your ad needs to be the length needed to tell the whole story unless you are selling an impulse buy.
**A motivated buyer will go through your long copy.
*People sub-conciously make the decision, and sub-conciously justify it.
**The reason they say they made the decision is the justification, but not the real reason.
**People make decisions on EMOTION, not on logic.
*Focus on the highest leverage areas (earlier in the funnel)

We need to help people through the decision making process in order to buy.

===Sales===
When one person sits down with another 1-1 live and walks them through the buying process.

 Eben suggests reading Spin Selling

*People imagine a sleazy used car salesmen using high pressure tactics.
*Professional sales is one of the most valuable service you can offer someone.  You help people get what they want in their lives or businesses.
**Don't try to override objections
**Don't pressure.
**We need to find out what their needs are.

Most objections are caused by the salesman.
*When the salesmen tries pitching a feature that the customer isn't ready to buy.

We don't want to push people into making decisions before we understand their needs because they will say we don't understand them and they leave.
*Ask what their problems and frustrations are
**What is motivating them to seek a solution in the first place.
*ask what the implications of their problem is.

When we start to ask the deep questions we find out what is motivating our customers is not what we thought.  This means our marketing needs to be different, and ultimately the product is too.

====Feature - Advantage - Benefit====
*A feature on a car might be air conditioning.
*Advantage is the air conditioner cools the air.
*The benefit is what the customer gets to experience - cooled cabin.  Ride around on a hot day and be comfortable.

Sell the benefit, not the feature.
*most salesmen and marketers sell the feature.
*The successful salesmen/marketers sell the benefit.

You might have to dig deep into the benefits.  The first benefit may not be all that great in the long run - need to dive down to the end result the customer wants.  Ex - SEO - 1st benefit is appear on first page of google.  But so what?  If that doesn't get any extra customers there is no point.  So benefit is more customers giving you money.

===Core motivators===
Identify the top motivators.
*change the viewpoint on the motivator, and your audience will keep listening/reading.
*staying on the same motivator will build a better relationship and produce better results than changing topics frequently

===Emotion Sequence===
Most people are not truly in touch with the emotions they are experiencing.
*They say "I didn't want to be there" rather than "fear, anger, sadness"

Our emotional brain runs most of our life without our thinking brain noticing.
*Jealousy manifests as anger.
Emotions hide inside others.  When you can identify the one that is driving someone, you can communicate with clarity.

When someone is going through a buying process, there is something that triggered them and activated them.  80% of the time this is fear, and the other 20% is desire.

If you listen carefully, a buying decision is an over compensation for insecurity or fear, or it is an over reaction to a triggered emotion.

#First we get activated.
#Then we do research on our options.
##If we find a marketing piece that grabs our attention, our curiosity can become fascination.
##Then they need to make a decision.  They need to come to am amplified fear/desire state.
#Then we need to offer relief so the fear/desire is solved.

If we forget this loop, we tend to try making the buying process too quickly and they fall out of the loop.

The emotional hot button is the one we need to best understand.
*What situation is being solved
**what is the emotions being felt driving this
**what is the underlying triggers/emotions.

===What is Value?===
By understanding what our customers value, we can know what motivations, emotions, words, images, phrases, sequences of communication we need to get our customers to convert (buy from us).

Value is an abstract where we took a process (value-ing) and converted it to a noun.

There is an outcome we want (or want to avoid) and a related set of emotions that move us toward or away from.

Observe people and you will eventually find the "incentive" that is leading to their behaviors.  In almost every case there is a real or imagined incentive.  If you don't know what the incentive is, you won't know what the value is, so you are left with product features.

We come with pre-wired ways of seeing things, thinking about things (cognitive biases).

A human will expend twice as much to avoid losing something than to gain something.  This can lead to self fulfilling prophecies.
*Car sliding on icy roads
*look up, see telephone pole.
*fear hitting the pole
*while trying to avoid the pole, end up aiming into it.
