It is not our fault we see the world the way we do.  It's not our fault we were never taught how to think about nor how to handle money.  On the other hand, it is 100% our responsablility to figure it out.

Everyone has their own internal model (reality) of wealth and success.  Without the big model, it is difficult to get there.  If you can't envision being rich, you will never get there.  You need to develop the new set of perspectives.

Money is valuable because it allows us to trade one piece of value for another.  But it intrinsically has no value itself.  We get programmed that the value is in the paper/coin.  It gets programmed into our language/thinking (take some money out of our house.  Take a cash advance against our credit card.)  We start thinking in money (I need to make some money) which is 2 levels of separation from reality.

What are my real needs (see Maslow).  What are the things in the world that has value that will help me meet these needs?

Focus on achieving these things directly rather than working on/for/through money.

===Psychology of money===
We have certain emotions that help us build relationships with people.  Helps keep families close, keep us safe and away from harm.

BUT

because we don't live in the environment we did when these developed in the species, this trips us up.

Once we understand our emotions we can then channel and use them.

====Crowd Psychology====
When there are more than a few people, these take on a completely different level of power and influence.
*Negative emotions in one person makes them do certain things
*Negative emotions in a whole crowd affects the individuals at a deeper level.

====The Ah-ha====
We think we are rational people that sometimes have emotions.

However, upon observation, people are emotional and our body and emotion uses our mind to get their needs met.

So our "logical" approach is really our mind justifying actions taken on behalf of our emotions.

Our logical brain evolved on top of the emotional brain.

No amount of high IQ will help if you don't have emotional intelligence.
 
====Self Deception====
Our perceptual systems prevent us from receiving most of the info coming in. (Info overload)

Our mind focuses on the thing that is most important to us.  Filters out everything else.

This leads to self deception.  

When having an emotional conversation with someone, you remember the 1-3 things that were your focus.  Everything else taht is remembered is in context to those things.

====Forms of Self Deception====
#Deletion - we delete huge patches of what happened in our memory and perception
#Distortion - we distort what happened
#Generalization - we lump everything into one idea.
#Confabulation - we ''make up'' stories about what happened constantly.  We don't understand something until we've put it into the story.

Self deception is a self preservation mechanism - otherwise we would have information overload.

However this really screws us up with respect to money, wealth, relationships.
=====Confabulation=====
We make up stories to explain how we got into the position we are in.  The Victim Mentality uses this.

We make very quick snap decisions.  We then use our conscious mind to make up the story that explains why we chose that choice.
*Most decisions are made 7 seconds before the conscious mind becomes aware.

====Secondary Payoff====
This is where you get a secondary benefit from staying in the situation.
*Attention
*The Story we get to tell.
**The sympathy and pity we get when telling the story to others.
*Not having to endure criticism, disapproval, ridicule and shame from your current peer group as you start to leave the pack.


====Influence====
Weapons of Influence
*Reciprocity
**IF someone does something for us we feel the need to return the favor.
*Authority
**We unconsciously act when told to do something 


When we are in the presence of one of the 6 major influence factors (weapons of influence), we react to it.
====Justice Mechanism====
Experiment - 2 people offered $100.  1st person decides how it will be split, 2nd person determines if the money is actually distributed.
*When person 1 says 50/50 then person 2 inevitably says OK
*If person 1 says 80/20 person 2 usually says neither of us gets some even though its free money either way.

Most people are stuck on this fairness emotion.  It prevents most people from generating massive amounts of wealth because they are expecting that reciprocity immediately, looking for people to give us value at the same rate as we give them value.  

If we are always waiting to be paid, we make less and less value, and end up receiving less and less as other's mechanism activates on their side.  To break this cycle, you need to just make and give away value continiously, and eventually the people will repay the value because they will have a feeling of debt/obligation due to the same mechanism.

He also spoke about giving away your best ideas for free - if you work for a company and patent an idea, your royalties will be really tiny because they are going to be the ones who spends the time, money and effort taking to market the idea.  So the value of the idea itself is extremely tiny; it is the execution of the idea that has the value.  So if you can't execute the idea, the idea itself is of very little real value so giving it away isn't really costing you anything.
