```markdown
“Naming Your Concept To Increase Value”
Key Points...
1. Without a name, each of your concepts is just "another idea." A good name can increase the
perceived value of your idea by 10x-100x. It's one of the most important things you can do in
your business to set it apart from your competition. Consciously naming your concepts
increases perceived value massively.
2. Teaching your customer how to bake a cake vs. revealing to them the "perfect cake method"
3. Don't just create catchy or cute names. People are serious about geN̂ing their needs met.
Create a name that grabs aN̂ention, promises a benefit, triggers emotions, and sticks in the
mind.
4. Power Words vs. normal words: Power Words are emotional, distinctive, and result-oriented.
Normal words are logical, common, and uninteresting
5. Teachers like to zoom out and talk about concepts, but many customers want brass tacks
and RESULTS. Compare "Total Health" vs. "Lose Fat Fast". Also consider:
• currency vs. cash
• mating vs sex
• expiration vs death
• lose weight vs. burn fat
• promptly vs. instantly
6. Here are 5 of the names Eben came up with that he thinks are his "best":
• The Kiss Test
• Double Your Dating
• Mental Money Maps
• Wake Up Productive
• Self Made Wealth
7. Avoid words that can be misinterpreted - even if they ARE powerful
```
