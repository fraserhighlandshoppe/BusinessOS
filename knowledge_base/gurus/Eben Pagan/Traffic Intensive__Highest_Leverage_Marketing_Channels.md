The Highest Leverage Marketing Channels
(and the Secrets to Taking Advantage of Them)

Key Points:
1.

Affiliate marketing: when other people sell your stuff to their list and you pay them a
share of the sales you make. If youe stuff doesn’t convert, an affiliate won’t send traffic
to you anymore.
• Don’t just put up your offer on an affiliate network and hope that others will send
you traffic
• Contact affiliates individually in-person or on the phone. Events and conferences
are great places to find affiliates. Connect them with socially
• Offer the person value first. Help them make more money. Help them re-design
their website. Give them what they want. Show them you know what you’re talking
about
• Make it really easy to work with you. Create valuable free stuff they can give to
their list, and create the marketing pieces for them (videos, templates, blueprints,
emails). “If your list decides to buy something, I’ll give you part of the money.”
• Create a great 60 minute webinar with 5-7 of your best tips and tricks

2.

Banner Ads and Media Buys
• How to buy inventory
• CPM is cost per thousand time your banner ad shows on a website. .05 cents is a
much better rate than $1 CPM
• “What’s your pricing if you buy 3 different amounts (low to high)? I’d love to buy a
ton if it makes a profit. I’d love to spend $50K a month. I need to test the traffic
and see what quality it is. Let’s do a test for $1K. If it works, and I hit my numbers,
then I’ll buy X (larger amount) from you within X period. So, do me a favor and
give me your best rate, and I promise I’ll spend the high volume with you if it
works”
• Look to do an ad buy the last day of the month or the quarter to get a discount

3.

Product Launch: affiliate marketing that is organized around an event (like a product
launch), which includes elements of anticipation and community.
• The blog is home base of your launch. It’s where prospects come back and
comment to keep getting more cool, free stuff
• Set-up an opt-in page every time you release more free stuff
• You need to have partners along with great free stuff they can offer to their lists
• The quality of the free stuff you give away is what attracts partners
• Over the product launch of two to three weeks, giveaway 3-5 pieces of cool content.
When they come to get their free stuff, let them know about the product you have
coming up
• Ask people to leave comments about the free stuff you’re giving away
• Only offer a limited amount of spots, and suggest people get on the early interest list

• Have a limited sign-up time frame: “the program closes on Tuesday”
• Do promotions: offer affiliate prizes. The number one affiliate gets a prize or money.

Organize the top experts in your group to start networking with each other. Be the hub.
