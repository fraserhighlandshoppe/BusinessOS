2. The Virtual Assisting Professional helps thousands of entrepreneurs worldwide run
their businesses and maintain their sanity, and the demand for GOOD virtual
assistants is sky-rocketing.
3. A Virtual Assistant (VA) is an independent entrepreneur providing administrative,
creative and/or technical services. Utilizing advanced technological modes of
communication and data delivery, a professional VA assists clients in his/her area of
expertise from his/her own office on a contractual basis.
4. Three things you MUST know before choosing to be a Virtual Assistant (VA)
• Your “WHY:” what is your motivation?
• Is Being a VA right for you? Do you enjoy being a service provider, take pride in
your work and can communicate professionally and positively with other people?
• The Pro’s and Con’s? You can start your business with limited funds, and use your
skills to earn income in a growing industry as you work with extraordinary
entrepreneurs. On the other hand, you are reliant on yourself, sometimes
isolated socially, and there are no benefits or consistent income.

10. Finding and Accepting Your First Client
• Use general marketing principles, networking, referrals and VA Associations, job
