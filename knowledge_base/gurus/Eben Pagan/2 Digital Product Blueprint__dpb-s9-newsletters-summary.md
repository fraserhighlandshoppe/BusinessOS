```markdown
How To Create Newsletters
Key Points...
1. Many sales are made in the follow-up - often more than are even made on the "front-end" (the "frontend" refers to the rst time someone visits your site).
2. Follow-up is about building a relationship with your reader.
3. Elements of an effective follow-up:
• Relevant: match what you're talking about with what the prospect needs
• Speci c Techniques: people love techniques, tricks, and action steps
• Involving: ask them to write in, comment, or do an exercise
• Fascinating: show a new way of looking at something or a counter-intuitive insight. Throw out any
content that is not fascinating... you'll bore your reader
• News: new, timely information has a high perceived value
• Discoveries: share discoveries that you made or new discoveries in science/psychology/etc.
4. Remember to use the why, what, how, and what if in each newsletter.
5. 3 Types of newsletters:
• Tip: the whole newsletters is you giving your content
• Q&A: take one question from a reader and spend the whole newsletter answering it in depth
• Mailbag: take a bunch of questions from readers and answer each briefly
6. For Tip and Q&A newsletters, here’s a general structure to use:
• Describe the problem
• Describe the general solution
• Give specific action steps of your solution
• Mention the benefits of getting the problem solved
• Pitch your product for the complete solution

7. Serialize. Don't make every piece of content about a different topic. Instead, choose your few
biggest hot-button topics, then address them from different angles.
```
