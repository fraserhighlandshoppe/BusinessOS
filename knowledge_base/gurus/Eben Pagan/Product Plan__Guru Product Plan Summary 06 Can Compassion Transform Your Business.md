Session 6: Can Compassion
Transform Your Business?
1. The most profitable business skill to learn in business is COMPASSION, and
imagining what it’s like to be another human being.
2. If you really understand another person, you lose the ability to be selfish and
blame them. You “walk a mile in their shoes.”
3. When you can understand from another’s perspective, you can say everything
in a way they relate to. You can talk to them about their problems without
them getting defensive, because you demonstrate you are on their team.
4. Ask yourself the question (AGAIN), “What does my customer think he or she
needs?”
5. What is your “magic bullet” equivalent of the “Instant Energy Smoothie for
Moms?”
