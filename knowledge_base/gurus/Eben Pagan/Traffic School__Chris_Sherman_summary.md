Key Points:
Here’s the Big Trend in Search

• Two major search engines now have 90% combined market share: Bing (Microsoft) and
Google

• There will be no major challengers for the next 5+ years
• Bing and Yahoo! recently paired up; actually, Bing’s search engine technology now drive’s
Yahoo!’s search engine. Microsoft does the heavy search lifting, both organically and
with paid search ads. Yahoo! sells “premium” ads: display, video, mobile and events

• “BingHoo’s” combined 30% share is too attractive to ignore
• Competition will heat up as advertisers send more dollars to Bing – possibly aﬀecting
auction pricing on both Bing and Google ad platforms

Bing

• It tries to understand the concept you are looking at, and tries to come up with search
results based on the concept you’re looking for (not just keywords). Bing is looking to
expand your options and ways of finding information

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
• Should you optimize your sites diﬀerently for Google and BingHoo? Generally, no. Good
SEO is the same for all search engines. Consider what the editor of a book would be
looking for: good, well-formatted, deep content. That is what the search engines are
looking for too
Webmaster Guidelines and Tools is a great resource to let you know EXACTLY what the search
engines are looking for: You can see it with the two links below:
http://www.google.com/webmasters/
http://www.bing.com/toolbox/webmasters/

Overlooked Opportunities: SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Images should include descriptive ALT IMG tags, filenames, and keyword-rich surrounding
text. This way the search engines can “see” them.
Local business? Take control of your place pages and include tons of location-specific
information on all of your web pages (address, phone numbers, etc).
Video:
• Video is overtaking text as the predominant web content. Have a strong video strategy

• Metadata is huge; be sure to include rich, descriptive, information about the video
• Like images, use lots of descriptive text on the page where the video is embedded
Ping blog search services whenever you have fresh content.
Tweet new content and consider adding a Facebook “Like” widget to your web pages.
Overlooked opportunities: PPC (Pay Per Click Advertising)
In your Google AdWords Account, experiment with “match types” and small bid changes.
Review and revise your ad copy! Ads should be well-targeted, speak to your audience, oﬀer
clear, dramatic benefits, use filtering language, have a succinct headline, and a simply stated
oﬀer.
Take advantage of the tools at adlab.microsoft.com
Experiment with ads on content networks (Google’s too) – the quality has improved over the
past few years, and content ads can be useful for brand building.
Local business? Big Brand? Advertise on maps (Google).
Retargeting (remarketing):
• Retargeting lets you display multiple ads to the same web user as they move across the
web

• These ads can be tailored to user behavior, even to the point of targeting “purchase
readiness” in a conversion funnel

• This is available for Bing & Yahoo! and Google (content ads only)
Tracking:
• You need to model the conversion process, and then collect data to see what’s
contributing and how

• You need to “attribute” or look at what is coming from where. Figure out which traﬃc
sources are giving you the highest-quality leads

Trends in Social Media
The global time spent online is Facebook at 16%, Youtube at 9%, and Google at 5%.
Twitter:

• It’s now a “real” search engine
• It has a “promoted tweets” marketing platform.
• TweetUp provides contextually sponsored tweets displayed on publisher sites, using a
CPM model with cost-per-click & cost-per-new-follower later

Facebook Insights – great analytics that tell you about the impact of your marketing
campaign and your target audience. Check out facebook.com/marketing.
Personalization is also aﬀecting search results:

• Google is leading the way here
• The opportunities to gain a strong positioning via universal search are good
• Think “digital asset optimization”
Google Instant: Let’s you see potential search results as you type

Conclusions:
• Get a presence on Bing
• There are more opportunities than ever for savvy search marketers
• Even though social media isn’t search, it plays well with search and should be part of
your online marketing arsenal
