Session 1: Modern Methods Of
Customer Relationship Building
This program is about getting more customers. In Session 1, you’re going to about building
relationships and conversion. You’ll learn the mindsets for conversion success and you’ll be doing an
exercise to intentionally create friendships with your customers.

•

In this program we’re going to learn how to build stronger relationships with our customers, and
at the same time, sell a lot more of our products and services.

•

Conversion is when a prospective customer takes action...typically when they buy something.

•

Sophisticated conversion tools are now available to everyone.

•

In the modern business environment, your success depends on you mastering conversion.

•

If you only give people one chance to buy you’ll only make a fraction of the sales & profit that you
would have if you created a system of follow-ups that builds trust & rapport over time.

•

Conversion is now about long-term relationships.

•

Mindsets for Conversion Success
‣

Long term business success results when customer needs are put first and they are treated like
long-term friends right from the beginning.

‣

Start adding value to your customers immediately.

‣

Use all the tools available to add value to your customers for the long-term.

‣

Become an advocate and a friend to your customers.

‣

Give away more value up front.

•

The Business-Friendship Model - The lines of business and friendship are blurring. The objective is
to get more people to trade value with you.

•

Customers like to buy from real people they like and trust.

•

Get out of the mass-marketing mindset and switch to an individual marketing mindset.

•

Take away: “Be the only place your customer needs to go.”
