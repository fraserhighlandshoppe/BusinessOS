Introducing the Other
eCommerce Giant:
Amazon.com
with Steve Lindhorst
Key Points:
1. What sells on Amazon? Well, it started with books, but now includes most major
shopping categories (primarily new items) and generally things that have a UPC or
ISBN.
2. Amazon and eBay are head to head with the volume of monthly shoppers. You’re
getting exposed to 62 million shoppers per month on Amazon! And these buyers will
typically pay a bit more – they don’t have the time or patience for the eBay listings.
3. You can open an Amazon Seller Account and sell non-media (Seller Central) or media
(Amazon Marketplace).
4. You can set up two types of accounts: 1) Individual Seller (free, but you can only sell
things already on Amazon, with 8-15% seller fee) and 2) Pro Merchant ($39/month,
ability to sell new products, with 8-15% seller fee).
5. Use “condition comments” to stand out from other sellers on the same product page.
6. Highlight your business credentials.
7. Mention shipping.
8. Mention that accessories are included.
9. Amazon requires shipping within 2 business days.

Action Steps:
1. Research your product options to see what you want to sell.
2. Set up either an Individual Seller (good for beginners) or Pro Merchant Account and
complete all sign-up information.
3. List your first product using the Tips above.
