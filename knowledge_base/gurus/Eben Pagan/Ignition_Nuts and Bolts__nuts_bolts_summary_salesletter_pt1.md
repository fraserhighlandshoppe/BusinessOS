Nuts & Bolts:

How To Write A Killer Sales
Letter - Part 1
Key Points:
1. Your sales letter is vital to the success of your online business.
• If your conversion rate (the percentage of visitors that turn into customers after
reading your sales letter) is high, you don't need a lot of traffic to make a good
income
• If your conversion rate is low, it doesn't matter how much traffic you get...you will
lose money. In fact, the more traffic you buy, the more money you will lose
2. You must speak to your prospect’s deepest wants and desires - to the degree you miss
the mark, your business won't grow as it could. The better you do this, the bigger your
profit.
3. "The Persuasion Interview" - a series of questions to discover your prospect's deepest
needs - and help you write your sales letter. Here are the questions:
• Why did you create this program, besides money? Think of 10-25 reasons
• What are the biggest benefits your prospect will receive from this program?
• What are your prospect's three biggest fears & frustrations? (Make a big list,
narrow it down to the top three, then repeat these over and over again in your
marketing)
• What is your proof that your advice works? Why should they listen to you?
Testimonials are great here - collect them as soon as you can, or get friends to
read your content and write your first testimonials
• Who is this program for? Who is it NOT for?
• What tasty information can you give away to let your prospect know that you
know what you're talking about? Lists are great - 3 critical mistakes to avoid and
5 tips to use right now are examples. Eben calls this "moving the free line." Share
advice that is unique or counter-intuitive
• Why won't people buy? What will be their objections? Some common ones
include: it costs too much, it won't work, it won't work for ME (my situation is
worse than normal), I'm afraid to give my credit card information over the
internet, I'm afraid it's not discreet and someone else will see what I ordered
• What is your prospect's irrational dream? And what is their nightmare? (Don't
push too hard here and stir up deep emotional trauma - just be realistic)

Action Steps:
1. Take 1-2 hours and answer each of the questions from The Persuasion Interview as
completely as you can. You'll use that information later when you write your sales
letter.
