Getting Customers Is Expensive
★ It costs approximately five times as much to get a new
customer as it does to sell something else to a customer
that you already have
★ In order to maximize the lifetime value of every
customer, it pays to invest in making the experience
of doing business with you emotionally positive

Customers Feel Out Of Control
★ When buying something, each of us wants to avoid
making a mistake, looking foolish, or being ripped off
★ The less we know about the product we’re buying, the
more out of control we feel - and the more fear we feel
★ Anything you can do to help your customers feel like
they are in control will make them love you

Innovation Is Evolving
★ In the past, in business, it was “enough” to create a
product or service that was new and innovative
★ Today, it’s not enough to just have a good product customers now want a great experience as well
★ Innovation itself is evolving to encompass new ways of
delivering a great experience to customers

Emotional Windows Of Opportunity
★ Every time you interact with a customer is an
opportunity to make an emotional impression
★ Each time your customer sees your marketing, contacts
your business, visits your site, talks to you or your
team... is an “emotional window of opportunity”
★ Some windows of opportunity are more powerful than
others - which are most important to your customer?

How Humans Bond & Get Attention
★ Consider for a moment - how do people bond with
each other and get attention?
★ We tell each other STORIES
★ Since your customers are going to be telling stories
anyway, give them stories that help them get
attention, impress their friends, and feel important

How Can You WOW Customers?
★ My first experience with the Lexus company
★ Nordstrom offers a refund for tires?
★ Zappos “delivers happiness” - how delightful is it to be
able to return your shoes or other merchandise for a full
refund - and have them pay the shipping both ways?
★ The story of Paddy Lund

Don’t Lose Momentum After A Sale
★ It’s easy to “lose momentum” after you’ve made a sale
★ It feels like the “hard part” is over, so most businesses
move on to getting the next customer
★ What if, instead of moving on, you took a bit of extra
time to make sure your customer is getting the most
from your product or service?

What Would Be MAGIC For Them?
★ For a moment, suspend all limitations and disbelief, and
imagine what a “magical experience” would be like for one
of your customers
★ What would happen, in what order, that would absolutely
astound, delight, and blow the mind of your customer?
★ From that beginning, what could you actually add to your
customer experience, then automate for all customers?

Plan The Series Of Experiences

The Power Of Customer Events
★ Many companies have regular events for their customers,
and get them together in the same place to learn,
network and benefit from group interaction
★ From companies like Apple, Microsoft and Google... to local
small businesses... any companies can benefit from
getting their customers together for an event
★ What kind of event would your customers enjoy?

Your Emotional Touch Points
★ What are the key points during purchase and use of your
product - that your customer is experiencing the
highest level of emotion?
★ What are the emotions that they are feeling, and
why are they feeling those emotions, specifically?
★ What would be a dream or fantasy for your customer
during each of these emotional experiences?

A “Wow” Experience
★ What is your customer expecting to have happen
during a buying experience with you?
★ What would go beyond their expectations?
★ What would be a “Wow” experience for them - to the
point where they would actually call their friends to
tell them, or share during a social experience?

Who Are Your Best Customers?
★ Make a list of your 20 best customers
★ Allocate a budget of 10% of this month’s
advertising or promotion, and then go spend it on a
personalized gift for each of these customers
★ Tell each of them how important they are to you,
and the difference they make to you and your life

Keep Up Your Momentum
★ What is one thing you could do right after a
customer buys from you - that would get them
engaged with your product faster and better?
★ How could you test it out immediately with your
next customers?
★ How can you keep the momentum up after every
customer buys from you in the future?

Design Your Customer’s Experience
★ Plan out a minimum of 5 steps of your customer’s
experience with you and your product - from the first time
they learn about you, until they refer others
★ Write the script for your customer’s experience with you,
from their perspective, in their words
★ Make sure that there is surprise, delight and relief in each
step for your customer - a positive emotional experience

Have An Event For Your Customers
★ One of the most powerful experiences you can create is a
“group wow” experience for all of your best customers
★ How could you have an event, and invite your customers to
meet, network with, and spend time with each other?
★ How could you do it in a way that actually wound up selling
more of your products - and paying for the event?
