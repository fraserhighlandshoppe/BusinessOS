Day 23:
The Wealth Formula
In this session you'll learn the Wealth Formula, how the rich use it
and the poor don't, how you can immediately apply it to your own
life to create more wealth for yourself and others, and much
more...
The greatest miracle is "compounding growth."
1. When you grow something over time, and it compounds (1 becomes 2

becomes 4 becomes 8 becomes 16...), it has an incredible power.
2. This is growth stacked on top of growth.
3. Hitch your wagon to it if you want to be successful with money and wealth.

Business and ﬁnancial success is both a game of skill and chance. We want it to be
all one or the other, but it's most valuable to think of it 50/50. Half of it you can
inﬂuence, half of it you can't. Similar to poker - you can bluﬀ, change your bets,
read others faces... but you can't control the cards you get. Take advantage of the
lucky breaks you get, and you can win big.
When most people get resources, they spend it right away.
"The discipline of stock trading... is the discipline of NOT trading..."
There's a diﬀerence between Good Fortune and luck. Luck is random. Good
Fortune is something that only comes around every once in a while, but it visits
EVERYONE.
1. Prepare for when Good Fortune comes, because although it doesn't come

often, it will come.
The 80/20 Rule, also known as the Pareto Principle.
1. 80% of the results come from 20% of the eﬀort.
2. This also means that 80% of your eﬀort only creates 20% of your results.

Some of your work is much more productive than other work.
3. The Pigeon Experiment:
a. They put two buttons in front of a Pigeon - one button gave twice as

much good as the other. Instead of pressing the button that gave
more food, it would press both buttons so it got the same amount of
total food from each button.

b. Humans do the same thing - we don't focus on where we get the

most results - we spread our eﬀort.
c. It's not intuitive for us to focus where the smallest amount of eﬀort

gives us the greatest reward. But if we want to become wealthy, we
must.
If you make a choice that's 1% better, you won't see it when you make it. But if you
repeat 1% better choices a thousand times, you'll wind up at a completely diﬀerent
place than if you hadn't.
We're wired for a time that no longer exists. We must resist our natural pulls in life
when we're in business situations. We need to zoom out, consider things, and do
the logical thing - not the thing we're emotionally triggered to do.
Stock traders and poker players make most of their proﬁt from just a few trades or a
few hands. They wait for Good Fortune to visit them, and when it does, they go and
go and go and milk everything they can from it.
Another mistake people make is confusing "money" with "wealth."
1. Poor people say being rich is having a lot of money.
2. Rich people say being rich is about creating value and accumulating assets

that create value.
3. Kiyosaki: poor people buy liabilities, rich people buy assets.

Wealthy people create value, and then as soon as money comes in as a result, they
reinvest that money to grow their assets.
Poor people work and get money, then instead of investing the money in their own
education or an asset, they spend their money on something that immediately
becomes worthless.
