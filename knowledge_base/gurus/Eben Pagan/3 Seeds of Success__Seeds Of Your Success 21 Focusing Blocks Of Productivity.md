Day 21:
Focusing Blocks of
Productivity
In this session you'll learn why you want to be a laser not a light
bulb, my 5 part recipe for maximum productivity, a simple
exercise that'll get you getting more done in an hour than you do
in a whole day now, and much more...
What's the diﬀerence between a light bulb and a laser beam?
1.

A light bulb sends photons in all directions. A 100-watt bulb can light up a
room.

2. A laser beam sends photons in just one direction. A 100-watt laser will not

only light up a room - it will cut through a plate of steel several inches thick.
Most people shine their focus in all directions, and wind up not getting anything
signiﬁcant accomplished.
Humans are great productivity prevention mechanisms. We are very instant
gratiﬁcation oriented, and very distractible. This makes sense for the ancient
environment our ancestors lived in and that we've been bred for. But it holds us
back in today's modern environment.
Productivity is about results, not work.
Productivity happens when your strength is focused in a block of uninterrupted
time, creating value in a situation where you have your biggest opportunity, and
doing it consistently over time.
Start this by doing one hour of pure focus time. Put a timer on to go oﬀ in an hour.
Say to yourself, "For this one hour, I am only going to apply my strength to creating
value on my greatest opportunity." Don't check email, voicemail, or anything else.
1. When you can do 1 hour, work up to 2 hours. Eventually an entire day is

possible.
Separate what you're focusing on from what's distracting you. This can be
challenging - we're addicted to constantly checking email, etc.
After you're distracted, it takes about 15 minutes to get back to the level of focus
before you were distracted. So if you're distracted every 15 minutes, you'll have

worked hard putting out ﬁres, but you won't complete anything signiﬁcant and
valuable.
Even turn oﬀ your internet connection so you're not tempted, if that'll help.
You should either be focusing your strength on creating value, or doing something
completely fun and rejuvenating. Don't get stuck in between.
Have clean focus, clean rejuvenation, and clean "cuts" from one to the other.
Other people and the world won't help you become productive. That's not their
agenda for you. They want to get your attention and distract you, especially other
businesses. They're trying to get your attention to get you to see their product. You
must develop the ability to be productive instead of succumbing to these
pressures for distraction.
Rehearse what you'll do to create your blocks of uninterrupted focus (turning oﬀ
your email, etc.).
