Day 4:
Creating Your First
Leverage Ritual
To create success in your life, one of the most powerful things you
can do is to create "habits" that pull you toward success without
eﬀort.
In this session you'll learn how to take control of the power of
habits, the hardest part about starting a new habit, how long you
must repeat a behavior before it becomes habitual and takes on a
life of its own, and much more...
Leverage: we can take a long stick and a fulcrum, and use a little eﬀort on our end
to move something huge on the other end.
This session is about ﬁnding and using "Inner Leverage."
The challenge right now is "change or die."
1. When given the choice, most people don't change. They die. Doctors tell

smokers stop smoking or you'll die in a year. Most keep smoking, and die.
Getting leverage allows you to take control of your life and create success.
We humans are creatures of habit. But we didn't choose most the habits. It's time
for that to change.
Take the natural process of habit formation, and use it to put the habits you want in
your life to create success.
Once you install a habit, it continues on its own power. The trick is to install the new
habit in the ﬁrst place. It can be hard to replace existing habits.
Trying to stop habits is a waste of time. Instead, start a new habit to take its place.
"Habit Gravity" - when ﬁrst starting a new habit, it's diﬃcult. We need enough
momentum to break free of the atmosphere, just as a rocket spends 85% of its
energy just to reach escape velocity. With habits, this takes about 30 days.

Humans get very little will power. Most of us burn it on internal and external
conﬂict. Use the little will power we get every day, and focus it on building a new
ritual to create a new habit. Do it for 30 days, then the resistance goes away, and
you'll feel pulled into your own habit.
You may need to use pain and pleasure to get yourself to do your ritual for 30 days
to reach escape velocity…
Best time of day - early, so you still have your will power.
Personal success ritual - what you do when you ﬁrst wake up
Business success ritual - focus two hours of each day on your top priorities, with
zero interruptions.
