Nuts & Bolts:

How to Write a Killer Sales
Letter - Part 2
Key Points:
"The Sales Letter Formula" is a fill-in-the-blanks template you can use to write a powerful
sales letter EASILY. Just write each individual section, then place them together in this
formula... and your chances of hitting a "home run" sales letter go up dramatically.
Here are the 18 pieces of the formula, in order... feel free to tweak the sequence... but
try to include as many of these as you can:
1. Benefit driven headline
• The most important part of the sales letter - you need to grab your reader's
attention
• Hit them with the biggest benefits of your product - these should be the biggest
needs they have
• "How To Add 20-40 Pins To Your Bowling Score - Guaranteed"
• Capitalize first letter for each word and put in quotes
• One formula - ask a question in your headline, then answer yes in your subhead
• Example: "Could it really be this easy to create a lasting connection with a
man..." "The answer is yes... and if you want..."
2. Subhead
• "If you want to x, y, and z... then this will be the most important letter you ever
read..."
3. Questions that show you understand & remind them of their pain
• Here's where you put the top fears and frustrations you listed in the previous
sales letter training, in the form of questions
4. Story
• Your story of how you had the same problem and how you struggled
5. "There is hope - and it's not your fault"
• Can be very short - it's a turning point in your letter from talking about their
pain... to talking about the solution
6. Give away free advice
• Give them some of your BEST ideas that they can use RIGHT AWAY and see
results
• This builds your credibility

7. Proof
• Why they should believe your product will work for them
• It could be that it worked for you in your own experience, it's worked for other
people, etc.
8. What's in your product?
• The story of what motivated you to create this product, how you created it, and
an overview of what they’ll learn and how it will help them
9. Bullets
• Bite-size "teasers" that makes them hungry to get the product
• Bullets tell them what's in your program, without actually giving them the
solution…they have to buy your product for that
• Check out a doubleyourdating.com or catchhimandkeephim.com sales letters for
examples
• You may want to write bullets first, then write the headline and the rest of the
letter
• Put the best bullets toward the top to help keep your reader "hooked"
10. Who needs it and who doesn't
• You can title this section "How can you know if this program is for you?"
• You may want to talk about who DOESN'T need it first, then who DOES... this
flows better
11.What makes your product different?
• How does your product differ from other products out there?
12.Here's what you're going to get
• Include images if you can
13.Price
• Give a comparison - spell out the bargain
• Example - compare hiring a professional one-on-one to help you... to the price of
your product
14.Guarantee
• Every great sales letter has a great guarantee
15.Close
• Do they want the dream... or the nightmare?
• Be realistic
• Ask for the order
16. Signature
