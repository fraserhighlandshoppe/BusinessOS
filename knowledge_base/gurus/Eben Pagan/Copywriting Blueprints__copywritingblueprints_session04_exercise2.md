“Power Headline” Formulas
Now let’s create the “ad for the ad” - a powerful headline. Go back over your notes and mentally “load
up” your most powerful emotional motivators again. Then, consider the benefits that your product/
service offers. Finally, use the following formulas to write at least 10 variations of this headline - that
will grab your prospective customer’s attention and draw them in instantly.

Result (Benefit) Focus
How To Get [Result] [Quickly] [Without Risk]
Example:	
  “How	
  To	
  Get	
  Out	
  Of	
  Debt	
  In	
  90	
  Days	
  Or	
  Less	
  With	
  This	
  Simple,	
  Guaranteed	
  System”

Pain Focus
How To [Eliminate Specific Pain] [Without More Pain]

  “How	
  To	
  Lose	
  33	
  Pounds	
  Of	
  Fat	
  Without	
  Torture	
  Diets	
  Or	
  Hard	
  Exercise”

Situation Focus
If [Specific Emotional Hot Button Situation Is Happening], Then [Worst Fear Might Be True]
