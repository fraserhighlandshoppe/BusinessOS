Nuts & Bolts:

Get Free Search Engine Traffic
with Your Blog
Key Points:
1. Use a wordpress blog, or if you must have free hosting, get a wordpress.com blog
(that's where THEY host it).
2. Focus FIRST on writing good content that your readers will like - optimize for the
search engines LATER. Keep your posts to 250-500 words.
3. To succeed with your blog, don't hide your content behind a membership site. Give
away great content, and give the content in complete ideas. This will help you get
backlinks... and that's a big part of SEO (Search Engine Optimization - getting free
traffic from search engines like Google).
4. Write the shortest possible blog post title that grabs your reader's attention. Include a
keyword in the title as close to the front as possible. Google favors this.
5. Continually publish & watch when Google picks up your post. Figure out why... then
improve it... to move the post up onto page 1. That's "Retro Optimization".
6. MyBlogLog will show you where you're listing in the search engines. (Install the
widget). Don't worry so much about the numbers of visitors... but do pay attention to
the keywords you're ranking for so you can retro-optimize and rank higher. Move the
keyword more toward the front of the title, more keywords in the first paragraph, and
more links.
7. "Keyword Density" doesn't matter anymore. Just put your keyword in your title, first
paragraph, and a couple more times throughout the post.
8. When you comment on another blog (if it allows links), link back to SPECIFIC posts on
your blog... not your homepage.
9. Ping.fm - When you post, it'll automatically put that post into all the accounts you
entered into it, like facebook, twitter, etc.
10.Add a "subtitle" as the first line of the post... and include your keyword. It helps with
ranking.
11.If your keyword is "dog training tips," it's okay if "dog training" is separate from "tips"
in your post. In other words, it's ok to break up long keywords.
12.To have your video rank on the first page, you need to be the top 1 or 2 videos for
that keyword on YouTube. To rank high, get lots of comments, votes, views, and links.
13.YouTube Videos: Put a link back to your site at the beginning of videos you post, and
in the description of your YouTube channel. These links are backlinks and will help
you rank.
14.Google will naturally find your blog post, but to speed up the process, go to a site like
pingomatic.com or pingoat.com and let them know each time you have a new post.
It's free.

15.Google Sitemaps - use this plugin - it automatically gives Google a sitemap and that
helps you rank. Just search "wordpress sitemap plugin" under Plugins on your blog.

Action Steps:
1. Install the Google Sitemaps plugin on your blog.
2. Sign up for MyBlogLog.
3. Write 5 posts using the suggestions above, each time letting Google know you posted
through pingomatic.com.
4. When one of your posts rank, "re-optimize" to move up to the front page.
5. Rinse and repeat...
