Day 29:
The Science Of Human
Transformation
In this session you'll learn how we all develop through predictable
stages, what the stages are, why they fuel much of the conﬂict in
the world, and much more...
The image for this session is the transformation of a caterpillar into a butterﬂy.
You can have a transformation just as profound, only your transformation is on the
inside... on an emotional, psychological, mental, and spiritual level. It's a
transformation like growing wings on the inside... and then being able to ﬂy. Even
better, you can keep transforming, many times in your life.
Over the past several decades, an area of psychology has been getting more
attention. It's called Developmental Psychology. It can also be called Emergent
Psychology. The idea is that as we mature, we go through levels of transformation.
Each level repurposes all previous levels.
Piaget showed that after humans are born, we go through several mental stages of
development.
The Crayon Experiment: What does the other person think is in the crayon box? At
around age 4, kids go from thinking everyone sees the world the way they do, to
recognizing that others have other perspectives. Some kids also learn how to lie
around this age.
Clare Graves tried to validate (or not) Abraham Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs. He
kept giving people the same questionnaires over the course of their lives. People
changed their answers over the course of their lives, but they did it in a predictable
sequence.
Development is about Emergents - taking what you had before, adding additional
elements, and having something completely new Emerge at a higher level.

A summary of Graves' model:
1. Level 1: Survival.
2. Level 2: Tribe. We survive better in groups.
3. Level 3: Self Gratiﬁcation. Think of street gangs.
4. Level 4: Structured Civilization. Everyone does better with rules & hierarchy.
5. Level 5: Personal Success. I want to bend the rules to get more for me.
6. Level 6: Community of Caring. Switch from business person or professional

to environmentalist.
7. Level 7: Self Actualization. The monumental leap. We become aware of

complexity and start thinking in terms of systems.
8. Level 8: Group Actualization. We're on a complex round planet, we're all in

this together, let's use systems thinking to beneﬁt everyone.
Much of the conﬂict in the world is actually these levels ﬁghting it out.
