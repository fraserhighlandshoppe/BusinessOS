Session 7:
Clear Business Communication
In session 7 you’ll learn how to use communication in business and why it’s so important. You’ll learn
a set of effective communication tools, the best ways to network, and an important exercise you can
do to start applying these tools in your life.
• Professionalism has certain connotations where most people can’t define it but say they’d know it
when they see it
• Professionalism is important because you need to interact with other people to be successful in
business
• Professionalism is being on time, doing what you say you’re going to do, following up, and
looking professional.
• What areas in your life are you not showing up as a complete and total professional? This deals
largely in your communication with yourself and how you hold yourself accountable in your own
actions.
• If you’re not operating in a context where other people see you as a professional, then you’re not
portraying an image of being trustworthy and people will not work with you.
• Success in business is often times looked at as the material success of money and profitability.
However, the people that are doing well in business are the people that don’t get caught up in
ego issues or approval seeking.
• In business communication, instead of talking about the “niceties”, you’ll want to show factual
and scientific measures of how things are working. Business is about efficiency, effectiveness, and
doing things consistently.
• A good mindset is get to the point, avoid the social conversation, get down to the real issues, and
talk clearly about them.
• If prospective customers don’t seem interested in what you are selling its because you’re selling
the wrong thing to the wrong person.
• A business case is something that you propose in business where you put down the logical and
profitable reasons for why you should do something. This should show an analysis that it works.
This shows that you’ve done the math. Get to the point and talk about the money, profit, and
growth.

• Phone and email are black holes of time, money, and energy. People are waking up in the middle
of the night to check email and messages because we have become very undisciplined in our
communication.
• Effective communication tools:
• Be short and to the point.
• Emails should be 2-3 lines with no ambiguity - just the facts.
• Don’t check email and voicemail constantly throughout the day. You need to work in focused
blocks of time that will prevent communication clutter.
• The social act of business is very important. When you’re doing business and partnering, you
need to build rapport with people and build new relationships continuously. This builds a
powerful network of opportunity.
• The best ways to network:
• Don’t confuse networking in business with making friends. Just learn how to add value.
• Often times the real value is introducing them to other people. Establish social networking
groups. The more successful and affluent people become, the more they rely on establishing
networking opportunities with others.
• To do this, you must really understand what they are trying to do. Then, try to connect them
with other people that can add value.
• When you introduce two people that have a commonality, tell them about it to begin building
automatic rapport.
• The process of being the connector is “triading” or fusing people together. When there are 3
people involved the relationship structure is stronger. Example: a chair with 2 legs will
certainly fall, but if you add a third leg you’ve then created a stable structure.
• The most important form of communication in business is sales and marketing communication.
• Sales is about finding out the exact need of that individual person and understanding the
problem, want, or outcome they want to achieve. Then you begin the sales process of matching
your sale with their need.
• The #1 mistake made in sales is just telling people about your product or service, and not ask the
client about their needs.
• The most successful sales person will ask a lot of questions and qualify to make sure that they can
meet the need of the customer.

• Feature, Advantage, and Benefit - Humans don’t buy features, they buy benefits.
• In order to be successful in business, you must take away the risk that the customer perceives that
they have.
• Call to Action - You need to tell the customer exactly what to do to get the item that you are selling.
• EXERCISE:
• Make a list of 3 people you should add to your professional network that are more successful
than you. Reach out and set up a time to meet with each of them to talk.
• Ask them what their challenges are right now and what their opportunities are right now.
What are they excited about? Ask them if there are any people that they are looking to meet.
See if you can connect them with other people.
• For extra credit create a business social event where you invite 10 people and get them in the
room with other people that they have something in common with.
• Talk to a prospective customer. What is a problem is that they are trying to solve, and what is
the benefit that they are trying to create?
• Introduce them to your product and its benefits. Then, walk them through a call to action.
