Session 7: Planning The Prospect’s
Experience
In Session 7, you’ll learn how to script out your business friendships. You’ll also get a step-by-step
formula for doing this and steps to take action once you’re done.

•

Typically, when 2 people meet they start figuring out where they have something in common.

•

When you have a substantial connection with someone you tend to open up to having a
relationship with them. As you’re connecting with new customers, try to connect on something
substantial.

•

The next step in building a friendship, is when one person discloses something personal about
themselves. By sharing a vulnerable part of who you are it will help others feel more comfortable
with you.

•

Next, make sure the other person is on the receiving end of more value. The relationship should be
tilted in the favor of your potential “business friend”. Formula: give, give, give, get.

•

Remember: You’re going to be creating an experience for another person. You have to project
yourself into their shoes. Here is where it’s important to think about being your customer avatar.

•

Next, script the entire relationship from start to finish.

•

‣

Start with a 30 day timeline.

‣

Script out the entire experience from day 1 to day 30.

‣

What’s their first contact with you going to be?

‣

When they come to your website, what are you going to give them for free in return for opting
in?

‣

What’s your website going to look like?

‣

What’s your follow up sequence going be?

‣

How are you going to create content that’s interesting to them and leads to marketing your
product or service?

‣

Once they buy, are you going oﬀer another product in line?

Then, go to work to create this experience. Start putting individual people through it and see how
they respond.
