Week #6: FEAR - The
Ultimate Emotional Block to
Creating Wealth
In this session we’re going to confront the most powerful negative emotion:
FEAR. We’re going to learn how the emotion of fear prevents us from
creating wealth – and what to do about it.
1.

If you spend most of your life living in fear, you will have a very diﬃcult time creating lasting
wealth. In order to create big success you’ve got to go for it and overcome fear.

2.

The “fear” of success can be one of the most damaging. This can happen when you start to see
some success in your life, but you start seeing the pain of change, and it feels like a burden. This is
counter-intuitive, but you must address it when it comes up and push through it.

3.

The amygdale in our brain triggers the fear process in our body.

4.

There are several types of financial fear:

• Fear of losing money.
• Fear that prevents us from creating wealth.
• Fear of injustice or not getting the fair end of the stick.
5.

Don’t keep credit cards around as “credit card insurance.” Get rid of your fear of needing insurance.

6.

If you feel fear about something, it is almost certainly not happening. If it were, you’d be in action.

7.

When we are in our “Higher Self” we see things as they are. When we are in our “Lower Self” we see
everything with pessimism and fear. We should not make important decisions, take important
actions, or communicate important things when we are in our “lower self.” Make big decisions
when you are in your “higher self.”

8.

Get out of fear of people stealing your ideas. You can always create more value and ideas.

9.

3 Fear De-Programming Steps:

1. Skip watching the general news.
2. No more negative gossip.
3. Eliminate worry about finances.
10. Don’t worry about finances; when you worry about finances, you almost never make wise
decisions. Instead get in a flow state and focus on creating value.

11. You should never make any important financial decisions, spend money, or invest when you are in
fear.

12. “Cognitive Re-appraisal” is when we take things that give us fear and consciously see them in a
diﬀerent way, and take away their power to control our lives.

zero. If the stock goes up, the amateur will sell cause he doesn’t want lose the profit. Pros do the

14. Become fearful of credit cards.
15. List the 10 THINGS you fear the most, and then use cognitive-reappraisal. Look at the worst things
