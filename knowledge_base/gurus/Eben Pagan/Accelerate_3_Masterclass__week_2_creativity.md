Why Creativity?
★ The faster the world changes, the more important
creativity is to succeeding in it
★ New opportunities and challenges require new solutions and the skill of creativity is the main tool that allows you
to develop those solutions
★ “Opportunities multiply as they are seized.” -Sun Tzu
★ Creativity allows you to manage increasing opportunity

What Is Creativity?
★ A key aspect of creativity is the uniquely human skill of
being able to conceptualize new combinations and
solutions, then translate our ideas into physical reality
★ The root of creativity is “create” - which points to the
idea that creativity is about ultimately doing things in
the real world - not just “coming up with ideas”
★ Creativity successfully applied to business can yield
exponential growth, profit and income

This Is About Adaptability
★ Our human reality is rapidly evolving - socially, culturally,
economically, technologically
★ Each step forward that one of these areas takes is also a
step further away from the type of environment that we’re
built to thrive in
★ To survive (and then to thrive), you must successfully
adapt to each new environment
★ The more environments you successfully adapt to, the
higher your confidence, esteem, success and fulfillment

New Connections Are The Genesis
★ Essentially every successful company I have built or
worked to help build had at least one creative insight
- a new connection, integration, combination of
elements - that gave them their powerful advantage
★ Google: Applying the academic reference model to a
search engine
★ Facebook: Only Harvard students could be members

The Pragmatics Of Creativity
★ Your success point will come when you have several
things working together at once - which you must first
discover and combine together
★ Most things you try won’t work, so you must generate at
least 5x-10x the number of insights that you will ultimately
need to succeed
★ Most insights will be worthless, but a few will be the
winning lottery tickets that power your ultimate success

Ultimate Use Of Creativity
★ Creativity, in its highest form, is about discovering and
implementing success patterns in our lives - and then
figuring out how to make sure we use them
★ Knowing what to do is valuable, but figuring out how to
actually make it happen is the greater use of creativity
★ The more success patterns you create, develop and
implement, the more you can create, develop and
implement - and the more valuable they all become

Creativity As Imagination
★ According to Gregory Berns, imagination is essentially
running the visual system in reverse
★ We can imagine passively - for example, by reading a
book - or actively, by intentionally visualizing how
new things might look
★ The “test” for active imagination: If you’ve never
imagined it before, then it’s happening

Creativity As Perspectiving
★ We develop “theory of mind” at around age 4
(children demonstrate this with charm)
★ But after that, we rarely use the ability to take the
perspective of another person - and almost never
practice it intentionally for extended periods
★ Taking the perspective of your customer, team
member, etc. leads to a gold mine of insights

Mindstorming To Create
★ Brainstorming is when a group of people
generate ideas together
★ “Mindstorming” is when you generate a list of
ideas yourself - with intention
★ When Mindstorming, write a specific question,
then try to come up with at least 20 answers

Exercise #1: Imagination
★ Choose a number from 0-9
★ Imagine that your number is something else for example, Picasso saw the number 7 as an
upside down nose
★ Repeat 5 times in a row, coming up with a
different thing that your number could be

Exercise #2: Perspective
★ Take the perspective of your customer, and imagine
that you are in their situation right before they realize
that they need your solution
★ Stay in their perspective for a full 60 seconds, seeing,
hearing, feeling what you would see
★ When you get the “click” of an emotion being
triggered, you’re doing it correctly

Exercise #3: Combination
★ Draw two overlapping circles - and inside of the first write
“emotional motivator” and inside the second write “images
that would trigger it”
★ List 5 powerful images you could use in your marketing

★ Start with the question “What product could I
launch that would meet the biggest emotional
need of my customer, and sell itself?”
★ Start with 5 quick answers now
