Nuts & Bolts:

Google Pay-Per-Click
Marketing
Key Points:
1. With Pay-Per-Click (PPC) marketing, you write an ad, then only pay when someone
clicks on your ad and comes to your website.
• Google's PPC system will get you the most traffic. But there are certain rules you
must learn... otherwise you get penalized
2. Set up your Google Adwords Account ("Adwords" is what Google calls their PPC
system).
• Set up your account, then click on “start my first campaign”
3. Important campaign settings to get right from the start:
• Networks & Devices: Networks - choose "let me choose" and only check the box
for "Google search.” Don't check the boxes for search partners or display
• Networks & Devices: Devices - choose "let me choose" and only check "desktop
and laptop computers”
• Bidding: Choose manual bidding
• Budget: This is the most important setting of all. Put the amount you're
comfortable spending each DAY. If you don't want to spend more than $20 or
$50 per day on ads when you first start out, enter that here
• Position Preference: your choice. Can start with automatically manage maximum
CPC
• Delivery Method: choose accelerated, so your better ads are given more traffic
4. Your campaign will have ad groups, keywords, and ads in it.
• Putting your keyword in any part of your ad will bold the keyword, and that will
help improve your Click Through Rate (CTR). Your CTR is the percentage of
people who see your ad... that actually click on it
5. Google rewards ads that get a lot of clicks, so the higher your CTR, the less you will
pay per click.
6. Always test two ads against one another. If one ad has many more clicks than the
other by the time they reach 30 clicks, keep your winner, and write another ad to
"test" against it.
7. Capitalizing the first letter of each word in your display URL tends to help your CTR a
little. So your display URL should look like this: YourWebsite.com, not
yourwebsite.com

8. The Google Keyword tool is a powerful keyword research tool. Use it to find
keywords in your market with a lot of traffic and not much competition.
• The keywords tool is in your Google Adwords account. You can also do a
Google search for "Google external keyword tool"
9. There are four keyword match "types": broad, phrase, exact, and negative.
• Broad: searches that contain your keywords in any order will trigger your ad
• Phrase: searches that contain your keywords in the exact order you entered them
in will trigger your ad. Searches may contain words in addition to your keywords
(this is true for broad as well)
• Exact: searches must match your keywords EXACTLY (searches will not contain
extra words)
• Negative: put a negative in front of your keyword and Google will not show your
ad on any searches that contain the negative keyword. For example, if you're
bidding on dating advice, but have the negative keyword “–free”, your ad
wouldn't show for the search “free dating advice”

Action Steps:
1. Create your Google Adwords Account.
2. Set your daily budget at less than $50.
3. Create your first campaign, your keywords, ad, and ad group. Watch your traffic for
a day and write different ads to improve your CTR and lower your cost.
