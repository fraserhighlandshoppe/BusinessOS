Nuts & Bolts:

Selling As an Affiliate
Key Points:
1. Affiliate Marketing is selling other people's products and service online, and making a
commission.
• When someone buys a product through your "affiliate link" you get paid
• Your "affiliate link" is unique to you... when someone clicks on that link, they get
redirected to the merchant's site...then when they make a purchase, it gets
tracked back to you... and you get paid.
2. Affiliate marketing is one way to test a market before you invest in creating your own
product
• This can save you time and effort
• You may need to test several different markets before you find a "winner"
3. Typical Affiliate Tactics:
• Review Site (you are an affiliate for each product)
• Build your list
• Hybrid - sell your product first, then up-sell or cross-sell them to an affiliate
product, either on your thank you page or through autoresponders
4. Find information products to sell on ClickBank:
• Pays out automatically - so no chance of human error (in other words, you'll get
your check)
5. To sell a product from ClickBank, search the marketplace to find a product, then get
the "hoplink"... enter yourClickBank id... then use the link created when you promote
that product.
6. When affiliates sell your stuff, they handle getting their own traffic...you only pay
them after they sell yourproducts...which is great.
• Using ClickBank for your affiliate platform has the advantage of paying your
affiliates on autopilot - you don't have to remember to pay them yourself
• Have a link on your site for potential affiliates, and take them to a page that
motivates them to sign up

Action Steps:
1. If you're interested in Affiliate Marketing, find a product to promote on ClickBank and
try promoting it.
2. If it's not a "winner", try a different product or market.
3. If you have a product of your own, start the process of putting it on ClickBank to get
affiliates to promote it for you for free.
