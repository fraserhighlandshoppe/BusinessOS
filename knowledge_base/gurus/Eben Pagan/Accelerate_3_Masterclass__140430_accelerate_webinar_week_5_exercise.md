```markdown
Accelerate Your Product Development
What is your customer’s biggest fear or frustration - and their
biggest want and aspiration?
Ask your customer what challenges them most, EMOTIONALLY - then make sure that the product
you design delivers not only the “phsyical” result, but also the emotional result they want - and use
that emotional motivator as the foundation to design your product.

What is the “Minimum Viable Product” that you could create and
get into the hands of customers to see if it works as a solution?
It’s not just the “minimum” product, but minimum “viable” product - meaning that it has to be good
enough to actually give customers results - but it doesn’t have to be perfect. Think “Apple 1” not
“Apple Macintosh.”

What is the new product category that you could create, that
would position you as the original, and as the leader?
Consider the evolution of more specific, “next evolutions” of products and services that already exist
- and then ask yourself what category is going to emerge anyway in the future - that you could
create right now, and be seen as the “original” and the “leader.”
```
