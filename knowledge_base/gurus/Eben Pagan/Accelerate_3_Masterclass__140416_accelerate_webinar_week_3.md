Accelerate Your
Productivity

The Roots Of Productivity
★ Productivity is about producing
★ Productivity isn’t about busywork, lots of activity,
or constantly “doing”
★ The point of productivity is effectiveness - or
producing the result that we wanted

Reality Model For Productivity
★ People who continually increase their productivity live in a
reality that is always evolving
★ Highly productive people see increasing productivity as
getting into alignment with natural laws, then using them
to your advantage
★ Reality is cyclical, and increasing productivity is about
mastering cycles of time, attention, energy and behavior

Self-Image Of High Producers
★ Highly productive people see themselves as
responsible for producing results first and foremost
★ High producers see themselves as people who say
“no” a lot more often than they say “yes”
★ High producers are focused on creating value for
others as the most direct path to earning money

Beliefs Of The Highly Productive
★ I only get a small amount of “willpower” - and the highest
return I’ll get investing it is creating new habits
★ The optimal amount of new habits to practice and install is
ONE over 30 days
★ The people I spend time with regularly is the highest
leverage “meta habit” I can develop
★ The more high-quality feedback I can get about how I’m
investing my time and the results I’m getting, the better

Mindsets For Increasing Productivity
★ Productivity is about getting more things done, and
getting more results - not about more busywork and more
activity
★ Habit patterns of thought, feeling & behavior manifest and
translate into the results I achieve in “real life”
★ The highest leverage when it comes to productivity is in the
areas of motivation, habits, energy and accountability

Values Of Productive Entrepreneurs
★ Getting the result they want over what other people
think of them
★ Producing value for others over having flexibility for
themselves
★ Knowing the truth about how they’re spending their
time and the results they’re getting over avoiding the
discomfort of self-examination

Business Productivity Isn’t
Accidental - It Must be Learned
★ Remember: The further we go from our native
environment, the more adaptation requires
concentrated effort and superior knowledge
★ Business productivity requires us to use our highest
abilities to learn and refine our potential to produce the
highest possible value in our business

The “Effective Entrepreneur”
★ Measure how your time is used
★ Intentional, focused blocks - longer is better
★ Uninterrupted - no distractions
★ Highest value activities & decisions
★ Focus on strength & contribution

Remember The “Triune Brain”?

Power, Affiliation, Achievement
★ We’re motivated by 3 key things: Power,
Affiliation, and Achievement
★ Affiliation is about love, approval, relationship
★ Power is about status, influence and control
★ Achievement is about getting a specific result in
the real world

3 Productivity “Styles”
★ For an “Affiliator,” productivity is about making the
emotional impact that they want, being liked, getting love,
being admired, not being rejected, creating feelings in
themselves and others that they want
★ For a “Power,” productivity is about getting more influence,
making a bigger impact, and controlling more
★ For an “Achiever,” productivity is about hitting a goal,
making a measurable change, getting a tangible result

Master Achievement To Win
★ If you want to win the Entrepreneur game, and win the
income and money game, it’s key to focus on your
achievement motivation and achievement values set
★ Our values are essentially invisible to us, so we have to
pay careful attention when we want to affect them
★ To master achievement, we must make it our highest
value for a period - most likely, years, until we’ve
realized the business success we want

The Elevation Of Achievement
★ If achievement is over-expressed, and elevated at the
expense of all other values, it results in an extreme
form of “the ends justify the means” - which is the
shadow side of Machiavelli’s pragmatism
★ We aren’t looking to “over-shoot” this process, but it’s
critical that we understand that to achieve the success
we want in business and with money, it must become
the highest priority for a period of time

What Do You Want To Achieve?
★ What is the most important thing that you want to
achieve with the economic success that you will create
with your growing, profitable business?
★ What is the measurable objective that you’ll need to
achieve inside your business to be able to afford to
comfortably realize that goal for yourself?
★ What changes in your life will you need to achieve in
order to realize that business objective?

The Importance Of Pragmatism
★ Your customers and your business don’t care if you’re
tired, worried, bored, anxious... or if you are passionate
about what you do or not...
★ From the perspective of your customers and your
business, what matters is that you do the things you
need to do every day to grow it
★ So how do you make sure you do the money-making
activities in your business every single day?

Productivity Cycles
★ We begin our lives, as soon as we’re born, going through an
attention and energy cycle - sleep... drowsy awake...
quiet-alert... active-awake... fussy... cry... sleep
★ As adults, we go through layered, natural cycles of
attention, energy and action through our day
★ To succeed in becoming optimally productive, we must tune
in to and optimize this natural set of cycles

Aligning Your Energy
★ We tend to have more energy, willpower and focus
available earlier in the day
★ Because we expend our energy and willpower slowly,
we don’t notice the change real-time through the day
★ The alignment of our highest quality attention and
our highest quality actions is key to high productivity

Context Creates Action
★ Similar to the way beliefs influence specific thoughts,
your environment and social context influence your
specific behaviors
★ The most powerful impact you can have on your
behaviors comes from the environment and context
that you create around yourself
★ “Context is decisive.” -Werner Erhard

Follow The Gold Upstream
★ From money to a business that produces money
★ From the business that produces it to the key actions
within your business that result in the production of
the most value to the most people
★ From golden eggs to golden geese
★ The further upstream you can think, the better

Build Your Productivity Dojo
★ Dojos and Zendos are consciously kept clean and free of
clutter in order to facilitate the practices they are used for
★ The elimination of distraction and interruption from the
practice space ensures proper mental focus
★ Another element is the intentional nature of the rituals
and practices performed inside
★ Productivity multiplies with intentionality and practice

Design Your Rituals
★ Focusing your energy on creating habits is another
key leverage point in developing high productivity
★ It takes approximately 30 days of practice to
“record” a habit into your physical, emotional and
mental behavior
★ The more specific and granular you are in your
ritual design, the higher your chances of success

Attend To Your Incentives
★ Incentives are those things that motivate us to action - in
order to get or avoid situations and outcomes
★ An incentive is created by matching a personal value with the
opportunity to achieve it
★ Charlie Munger: Look behind any “crazy” human behavior, and
you’ll find incentives (if you don’t see them, keep looking)
★ What are the “secret” incentives that drive you?

Shifting Focus To Achievement
★ What is most motivating to you in life? What do you naturally
seek to achieve? Are you more motivated by love and approval,
power and influence, or by achievement of specific goals?
★ To maximize the productivity in your business, develop your
achievement motivation
★ Create a “vision board” - and use goal visualization and
affirmation in order to motivate yourself to take specific actions
in order to create the value you’ll need to build your business

Designing Your Dojo
★ What is the ideal physical space for you to
practice your productivity rituals?
★ What needs to be removed from your current
space in order to maximize your productivity?
★ What needs to be added to your current work
space to maximize your productivity?

Designing Your Energy Cycle
★ What is the best way to center and prepare yourself
to do a focused 60-120 minutes of concentrated
value-producing work?
★ What is the highest value work you do in your
business, that pays off big in 1-2-hour chunks?
★ What is the best way to do a focused 30-minutes of
relaxation and rejuvenation during a work day?

Incentivize Yourself
★ What are the strongest incentives in your day-today life - the things that motivate you to either take
action or avoid situations - that you could use to
motivate yourself to action?
★ How can you layer multiple incentives to make sure
you do whatever it takes to get the results you want?
★ Use pictures, affirmations and visualization daily!
