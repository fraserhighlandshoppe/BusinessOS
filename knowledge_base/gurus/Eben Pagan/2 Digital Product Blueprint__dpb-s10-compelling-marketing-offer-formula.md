```markdown
The Compelling Marketing Offer
1. Headline: Benefit-driven, specific, clear. “How To...”

2. Set up challenge / expectation of learning how to get result: Talk directly
to the challenge, and talk directly to the fact that you’re going to be showing
them how to get what they want.

3. Story: Share your story of being in their situation, trying and failing, finding
a solution, creating a system.

4. Product & bullets: Introduce the product, share at least 20 solid bullets
that focus on solutions to problems, specific benefits and results.

5. Value-build and price: Frame price in a way that translates the value into a
minimum of 10x what they will be investing, and give a good reason why
you’re charging less than 1/10th of what it’s worth.

6. Risk reversal: Offer a 100% guarantee.

7. Act now: Give a specific direction to action, with exact steps.
```
