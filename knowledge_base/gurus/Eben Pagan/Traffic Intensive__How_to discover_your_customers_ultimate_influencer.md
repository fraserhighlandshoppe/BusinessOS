```markdown
How to Discover Your Customer’s “Ultimate
Influencer”

Key Points:
1.

In business you are not only competing with others that offer products like yours, you’re
competing with everything that wants your prospects attention and money.

2.

Some things capture attention more than others and the difference in “emotional value”
between two words or phrases is typically VAST. A $1 bill and a $100 bill are printed on
the same paper with the same ink. It’s the “message” on the bill that makes the
difference.

3.

You must learn to differentiate the emotional and “motivation” values of words and
phrases so you can dramatically increase your response and conversion in the marketing
world.

4.

Your must also learn how to identify the “Ultimate Influencer” of your customer, and
how to focus in on the part of their influence you can use to create powerful marketing
and advertising. Your customer has many different people around them who influence
their self-image, emotions, and decision-making process. One of these influencers exerts
MUCH stronger influence than the others, and you can focus in on the part of it to
create powerful marketing and advertising.
```
