Creating a Profitably
Successful Business on Etsy
“Where All Things Handmade Go to Be Sold;
Etsy”
with Janelle Elms
Key Points:
1. Etsy.com has hit the e-commerce world in an enormous way in the last couple of
years. Everything on Etsy is made (as in arts and crafts), but we’re not talking about
the crocheted apples of your grandmother’s time, we’re talking gorgeous arts and
crafts with prices of up to $30K!
2. Why should you be on Etsy?
• 110% increase in sales over the last year
• Members have increased by 137% (over 154,000 people joined last month!)
• Annual sales are over $100,000,000 (2009)
• College educated and tech savvy buyers
3. The best shop designs and packaging are almost as delightful as the items inside of
them. Design of the packaging is a BIG DEAL on Etsy.
4. After you get logged in for the first time, go to “Your Etsy”.
5. Click “Public Profile” then pick your “Avatar” (this is the mini 75x75 pixel logo or
picture that represents your product to others around the site).
6. You can pick your own “location” if you have places to buy your products in person.
7. People love to connect with you on Etsy – there is quite a bit of relationship
marketing, so your “Bio” should be well thought out.
8. “Favorite materials” are things you like to work with.
9. Complete your “Public Profile”.
10.Now set up your “Shop” by clicking on “Appearance”. Choose your Shop Title
(which is a subtitle that goes underneath your Banner).
11.Your Banner is 100 pixels high and 760 pixels wide.
12.Shop Announcement: this is where you put the latest news. Keep them to 2 or 3
sentences.
13.Alchemy: this is where someone asks you to create something custom. Buyers also
post there saying, “I need a bid for the following…”.
14.Message for Buyers: this is where you say “thanks” and where you can suggest to
them what to do next or where to go.

15.You also have the option to invite people to subscribe to your “Newsletter” on Etsy.
Important part of your marketing plan.
16.If you need help creating Banners, Etsy has “Select Your Banner” resources that will
create one for you.
17.Always offer refunds to your customers.
18.Pick which categories you want in your shop (up to 10).
19.Shipping: Etsy has “Shipping Profiles.” You can set up a shipping profile for each
different product. Etsy prices are rounded to whole numbers (i.e. $10 not $9.97). You
can add additional shipping information depending on other locations. Choose
payment methods. Use the “Vacation” notice when you are away from selling on the
site.
20.Use Google Analytics to measure how many buyers are coming to your site, how they
are moving around, what keywords they are typing in, where they are coming from,
etc.
21.How to List Products for Sale:
• Click on “Sell” and “Get Started”
• “Title”: call your products what your buyers call them
• “Description” is what your product is. Give plenty of information. Give features
(facts) and benefits: “So what?” Describe something as if you didn’t have a
photo
• You have 14 “Tags” to talk about your item. These tags are searchable by
potential buyers
• “Price” – don’t price too low. Quantity = “1”. Pick a “Category” and “Shipping
Profile”
• Pictures: should be GREAT pictures. Upload your images
• Click “Finish”
• Your item will show up in the “time machine,” in the category, and in any sort
other marketing opportunity you select (you can also pay to be featured in a
“showcase”). New items are featured at the top of searches, so older items get
pushed down. You can be on for 4 months total
• Feature items in your shop by clicking “Shop”
• You can use “TeraPeak” to find out information on what is selling on eBay
• You want to throttle your listings. Release a couple each day
• When the 30 days of an item is up and no one has bought it, then re-list it
22.If you can get started on Etsy NOW, you won’t have to spend so much time or effort
in the future competing for attention. Etsy is about relationships and product loyalty,
so get exposure NOW.
