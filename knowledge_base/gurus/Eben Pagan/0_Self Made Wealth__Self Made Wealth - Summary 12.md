```markdown
Week #12: Build Long-term
Wealth
In this session, you’re going to learn the final set of critical mindsets and
practices for building long-term WEALTH.
1.

Money follows action.

2.

Wealth = Freedom, and freedom comes from doing the things in this program.

3.

Wealth consciousness: there is so much money looking for a place to go, we just need to create
the value so that it can flow to us.

4.

Most people in debt got there a little at a time. Building wealth is exactly the same except the
opposite. YOU can create wealth. 1 in 50 people in the US are millionaires.

5.

Choose an area of wealth building that you understand, pursue it over years, and get even better
and better at it.

6.

The media shows exceptions, not how MOST people that are wealthy get there; it’s not about a
kid in a ghetto becoming a rapper or basketball star. If they would focus on learning an area of
business instead, and really focus on knowing everything about it, they’d have a MUCH greater
chance of success.

7.

Wealth is not taught, it’s learned. If you want to learn how to create wealth, than you have to
become enamored with it, intrigued with it, study it, and figure out how it works.

8.

The most important word in your wealth-creation vocabulary is “no.”

9.

Wealthy people would rather control an asset or resource, than own it. Wealthy people don’t
identify with any one asset.

10. Sometimes you’ll have to consciously do things that alienate others. They may make you the bad
guy.

11. Manage risk. Risk is something to be managed and balanced, but not avoided.
12. When you face an obstacle, learn a new skill.
13. In order to succeed in creating wealth, you may have to do it in secret. In communities of poverty,
someone that becomes wealthy will become the target of everyone around them. Your friends
and loved ones can become the enemy to creating wealth.

14. Every time you earn money, invest some of it in learning a new skill. Wealth-building skills
exponentially make each other more powerful.

15. The decision to follow healthy rules in your own life (i.e. not credit cards or pay yourself first) is a
superpower.

16. Master things in sequence. Combine them together. Master health, then relationships, wealth and
assets.

17. Investing is like farming. Farm your assets metaphor. Learn to eat FROM the harvest, not from the
seed. Plan to harvest later.
```
