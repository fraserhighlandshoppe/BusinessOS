# 6_Human_Motivation_Formulas.pdf

```markdown
Six Human Motivation Formulas That Get
People to Take Action

Key Points:
1.

There are three things that motivate humans:
1. Power is the ability to influence yourself and others in the direction you want
them to go.
2. Affiliation is about love, approval, acceptance and connection with others.
3. Achievement is about being able to get things done in the real world, to
achieve goals and finish projects.

2.

There are also two different directions a person can be motivated in:
1. Towards the things they want (desire).
2. Or away from things they don’t want (fear). You’ll do more to avoid losing
$100 than to earn $100. You work more to “not lose.”

3.

If you look at anything a human being does, you can see one of those motivators behind
it. These are very abstract ideas, so in this session you’ll explore how to overlay them
and make them more tangible for your target audience.

4.

Survival and reproduction are also base motivations, underlying most any behavior.

5.

In marketing you need to communicate with all three types of people, and in both
directions (toward and away from).
```
