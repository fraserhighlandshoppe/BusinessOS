A lot of marketing out there makes people with a conscience feel uncomfortable. You

As long as you struggle to sell, you’ll be working more than you have to. It will take time

The essence of successful sales is this:
• The buying decision is an emotional decision
• Sales is a relationship
• The most important aspect in sales is the sequence of emotions involved in building
the relationship
The RIGHT Sequence of Emotions of a Successful Sales Process:
• The first thing someone has to be is curious. How do you generate curiosity?
Conceal as much as possible (don’t tell everything). You want them to want to
know what’s there.
• They have to feel empowered to spend their money. And you have to be dealing
with the decision maker. “Are you curious about how the life you’ve dreamed of
could actually be real for you, and the people I’ve worked with proclaim themselves
to be free. Are you curious about that? Assuming that we are going to work
together, and assuming that you are the decision maker, is there anyone else
you’d need to check with before you make the decision? If they say, “check with
husband.” Reply, “Great. When can we schedule a call between your you, your
husband and I? Because your husband will have concerns that you might not think
of, and I want to make sure that your husband is as much of an advocate as I am for
your success in the program that we’re going to do together.”
• Experience possibility. You have to imagine how much better your future is going
to be because you can take advantage of the product or service. The possibility has
to feel real to you, and the customer has to trust that the product or service can
take them there. Figure out what problem they’ve got and what solution they want.
• Urgency. There has to be a reason why it has to be done. Ask, “Are you ready to do
something about that now?” (“Are you ready to solve that problem,” not “are you
ready to buy my product.”) “I know lots of people just like you that have solved that
problem.”
