YouTube Mastery
Top 10 YouTube Optimization Tips:
1. Name your videos the same thing as what people are searching for.
2. Use examples talking about current events or powerful figures that are more recognized
than you.
3. Start out with a URL in your description box. It needs to be fully formed (i.e. http://……).
4. Your description should be at least 150 words with target keywords in the first sentence.
5. Look at a few popular videos that are already ranking well for your keyword. Find the tag
words that all top videos have in common and use those.
6. Build backlinks specific to your videos using your keyword in the anchor text.
7. Select best video thumbnail. YouTube gives you 3 to choose from. Find this under My
Videos >Edit Video. Located in lower left side of screen. Choose the thumbnail that you
think will get the most clicks.
