Putting Stats & Analytics On Your Site

Overview

Overview
• Obviously, Google Analytics is complex and

sophisticated software
• We’re not going to “master” analytics, but rather

get a working understanding of what’s available
• Then focus in on a few key things to implement

and monitor

Caution:
• Analytics can become a vortex of your attention
• Wait until you have enough analytics to warrant your attention —

and that’s typically a long way down the road
• Monitor only as frequently as is warranted, and do mostly high-

level scans … drill down when you see a significant trend or data
point
• Or: Use goals to monitor your current project
• BUT: add GA now - start accumulating data

Caveat: Analytics are Inaccurate
• There are many ways and reasons that the data in

Google Analytics may be wrong.
• Don’t get hung up on it: just look at the data as trends

and “orders of magnitude”, not absolute numbers
• Relatively speaking, it’s consistent
• (Need to dig deeper? Search for “google analytics

inaccurate”)

What Key Events?

• What “remarkable” events are happening lately?

What’s Happening Now?

•What’s happening right now on the website?
•Are people responding to my event CTA?
•Are people responding to my email campaign?
•Are people flowing through my funnels or getting snagged?
•I just ran an ad campaign — is it working?
•I just released a blog post - are people reading it?
•How many people are making it to the sale page and buying?

Visitor Information

•Who’s visiting my site?
•How old? What Gender?
•Where are they from, and in what numbers?
•What are they interested in?
• How many visitors are on mobile?
• What devices and operating systems are they using most?
• What pages do they land on?
• What pages do they navigate to most?
• Are they reaching my optin and sales funnels?

How Did They Get Here?

• Where’s my traffic coming from?
• How well is my paid advertising working?
• Which of my paid ads is most effective?
• What keywords are bringing organic traffic?
• On what social networks do people most engage
with my content, and which converts best?
• How effective are my campaigns and which is
performing best?

Where Do They Go?

• What is my most popular content?
• How long do people stay on my site/pages?
• What are the highest/lowest bounce rate pages?
• How do visitors flow through my website?
• How fast are my website pages?
• What are people searching for on my site?
• What events happen most (clicks, views, etc?)
• How are my A/B tests (experiments) working?
• My page seems to have problems — what’s the cause?

Are My Site Goals Met?

• Are my site goals being met?
•Which goals are performing best?
•Which pages are converting best?
•What funnels are working best?
•Where are my funnels leaking?
•Are people getting to my landing pages
and converting at a reasonable rate?
•What products are selling best (volume or quantity)?
