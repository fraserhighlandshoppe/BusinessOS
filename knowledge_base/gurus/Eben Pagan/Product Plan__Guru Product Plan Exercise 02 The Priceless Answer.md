The Priceless Answer
Every	
  person	
  has	
  a	
  “Secret	
  Question”	
  -­‐	
  driven	
  by	
  a	
  challenge	
  in	
  their	
  life,	
  a	
  need	
  or	
  desire,	
  a	
  fear	
  or	
  
anxiety	
  -­‐	
  that	
  they	
  can’t	
  find	
  a	
  satisfactory	
  answer	
  to.	
  If	
  you	
  can	
  identify	
  this	
  Secret	
  Question,	
  clarify	
  
and	
  refine	
  it

  create	
  a	
  unique,

  system	
  that	
  
anyone	
  can	
  use	
  to	
  solve	
  it,	
  you	
  have	
  created	
  a	
  “Priceless	
  Answer.”

What are the ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS of the Secret Question that your prospects
are searching for an answer to? The problem or goal they’re facing, the emotion
that’s motivating them, the tangible outcome they desire...

How can you “boil down” and refine the question to put a sharp point on it? Ask
the question at least 5 different ways, each time trying to “nail” the phrasing...

What’s the Priceless Answer to this question? Start with “Idealized Design” and
phrase an answer that describes a system, technique or method that offers them
a new, TANGIBLE solution... quickly, easily, guaranteed, without hassle...
