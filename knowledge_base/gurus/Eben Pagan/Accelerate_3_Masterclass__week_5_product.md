The Transition To The Customer
★ We’ve learned new skills for ourselves as
Entrepreneurs in the areas of creativity, productivity
and leadership
★ Now we’ll transition to learn key skills for developing
our customers: Product development, marketing
and customer experience design

Your Customer Wants To Feel “Seen”
★ In the movie Avatar, they had a phrase that they said
throughout the movie - it was:
★ “Oel Ngati Kameie”
★ As an Entrepreneur, it’s your responsibility to
continually figure out how to say “I See You” through
your product, marketing and experience
★ Apple and first impressions

When You Hit The Bulls Eye...
★ By seeing your customer’s need clearly, then create a
powerful solution in your product, you create
something that can scale and make increasing profit
★ If you don’t continually refine your understanding of
your customer’s need, and also the way your product
meets it, you’ll fight an uphill battle all the way

The Power Of Feeling Understood
★ One of the rarest and most valuable experiences as a
human is feeling understood
★ Steven Covey: “Seek first to understand.”
★ Haim Ginott: When a child feels understood, they feel
less lonely - and also more love toward the person
who understands them

The Search For An Irresistible Egg
★ Ethologists have found that animals often respond
powerfully to one aspect of a stimulus
★ For example, a species of goose will ignore her own
eggs if a gigantic model of a goose egg is put in front
of her - and sit on the “irresistible” egg instead
★ As an Entrepreneur, you’re continually on a search
for your own “Irresistible Egg”

Look For Unmet Needs
★ Dissatisfaction seems to be “hard wired” into the human
condition
★ No matter how much wealth, fame and success we have,
we all still have big problems and frustrations in our lives
★ New solutions create new problems, which create
opportunities to identify and solve them with your product
★ What are the unmet needs your customer has?

Three Questions To Ask Every
Customer You Speak With
★ What is your biggest fear or frustration?
★ What have you tried, and how did it work?
★ What is the specific result or outcome that you
want to achieve?

The 20% Inside The 20%
★ Pareto figured out that 80% of the results in a
system come from 20% of the efforts, work, etc.
★ We tend to think in terms of “one level” of finding
the high-leverage 20% in any system
★ As you develop your product, continue drilling down
to find the 20% INSIDE the 20%

This Even Works With Ketchup
★ Heinz “won” the ketchup wars with a powerful insight - by
looking for the 20% inside the 20%
★ They realized that people wanted to buy and eat the besttasting ketchup, so they went to work to make it
★ But they went further, and identified the aspect of ketchup
that was “irresistible” - it turned out to be thickness

Examples From My Career

★ Growing A Business > Information Product
Business > Time Management & Productivity

The Power Tool: Perspectiving
★ We develop a powerful skill around the age of 4 years old it’s the skill of being able to imagine what it’s like to be
another person - and see things through their eyes
★ To maximize your success with your product and marketing,
practice this skill daily
★ Invest 5 minutes per day imagining what it’s like to be a
customer (and supplement by talking to real live customers)

Exercise: Drilling Down: Desire
★ What is your customer’s biggest want or desire?
★ What key 20% of that want is the most motivating to
them?
★ What 20% of that 20% is most motivating to them?
★ How could you develop your product to specifically help
your customer get this specific desire?

Exercise: Drilling Down: Fear
★ What is your customer’s biggest fear?
★ What key 20% of that fear is the most motivating to
them?
★ What 20% of that 20% is most motivating to them?
★ How could you develop your product to specifically help your
customer avoid this “20% of 20%” fear that they have?

Exercise: Talk To Customers
★ Set up live conversations with 3-5 customers per day for
the next 10 working days
★ Schedule 15 minutes for each of these conversations, so
you get them done in an hour or so
★ Ask your customers what their biggest fears and desires are,
and drill down at least 2 levels into each
★ Take notes so you can see the patterns emerge over time

Exercise: Perspectiving
★ Who are the 3 most important people in your life right
now?
★ Invest 60 seconds imagining what it’s like to be each of
them, and imagining how they see and experience you
★ Schedule 5 minutes at the beginning of each work day to
perspective - and imagine what it’s like to be your customer
(let’s do one session right now)
