Day 22:
Building Your MasterMind
In this session you'll learn the 5 step Friendship Formula for
making business friendships quickly, why your social connections
are your biggest business asset, how to start your ﬁrst
MasterMind group, and much more...
Napoleon Hill - "Analyze the record of any person who has accumulated great
fortune, and you will ﬁnd they have either consciously or unconsciously employed
the Mastermind principle."
Humans become who we surround ourselves with.
Since we're going to become like the people we're around anyways, it makes sense
to consciously surround ourselves with people who we aspire to be like.
Connecting with high inﬂuence people may be the most important job for you too
if you want to become successful, and it's a job you don't get paid for.
1. Invest several hours a week doing this. It's worth it.

One thing that separates professionals from amateurs is that professionals focus on
building assets and resources.
Your most important asset in the business world is your connection to other
people.
In order to build a community of inﬂuential people, we need to talk about
friendship... because ultimately these are social connections. If people don't like
you, then your network building is not going to go very well.
The Friendship Formula will allow you to build friendships very rapidly.
Step 1: Connect on something substantial you have in common.
1. When car salesmen try to connect with you using common sports or

hobbies, it feels weird because it's not a substantial or speciﬁc topic.
2. If you want to connect with someone, ﬁnd out what that person is really

personally interested in. Maybe it's chess, or an odd interest you share, etc.
3. The more speciﬁc and the more substantial, the better.

Step 2: Disclose something to deepen the relationship.
1. Developing friendships is about opening the channel of intimacy. It's a game

of chicken - who is going to go ﬁrst. There's pressure to not reveal.
2. Fears, frustrations, embarrassing mistakes we made, embarrassing but funny

stories, etc.
3. Once you disclose, they'll disclose, and then you get into a much more

meaningful friendship.
Step 3: Give, give, give, get.
1. Most people try to get get get. Don't be one of these people.
2. Once you've established a real nice bank account of giving, THEN you get.
3. If there's an expert you want to connect with, send them articles, valuable

news, etc.
Step 4: Balance the polarity between being an individual and being part of
the group.
1. Most people either totally stay an individual, or totally become a member of

the group and get absorbed by it.
2. It's important to balance the tension between staying an individual with

your own ideas and opinions, with adopting the views of the group or new
friend.
3. Don't stay a complete individual, but don't be a complete conformist either.

Strike the healthy balance between.
Step 5: Create a unique shared space within a larger group.
1. Have inside jokes. Make fun of each other and the group.

One of the most powerful activities to build a MasterMind or community is to
continually connect others with each other.
1. If you meet someone you want to bring into your community, you can either

do all the work yourself, or you can introduce them to 2 or 3 other people in
your group... and now you've "outsourced" a lot of the work of keeping them
in your community.
2. This also tightens your relationships with the other people you introduce

them to who are already in your community. You're adding value.
3. Get over your fear that they'll go do work without you. In reality, the more

they succeed without you, the more they'll remember that you were the
initial catalyst.

4. Most people don't have time to connect with others, so if you can do it for

them, it becomes very valuable to them.
5. When you introduce people, connect them to each other with something

speciﬁc, substantial, and unique. "Joan, this is Fred. Something interesting
about both of you is you both like chess."
Facilitate a group meeting. This is where the ultra-leverage comes in. It can be a
formal MasterMind meeting, 6-10 people, in person or on the phone.
An easy way to get this started is to invite 6 people, tell them "I'm going to do 1
hour call, I'm getting together a group of interesting people, and we're going to be
discussing x."
The highest level is when it goes beyond being formal, and it becomes a social
event where you are introducing people to each other, everyone's a high
performer, but it's in a more relaxed and fun atmosphere. Ultimately we are social
creatures. If you can be the one to get the social group together, that's when this
really takes oﬀ.
Go after the most inﬂuential people ﬁrst. If you can get one of them to come, tell
others they're coming, and they'll be knocking down your door.
