Nuts & Bolts:

Write Your eBook Fast
Key Points:
1. An eBook is a great product to start off with.
• It's an inexpensive first product for your customers to buy... if they aren't willing
to buy your eBook, you're probably in the wrong market
• With an eBook, you don't need to find, hire and pay a fulfillment company as
you would if you were shipping a physical product
2. Start with a great topic.
3. Create the outline for your eBook. You can split it into customers’ top questions... or
the steps the reader will need to follow to get the result they want.
4. Write a compelling, benefit-driven headline.
5. Write an introduction that shows you understand the reader... that you've been in
their shoes.
6. Use stories and examples to make your points.
7. The value of your eBook isn't determined just by its length, but here are some things to
add to your eBook that make it longer and ALSO add more value:
• About The Author section
• Table of Contents
• Headings & Subheads
• Start new chapters on new pages
• Use bullets and lists
• End chapters with summaries or action plans
• Add images from websites like istockphoto.com or dreamstime.com
8. Don't be concerned that your eBook isn't long enough. Just makes sure the content
that's in it is top-notch. A 50- page high quality eBook delivers more value than a 150page low quality one.
9. If you're a better speaker than writer, use the "Interview Technique" - write down a list
of questions that you want your book to answer... then get with a friend and a voice
recorder, have your friend ask you the questions... and "talk out" your book. Get it
transcribed, clean it up... and you're done.
10.Don't try to make your eBook perfect the first time. You can always improve it later if
customers are asking for refunds.
11.Converting your Word document into a PDF helps prevent people from making
changes to it, then selling it as their own. To convert your Word document into a PDF:
