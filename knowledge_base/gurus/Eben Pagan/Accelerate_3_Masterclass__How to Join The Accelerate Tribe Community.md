How to Join The "Accelerate Tribe" Community
Go to your G+ profile

You can go directly to your G+ profile at any time in one of the following ways:
1) Click your +profilename link from any google app
2) Click the icon next to your +profilename that looks like a 3x3 matrix of squares to display the
dropdown menu of google apps, and click the G+ icon labelled with your +profilename.
Or, simply enter http://plus.google.com in your browser when you are logged in to your google
account.

How to Join The "Accelerate Tribe" Community - 1

Navigate to your communities page

Step 1: Hover over the "Home" menu to display the navigation menu.
Step 2: Click on the "Communities" link

Search for the "Accelerate Tribe" community

How to Join The "Accelerate Tribe" Community - 2

Select our "Accelerate Tribe" Private Community

Please note that the icon / logo for the community in the diagram is the default google image and
will be updated with an image that represents the "Accelerate" brand that will be more familiar to
you.

Ask to join the community

Click the "Ask To Join" button on the top right of the screen to request access to the community.
How to Join The "Accelerate Tribe" Community - 3

Wait for notification from the moderator that you have been added to the community.

To cancel "join" request

To cancel your request to join the community, simply click the "Cancel Join Request" button at the
top right of the screen. For example, if you want to cancel membership with the email address you
chose, and request to join using another email address.
