YOUR MARKET: Session 4
Power Words That Boost Sales I
In this session, you'll learn how to craft headlines that grab attention, how to give the customer the
experience of you "getting" them, and how to boost your sales by using metaphors
in your sales copy.

• One Double Your Dating headline I wrote was, "How To Lose A Woman Fast." The way it's worded
is sounds like you're going to learn how to leave a woman, which is weird, but it's actually about
how if you don't become attractive you're going to lose her before you even get her.

• Another headline sounded like the article was going to be about how to get a woman fast, but it
was really about becoming a great person to attract a quality woman. I'll take the prospects
fantasy - where they're at right now - and use it as the hook or grabber for the email. Then later on
- in the article itself - I'll turn it around and show them how what they think they want will get
them the exact opposite of the result they really want. I may even say "Hey, I used to think that
too, and I did it many times, and I would always get a bad result. Then I learned this great tip and
it turned around everything for me... here it is..." That's one winning formula.

• In NLP, they have a concept from Milton Ericson that I like to talk about when it comes to
marketing. It's called Utilization. Whatever is already going on in your prospect's mind, that's what
you're going to use or "utilize" to build your case. You grab it and channel it in the direction you
want to take them. When you "get" the mindset of Utilization, you lose the victim mindset of "My
prospects are so screwed up, how can I ever help them..." Instead you think, "My prospects are
who they are. I accept them where they're at. I'll learn where they are and really get into it, then I'll
use their own language and fantasies and channel their energy in the direction that will help
them the most."

• You hear me use the words "you get me" a lot. I think that sums up what we're talking about here
- to create that emotional response in our prospect of "He gets me." It's about describing their
situation in such a compelling way that you have 100% of their attention... and they feel you "get"
them.

• Diﬀerent words have diﬀerent amounts of value and impact for your prospect, so it's important to
identify your prospects' high value words. I call these "hot-button" words and phrases. Take a
headline, change a few words for other words that essentially mean the same thing, and you can
get a HUGE diﬀerence in the amount of people buying your stuﬀ. No two words ever really mean
exactly the same thing or have the same impact - so pick your words carefully. Use words that
your prospects are already using in their minds.

• Another important way to maximize the impact of words is through Metaphor. The great teachers
throughout history communicated in Metaphors. Somehow they understood that humans learn
better this way. They said things in a way that immediately relates them to some experiential
thing that's happening in the life of the prospect.

• The Buddha said, "An evil deed follows an evil thought, just like the cart follows the ox that draws
it." If he just said "an evil deed follows an evil thought," the message wouldn't have nearly as much
impact as when it has the metaphoric language of the ox and cart. It's visual, and it takes the
communication to a whole new level. As you're trying to figure out what's relevant to your
prospect, look for metaphors to which they can relate. It'll help tremendously.
