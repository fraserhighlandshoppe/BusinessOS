Day 25:
Deepening Love & Intimacy
My partner Annie, who is an incredible relationship coach, leads
this session.
You'll learn many diﬀerent deﬁnitions of love from both poetic
and scientiﬁc points of view, how it can unlock your potential in
life, why your view of love limits (or expands) how you experience
it, and much more...
Annie Lalla is my partner and a relationship coach. I believe she's the pinnacle of
understanding relationships, intimacy, and love.
Love is dancing with your partner in every aspect of your life.
Whatever you think love is, that's all it can ever be for you. So work on expanding
your view of what love is.
Annie's deﬁnition of love: The experience of feeling acutely alive, triumphantly
understood and appreciated.
Intimacy:
1. Into - Me - See.
2. A close personal association characterized by trust, openness, and honesty.
3. The state of minimal diﬀerence between you and another.

Love is an Emergent.
It's an emotion, like fear. There may be just these two emotions, with all other
emotions a derivative of them. Both are part of a balanced life.
Love matters because it's the most powerful creative force on the planet. It's a
biological urge to mate, unite at a genetic level, to join your DNA with another and
create life.
Creating life from a sperm and an egg is the most miraculous magic trick on the
planet.
Love creates life. Not just babies, but new "life" to your life. It also reveals our
insecurities and shadows. We see the parts of ourselves we've been running from
our whole life.

Without love, some of your potential will remain unrealized. Love is the space
where you get to manufacture new aspects of yourself.
Romantic love is unique to humans and characterizes our species. Imagine being
human and never experiencing love.
Success in love is the pinnacle of the human game. It's the work for which all other
work is just preparation. It's the most meaningful and fulﬁlling enterprise.
Many of us fear the naked vulnerability of being emotionally exposed to another.
Cynics are failed idealists. They once had fantastic dreams, but became
disappointed, and turned oﬀ that part of themselves that dreams.
Watch for cynicism in your own life - cutting down high hopes, pointing out what's
wrong with the vision, rarely crying, etc.
Cynics don't believe in love, at least the kind the poets speak of - and that's why
they never can experience it.
Until you think it's possible, it's not.
Not only must you believe in it, you must never give up - through heartbreak,
through pain, through doubt. So never settle. If you have to ask yourself if you're
settling, then you most likely are. If your heart, head, and body all know you're not
settling, then you're not.
To be in love is to be overwhelmed.
Faith in the relationship is what connects you and your partner. It has no reasons,
no proof. Just a blind resolve and belief in something you ﬁnd beautiful.
Love is the most rewarding and complex game to play, and the trust expression of
your humanity.
