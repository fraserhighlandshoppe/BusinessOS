Week #8: Inner Wealth Creation
(& How Inner Wealth Creates
Outer Wealth)
In this session, you’re going to learn about how to create a very powerful
“Inner Wealth” – that will serve as the long-term foundation for the creation
of wealth in the “outside world.”
1.

Why focus on inner reality and inner game? Because it reflects in the outer reality. If we want
changes externally, we have to go to work on the inside.

2.

Your physical reality is just a projection of your inner ideas, thoughts and emotions.

3.

Integrity is the alignment of your thoughts, words and actions. Integrity over the long-term is the
foundation for a strong reputation. The more you build these, the better you’ll do long term.
Integrity is an asset.

4.

Financial Integrity is the wise use of money, value and assets over a long period of time. The
consistent creation of value over a long period of time. Strategy (thought), words (how you speak
about strategy) and actions (how you stick to strategy long term).

5.

Which single skill will be one of the most important wealth creation skills and abilities over the
next century? Receiving. When you aren’t receiving and enjoying the experience of it, you are
actually taking something away from the person trying to give it to you.

6.

Wealth creation is the cycle of contribution and receiving. Contribution leads to others wanting to
ultimately give back to your life, but if you have the “get” agenda it won’t work.

7.

When you don’t receive fully, you are robbing the person trying to give you something. Show you
get the value that they are sharing with you. Say, “thank you.”

8.

On the other hand, when you give value, stop to receive thanks and take the time to give a
sincere “welcome.”

9.

Napoleon Hill (author of Think and Grow Rich) says a “burning desire for riches” is critical to
becoming rich. Focus on what it is you want to create and imagine it as if it was true.

10. Gratitude and appreciation are “magic” emotions that have the power to not only heal negative
emotions, but to spring all kinds of positive emotions. Feeling them is also the fastest way to get
into a “flow state.”

11. Six emotions that will help propel you to success:
1. Desire – wealth creation is a long-term game, and desire keeps you going.
2. Responsibility – take responsibility for the circumstances in your life. You feel empowered
to get results.

3. Commitment – includes determination, persistence and giving your all. 100%. You will
figure something out no matter what.

6. Faith – trust the part of you that knows what your purpose is.
