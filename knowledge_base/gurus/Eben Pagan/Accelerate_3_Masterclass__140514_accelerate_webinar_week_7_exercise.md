```markdown
Marketing & Hot Button Exercise
What are your customer’s fantasy and nightmare scenarios, as
specifically and tangibly described as possible?
Remember to use words that describe real things and experiences, and avoid generalities and
abstractions. Describe situations and actions, not judgments and opinions.

What are the words that are most likely to trigger strong
emotions in your prospective customer?
After writing a list of the most powerful words and phrases, rank them on a scale of 1-100 to
choose the words that have the highest emotion value.

Use the situations, fantasies, nightmares, words and phrases
you’ve identified in your marketing and follow ups.
Once you’ve found the few key ideas, words, and phrases that trigger the strongest
emotions in your prospects, make sure to use them over and over in your marketing and
follow up sequences.
```
