Key Points:
1. Social Marketing allows you to sell more, with instant search engine rankings and

3. The Ladder of Intimacy: the process of relationship building with someone before you
sell to them. >> Social Media solves this by creating trust.
4. Building Your Tribe
• Foundation – your followers
• They refer you to their followers
• Those followers join your tribe
• You lead them
5. The 6 Most Important Social Media Tools
• Twitter.com
• Facebook.com
• LinkedIn.com
• Your Blog
• Social properties specific to your niche
• Content sharing sites
6. Unless you are a celebrity, you need to follow at least 80% of the people following
you.
7. Twitter: post great stuff
• Figure out what your tribe wants to know and POST THAT. Give them value and
information
• Follow top people in your industry
8. Facebook: the process of building out a profile and then gather friends who you share
information with

11.Social Media Mindset includes an effort to give, then receive, build relationships,

12.Rule: 200 value-adds to every self serving action, and when you do pitch, offer
something FREE.

Action Steps:
1. Set up a Facebook Business Page or Fan Page.
2. Set up a complete Personal LinkedIn Profile.
3. Set up a Twitter Account and complete profile.

know: business contacts, friends and family.
