Session 7: How To Present Your
Price And Value Proposition
In Session 7, you’re going to learn how to frame the price of your product in a way that your prospect can
understand in terms of money. You’ll also be doing an exercise to take these new tools and put them into
your copy.

• Questions your prospect is unconsciously asking at this point:
‣

How can I know this is worth a lot more than I’m paying for it?

‣

Can you prove the result you are promising?

• Frame the price of your product as being much more valuable than the price you’re charging.
• The first step in framing your price is to ask yourself, “What is my prospect’s unique currency?” In other
words, what is the thing they’re trying to accomplish and how can you consider it a “currency”?

• Make sure your offer is at least 3 times, and ideally 10 times or more as valuable than what you are
asking them to invest.

• If possible, answer the question “Can you prove it?” Do this by sharing a personal story, a story of how
others have done it, or some scientific research backing up your claim.
