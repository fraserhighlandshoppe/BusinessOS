```markdown
Niche Survey Blueprint
Your testing survey or interview can either be open-ended or it can be “multiple choice.” Either
is fine, but remember: If you’re going to give options, make sure that they’re as objective as
possible. Don’t “lead the witness,” so to speak. You’re looking for real insights, not what you
want to hear. Ask these questions to get maximum “testing insight”:

Question 1

What’s your biggest frustration with [CHALLENGE]?

Question 2

What have you tried so far that hasn’t worked for you?

Question 3

What’s your biggest fear when it comes to [CHALLENGE]?

Question 4

What worries you - what are you afraid will happen if you don’t do something immediately?

Question 5

What would you be willing to do to solve/get [CHALLENGE]?

Question 6

If you could have one question answered about [CHALLENGE], what would it be?
```
