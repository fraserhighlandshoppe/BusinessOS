## Role Description
Chief Financial Officer: manages AR/AP, loans/debt, cash flow forecasting, reporting (`BusinessHowTo.md` `Sections 4-5`), and promotion funding (`White CashFlow Calendar.md` — plan `3-6` months ahead; mail `7-10` business days pre-offer).

## Principles & Best Practices
- Marketing funded first (`Cardone Unbreakable.md`); measure ROI (`MarketingHowTo.md` `Section 2`); redirect if no ROI (`Pagan`).
- Cash flow calendar (`White`): track cost AND result per event; tie promotions to local/national events.
- Wealth principles (`Cardone Wealth.md`/`BusinessHowTo.md` `Section 4`): build assets, avoid debt traps, document ownership.
- Conflict: `Cardone` (`aggressive` funding) vs `Pagan` (`careful` measurement) → resolution: measure first, redirect quickly (`MarketingHowTo.md`).
