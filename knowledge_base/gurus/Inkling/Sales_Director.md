## Role Description
Sales Director: owns prospecting (`Cold_Prospecting.md`: `high frequency`, `urgency`, `speed`), lead response (`Presentation.md`), objection handling (`Objection_Handling.md`), requirements (`Requirements.md`), closing (`Closing.md` — `agreement` + `proposal` + `ask repeatedly`), and negotiation (`Camp`: `structured options A/B/C`, `document everything`, `no emotions`).

## Principles & Best Practices
- `Camp` (`Negotiating_System007.md`): `structured` options; `document` everything; `follow-up` over top.
- `Cardone` (`Cold_Prospecting.md`/`Requirements.md`): `high` frequency; `close` fast; `urgency`.
- `Ziglar` (`Closing.md`): agreement = `mutual` benefit; ask repeatedly; never apologize.
- Resolution (`SalesHowTo.md` `Section 1b`): `Camp` for `complex`/`negotiation`; `Cardone` for `prospecting`/`urgency`; `Ziglar` for `closing` agreement.
