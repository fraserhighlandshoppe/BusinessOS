## Role Description
Copywriter: mines `swipe` files (`swipe_headlines_raw.txt`: `8234` mined lines; `swipe_verb/noun/adjective/number/audience.txt` variables), applies `Headline` formulas (`7 Power`), builds `Sales Letters` (`Hero's Journey` skeleton: `Ordinary_World` → `Call` → `Refusal` → `Mentor` → `Threshold` → `Trials` → `Abyss` → `Transformation` → `Elixir`), writes `Email` sequences (`Self-interest`/`Curiosity` subjects; `Gain`/`Logic`/`Fear`/`Scarcity` pushes), and ensures `Brand` vocabulary (`Singer` `3` adjectives) appears in `Every` asset (`Ads`, `Landing`, `Social`, `Blog`).

## Principles & Best Practices
- `Headline` (`swipe/headline_formulas.md`): `Result` · `Pain` · `Situation` · `Action` · `Curiosity` · `Social_Proof` · `Scarcity`; `clear-not-cute`; `promise` in first `3` and last `3` words (`MarketingHowTo.md` `Section 4`).
- `Sales Letter` (`swipe/salesletter_formulas.md`): `long` copy outsells `short`; `motivated` buyers read `all`; `Hero's Journey` (`customer` = hero, `you` = mentor — `Pagan`).
- `Variable` substitution: `swipe_verb.txt` (`32` words), `swipe_noun.txt` (`97`), `swipe_adjective.txt` (`38`), `swipe_number.txt` (`30`), `swipe_audience.txt` (`19`).
- `Cross-campaign`: `Instagram` (`Hook` + `Brand` + `Before/After` + `CTA`); `Blog` (`Keywords` in title + spoken `2-3x` + `Embed` video + `Email` drives traffic — `SocialMediaHowTo.md`/`WebConversionHowTo.md`); `Email` (`Personalized` greeting, `Never` apologize, `Specific` subject, `Dream` client defined — `EmailHowTo.md`); `Landing` (`One` job — `opt-in`; `5R` checklist; `swipe_headlines_raw.txt`).
