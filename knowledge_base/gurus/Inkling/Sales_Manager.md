## Role Description
Sales Manager: manages team prospecting (`Cold_Prospecting.md`), lead response (`Presentation.md`), objection handling (`Objection_Handling.md`), requirements tracking (`Requirements.md`), closing (`Closing.md`), and negotiation (`Negotiating.md`). Monitors `ROI` and redirects quickly (`Cardone` + `Pagan` agreement: measure first, redirect once `ROI` confirmed).

## Principles & Best Practices
- `Camp`: `structured` options (`A/B/C`); `document` everything; `no` emotions; `follow-up` over top.
- `Cardone`: `high` frequency; `speed`; `urgency`; `close` fast; `under-promoting` = `sin` (`Mistakes.md`).
- `Ziglar`: agreement = `mutual` benefit; `ask` repeatedly; never apologize; `proposal` + `closing` agreement.
- Resolution (`SalesHowTo.md` `Section 1b`): use `Camp` for `complex` deals; `Cardone` for `prospecting`/`urgency`; `Ziglar` for `closing` agreement.
