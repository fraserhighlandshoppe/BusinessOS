## Role Description
Chief Executive Officer: defines vision, builds leadership team, ensures capital allocation aligns with strategy, monitors organizational health, and owns exit planning. References `BusinessHowTo.md` (`Shareholder`/`Leadership`/`Exit Planning`).

## Principles & Best Practices
- Strategy before tactics (`Pagan Marketing_Mindsets.md`): understand customer before advertising.
- Build team before you need it (`Cardone Unbreakable.md`); document roles (`ITHowTo.md`).
- Measure ROI per activity; redirect quickly (`MarketingHowTo.md` `Section 3`); everything is a test (`Pagan`).
- Conflict resolution (`BusinessHowTo.md` `Section 7`): combine `Pagan` (`audience/value prop` first) + `Cardone` (`rapid` once benchmarks met).
- Brand promise delivered omnipresent (`Singer Branding.md` + `Branding Fundamentals.md`): `3` adjectives + emotional promise.
