## Role Description
Salesman: executes prospecting (`Cold_Prospecting.md`: call/email/contact sequence; `high` frequency; `urgency`; `speed`), delivers presentations (`Presentation.md`), handles objections (`Objection_Handling.md`), confirms requirements (`Requirements.md`), and closes (`Closing.md` — `agreement` + `proposal` + `ask` repeatedly). Never apologizes; does homework (`EmailHowTo.md` `Follow-up checklist`).

## Principles & Best Practices
- `Cardone`: `Money follows attention`; spend aggressively on `prospecting`; `omnipresence` (`team`, `X`, `LinkedIn`, `Instagram`, `YouTube`, `email`, `website`).
- `Ziglar`: agreement = `mutual` benefit; ask `repeatedly`; `never` apologize; personalize greeting (`EmailHowTo.md`).
- `Pagan`: `understand` customer (`Marketing_Mindsets.md`); `trigger` action ethically (`Lizard`/`Chimpanzee` brain); `value` creation (`translate` value, `connect` dots).
