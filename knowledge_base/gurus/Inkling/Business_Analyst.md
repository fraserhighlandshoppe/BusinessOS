## Role Description
Business Analyst: owns funnel benchmarks (`Levis 5R`: `5-10%` site conversion; `1000` visitors → `50-100` opt-ins → `1-2` sales), ROI measurement (`MarketingHowTo.md` `Section 2`: `no ROI` = redirect), lead scoring (`MarketingHowTo.md` `Section 3`: point values on actions trigger CRM handoffs), conversion tracking (`conversion_tracking.jsonl`).

## Principles & Best Practices
- `Levis 5R.md` (`conversion benchmarks`): average site `5-10%`; funnel sequence (`Ads` → `Landing` → `Lead` → `Nurture` → `Tripwire` → `Core`).
- `MarketingHowTo.md` (`Lead scoring`): marketing owns model; `point` values trigger `CRM` handoffs.
- `DigitalMarketer`: `ROI` per channel; `benchmark` before redirect; `measure` first (`Pagan` + `Cardone` agreement).
