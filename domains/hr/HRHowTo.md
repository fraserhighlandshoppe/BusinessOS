## 1. Foundations: Team & Culture

Based on `Grant Cardone How to be a Leader.md`, `Leadership and High Performance.md`, `Unbreakable Business Challenge.md`, `BusinessHowTo.md` (shareholder / C-suite team framework).

---
**Common paragraph — Leadership = Competence + Presence + Communication (`Cardone` + `Singer` agree)**
Leadership requires three components (`Grant Cardone How to be a Leader.md`): competence (know the work), presence (show up consistently), communication (clear expectations + feedback). `Blair Singer Branding.md` adds: brand promise delivered through team behavior. Agreement: both say lead by example, not just by instruction. Conflict: Cardone = high-frequency accountability; Singer = emotional response / presence. Resolution: high-frequency presence (Cardone frequency + Singer emotional delivery).

## 2. Hiring Process (Step by Step)

Based on `FHS Job Offer to Hired Process.md` (`domains/project_management/`):
1. Define role (`BusinessHowTo.md` — document roles before hiring; `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`).
2. Post position / recruit.
3. Interview: assess competence (skills), presence (culture fit), communication (clarity) — `Cardone How to be a Leader.md` framework.
4. Offer (`FHS Job Offer to Hired Process.md`): include role, expectations, compensation, start date.
5. Hire: sign agreement; set up access (`ITHowTo.md` — role-based access, 2FA).
6. Onboard: give access to OS guides (`BusinessHowTo.md`, `MarketingHowTo.md`, etc.); assign mentor; review SOP ownership; review brand promise (`Branding Fundamentals.md`).
