## 1. The Operating System

Sales runs on the Cardone cycle: **Idea/Target → Attention (marketing) → Leads → Manage Activity → Monetize → Manage Revenue → Scale.** All successful activities require precise formulas — otherwise it's luck. *(Grant Cardone Sales System.md)*

Mindset rules that override everything:
- Attitude is senior to everything: products can be shopped, a great attitude can't. Treat every customer like a millionaire - people act the way you treat them. *(Live Sales Training)*
- Sales is simple: find the person who can buy, has power, is interested → offer → price → ask. A contact sport. *(Keep Your Sales Simple)*
- You are always EXPANDING or CONTRACTING; follow-up calls and cold calls are expansion activities. *(10X Growth Con 2018)*

## 2. Prospecting & Lead Response

- **Speed is power**: respond to leads immediately - 100% better conversion with speed alone. ~8 touches to re-contact after a web visit. Use every channel (texting during a call lifts closing 328%). → `Lead Response Strategy.md`
- **Assume leads did their homework**, aren't always the decision-maker (100-500 employee companies average 7), and still need to be sold.
- **Cold calling** puts you at cause of your production: define contact → do the math (17 dials/contact; 6% reach senior people) → build list → script → role play daily → BIG Claim → qualify → close. Voicemails <20s, one message each, never sell in them. → `Master the Cold Call.md`
- Qualifying exists for ONE purpose: to know how to close the client. → `Requirements to Close.md`

## 3. The Meeting / Presentation

1. **The Always of Sales**: communicate, write a proposal, ask to buy, ask again (and again), introduce a 3rd party, ask why they didn't buy, close the distance (phone/drop-in, not email), ask why they DID buy. → `The Always of Sales.md`
2. **Sell solutions, not products** - people buy only to solve problems. Ask "if I could solve two or three recurring issues in your business, what would they be?" → `10 Mistakes That Kill Your Deals.md`
3. **State your intention to close up front** ("my goal is to have this at your company by end of week"); break presentation into stages and trial-close after each ("have you seen enough to decide?").
4. **Reveal price early** so they think with it while you demonstrate value; "too high" = "of course, you haven't seen what it does yet."
5. **Handle influencers** - ask each what problem they want solved; ignored influencers kill deals.
6. **Ask hard questions and get answers** (money? decision maker? readiness 1-10? any reason you couldn't complete?). → `Hard Selling Questions.md`
7. **Use humor** to relieve pressure; evoke emotion; highlight personal benefits. → `Presentation.md`
8. Never use free trials to close; never let urgency lapse - insist on closing now.

## 4. Objections & Negotiation

- Almost never price: build relationship, tailor solution, install confidence in value.
- **Feel, Felt, Found** framework + scripted comebacks (too busy / have an advisor / send info). → `Objection Handling and Stuck Deals.md`
- Stuck deals: inertia is the toughest competitor - create urgency, hand-marked materials, SPIN questions.
- **Negotiating**: options A/B/C (C = ground zero); no emotions; write everything down (people believe what they read); White Flag surrender-then-trade; Back to the Future framing; remain seated. 97% of deals don't close on first negotiation - follow-up over the top. → `Negotiating Techniques.md`
- Trust > tricks: sell with documentation, never negotiate with words. → `Sell Without Selling.md`

## 5. Closing Requirements

A prospect is closable when you have ALL 7: decision maker · wherewithal · sold on product · believes in YOU · confident it solves their problem · written offer · urgency (why now). → `Requirements to Close.md`
Second sale is easier than the first - go back to past buyers and demo'd-but-didn't-buy prospects before chasing strangers. *(10 Truths of Conversions)*

## 6. Follow-Up & Proposals

- Follow-up is where sales are won; most sales don't convert on first contact. Define follow-up process per lead stage. → `Lead Response Strategy.md`, `Tom Ferry Million Dollar Follow-up` (wiki)
- Always write proposals (see `Proposals.md`); people believe what they read.

## 7. Team Development

- Onboard with the structured program (logistics checklist → daily training rhythm → 25-topic curriculum → role play). → `Sales Team Onboarding Program.md`
- Daily training topics start with ATTITUDE; drill scripts until natural (actor, not robot).
- Fix employee side-hustle disengagement with in-office bonus structures.
