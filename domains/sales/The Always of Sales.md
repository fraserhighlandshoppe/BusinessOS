# The Always of Sales

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales

"If you don't have always, you end up with never."

- Always communicate (shake hands, look in the eye, introduce yourself)
- Always write a proposal
- Always ask the customer to buy
- Always ask again (and again)
- Always introduce a 3rd party
- Always ask why they didn't buy
- Always close the distance - quit using email; phone or drop in
- Always ask why they DID buy
