# Sell Without Selling

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #trust

Smooth the process until neither side feels like they're "selling". Sales are built on trust - tricks, gimmicks, mirroring and "getting into state" produce only "I need to think about it". Those objections are decisions made from **lack of trust** (in you, the product, or the process). When they don't trust, they don't buy.

## Never Sell With Words
Sell with documentation: what's the deal, what's the value, what's in it for the customer? Show, don't tell.

## Never Negotiate With Words
Put everything in writing.
