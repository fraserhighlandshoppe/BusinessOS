# 7 Lead Response Strategy Mistakes (Grant Cardone)

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #followup

Businesses lose sales daily because leads aren't followed up or prospects don't get requested info. Convert 100% of leads by avoiding:

1. **Slow responses** - Speed is power. 100% better chance of converting a digital lead with speed alone.
2. **No defined follow-up process** - Second contact should differ from first. Define schedule, channel, message. Process gives predictability, consistency, scalability.
3. **Assuming the lead hasn't done their homework** - Assume they researched you and competitors. Correct misconceptions before convincing.
4. **Not giving the info requested** - People trust what they see, not hear. Online buyers are informed AND misinformed; give exact detail requested.
5. **Forgetting they still need to be sold** - Takes ~8 tries to re-contact after a website visit. Prospects need reassurance at the right time.
6. **Assuming the lead is the decision maker** - Companies with 100-500 employees average up to 7 decision-makers. Identify influencers.
7. **Relying on only one form of communication** - Use video, text, phone, email, DMs. Texting during a sales call can raise closing ratio by 328%.
