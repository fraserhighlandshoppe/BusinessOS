# Keep Your Sales Simple

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales

Everyone is in sales regardless of job or business - and most overcomplicate it.

Sales is a contact sport:
1. Find the person who can buy, has power to decide, and is interested
2. Approach with an offer
3. Talk price
4. Make an ask

Simple - though not always easy. The most successful salespeople focus on simplifying their sales process.
