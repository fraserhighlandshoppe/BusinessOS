# Requirements to Close the Sale

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #closing

## A prospect is closable when you have all 7
1. Decision maker present
2. Wherewithal (money)
3. Sold on the product
4. They believe in *you*
5. Confidence the product solves a problem
6. Written offer
7. Urgency - why now

## Closing Mindset
Go into every sales situation believing the deal can be done.

## Qualifying
- Purpose of qualifying: **to know how to close the client**.

### Qualifying Mistakes
Not asking qualifying questions; not getting real answers; irrelevant questions; not listening; not digging ("why is that important?"); not learning what they value; never discovering how they decide; not asking questions that set up the close; not finding the decision maker; not saying the price.
