# Master the Cold Call

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #cold-calling

The cold call is the single most powerful way for a salesperson to exceed quota - it puts you at the cause of your production, not the effect of a company's leads. Most salespeople despise and avoid them; mastering them makes you one of the most valuable employees in a sales organization.

## Preparation
Define a contact -> do the math -> build a list -> call prep -> your script -> **role play every day** -> prepare for objections -> take notes.

## The Cold Call Process
Greeting -> **The BIG Claim** -> Qualify/Fact-find -> Close.

## Types of Calls
Outbound cold call, outbound lead response, outbound referral call, outbound follow-up call.

## Gatekeepers
Act the part; skip small talk until needed; use a power greeting.

## Voicemail That Gets Attention (4 steps)
1. Decide the ONE message: recommended by someone they trust / I understand your challenge / I've solved this for others / urgent matter
2. Write multiple scripts, one per message
3. Deliver: under 20 seconds; upbeat energetic tone; urgency; use both names; DON'T sell in the voicemail; DON'T sound desperate with deals/discounts; DON'T leave only one
4. Track which scripts work; keep improving
- Tip: lead with a loss-aversion insight before your name ("40% of companies that take a bank loan still fail" beat benefit-first pitches by 10% - Kahneman's loss aversion).

## 20 Field Lessons (2,000-call experiment)
- Senior people DO pick up: 6% of calls reached CEOs/SVPs/VPs of Fortune 500 firms
- Average 17 dials per contact; 50%+ go to voicemail; <1% respond to voicemail
- 1 in 10 first conversations led to follow-up meeting
- ~600 prospects per 2,000 dials/month; ~7,200 prospects per rep to stay busy all year
- Follow up immediately; stay relaxed; list quality is everything; persist
- Time of day doesn't matter much; bank holidays worked best (managers in, not in meetings)
- De-emphasize email/text - phone teaches more; don't multitask while dialing
- Start easy to build confidence; rejection isn't personal; attitude is everything
- If you truly can't sell, hire or outsource - the business depends on it

## Physical/Mental Rituals
Script + daily call goal; mirror while calling; smile (changes voice pitch); stand up/move; power pose; background music; find your routine like a free-throw shooter.
Daily success ritual: get up early, workout, focus, KNOW your call calculations, start with training/role play, clean space, list of 5, music.

## Objections Covered
Not interested | Busy/no time | Send info/email | Not decision maker | No budget | Let me try it free | Need to talk to director/board/spouse.
