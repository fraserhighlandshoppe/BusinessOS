# Grant Cardone - Presentation Techniques

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #presentation

## Use Humor to Relieve Pressure
- Humor is one of the oldest practices in sales. People connect with stories, especially humorous ones.
- Eases pressure, relaxes prospects; laughter associates positivity with you.
- Keep it inoffensive; make prospects feel good, inspired, hopeful - they'll want to continue doing business.

## Elements of a Great Sales Presentation
- Evoke emotion; highlight personal benefits and the emotional satisfaction of ownership.
- Structure: capture attention -> communicate value proposition convincingly.
