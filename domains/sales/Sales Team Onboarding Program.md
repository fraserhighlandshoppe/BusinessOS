# Sales Team Onboarding Program

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #onboarding #hr

## Logistics Checklist (before day 1)
- Offer letter; domain account, email, phone number, computer, role software; desk, phone, chair, tools
- Contact info distributed to team leads/managers
- First day: tour, team intros, onboarding binder (how-to sheets for software/phone/email), HR (ID, photo, parking, benefits/insurance, policies, code of conduct)

## Training Setup
POS as applicable, phone dialing, office software, CRM, videos, scripts, role play, order submission process.
Work targets start immediately: 200 leads/day + end-of-day check-in (questions/concerns).

## Day 2+ Rhythm
Daily training, team meeting (take-aways, share stats/previous-day sales), role play, sales calls.

## Training Curriculum (Cardone sales principles)
1. **Daily attitude** - optimism/resilience/growth mindset; you're a product of your environment; gratitude, visualization, self-motivation
2. **Intro to selling** - commission vs salary
3. **Volume and profit** drive growth
4. **Commitment** - eliminate alternatives; no greener pastures
5. **Power of observation** - observe, record, recognize patterns
6. **Sell yourself first** - personal branding; transfer enthusiasm (can outweigh product features)
7. **Eat your own dogfood**
8. **Phone skills** - prepare, ALWAYS leave a concise compelling message, convey enthusiasm, active listening; callbacks: they called back = interested, don't get defensive
9. **Objections** - almost never price: relationships, tailored solutions, communicate value; fear-based objections need empathy + reassurance
10. **Making sales** - build mental ownership in buyer's mind; customers don't make sales, salespeople do
11. **Money mindset** - there is no shortage of money
12. **First rule of sales** - align with customer perspective; agreement builds rapport and beats objections
13. **The second sale** - upsell; selling to satisfied customer easier than to leads
14. **People business** - 80/20 rule; relationships over products; research buyers, anticipate objections
15. **Trust & credibility** - deliver on promises, expertise, social proof, visual/tangible evidence, third-party endorsements; document discussions in writing
16. **Give, give, give** - attention, energy, exceptional service; service is senior to selling; superior service stops price-shopping
17. **Price-sensitive buyers** - salesperson initiates the price discussion; set expectations early; flexible options
18. **Write-up discipline** - gather/document preferences, handle interruptions, stay focused
19. **Closing** - recipes/scripts; trial close vs commitment; stay engaged and persistent
20. **Massive action** - 4 degrees of action; proactive approach
21. **Power base** - work existing networks; help first, win-win
22. **Time management** - prioritize high-value activities; use every moment to sell (lunch = networking)
23. **Role playing** - pick objection ("not interested", "no time", "price too high"), assign roles, in-person or back-to-back phone
24. **Perfect call script** - name-drop referral, promise a proven tool (+40% sales), ask qualifying questions, book 10 minutes
25. **Great presentations** - evoke emotion, highlight personal benefits
