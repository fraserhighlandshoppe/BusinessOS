# Grant Cardone - Live Sales Training

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #attitude #closing

## Core Problem Areas Salespeople Face
Follow-up, closing, prospecting, finding good people / turnover, motivation, accountability, sales process.

## Only Two Reasons People Don't Buy
1. They are not the right person
2. They don't believe it will work

If a prospect asks the price, they are interested in buying. You won't get attention until you are solving a problem for them.

## A Great Attitude Is Worth More Than a Great Product
- Products can be shopped; a great attitude cannot be shopped. Price can be beaten; attitude is priceless.
- People pay more for an agreeable, positive, great experience than for a great product.
- Easy to say "no" to a product; hard to say "no" to a positive human experience.
- Positive attitude is contagious; negativity causes fight-or-flight. Positivity melts negativity.
- Use the phrases: "Great", "super", "excellent", "love it", "no problem", "I'd be happy".
- Attitude is senior to everything. Massive action with a bad attitude just makes a wreck of the marketplace.

## Treat People Like Millionaires
- Treat every customer like they have money and they'll spend money they don't have.
- People act the way you treat them - decision-maker or not, young or old.
- Agree with the customer to build rapport (e.g., car buyer wanting invoice price); change their mind in the close, not the greeting.

## You Are a Product of Your Environment
You are what you surround yourself with: people, beliefs, TV, magazines, friends, movies, hobbies, interests, family.

## Top Issues Companies Report
Only two real objections exist: wrong person, or disbelief. Everything else is handling these.
