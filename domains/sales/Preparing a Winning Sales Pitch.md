# Preparing a Winning Sales Pitch

Tags: #sales #pitch

- Modern pitches are **two-way conversations**: listening to wants/needs is as important as presenting. Ask follow-up questions, pinpoint pain, offer a solution to THEIR challenges.
- Prepare, but don't over-rehearse into robotic. People buy from people they like - friendly+professional beats stiff+formal.
- First impressions matter; know your products cold.
- Attention is scarce - keep them immersed with buyer-relevant info and active engagement.
- **Due diligence**: research their company, competitors, industry trends before contact; tailor the message to the challenges they face.
