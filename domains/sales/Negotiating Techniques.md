# Negotiating Techniques

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #negotiation

## Principles
- Negotiation is discussion toward agreement. "You don't get what you deserve, you get what you negotiated."
- You negotiate EVERY transaction. Offer options A, B, C - C is ground zero.
- No emotions during negotiation. A deal happens when each side feels it gets more than it gives up.
- Deals are done when problems are solved - find the sticking point and fill the gap.
- Always write it down: people don't believe what they hear, they believe what they read.
- 97% of deals aren't closed on the first negotiation - follow-up must be over the top.

## Remain Seated
Hardly ever close someone on your feet. When seeking agreement, keep the prospect seated too. If they stand mid-negotiation, stay seated - it shows confidence and that you're not done. Standing up signals something changed.

## White Flag (surrender first)
Give them what they ask - then have them cover items you need done (e.g., real estate: roof, kitchen, AC).
- A: full price $410k and they do the work
- B: $410k less the $110k cost and you do it
- C: no deal

On option A, sweeten: extra payment or closing date to their benefit.

## Back to the Future
Move them into the future: "Assume the sticking point has been handled - what does it look like when I take your item and you take my offer? What problem has been resolved?"
