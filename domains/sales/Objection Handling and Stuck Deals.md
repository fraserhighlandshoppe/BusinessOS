# Objection Handling & Stuck Deals

Tags: #sales #objections

## The Core Framework: Feel, Felt, Found
1. "I get why you **feel** that way"
2. "Lots of my clients **felt** that way too"
3. "The clients that tried my way **found** it more effective because..."

## Scripted Comebacks
- **"I'm too busy"** = they don't think you have value. "I don't want to waste your time nor mine. Does this make sense? Great — what works better for you, ___ or ___?"
- **"I already have an advisor"** - "Great. I'd like to show you what I'm doing so you can maybe send people my way. If you like what you see great; if not, no problem."
- **"Heard negative things about XYZ"** - "What have you heard?" (wait) → a handful of complainers doesn't define everyone.
- **"Send me information first"** - websites overload; they don't show what's in it for YOU specifically.
- For virtual meetings ("email me the docs"): we won't know we're on the same page; flexibility to demo on the fly; faster meeting. Compromise: cameras-off virtual with screen share.

## Signs of a Stuck Deal (Brad Zingle, HUB)
Low lead→opportunity conversion | deals stuck at 2nd meeting | lengthening sales cycle | prospects rolling to "next renewal" (procrastination). **Inertia is the toughest competitor** — some choose to do nothing.

### Getting Unstuck
Create urgency; send a pamphlet directly related to their situation — marked up by hand (comments, highlights, circles).
Non-feature worries to probe: migrating to new procedures, trusting the service, getting support, retraining staff.
Use **SPIN** questions (Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff) structured to generate urgency.
