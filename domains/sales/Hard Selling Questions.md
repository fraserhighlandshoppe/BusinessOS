# Hard Selling Questions

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #questions

Don't miss chances to build trust: real closers have real communication. If you don't get answers, you won't close deals.

Ask:
- Who else is involved? Who are the decision makers; who will use the product?
- When is the last time you invested this amount?
- Why are you considering this NOW? (creates urgency)
- How do you make sense of an investment of this size?
- Is there any reason you would change your mind?
- How liquid are you at this time? Where is the money coming from?
- Why didn't you buy our product?
- When is the last time you spent this much on anything?
- How much do you believe of what I've shared with you today?
- Why would you change your mind?
- Have you seen enough to make a decision?

Variants for competitors: "How do you feel about our pricing/terms?" "Why do business with me when you've used our competitor so long?"
