# 10 Mistakes That Kill Your Deals

Tags: #GrantCardone #JeffSarault #sales

Deals die when salespeople focus on themselves instead of the customer, or lack understanding of buyer needs.

1. **Not selling the solution** - People buy ONLY to solve a problem, no exceptions. Ask "if I could solve two or three recurring issues in your business, what would they be?" then prove you solve them.
2. **Missing buying signals** - Stay present; buyer signals matter more than your presentation. Watch every decision-maker and influencer in the room.
3. **Refusing to ask hard questions** - pricing, terms, why switch from competitor. No answers = no close.
4. **Believing price will solve problems** - Nobody ever buys a price. Every buyer will pay more; the money will go somewhere else anyway.
5. **Presenting without intention to close** - State it up front: "my goal is to have my product at your company by end of week." Confidence, not arrogance.
6. **Not asking for the close early enough** - Break the presentation into stages; after each ask "have you seen enough to make a decision?" Close often, close early.
7. **Price mystery** - Reveal price EARLY so buyers think with it while you demonstrate value ("that's too high" -> "of course, you haven't seen what it does yet").
8. **Ignoring influencers** - Ask each one what problem they most want solved; solicit objections/concerns throughout; get names before presenting. Ignored influencers kill deals.
9. **Using free trials to close** - Trials drive traffic but almost never close; they cost time and create cash-flow problems and poor-quality customers.
10. **No urgency** - Insist on closing now (Now-or-Later Close). Train your team on using pressure - it's an art.
