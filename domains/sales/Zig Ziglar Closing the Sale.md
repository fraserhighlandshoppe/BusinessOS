# Zig Ziglar - Closing the Sale

Tags: #sales #closing #ZigZiglar

## The Professional Understands
- Thinks in terms of service AND expects to make the sale on the specific call.
- Works from a plan, not a can — versatile in knowledge and approach; includes verbatim phrases/explanations in every presentation (there are many ways to say things, but one best way).
- Responsibility to sell so clearly the prospect understands it's in their best interest to buy.
- Abusive prospects are usually defending against fear/inferiority — don't take it personally.
- The universal rule: **find out what the prospect wants, then help him get it.**
- Professional = "assured competence"; has a good sense of humor — takes the profession and prospect's needs seriously, but not himself.
