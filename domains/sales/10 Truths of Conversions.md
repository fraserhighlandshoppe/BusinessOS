# 10 Truths of Conversions (Sales)

Tags: #GrantCardone #sales #conversion

1. **Always agree with the prospect** - agreement ≠ they're right; it builds rapport
2. **Be transparent** - state your intention; project confidence and certainty; get them to accept the problem WILL be solved
3. **Clear intention**
4. **No one thing will blow a sale** - don't walk on eggshells
5. **100% write-up** - proposals and contracts always
6. **People shop price; they don't buy price**
7. **Use hard questions and ALWAYS get answers**: Do you have the money? Are you the decision maker? 1-10, how ready are you? Any reason you couldn't complete this deal? Are you in a position to purchase? Ever spent this much before?
8. **Decision phenomenon** - buyers decide BEFORE you ask; "have you seen enough to make a decision?"
9. **The 2nd sale is easier than the first** - go back to previous buyers and to demo'd-but-didn't-buy prospects instead of chasing strangers
10. **Always use a 3rd-party touch** - "why didn't you buy?"
