# Network Marketing / Recruiting Playbook

Tags: #sales #networkmarketing

## The Brutal Statistics (Direct Sellers Association)
Only 0.25% earn significant residual income. 80% quit within 90 days; 70% feel disenfranchised (no upline support); 97% never break even.
Why: anxiety/uncertainty peak exactly when skills are lowest — and companies just say "follow the start-up kit: make your list, call."

## Recruiting Mindset (financial-planning framing)
Recruit because of the ripple effect on lives; it aligns with mission; it changes both lives. Everyone recruited gains: a financial plan, better environment, leadership development.

## Team Building
**People join people, not organizations.**
- Have a team NAME (identity)
- Hold WEEKLY meetings (excitement, support, belonging, unofficial accountability)

## Closing Questions (Eric Worre)
If you only pitch facts without asking questions at the end, you'll struggle:
1. "Part-time, how much would you need to EARN per month to make it worthwhile?" (their number, not yours)
2. "How many HOURS per week will you dedicate to earn that?"
3. "How many MONTHS will you put in those hours?"
→ Reset unreasonable math honestly ("I don't know we can get you there in that timeframe with those hours").

## Friends & Family (Jim Ruta)
You don't HAVE to sell them to succeed — those sales alone won't make you a leader. But skipping them entirely is a mistake: they're the natural first practice ground of a long career.

## Lead Generation
LinkedIn profile completeness (phone, email, website, good picture, bio); see Social Selling.
