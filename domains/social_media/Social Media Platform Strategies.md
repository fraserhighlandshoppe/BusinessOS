# Social Media Platform Strategies

Tags: #marketing #socialmedia

## Facebook
Broadest platform (mobile+desktop, work+home); audience-dependent usage. Half-life of a post ≈ **90 minutes**; reaches 75% of potential audience within 5 hours. Repost only verified content (fake news risk); provide shareable engaging content.

## Instagram
Mobile-first; users on all day, most engaged off-work hours. Best: Mon & Thu except 3-4pm; videos best 9pm-8am; also test 2am, 5pm, Wed 7pm. Ads available via Facebook Ad Manager.

## Twitter
Hardest to grow - audience-dependent; treated like an RSS feed during downtime. Post half-life ≈ **24 minutes** (75% reach in <3 hours). 140 chars + media. Good as a customer-service LISTENING tool (listen and respond to issues); requires constant activity to grow followers.

## YouTube Explosion Strategy
Ranking depends on view count AND speed of views:
1. Create video with searchable keywords in the title
2. Speak keywords 2-3x so Google's transcription indexes them
3. Write a standalone 500-1500 word blog post on the topic embedding the video (keywords in title + body)
4. Email your opt-in list to drive them to the blog post/video

## Pinterest
Evening "TV-time" activity; skews female. Best evenings, especially Sat 8-11pm. It's bookmarking/inspiration - people post hopes and dreams.
