## 1. Foundations

- Omnipresence: team, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, email, website, live events (`Grant Cardone Unbreakable` — business master reference).
- Platform timing matters: FB half-life 90min; Twitter 24min; Pinterest evenings; Instagram off-hours (`Social Media Platform Strategies.md`).
- Brand = promise = emotional response; personal brand formula: Competence + Presence + Communication (`Branding Fundamentals.md`).

## 2. Platforms & Tactics

- Facebook (`facebook/`): destination not launch-pad; content fits friends/sharing/boredom context; likes = opt-ins (`Facebook Strategy.md`, `First Round of Posts.md`).
- Marketplace (`facebook/Facebook Marketplace Strategy.md`): listing strategy for product/service exposure.
- Kijiji (`Kijiji/Kijiji Strategy.md`): local market postings; image sizing per `Social Media Image Size Guide.md`.
- YouTube: keywords in title + spoken 2-3x; embed in standalone blog post; email list drives traffic (`Social Media Platform Strategies.md`).
- Instagram / X / LinkedIn: short-form content; link to long-form assets (swipe/salesletter skeleton for landing pages).

## 3. Content & Copy

- Hook → Before/After story → CTA (swipe formulas mapped to `swipe/salesletter_formulas.md` structure).
- Headlines: 7 Power formulas (`swipe/headline_formulas.md`); fill variables from `swipe_*.txt`.
- Images: follow `Social Media Image Size Guide.md` dimensions; brand colors consistent.
