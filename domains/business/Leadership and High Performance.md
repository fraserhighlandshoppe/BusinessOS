
# Leadership & High Performance

Tags: #leadership #management

## Eight Laws of Leadership (Gen. William Cohen, "The Stuff of Heroes")
1. Maintain absolute integrity
2. Know your stuff
3. Declare your expectations
4. Show uncommon commitment
5. Expect positive results
6. Take care of your people
7. Put duty before self
8. *(lead by example - see raw page)*

## Leadership vs Management
People follow managers' direction out of fear; they follow a LEADER. Leaders install confidence and trust. Not everyone is a natural leader — establish credentials; inspire and motivate.

## 9 Essential Skills (summary)
Deep understanding of the group's practices/history + continuous learning | strong communication | fostering respect, collaboration and growth. (full list in raw page)

## Elements of High Performance
Driven by passion (excitement/enthusiasm), excellence ("best I can be"), desire to serve (making a difference). It's HOW you work, not how MUCH.
The 5 P's: **Productivity, Persuasion, Psychology, Physiology, Purpose**. Psychology = your thoughts must support the journey.

## Elite-Athlete Lessons for Business
Lots of little things become big changes (100m finals decided by hundredths) — marginal gains compound; intelligent training philosophy beats raw effort.

## Team Building
Clear mental picture of leading thousands + written business plan covering recruiting/training/development and personal sales; know your funnel ratios (prospects → interviews → sales → new associates → final reviews).

## Priorities & Rituals
- Set priorities by asking: what's critical for success/development? what's urgent now? what can be scheduled or eliminated?
- Morning ritual: 7 minutes of exercise (short enough to keep, long enough to shake sluggishness), good breakfast, plan the day.
