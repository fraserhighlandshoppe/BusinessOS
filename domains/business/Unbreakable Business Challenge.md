# Unbreakable Business System & Challenge

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #marketing

## System Components
Curriculum | Coaching | Customized game plan | Critical thinking.

## Kevin Hart Lesson
Can't make $100M telling jokes - own the show. Hart needs no promoter to insure events: he takes ~95% of the door because he's his own marketer.

## Expanding or Contracting
You are always doing one of the two.

## Day 1 - Money / Mindset
- Time is money; lose the indoctrination that "money isn't important / won't make you happy".
- Set a target big enough that everything above becomes possible.
- Pete Vargas segment on stages/audiences; homework across mindset, marketing, sales, communication, scale.

## Day 2 - Marketing Pillars
1. **Marketing is senior to sales**
2. **Money follows attention**
3. **Assume no one saw your marketing**
4. **Speed is senior to quality of content**
5. **Frequency builds trust**

Key ideas:
- Don't expect others to promote you if you won't promote yourself.
- **Omnipresence** - be everywhere at once; that's the massive thinking most people lack.
- Announce commitment publicly (e.g., "I am committed to getting 10X rich") - if you're afraid to say it, you can't market it.
- Get attention: collaborate with a network, one specific topic, communicate the specific action AND a deadline.
- Money moves to confidence - low confidence means slower action for you and everyone around you.
- Channels: team, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, email list, website, TikTok, live events, webinars, collaboration.

## Russell Brunson - The Money Is in the List
- Marketing math: aim to earn **$1/month per name** on your list. Income correlates directly with your relationship with the list.
- Brunson's origin: bought a DVD of 3M emails, mailed his $10 booklet, made 6 sales - realized spam was wrong/unethical and built permission-based lists instead.
- List-building evolved past annoying popups (and Google's popup blocking) into value-first opt-ins.
