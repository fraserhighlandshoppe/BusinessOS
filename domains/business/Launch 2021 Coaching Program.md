# Launch 2021 Coaching Program

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #coaching

## The 5 Skills Needed
1. Marketing
2. Sales
3. Follow-up (most sales won't convert on first contact)
4. Money
5. Motivation

## Goal Declarations
- I am going to spend millions on marketing in my lifetime.
- I am going to become a sales genius.
- I am going to have 100 people on my team following up with customers.
- Who is going to be my coach this year? What am I going to work on this year?

## The 3 A's of Success
**Attitude, Approach, Action.**
