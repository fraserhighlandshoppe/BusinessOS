
# Brian Tracy - Success Principles

Tags: #BrianTracy #productivity #sales

## Daily Routines for Productivity
- **Think on paper**: write everything you intend to do (even lunch/workout), then prioritize: "If I could only do ONE thing today?" Start with #1 and discipline yourself to finish before moving on.
- **Don't check email in the morning** - one email leads hours astray; keep phone away during your most important task.

## Improve Your People Skills: Charm
Charm = creating extraordinary rapport, making others feel truly exceptional. It's learnable. The deepest craving of human nature is to feel valued and valuable — the secret of charm is making others feel important.

## Sales Prospecting Checklist
1. Understand your product/service and how it's used today
2. Define your product's specialty
3. Analyze current customers and why they buy
4. Describe your ideal customer in detail
5. Analyze competitors' advantage
6. Define how you're different
7. List customer segments that benefit most from your specialization
8. Anticipate weaknesses customers may find
9. Which markets are NOT buying?
10. Identify what prospects must BELIEVE to buy from you
11-12. Evaluate prospects' good/bad characteristics; focus energy on best prospects

Key question: "Where is my next sale coming from? Is what I'm doing leading to it?"

## Gratitude & Motivation
Voice an "attitude of gratitude": praise and encourage continually - deposits into the "emotional bank account". Thanking people raises their self-esteem and makes them feel valuable; every "thank you" also raises YOUR self-esteem. Gratitude flows make you popular and eager-to-help networks.
