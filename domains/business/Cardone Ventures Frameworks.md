
# Cardone Ventures Business Frameworks

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #scaling

## The 3 Lids
1. **Beliefs** - actions follow beliefs; missed targets lower belief (yours and your team's). You can't attract people better than yourself - you are the highest example. 85% of people must SEE it to believe it; successful entrepreneurs believe before seeing. Low belief = low activity = low results.
2. **Operations** - effectiveness drives results.
3. **Leadership** - not telling people what to do; making others' success easy. Create conditions for success; generate confidence about what's possible. Leadership must be contagious.

## The 4 Ms
**Model** (who/what) -> **Mimic** (the how - teachable massive action) -> **Master** (learn how/what/why, then train newcomers) -> **Multiply**.

## The 4 Ps
**Promote** (what you do + impact) | **Profit** (effectiveness measure) | **Process** (document proven processes into proactive roadmaps for customer experience) | **People**.

## Core Values
Inspiration | Discipline (without it, random things happen) | Accountability (no accountability = no discipline) | Transparency (staff must know where we are, where we're going, why - so they can adjust their own work) | Alignment (nobody is serious about your goals if you aren't serious about theirs) | Results (define the target, who owns success, who ensures progress).

## Scale vs Scaling
- **Scale** = maximizing what you have
- **Scaling** = rapid expansion and growth
- Most people skip scale and jump to scaling, missing optimization.
- Elements: Strategy, Market, Sell, Add People, Understand Finances, Leverage through Operations, Leadership, Data tracking, Tech, Investment.

## Why Businesses Fail
1. Lack of demand for product
2. Can't get good people
3. Can't get capital

Never just one person builds great value - brute force alone won't do it.
