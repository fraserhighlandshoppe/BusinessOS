## The 5% Question
Only 5% of entrepreneurs become millionaires; 95% struggle. Most started businesses for money, control, time freedom - yet end up overwhelmed, working harder for less, scared about money.

## The Branson Effect (Richard Branson)
When you are clear and committed to who you are, AND have a formula to follow, you can achieve whatever you set out to do. Branson built hundreds of companies on this simple strategy.
