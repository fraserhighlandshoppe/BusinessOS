
# Grant Cardone - How to be a Leader

Tags: #GrantCardone #leadership #business

Successful leaders inspire people and motivate action via storytelling/visioning: paint a picture of an ideal future state so followers want it for something bigger than themselves.

1. **Show up** - Opportunities come daily to those who show up. Consistency is the most important thing; no shortcuts.
2. **Be authentic** - People trust and respect the real you. Value-driven mindset; share success with everyone.
3. **No negativity allowed** - Negativity destroys happiness, career, family. Don't display or spread it.
4. **Work ethic** - Leaders are role models; the team watches everything and matches your hustle.
5. **Provide feedback** - Give AND receive it. Separate emotion from truth. Make it a habit; learn from failures and move on.
6. **Be someone worth following** - Create opportunities for others; play by the rules but bend them when necessary.
7. **Be a mentor** - Take time to grow the next generation of leaders.
