
# Eben Pagan - Productivity & Naming (Guru Success)

Tags: #EbenPagan #productivity #branding

## Naming Products
Well-named brands do much better. The phonological loop holds sounds ~5 seconds - make them bounce longer:
- Repetitive sounds: Coca Cola, M&Ms, Eminem, Blackberry, Bed Bath & Beyond
- Rhyme: Double Your Dating, You Snooze You Lose
- Rhythm: Da-vid De-An-ge-lo

We like what's familiar.

## Productivity: Remove Friction
Friction = anything that slows you down (snags, catches). Proactively remove it from processes.
- **"What you stick with you get stuck with"** - don't over-attach to processes/possessions; attachment leaves no space for better.
- Audit chores: doing low-value tasks out of "work ethic" decreases productivity; doing them for enjoyment has value.

## Stop Setting Goals - Solve Problems Instead
80% of people are problem-solvers rather than goal-setters. Ask "what problems do we need to solve right now?" instead of "what's the goal?" - it fulfills your team's needs first.

## Brainstorming Carefully
Ideas voiced to the team sound like orders. Lead with: "Let's brainstorm - let's not do any of these just yet."

## Share Your Thinking Process
Presenting a finished plan doesn't empower people - they weren't involved so they aren't invested. Have them participate: ask what problems to solve, walk through your reasoning, ask what they think.
