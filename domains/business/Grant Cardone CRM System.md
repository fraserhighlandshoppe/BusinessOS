
# Grant Cardone - Customer Relationship Management System

Tags: #GrantCardone #crm #marketing

## Core Idea
CRM is the relationship between you and the customer *after* all the time and money spent on marketing to them. How you follow up with your customer is the bulk of your business.

## The Journey
1. You ran an ad (competing against Brand Y).
2. Prospect lands on your site in **comparison mode**, not buy mode - they care about X vs Y differences, not your company history. Tell the story against the comparison.
3. Set expectations and experience fast to keep focus on you.
4. Questions go into the CRM; the CRM buckets info and routes it appropriately.
5. CRM should supply scripts for the salesperson.
6. Track website behaviour (e.g., service visits, interactions), purchases, customer value - nurture accordingly.

## Precision Marketing
- Message must be relevant: right message, right time, right customer.
- An opt-out is expensive: re-engaging costs double the ad spend (CASL anti-spam rules).
