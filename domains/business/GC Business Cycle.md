
# GC Business Cycle and 6 Stages of Business Owner

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #entrepreneurship

## The Business Cycle
1. Idea
2. Craft the offer
3. Market the offer (landing page, social media, mailing list)
4. Monetize (sell and close)
5. Deliver (book / pdf / mp3 / video)
6. Repeat

**Get the offer out ASAP; save sophistication for later.** Don't waste time on trademarks/LLCs before there's money - if you hit it big you'd be in court daily protecting it instead of working.

## Streamlining: Marketing is the most important part
Most people get stuck in service/delivery - no point building a product the market doesn't respond to.
- **Speed to market**: Millionaire Booklet written same night (10:30pm inspiration), landing page up, promoted on Instagram immediately.
- **Multiple offers**: free book -> audio -> system -> bootcamp -> conference -> mentoring -> mastermind.
- **Be omnipresent**: promote everywhere so they don't forget you.
- **Double down on successful campaigns.**
- **Promotion, not perfection.**
- **Automate and scale. Always be acquiring new customers.**

## 6 Stages of Business Owner
1. **Dreamer** - millions of ideas, no system/coach/accountability, never starts. Fix: write your dream in present tense.
2. **Startup** - no coach/mentor/system/money/marketing/traffic; one-man show.
3. **Flatline** - and all flatlines eventually drop.
4. **Comfortable**
5. **Scaler** - a dreamer WITH a system
6. **Going Public**

## Employee Problems -> Not Your Problems
Bonus structures that let employees earn extra AT the office keep them engaged; side hustles mean disengagement.
