## Become a Virtual Coach
- Only 13% of people like going to work; coaching = doing work you love, making a real difference, future-safe as computers replace work.
- **#1 skill of high-paid coaches**: average coaches help clients DO something; high-paid coaches support clients through *transitions and transformations*.
- Client acquisition: target the 99% looking for a SOLUTION but not yet looking for a coach (tip-of-iceberg vs base). Give away your best ideas free; use AdWords to find what solutions people search for; build client magnets; run them on Facebook.
- Speed matters: discover the profitable niche first.

## Digital Product Blueprint
- If you have a product, make a related much more expensive one - doubles your business. Physical products should get digital companions.
- Formula:
  1. Identify a best-selling topic/niche — don't choose it, TARGET it: people motivated to buy, looking for solutions, struggling to find the right answer. Ask what you're great at that people would pay for unexpectedly ("conversion story"). Being too general appeals to no one; people buy specific solutions.
  2. Sell them what they want; teach them what they need. Validate with Google Keyword Planner; use top-10 related keywords in the product; avoid data-dump products.

## How to Generate Referrals
Referrals are not accidental tips for good work - DESIGN a process or you'll get 5-10x fewer.
- Referrals squeeze more from marketing: your current customer knows the best prospects (Paddy Lund story: make interacting feel like a trusted friend).
- Make referrals part of the relationship from day 1: explain you rely on them and will earn them; plant the seed now.
- Connect during emotional "windows of opportunity" in the transaction; offer something memorable.
- Give customers stories to tell about your products so they refer automatically.
- Thank customers when they do what you asked.

## Affiliate Marketing (no list, no product)
Expect refunds (fact of online business). "Circle of Money": sell affiliate AND own products at the same time.
