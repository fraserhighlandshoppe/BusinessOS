
# Troy White - Maximum Profits In Minimum Time Blueprint

Tags: #TroyWhite #business #marketing

## Premise
No universal guarantee exists - every product serves a unique market. But people buy for similar psychological reasons, so one template can work broadly. Test it: either phenomenal results or feedback on what not to do.

## The Steps
1. **12-month goals in writing** - <5% write goals down. Revenue goal ÷ 12 = monthly; ÷ weekly; ÷ average transaction value = customers needed per week/day (e.g., $100k/yr → $2,083/wk → 22 sales/wk @ $95 → 4.4/day).
2. **Define your customer** - who they are, dreams/desires, fears/regrets.
3. *(Step 3)* Generate interest - lead generation.
4. **Convert interest** - what you do once prospects are interested.
5. **First-use experience** - what happens when they first use your product drives repeat purchase.
8. **Monthly system** - how you do all of this every single month without fail.

## Key Insight
People buy for similar psychological reasons as YOU do - use your own buying psychology to shape marketing.
