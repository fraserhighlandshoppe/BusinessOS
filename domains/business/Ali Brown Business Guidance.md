# Ali Brown - Business Guidance

Tags: #AliBrown #business

## Bootstrapping (started with <$20 in the bank)
"Don't spend money before you make money" - reinvest revenue into the company.
- Save on rent: work from home (dedicated workspace), coffee shop, or library
- **Don't start broke**: keep the job and build part-time until you have clients/prototype; if already out, take fast-paying projects to fund long-term work
- Think ROI on every purchase - expense must tie to bottom line

## The Power of Focus
- Information overload (podcasts/blogs/forums/clubs/telecasts/social) - more available than anyone can digest; evaluate what to do in the next 60-90 days instead
- Small businesses can change faster than big ones - that's the edge
- If floundering, ask "am I focusing on the wrong things?"

## Shake the Tree (revitalizing a stuck business)
You can't keep doing the same thing - do something radical; pruning lets the tree flourish.
- Ask what you've been RESISTING
- Change involves the unknown (scary); self-doubt and buyer's remorse show up just before change
- Make decisions from where you are GOING, not from where you are

## Web Sales Secrets
Your website sells 24x7; copy is the most effective connector with ideal clients. Passive/pretty placeholders get no action. 5 elements needed (engage, compelling headline, clear call to action, speak to ideal client, free offers). Your website is never done - review regularly as clients and products evolve.

## New Rules of Marketing (first date → happily ever after)
People want authenticity and engagement:
1. **Engage your target audience effectively** - WHO is more important than WHAT; multimedia works best
2. **Content is king** - share good relevant content; be a relevant problem solver; customers come back and share
3. **Marketing is queen** - channels: forums, blogs...
