
# Mistakes Grant Makes (by Grant Cardone)

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #mindset

Self-diagnosed failure patterns - audit your own business against these:

- **Doesn't promote enough** - under-marketing is the default failure
- **Targets aren't clear enough**
- **Doesn't think big enough** (10X thinking violation)
- **Fear-based reactions** - e.g., saving too much money (hoarding cash instead of deploying it)
- **Doesn't act fast enough** - waiting for "the great event" instead of acting now
