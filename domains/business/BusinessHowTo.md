## 1. Foundations: What this business does and who it serves

### 1.1 The business model (step by step)
1. **Identify the best customer** (`Grant Cardone Master Your Target Audience.md`; `Eben Pagan Customer Avatar.md`). Build ONE avatar: describe who you WANT as customers (self-fulfilling). Target ~150 data points per avatar.
   - Source: `knowledge_base/gurus/Grant Cardone/` + `knowledge_base/gurus/Eben Pagan/`
2. **Positioning** (`Eben Pagan Marketing Mindsets.md`). Create a new mental category you can be FIRST in. Never fight an established player head-on. Define the promise (brand = emotional response).
   - Source: `knowledge_base/gurus/Eben Pagan/Marketing_Mindsets.md`
3. **Marketing is the key money-maker** (`Eben Pagan Marketing Mindsets.md`; `Grant Cardone Mistakes Grant Makes.md`). Most business activities cost money; marketing makes the most. It is an ongoing PROCESS, not a one-time act.
   - Source: `domains/marketing/` + `gurus/Eben Pagan/`
4. **Build systems before you scale** (`Grant Cardone Unbreakable Business System.md`; `Cardone Ventures Frameworks.md`; `Rapid Scaling Formula.md`). Document SOPs in `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`. If it isn't documented, it doesn't exist.
   - Source: `knowledge_base/gurus/Grant Cardone/` + `knowledge_base/gurus/Cardone Ventures/`
5. **Know your wealth class and path to next** (`Grant Cardone GC Wealth Classes.md`; `GC Wealth Creation Formula.md`). Understand income, expenses, assets, debt, and the 4 I's (Identify, Improve, Invest, Insure).
   - Source: `domains/mindset/GC Wealth Classes.md`

CONFLICT NOTE: Cardone emphasizes rapid 10X growth and aggressive marketing spend; Pagan emphasizes process-first and audience-first before mechanics. RECOMMENDATION: follow Pagan for early-stage (audience + value prop defined first), follow Cardone for scaling (omnipresence + rapid action once systems exist). Both agree on documentation and measurement.

## 3. Operations — Purchasing, Inventory, and SOPs

### 3.1 Purchasing (step by step)
1. **Identify need**: check inventory lifecycle (`domains/operations/sop/Inventory Lifecycle Management.md`).
2. **Create purchase order** (`domains/operations/sop/Create Campaign.md` applies; for purchasing see SOP reference `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`).
3. **Approve**: manager approval for orders > $X (set in SOP).
4. **Receive** (`domains/operations/sop/Receive a Shipment.md`): verify against PO, inspect quality, record in inventory.
5. **Pay supplier** (`domains/operations/sop/Paying a Supplier.md`): match invoice to receipt; schedule payment; record in books.
6. **Update SOP** if process changed.

Source references: `knowledge_base/gurus/Grant Cardone/` (system documentation); `domains/operations/sop/`

### 3.2 Inventory management
1. **Track inventory lifecycle** (`domains/operations/sop/Inventory Lifecycle Management.md`): receive → inspect → store → pick → ship → record.
2. **Transfer inventory** between locations (`domains/operations/sop/Inventory Transfer.md`): document transfer reason, quantity, location, date.
3. **Review monthly**: check for dead stock, reorder points, supplier performance.

### 3.3 SOP structure (every SOP must include)
- Owner (who maintains it)
- Last updated date
- Inputs / outputs / metrics
- Reference to source guru/file (e.g., `Grant Cardone CRM System.md`)

## 4. Marketing: The Unified Marketing Plan

### 4.1 Market research (step by step)
1. **Customer avatar** (`Grant Cardone Master Your Target Audience.md`; `Customer Avatar Guide.md`; `Eben Pagan Marketing Mindsets.md`). Build from best customers' common attributes (~150 data points). Describe who you WANT.
2. **Positioning** (`Eben Pagan Marketing Mindsets.md`; `Branding Fundamentals.md`). Create a new mental category. Define the brand promise (clear, concise, loyalty-building).
3. **Marketing audit** (`MarketingHowTo.md`): list all current marketing activities; measure ROI per activity; eliminate anything with no ROI; redirect dollars to winners.
4. **Competition analysis**: know established players; never fight head-on; find the category gap.

CONFLICT NOTE: Cardone says spend aggressively (up to $1M/month) if conversion tracks; Pagan says spend only after audience/value prop is locked and measurement exists. RECOMMENDATION: follow Pagan for first 90 days; shift to Cardone model once ROI per activity is proven and funnel benchmarks met (5-10% site conversion, 1000 visitors → 50-100 opt-ins → 1-2 sales — `Levis 5R` reference).

### 4.2 Unified marketing plan structure (per DigitalMarketer / Mirasee framework)
Based on: `knowledge_base/gurus/DigitalMarketer/` (Launch & Audience series); `Mirasee Launch and Audience Playbook.md`; `MarketingHowTo.md`.

Plan sections:
1. **Audience definition** (who + niche + value prop) — from Section 4.1.
2. **Distribution channels** (where the audience is): see Section 5 (sub-domains: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Email, Hardcopy, Web/Blog).
3. **Content calendar** (`Troy White CashFlow Calendar.md`): plan promotions 3-6 months ahead; tie to events; track cost and result per event.
4. **Offer creation** (`DigitalMarketer CVO` / lead magnet / tripwire / core offer): freemium must be demand-driven (from FAQs, testimonials, client conversations — `Lead Generation and Conversion Funnel.md`).
5. **Launch mechanics** (`Mirasee Launch.md`): build audience BEFORE launch mechanics; use audience-first approach.
6. **Measurement** (`MarketingHowTo.md`): ROI per channel, funnel benchmarks, lead scoring model.

Source files for unified plan: `knowledge_base/gurus/DigitalMarketer/`; `gurus/Mirasee/`; `gurus/Troy White/`; `knowledge_base/gurus/Eben Pagan/`

## 5. Marketing Sub-Domains: Step-by-Step Execution

Each sub-domain below has: setup steps; content rules; measurement; source file references; and any guru conflicts.

### 5.1 Facebook Marketing
- Setup: business page; fit content to platform purpose (friends, sharing, boredom — `Social Media Platform Strategies.md`); likes = opt-ins (`Facebook Strategy.md`).
- Content: hook → before/after story → CTA (`SocialMediaHowTo.md`). Use swipe formulas (`swipe/headline_formulas.md`).
- Timing: half-life ~90 min; post when audience active.
- Marketplace (`Facebook Marketplace Strategy.md`): list products/services; clear pricing; image specs (`Social Media Image Size Guide.md`).
- Conflicts: Cardone says omnipresent (team, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, email, website, live events); Dubeau says YouTube builds KLT at scale. RECOMMENDATION: use both — Facebook for community + opt-ins, YouTube for authority + reach.

### 5.2 Instagram
- Setup: profile optimized with brand promise; bio includes CTA + link to landing page.
- Content: images/video; dimensions per `Social Media Image Size Guide.md`; brand colors consistent.
- Posts: off-hours timing (`Social Media Platform Strategies.md`); link to long-form assets (swipe/salesletter skeleton).
- Stories/Reels: use same hook/story/CTA structure; test 2-5 variants.

### 5.3 YouTube / Video
- Setup: keywords in title + spoken 2-3x (`Social Media Platform Strategies.md`); standalone blog post embedding video; email list drives traffic.
- Content: video = KLT builder at scale (`Dave Dubeau Web TV.md`). Build 1-to-many relationships.
- Production: script using Hero's Journey (`swipe/salesletter_formulas.md`); proof/trials included; CTA to lead magnet or tripwire.

### 5.4 Email Marketing
- Setup: deliverability (DMARC/SPF/DKIM/TLS — `Email Marketing Playbook.md`); segments (`Segments.md`); automation (`Automation.md`).
- Welcome series (`Welcome Series.md`): first touch defines expectations; personalized greeting; deliver value; no apology.
- Follow-up (`Email Followup.md`, `The Simple Email Trick...`): specific subject line; never apologize; do homework.
- Newsletter (`Newsletter Strategy.md`, `Amplify Your Email Marketing.md`): consistent cadence; single focus; mix of story + offer.
- Cold email (`Cold Email Marketing.md`): shorter; highly personalized; single clear ask; no attachments.
- Referral mechanics (`Joanna Lindenbaum Referral Shame Piece.md`): separate self-worth from referral volume; audit the ask (words, energy, timing, continuity); build quarterly "Referral Events" rather than ad-hoc asks.

### 5.5 Hardcopy / Direct Mail
- CashFlow Calendar (`Troy White CashFlow Calendar.md`): mail 7-10 business days pre-offer (14 cross-border); track cost + result per event; plan 3-6 months ahead.
- Lumpy mail: tie promotions to local/national events; use templates from swipe files.

### 5.6 Web Pages & Blog Posts
- Landing page (`Landing Page Checklist.md`): headline (swipe/headline_formulas.md), subheadline, proof, single CTA, no navigation, mobile-optimized.
- Blog post: standalone post embedding YouTube video (`Social Media Platform Strategies.md`); keyword-optimized; CTA to email capture.
- Checkout (`Checkout Page Essentials.md`): trust badges, single-page checkout, clear pricing, guarantee, urgency if appropriate.
- Benchmarks (`Levis 5R` reference): average site converts 5-10% of visitors.

### 5.7 Content Production Workflow
1. Define audience and goal (`MarketingHowTo.md`).
2. Choose channel (sub-domain above) and format.
3. Write using swipe formulas (`swipe/headline_formulas.md` for headlines; `swipe/salesletter_formulas.md` for long copy/story).
4. Fill variables from `swipe/swipe_verb.txt`, `noun.txt`, `adjective.txt`, `number.txt`, `audience.txt`.
5. Test 2-5 headline variants; test image specs.
6. Publish; measure ROI; redirect if no ROI.

## 6. Sales Operations

### 6.1 The sales process
Based on: `domains/sales/` + `gurus/Grant Cardone/` + `gurus/Jim Camp/`

Step by step:
1. **Prospecting** (`Grant Cardone Cold Prospecting.md`; `Master the Cold Call.md`; `Jim Camp Sales Techniques.md`). Use frequency + preparation; never blame circumstances.
2. **Lead response** (`Grant Cardone Lead Response Strategy.md`): respond fast; have a script; never apologize; personalize.
3. **Presentation** (`Grant Cardone Presentation.md`; `Perfect Sales System.md`): structure = ordinary world → call → refusal (objections) → mentor (you) → threshold (micro-yes) → trials (proof) → abyss (agitate cost of inaction) → transformation → elixir (offer) — Hero's Journey (`swipe/salesletter_formulas.md`).
4. **Objection handling** (`Grant Cardone Objection Handling.md`; `Objection Handling and Stuck Deals.md`): address objections as part of the story; use proof; never argue.
5. **Closing** (`Grant Cardone Requirements to Close.md`; `Zig Ziglar Closing the Sale.md`): have clear requirements; ask directly; handle final objections; confirm agreement.
6. **Post-sale** (`Keep Your Sales Simple.md`): follow up; deliver; request testimonial (`GettingtheBestTestimonialsFromClients.md` — wiki reference); ask for referral (`Joanna Lindenbaum` mechanics).

CONFLICT NOTE: Cardone emphasizes aggressive follow-up and high-frequency contact; Ziglar emphasizes closing techniques and agreement; Camp emphasizes negotiation and not giving away value. RECOMMENDATION: use Cardone for prospecting/frequency; Ziglar for closing/agreement; Camp for negotiation; all agree on preparation and documentation.

## 9. Conflict Resolution Between Gurus

When sources conflict, this guide presents both with attribution:

- **Marketing spend** (`Cardone` vs `Pagan`): Cardone = spend aggressively if conversion tracks; Pagan = spend only after audience/value prop defined and measurement exists. Resolution: follow Pagan first 90 days; shift to Cardone once ROI proven.
- **Scaling speed** (`Rapid Scaling Formula.md` vs `Pagan Launch.md`): rapid scaling requires capital + team + process; launch requires audience first. Resolution: build audience (Pagan), then scale rapidly (Cardone) once benchmarks met.
- **Sales approach** (`Cardone` aggressive frequency vs `Camp` negotiation discipline): use both — frequency for prospecting, negotiation for closing.
- **Brand** (`Singer` 3 adjectives + `Cardone` promise): both agree brand = emotional response = promise; Singer adds the 3-adjective test (`Branding Fundamentals.md`). Resolution: combine.

Every conflict note above links back to the source `.md` files in `knowledge_base/gurus/` or `domains/`.
