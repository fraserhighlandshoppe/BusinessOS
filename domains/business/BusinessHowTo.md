## 1. Foundations

- Marketing is the key money-maker; everything else costs. Keep marketing funded first.
- Build systems that scale: documentation, SOPs, repeatable processes (Cardone Ventures, FHS index).
- Know your absolute best customer profile before scaling (Cardone, Pagan).
- Brand = promise = emotional response; fix it systematically.

## 2. Growth & Scale

- Scale vs Scaling: understand the difference; rapid scaling requires capital, team, and process readiness (`Scale vs Scaling.md`, `Rapid Scaling Formula.md`).
- CashFlow Calendar for promotions (Troy White); plan 3-6 months ahead (`Troy White Maximum Profits Blueprint.md`).
- Launch audience before mechanics (Mirasee); value prop + niche defined first (`Launch 2021 Coaching Program.md`).

## 3. Leadership & Team

- Leadership framework: competence + presence + communication (`Grant Cardone How to be a Leader.md`).
- High performance culture: clear expectations, measurement, accountability (`Leadership and High Performance.md`, `Unbreakable Business Challenge.md`).
- Build the team before you need it; document roles (FHS wiki SOPs).

## 4. Systems & Operations

- CRM and tracking: point-based lead scoring, handoff rules, cost/result per event (`Grant Cardone CRM System.md`, `Cardone Ventures Frameworks.md`).
- Authority figure positioning: content + presence + social proof (`The Authority Figure.md`).
- Business quadrants and exit planning: build to sell, not just to run (`Cardone Ventures Business Quadrants.md`, `Grant Cardone Exiting the Business.md`).
