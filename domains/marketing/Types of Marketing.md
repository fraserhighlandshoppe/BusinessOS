# Types of Marketing

Tags: #GrantCardone #marketing

- **Awareness campaigns** - build familiarity
- **Straight promotion** - ads run even when conversion isn't expected, to get people thinking
- **List building** - capture and grow contact lists
- **Collaborative marketing** - grab someone else's audience; everyone should find collaboration partners, even competitors
- **Advertising**
- **Brand building** - decide that EVERYONE will know you
- **Testimonials**

## Related wisdom
People buy from people they like and trust, at a good price - ask "what's in it for them?" in every part of the marketing ecosystem.

## Digital mistakes to avoid (Tai Lopez at 10X Growth Con)
Put best content above the fold; fast websites (speed test); add a video explaining why work with you (longer video for complex products).

## Prospect focus (Sara Blakely)
Focus relentlessly on the person across the table - their words, tone, body language. Communicate fast what's in it for them and why you deliver it best.
