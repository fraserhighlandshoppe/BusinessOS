# Lead Generation & Conversion Funnel

Tags: #marketing #leads #conversion

## The Funnel
Every business has one - the process to acquire leads, convert them to buyers, and get buyers to buy more/often:
1. Pay for ads
2. Landing page captures email
3. Freebie/training (e.g., webinar)
4. Email follow-up
5. (optional) FB group
6. Book a call
7. Conversation/close

## Lead Magnet
- Must be **FREEMIUM, not free crap** — people remember their FIRST and MOST RECENT interactions with you; a cheap gift sets a cheap relationship tone.
- Offer what's in DEMAND, not what's convenient. Idea sources: FAQs, customer testimonials, popular activities, what clients talk about most.

## Squeeze Pages
Purpose: capture name+email opt-in for permission-based follow-up. Tactics: RSS feed on page; mirror your membership-home look so insiders feel at home; surface your affiliate program link early.

## Lead Nurturing
Periodically reaching qualified but "not ready to buy" prospects with contextually relevant communication (vs. the useless "ready to give me your money yet?" check-ins). Automation solves content-noise by personalizing.

## Lead Scoring
Assign point values to lead actions/data (web hits, email opens, title=CIO, whitepaper downloads); scores accrue and trigger automated CRM actions. Marketing should OWN the model (score determines marketing→sales handoff), built jointly with sales. Marketing automation without scoring is just souped-up email.

## 5R Web Conversion System (Daniel Levis)
Average site converts 5-10% of visitors: 1000 visitors → 50-100 opt-ins → 1-2 sales.
- **Right person**: be perceived as a person, not a faceless corporate brochure — use strategic images of yourself dressed for the target market.
