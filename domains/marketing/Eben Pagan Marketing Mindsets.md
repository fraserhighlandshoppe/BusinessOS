# Eben Pagan - Marketing Mindsets & Conversion Psychology

Tags: #EbenPagan #marketing

## 12 Key Marketing Mindsets
1. **Marketing is the key money-maker** - most activities cost money; of those that make money, marketing makes the most.
2. **Marketing is an ongoing process**, not a one-time act.
3. It begins with understanding customer needs/wants - not advertising or a website.
4. Customers hate to be sold but LOVE to buy - give them what they need to convince themselves.
5. Marketing triggers ACTION ("customer getting") - appeal to the lizard/chimpanzee brain ethically, only in the prospect's best interest.
6. **Everything is a test** - test fast; most things don't work; collect what works into a funnel. Expecting success compounds failure instead of success.
7. Run marketing like a business: measure it, verify it works - not by intuition.
8. Understand your prospect better than they understand themselves (desires, fears, fantasies, their exact words) → deep relationships → multiple products.
9. Successful marketing = system + sequence with consistent experience (learning → why buy → upsells).
10. Marketing success ∝ perceived value created - translate value, connect the dots, show exactly how you solve the problem.
11. Identity: "I'm a marketer, and I own a business" - not just a business owner.
12. Find customers where they are.

## Psychology of Conversion
- Conversion = convincing the prospect to take action; decisions are immobilizing for most people.
- Every communication needs a sales/marketing component; content and marketing are merging.
- **Long copy sells better than short** (counter-intuitive): tell the whole story unless impulse-buy. Motivated buyers read it all.
- People decide subconsciously on EMOTION, then justify logically afterward.
- Focus on highest-leverage points early in the funnel.

## Professional Sales (read Spin Selling)
- Professional sales is a valuable service: helping people get what they want. Not high-pressure.
- Don't override objections, don't pressure, find needs first.
- **Most objections are caused by the salesperson** pitching features before the customer is ready.
- Ask about problems/frustrations, what motivates the search for a solution, and the implications of their problem.

## 7 Critical Keys to Business Success
1. Right mindset - don't borrow when avoidable; make profit, not just activity
2. Right niche - most people don't want to buy most things
3. Right product/service - customers want a RESULT / pain relief, not your product
4. Right marketing - don't copy big companies today; copy what they did as startups. Response marketing over brand marketing; power words
5. Right focus - business is a distraction machine
6. Right actions - very few activities make money; most distractions appear urgent
7. Right people - success is mobilizing customers, employees, partners

Plus: no such thing as time management - only priority and energy management.
