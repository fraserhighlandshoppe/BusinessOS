# Grant Cardone - Master Your Target Audience

Tags: #GrantCardone #marketing #targeting

## Product & Funnel Front-End
Build a product so robust customers have no choice but to continue. Give so much value on the front end that the customer goes deeper into the funnel. It must deliver - fail once and they never buy again. Value builds trust.

## Marketing Essentials Checklist
Know what's missing in your marketing strategy, how to execute, and what resources you need:
- target audience, data, CRM, content strategy, content calendar, platforms, podcast, YouTube channel

## Know Your Audience (Michael Jackson principle)
Jackson listened to his own mixes through a transistor radio because that's how his audience heard them. Know the medium and context of YOUR audience.

## Build Your Target Audience
- Most people placate the audience they have rather than build the one they don't.
- Make it easy for customers; they'll hand out your contact info.
- Collect contact info -> build relationship -> retention.
- Flow: **flow -> conversion -> retention**. Retaining customers is cheaper than gaining new ones.

## Define Goals and ROI
Without goals all tactics are pointless. Measure every activity; if no ROI, redirect those dollars/hours/effort.

## Align With Your Absolute Best Customer
Pinpoint your single best customer; marketing misaligned with that profile is waste. Spending money reminds yourself you're in business, then employees, then competition, then picks up new customers - budget for each.

## Get Personal: 150 Data Points
Aim for ~150 data points per customer: favourite TV show, food, holidays, age, gender, marital status, kids, location (to zip code), interests, likes/dislikes, values, culture, hobbies. Deals are easier with something in common. Gather over time via surveys.
