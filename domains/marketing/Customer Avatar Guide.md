# Customer Avatar Guide

Tags: #marketing #customeravatar

## Why use a customer avatar
Collected info starts broad and partly assumption-based - get it ALL on one page where you can see it and begin testing assumptions. Use data you can validate; gut-only avatars miss targeting and cost time/money.

Fill out the worksheet describing your BEST customers - the people you WANT to sell to. Building an avatar is a self-fulfilling prophecy: you're actively choosing who you work with, so describe who you want to attract, not only who you've sold to.

## Creating your avatar (Get Altitude)
- "Be them": see the world through the customer's eyes - Keats' *negative capability*, shutting down your own perspective to feel what they feel.
- Customers want delivery FROM pain or delivery TO enjoyment; that want is a currency - translate it into your offer's currency (Drucker: "The aim of marketing is to know and understand the customer so well that the product fits them and sells itself").
- Project ONE persona from the common attributes of your complete customer set - then you can talk about them intelligently.
