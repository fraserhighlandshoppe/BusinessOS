# Dan Kennedy - Marketing Wisdom

Tags: #DanKennedy #marketing #sales

## Market Yourself Every Single Day
Do at least ONE thing daily to prod, poke and rustle up business: call customers you haven't heard from, forward a helpful article to a prospect, 15 minutes of social conversation with customers/prospects, send Giorgio Letters (Magnetic Marketing system).

## Do More and Different Stuff
"Kick up such a storm around you that you start feeling in peril of not being able to handle it all." Add something NEW each year (direct mail if all-online; speaking engagements; press releases) and give it a fair test.

## Put Everything in a "Financial Frame"
Define the marketing goal of each piece and package accordingly — e.g., same book, different formats: hardcover for shock-and-awe packages, softcover for bookstores, big format to sell from the speaking-stage front-of-room. Review every campaign so details match goals and audience.

## Avoid "Super Bowl Marketing Thinking"
Don't concentrate marketing resources into one event/month/season — spread it daily across the year.

## Sales Presentation Formula
1. **Promise statement** - attention-commanding headline + opening sentence; strong specific benefits promised
2. **Credibility building**
3. **Agreement with a problem** - Problem/Solution formula: mutual pain → mutual understanding → emotional angst → be the white knight with the solution
4. **Big global promise** of the solution in 3 big steps
5. Tell story; get people to PICTURE the solution (memory in pictures is stronger)

## Recession Survival Guide
Acknowledge threats; acquire financially strong customers unfazed by winter chills; look for opportunity in others' adversity.
