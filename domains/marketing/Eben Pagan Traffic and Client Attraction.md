# Eben Pagan - Traffic & Client Attraction

Tags: #EbenPagan #marketing #clients

## Marketing Starting Point
Map your market's **fears & frustrations** and **wants & aspirations**. Pick the 2 most powerful fears + 1 strongest aspiration, then run each through three motivation layers (David McClelland, Harvard) with toward/away framing:
- **Power**: authority, influence, control / loss of power
- **Affiliation**: love, friendship, acceptance / rejection
- **Achievement**: goals, accomplishment, progress / failure

## Features → Advantages → Benefits
Prospects don't know/like/trust you yet - they care only about the RESULT they want. Your product "comes between" them and it.
Feature = what it is | Advantage = what it does | Benefit = what they experience.
Example: A/C → cools the car → comfort on a hot day. List 5+ benefits; focus on irrational emotional hot-buttons including pain avoidance.

## The Client-Getting Model (coaching)
1. **Demonstrate** - give a taste of the outcome via a free "Conversation Starter" (report/guide/audio/checklist) containing authority content, social proof, clear next steps. Share free to prospects and referral partners.
2. **Discover** - filter who you can help and how; repels bad fits.
3. **Deliver** - do what you promised.

You don't need big budgets or fancy tech to start.

## Qualified vs Unqualified Clients
Qualified = has a specific need/outcome + willing to invest money to get it.
Unqualified warning signs: bargains before signing, 10pm "emergency" emails, scope-creep requests, demands without paying. You can't lead someone who isn't ready to follow - set standards and boundaries; unqualified leads waste time and joy.

## Web Design for Conversion
- Everyone trained by Facebook to ignore the right-hand side - nothing important there
- Images get more views; design must be welcoming, simple, uncrowded - supportive of copy, never an attention mis-director
- Black text on white background; strong contrast; avoid busy graphics
- Scannable short segments, plenty of white space; no horizontal scrolling; set page width inside typical window so readers see borders and feel they've seen everything (mobile width too)

## Value Difference (why people pay more for same core service)
Value differences come from expertise/service areas: exclusivity, access, guarantee - not the core item. If you enter via lowest price, race to switch that client from price-orientation to value-orientation before someone undercuts you. Compare apples to oranges (Ultimate Chopper: "4 machines in 1").

## Virtual Marketing Tips
1. Humans behind your numbers - every visitor is a person with wounds and hopes; talk to them as individuals
2. Get customers to come to YOU - Dean Jackson: direct response isn't getting your name out there, it's getting THEIR name in here (Drucker: business = marketing + innovation)
3. Find what drives customers - survival, sex, social status underlie decisions; find the one appeal that works best
4. Build the sales conversion process live first (phone/video/in-person) → live chat → email templates handling objections/fears/benefits
5. Automate only after manual mastery (autoresponders)
6. Keep it personal - script relationship-building from hello to trust-to-buy
