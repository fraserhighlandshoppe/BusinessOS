# Blair Singer - Branding

Tags: #BlairSinger #branding

## The Complicated Brand Problem
Insurance-style "if you're breathing, you can be a client" = trying to be everything to everyone. **The key to failure is trying to please everyone.** Define your target market and what those customers actually want; remove the complication.

## Brand Definition Questions
- What's your elevator speech? (30 seconds on what you do, why you're different, why you're passionate)
- What makes you different from competitors? (everyone claims great service or low prices)
- What THREE adjectives should be synonymous with your brand? (e.g., attentive, creative, funny)
- What are your core values? (honesty, integrity, service, fun, distinctive...)
- Which three vendors would you align with - do your adjectives align with their values?

Always answer with your target market in mind. **You ARE your brand** - live it in every aspect: client meetings, Twitter, Facebook, networking. The experience is what vendors and clients remember.
