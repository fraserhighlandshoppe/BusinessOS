# Troy White - CashFlow Calendar System

Tags: #TroyWhite #marketing #promotions

## The System
A month-by-month marketing & promotional calendar with thousands of unique promotion ideas. Schedule promotions **3-6 months in advance** and always READ AHEAD (production time for writing, printing, stuffing, mailing).

## Timing Rules
- Prepare mailing materials at least **30 days** before you want delivery
- Mail **7-10 business days** before offer begins (**14+ business days** cross-border Canada/US)
- Early beats late - never let the package arrive after the deadline

## Promotion Sourcing
- Tie into local community events (White built his Wild West Wealth Summit around the Calgary Stampede), national events, and anything media-worthy
- Declare your own holiday: unusual celebrations + company anniversaries
- Never let a marketing opportunity pass

## Lumpy Mail / Envelope Techniques
Get through the clutter so it MUST be opened:
- Live stamps (multiples, special editions, crooked/upside-down), blue ink
- Blind approach: no company name, just return address; FedEx/UPS-style or phony courier envelopes; placemats, brown paper bags
- "Lumpy mail" (3D grabber inserts) is nearly impossible to throw out unopened - curiosity factor
- Inside must be congruent with outside ("Confidential" inside and out) - avoid the conned feeling

## Tracking
Track marketing cost AND results per event; review to see which promotions were productive and feed future planning.
