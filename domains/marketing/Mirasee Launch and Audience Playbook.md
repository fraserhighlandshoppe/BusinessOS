# Mirasee Small-Business Launch & Audience Playbook

Tags: #marketing #launch #DannyIny

## Big Results From a Small Launch (Kathryn Aragon)
Every business starts at ZERO audience. Get foundations right first: who you serve, the niche, value proposition, and the marketing pieces that put your offer in front of the target audience.
Case: Firepole Marketing (2010) launched with no readers, no subscribers, no industry relationships — within a year it was a household name in internet marketing. The missing foundational piece in their first launch plan was AUDIENCE BUILDING; Danny backed up and fixed that before relaunching. Lesson: audience before launch mechanics.
