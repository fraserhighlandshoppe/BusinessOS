# Money Follows Attention

Tags: #GrantCardone #marketing #attention

- **Who knows you is more important than who you know.**
- Strangers have everything you want - learn to get, keep, and multiply their attention.
- It's impossible to spend too much on marketing/advertising; almost any business problem can be solved with attention.
- Cardone spent $1M/month on ads; income rose in correlation with ad spend, topping $55M/month in 2018.
- People don't invest in marketing because: trained not to, goals too small, or don't know how to convert marketing into sales.

## Related
"If you don't know my name, shame on me" - it is YOUR responsibility to ensure people know you.
