# Marketing Step by Step (Eben Pagan)

Tags: #EbenPagan #marketing

Full program covers: intro to marketing, why we think/feel/behave as we do, what customers really buy, discovering needs, customer avatar, branding vs response marketing, marketing as education, naming, positioning, stair-step, free line, headline formula, conversion story, copy that sells, asking for action, design workshop, partnerships.

## Stair-Step Marketing
"Don't ask her to marry you on the first date" - don't ask for the sale in your ad's headline. Offer value, information, solutions. Marketing should feel like building a friendship, not dealing with a corporate entity. Optimize the system for ONE person (your Customer Avatar) first; many small steps compound; then open the funnel to scale.

## Move the Free Line
Distribution online now costs ~$0 - so give away something of REAL value. The highest-leverage giveaway is content that educates your customer: free reports, videos, webinars, consultations. Create a package worth at least $100 and give it to as many people as possible. Your inner chimp resists giving-to-get; ignore it.

## Positioning - Own Your Mental Niche
Positioning is played in the MIND, not in reality. Don't position by comparing products - create a NEW mental category you can be FIRST in. Minds use categories as shortcut decision-makers; roughly-equal options get "thrown away". Competing with an established player is a lost game - they're already in the mind. Start from an aspect of the customer's need that is critically important.

## Marketing as Education
Most people with a problem have little education about it or its solutions ("quiet desperation"). Educate while marketing - helps them see AND solve problems, building credibility/trust essential to action. Content Marketing = ultimate education marketing, but beware the content trap: best content marketing still uses all direct-response power tools - don't skip selling.

## The Headline Formula
Headline = "ad for the ad". People skim headlines; weak headline = skipped content. Invest as much time in headlines/subheads/bullets as body copy. Use high-emotion words ("Lawyer" beats "Attorney"); use YOUR prospects' words. Promise a result, benefit, or relief.
