# Using Web TV to Position Yourself (Dave Dubeau)

Tags: #DaveDubeau #marketing #video

## Why Video
- **KLT: Know, Like, Trust** - people buy from those they know, like and trust; you won't attract investors/clients without it.
- Total strangers who watch your shows "know" you - video builds relationships at scale.
- Extends the lifetime of client relationships; enables 1-to-many relationship building.
- Easy, free, little-to-no investment.
