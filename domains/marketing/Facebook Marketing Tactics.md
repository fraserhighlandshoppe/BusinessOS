# Facebook Marketing Tactics

Tags: #marketing #facebook #socialmedia

Facebook campaigns cannot be run like AdWords campaigns:
- Pages take minutes to build (no FTP); unlimited traffic for under a penny; reach millions in any niche with little competition
- Communicating with your list takes only a photo or YouTube video; responses within minutes
- Your audience is spending hours on FB already

## Psychology difference
Google = launch pad (people searching for a specific item). **Facebook = destination** - people come to hang out. Why people are there: connect with friends/family, share news/photos, fight boredom. Create offers and communication that fit those motives.

## Mechanics
- When a fan clicks Like, they are opting into a list
- Facebook gives multiple methods to contact that list
