# Branding Fundamentals

Tags: #marketing #branding

## What is a Brand?
An identity that stimulates a meaningful EMOTIONAL response about the qualities/values you stand for. In corporate context: the feelings and interactions a customer has with the company. Others assign your brand — it's in evidence at every interaction (internal and external).

**Your brand is a promise**: clear (in your mind AND customers'), concise (wordy = forgettable), builds loyalty and trust.

## Determining Your Brand
Skills + assets + experiences + ideal customers' needs + differentiation from competition. Be SPECIFIC — niche the focus down: "Customer Service" → "for automobiles" → "for foreign-built automobiles". Use the Elevator Pitch template to narrow focus.

## Personal Brand Formula
**Competence + Appearance/Presence + Communication = Personal Brand**
- Competence: knowledge, critical thinking
- Appearance: dress, presence
- It's a gut feeling (first impression and/or long term) = interactions + feelings

## Emotional Branding
People-economy: the customer holds power. Engagement → Emotions → Experience. Focus on the most compelling aspects of human character; deliver emotional fulfilment ("we're there for them no matter what"). Understand buying-for-need vs buying-for-emotion.

## Using Your Brand (unfair advantage)
- Play to WIN, not to "not lose". Organize the business around the brand promise.
- ~5% of customers will try to take dishonest advantage of your promise.
- **UPS finding**: fix a customer's problem well and they become 6% MORE loyal than customers who never had an issue. Standing behind claims makes you stand out.

## Launching Your Brand
To impact people you must know where they hang out: content distribution + creation, traffic generation, platform choice, and a place for people to reach/buy.
