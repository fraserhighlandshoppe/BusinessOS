# Finance Operations Manual — Here's How To Run It

*Shareholder / C-Suite Finance Guide for Fraser Highland Shoppe.*
*Step by step so a new hire or AI agent can manage AR, AP, loans, and reporting.*

Tags: #finance #operations #howto #masterguide

---

## How to use
1. Read Section 1 (foundations) for the financial framework.
2. Read Sections 2–4 for daily/weekly/monthly processes.
3. All steps reference source `.md` files or SOPs (`domains/operations/sop/`).

---

## 1. Financial Foundations

Based on `Grant Cardone GC Wealth Principles.md`, `GC Wealth Creation Formula.md`, `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`.

- Wealth = compound + time + rate. Understand current wealth class; define path to next class.
- 4 I's (`GC Income - 4 I's to Increase Income.md`): Identify income sources; Improve them; Invest surplus; Insure/protect assets.
- Separate business and personal accounts (`GC Money Rules.md`, `GC Debt.md`).
- Document everything (`FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`).

---

## 2. Accounts Receivable (AR) — Step by Step

Per `domains/operations/sop/` (Customer Payment, Invoicing):

1. Create invoice (`FrontAccounting`).
2. Send to customer (email or portal).
3. Record due date; schedule reminder at day 7.
4. At day 30: follow up (phone/email); document response.
5. At day 45: escalate to management; consider collection action.
6. Once paid: match payment to invoice; update books; close invoice.

Metrics: DSO; collection rate; overdue percentage.

---

## 3. Accounts Payable (AP) — Step by Step

Per `domains/operations/sop/` (Purchase Order, Supplier Invoice, Paying Supplier):

1. Create purchase order (`FA Purchase Order.md` or SOP).
2. Receive goods/services; inspect; match to PO.
3. Receive supplier invoice (`FA Invoicing Supplier.md`); match to receipt.
4. Approve for payment (manager approval for orders > $X).
5. Schedule payment (`FA Paying Supplier.md`); record in books.
6. Confirm supplier receipt; archive records.

Metrics: DPO; supplier performance (quality, delivery, price); cost per event.

---

## 4. Loans & Debt Management

Based on `Grant Cardone GC Debt.md`, `GC Money Principles.md`, `GC Wealth Principles.md`:

1. List all loans: principal, rate, term, monthly payment.
2. Ensure payments on time; set calendar reminders.
3. Review refinancing options quarterly.
4. Separate personal and business debt.
5. Track debt-to-income ratio; maintain reserve before additional borrowing.
6. Document loan terms in `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md` (SOP / reference).

---

## 5. Cash Flow & Reporting

Based on `Troy White CashFlow Calendar.md`, `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`:

- **Monthly reports** (`FHS 0 Monthly Process.md`): P&L, balance sheet, cash flow statement, AR aging, AP aging, inventory valuation.
- **CashFlow Calendar**: plan promotions and cash needs 3-6 months ahead; track cost and result per event; redirect if no ROI.
- **Event tracking**: every promotion/event has: planned cost, actual cost, revenue generated, ROI, notes for next time.
