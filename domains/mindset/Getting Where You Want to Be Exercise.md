# Getting Where You Want to Be Exercise

Tags: #GrantCardone #money #mindset

Starting point: **wherever you are is where you start from.**

The exercise:
1. What is your current financial class?
2. List monthly finances: income, expenses, assets, liabilities
3. Who influenced your ideas about money? What did they teach you? How much did THEY have?
4. How has that shaped how you view money?
5. What opportunities have you missed because your finances aren't where you want them?
6. What is your target income? (Big enough to be meaningful - it's your IDEAL goal, not where you are)
7. What would you invest to reach it? What would you GIVE UP (habits, people, spending)?

You must make space for change - that means giving something up.
- Have a target (it should scare you)
- Get more money
- Time is the control of money
