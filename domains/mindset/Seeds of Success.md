# Seeds of Success (Eben Pagan)

Tags: #EbenPagan #mindset #productivity

## The Wealth Formula
- **Compounding growth** is the miracle: 1→2→4→8→16. Hitch your wagon to it.
- Success is 50/50 skill and chance (like poker - control your bets, not the cards). Take advantage of lucky breaks.
- Good Fortune ≠ luck: luck is random; Good Fortune visits everyone occasionally - PREPARE for it.
- When most people get resources they spend them immediately ("the discipline of stock trading is the discipline of NOT trading").
- **80/20 Pareto**: 80% of results come from 20% of effort - and vice versa. Pigeon experiment: we press both buttons instead of focusing where reward is highest. Wealth requires focusing on smallest-effort-biggest-reward.

## Focusing Blocks of Productivity
Light bulb vs laser beam: same wattage, one lights a room, the other cuts steel.
- Productivity = results, not work: strength focused in uninterrupted time-blocks, on your biggest opportunity, consistently.
- Start with 1 pure-focus hour (timer on, no email/voicemail); build to 2 hours, then full days.
- After distraction it takes ~15 minutes to regain focus depth - distracted every 15 min = busy but nothing significant.
- Turn off internet if needed. Clean focus, clean rejuvenation, clean cuts between them.
