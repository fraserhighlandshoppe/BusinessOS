# Grant Cardone - Procrastination Tips

Tags: #GrantCardone #productivity #mindset

1. **Know how to prioritize** - Complete what matters most first.
2. **Execute decisions quickly** - The gap between thought and reality is what moves business. Act as soon as the decision is made. Military method: Observe -> Orientate -> Decide -> Act (OODA).
3. **Speed equals power** - Focus on short tasks that move you forward; don't mistake speed for importance.
4. **Don't let fear hold you back.**
