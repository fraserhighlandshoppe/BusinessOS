# Self Made Wealth (Eben Pagan)

Tags: #EbenPagan #money #wealth

## Money Psychology Foundations
- **Write down every money worry** (fears, frustrations, spinning thoughts), star the top 3-5, then convert each into a HOW question ("I can't afford college" → "How do I fund my kid's college?").
- **Your money story**: first impressions, parent discussions, conflicts. If a story happens enough it becomes reality and filters your life; reflect on how current situations were shaped by it - that's leverage.
- **Feelings about the wealthy**: you'll never become successful while projecting negative feelings onto wealthy people (you'd avoid success to avoid those feelings). Hawaiian tradition: *"Bless that which you want"* - list 5-10 wealthy people and genuinely bless them; later, bless yourself so you feel permission to succeed.

## Becoming a Financial Adult
- Conspicuous consumption = buying things we don't want to impress people we don't like.
- Consumerism is engineered: big companies profit when you feel insecure and self-express via purchases. It doesn't work and robs your future.
- A job (vs a craft or working for yourself) is precarious; release "the job" as a paradigm - the world owes you nothing.
- Grow into someone others seek knowledge from.

## The Wealth Game
1. Create something new with value
2. Create assets - things that grow value over time
3. Clear dead weight: unused kitchen stuff, unworn clothes, excess possessions/hobbies; clean desk/bedroom - making space directs your life.

## Self Image & Goals
- Avoid fixed identity statements ("I am...", "the world is...") → say "I am-ing"; see yourself as a growing person consciously constructing your world.
- Prefer **mastery goals** (learn a skill you can repeat) over pure achievement goals ($100k, buy car).

## Practice
Give one thing of value away free daily and help someone implement it; notice your justice mechanism and confabulations that argue against it.
