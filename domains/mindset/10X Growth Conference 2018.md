# 10X Growth Conference 2018 - Speaker Takeaways

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #mindset #conference

## 10X Side Effects (humour list)
Sleeplessness from excitement; no longer content with average friends; massive production streaks; criticism/hate/envy from others; new network, problems, challenges; abnormal energy; big tax bills.

## Grant Cardone
- Be great - nothing else pays. The middle class is doomed. Be obsessed or be average.
- It is your duty to succeed. Set goals so big your problems pale in comparison.
- Dominate, don't compete - own the space. You are either building an empire or destroying one.
- Never fear the haters; only fear those who listen to them. The ambitious are criticized by those who quit.
- "Who's got my money?" Money is to be used, not saved. Never take advice from a quitter.
- Pay the price now so you can pay any price later. Most people starve between idea and execution.
- Decision: EXPAND or CONTRACT. Sales is an expansion activity (follow-up calls, cold calls).
- You fail at 10X because you don't sell enough (volume) products with high enough margin.

## Gary Cardone
- Globalized marketplace is a firehose; most lack the spiritual, intellectual and financial capital (in that order) to do it alone.
- Fear suppresses dreaming. Learn from great people then do what they do. Continuous training - envision the outcome.
- Burn the bridge behind you intentionally: forward only.

## Naveen Jain
- No problem in the world can't be solved; believe impossible and it becomes impossible only for you.
- Scarcity mindset limits action. Pay forward - helpers don't need your help.
- If nobody calls your idea crazy, you aren't thinking big enough.
- Don't enumerate problems up front or you never start; cross the bridge then burn it.
- Selling creates buyer's remorse; educate instead so they buy for themselves.
- Humans fail, entrepreneurs pivot. Look for root causes, not symptoms.

## Daymond John (brand)
- Everyone is a brand; everything affects it: associates, walk, talk, dress, work, posts ("a digital tattoo").
- Clients hire you for expertise + like + trust + inspiration.
- Know your customer until you're relatable; if you know where they drive, put up a billboard.

## Jarrod Glandt
- Good attitude and effort alone aren't enough; consume information daily; join accountability groups.
- Push of sales must align with pull of marketing.

## Anik Singal (lead funnel demo)
- Simple 2-page funnel: free-report PDF -> opt-in page -> scheduling app link; email platform (Sendlane); affiliate offers okay.
- Create easily/quickly/automatically, then qualify.
