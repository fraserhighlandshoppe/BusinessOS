# 10X Growth Conference 2021 - Speaker Takeaways

Tags: #GrantCardone #business #mindset #conference

## Jesse Itzler - 10 Tips
1. Word of the day: **soul** - put your soul into what you do
2. More isn't better, better is better - fewer, bigger, better goals
3. Go where the action is; show up and make it happen
4. Evening routines planning the next day
5. Set goals by weeks, not days
6. The 3 Cs: Compliment, Congratulate, Console
7. One job: stay healthy
8. 3-minute miracle: spend 3 minutes daily messaging people
9. Handle the lows
10. Take chances on people; care for mindset; be grateful

## Kevin O'Leary
- Cash flow is KING.
- 3 key success factors: confidence + why YOU solve the issue; summarize the business in <90 seconds; know your numbers (revenue, margin, TAM, market growth, traction).

## Glen Stearns
- Be the middle-man of conflict; breed understanding to cut red tape and unlock untapped opportunities.
- It's more painful NOT to act than to act imperfectly. Surround yourself with a great team.
- Many currencies exist: kindness, money, people.

## Mike Rowe
- Authenticity sells; production is the enemy of authenticity.
- "Don't follow your passion, always bring it with you" - followed opportunity instead (went opposite to the crowd); curiosity leads to opportunity.
- Practiced selling random objects to a mirror 8 minutes/day - built his QVC toolbox.
- Submissive humility wins information (testimonials); the greatest enemy of a good plan is a perfect plan. Adapt or die; pivot or perish.
