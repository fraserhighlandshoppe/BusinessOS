# 10X Mindset: Goals, Self-Work and Failure Patterns

Tags: #GrantCardone #mindset #goals

## Ideal Life Exercise
"If I can do anything, what would my life include?" - write it down.

## Most people quit goals within 72 hours
Four paths to reach them:
- **Insanity path**: bits and pieces here and there. Cost: time.
- **Old school path**: college degree. Cost: $40k+ debt; doesn't teach making money.
- **Baller path**: hire someone to teach you directly. Cost: $100k+.
- **10X path**: continuous direct training (CardoneU model).

## Becoming the Best Version of Yourself requires
Money, resources, people, time, team, honesty, goals. Money buys resources, gets people, builds the team, buys your time to be happy.

## Work on yourself daily
- Demand daily improvement through skills training, for you and your team.
- Once a target is set, INSIST on reaching it. If the target seems too high, raise your energy - never lower the target. Target attainment is the one thing that makes you happy.
- Bethenny Frankel: most people don't work hard and are entitled; just work hard and be better than the person next to you.
- Pick role models by where YOU are going and where they are in their cycle (Steve Jobs saying "money isn't important" was already-rich Jobs talking).

## Your biggest problem is you (Jesse Itzler / Sara Blakely)
- Crush your fear of embarrassment; self-imposed limitation is the #1 blocker.
- Experience is overrated - Itzler had none for any business he sold.
- Blakely: ask yourself "do you feel you deserve to be successful?" - the answer gauges how much you're holding yourself back.

## Why People Fail
Skeptical | pride and ego | know-it-all | won't go into debt | won't invest | negative environment | don't follow up | **going it alone - the #1 reason**.

## Pressure is a privilege
Jesse Itzler: put MORE on yourself, not less - pressure is a route to success in sales and life.
