# GC Wealth Principles and Secrets of the Wealthy

Tags: #GrantCardone #money #wealth

## Three Types of Wealth
Income | Monetary savings | Capital wealth (investments).
Get wealthy in 3 steps: **earn it, keep it, invest it**. Reframe: you don't "make" money (a job), you "get" money - who has it, where is it, what must I do to get it? $1M = 1M people giving $1 or 100k giving $10.

## Wealth Creation Principals
1. **40% revenue rule** - 40% of gross income gets invested
2. If you can't write it off, don't buy/invest in it
3. Use it, don't own it (rent); if you can't afford a 2-year lease payment, you can't afford it
4. Multiple income flows - a second flow connected to the first that needs no extra time
5. **40/7 rule** - for every 40 hours worked, invest 7 hours learning
6. Invest, don't save - saving makes banks rich; invest in tax write-offs and leverage ($1,000 cash → $4,000 leveraged asset)
7. Never lose money - invest in sure things
8. Buffett rule: don't lose money; buy cash flow; no startups; be an investor not a boss
9. Rule of 5 - audit who you spend time with
10. Go ALL in - Musk invests 100% in his own companies; diversification myth disproved

## Secrets of the Wealthy
Pay yourself first | live on 20% of income | don't lose money | cash-flowing assets only | more passive than earned income. Be patient - long term; immediate gratification = expense, not investment.

## Money Mindset
- A penny saved is a penny WASTED - money is only valuable when used.
- Passive income frees you from earned income (Buffett).
- Your current financial plan was designed by banks/Wall Street to transfer your wealth to them; Wall Street profits on transaction churn - buy what you never have to sell.

## Biggest Threat to Wealth: Disengagement
- Stay curious and engaged; disengagement means settling.
- Audit: your engagement level daily; the people around you (probably lower); whoever you take advice from.
- Greats figure out how to do MORE with their time (contrast the 4-hour-workweek mindset: if you can get rich on 4 hours, what could you do with 100?)
- People don't change behavior until the payoff is big enough.
- Context: 64% of small businesses have no employees; 67% lose money or break even.

## Invest In Only 3 Things, In This Order
1. Yourself - self improvement/learning/growth
2. Business
3. Other assets producing passive income / tax write-offs
