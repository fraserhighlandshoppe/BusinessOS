# Andy Tanner - Four Pillars of Stock Investing

Tags: #AndyTanner #investing #stocks

- Markets are more volatile now than historically; crashes have happened before and will happen again.
- Ideas don't make money - BUSINESSES make money (dot-com bust). Subprime "ninja" loans bundled into collateralized debt shows packaging ≠ value.
- People get irrationally exuberant, thinking prices go up forever.
- **Shiller P/E ratio** as gauge: mean 16.73 / median 16.12 / min 4.78 (Dec 1920) / max 44.19 (Dec 1999). When current P/E sits well above the mean (e.g., ~29), readjust holdings toward companies with real earnings and lower P/E - price can come crashing down fast.
- Context numbers: US GDP $18T; tax income $3.1T vs expense $3.7T = $600B deficit.
