## 1. Foundations

- Trading requires habits, not luck (`7 Habits of Highly Effective Traders.md`).
- Stock investing: focus on value, long-term holdings, systematic entry/exit (`Andy Tanner Stock Investing.md`).
- Real estate investing: business approach, not speculation; build a portfolio with clear exit strategies (`Don Campbell Real Estate Investing.md`, `Real Estate Investing Business.md`).

## 2. Key Frameworks

- Effective trader habits: discipline, risk management, continuous learning, emotional control, planning, review (`7 Habits of Highly Effective Traders.md`).
- Investment business: treat real estate as a business (systems, team, metrics) (`Real Estate Investing Business.md`).
- Wealth creation formula: compound + time + rate (`GC Wealth Creation Formula.md` — mindset master reference).
