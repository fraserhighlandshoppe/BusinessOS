# Don Campbell - Real Estate Investing Wisdom

Tags: #DonCampbell #realestate #investing

## Don't Jump In - Do It Right or Don't Bother
Real estate is a long-term, repeatable wealth strategy, but first-time investors must recognize the obstacles before plunging: **you're becoming a landlord** (business obligations, tenants, systems).

## The 5 Do's and 6 Don'ts of Successful Investors
- Obsessing over NOT making mistakes prevents the actions that create success — like focusing only on expenses and never raising rents.
- All investors make mistakes; the job is mitigating impact while moving forward strategically.
- Keep a **mistake log** for two reasons: 1) SYSTEMS - build checklists so mistakes aren't repeated (systems reduce risk); 2) MEASUREMENT - if you aren't adding mistakes to the log, you aren't taking enough action.

## Classified Ads → Big Profits
Quality of small ads can make or break a real estate business: better tenants who pay your mortgage, higher rent, more money when selling. Four critical points to writing them (see raw page).

## Strategic vs Gambler Mindset
- Non-strategic investors roll dice and hope. Strategic investors see elements others don't, opportunities where others can't, dangers where others see blue skies - and adjust their system per changing element.
- Being strategic dramatically reduces chance of landing on the financial snake. Some "investors" are really gamblers chasing endorphin hits instead of sustainable wealth.

## You Are the CEO of Your Financial Future
- Financial independence = assets/income for freedom to do what/when/how you want.
- Accelerate it by taking the LEADERSHIP position: run your life like a profitable business.
- Be responsible for ALL wins and losses. **Delegate but do not abdicate.**
