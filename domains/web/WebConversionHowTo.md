## 1. Foundations

- Average site converts 5-10% of visitors (Levis 5R). Funnel: Ads → Landing page → Email capture → Nurture → Offer.
- Every landing page has ONE job: opt-in or purchase (`Landing Page Checklist.md`, `Checkout Page Essentials.md`).

## 2. Landing Pages & Checkout

- Landing Page Checklist (`domains/web/Landing Page Checklist.md`): headline (formula from swipe/headline_formulas.md), subheadline, proof, single CTA, no navigation, mobile-optimized.
- Checkout (`Checkout Page Essentials.md`): trust badges, single-page checkout, clear pricing, guarantee, urgency/scarcity if appropriate.
- A/B test 2-5 headline variants (`swipe/headline_formulas.md` rules).
