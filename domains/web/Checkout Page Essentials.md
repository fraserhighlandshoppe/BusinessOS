# Checkout Page Essentials

Tags: #web #conversion #ecommerce

Elements every checkout page should have built in:
- Company logo - visitors know who's behind the product
- Contact / customer-support info - a real business exists
- Customer testimonials - the product helped others
- Sales video spot - see the product in action
- Guarantee section - no-risk purchase
- Product image + short description of why to buy
- Benefit bullet points - personal benefit after purchase
- Credit card AND PayPal options - pay how they want
- Trust-seal graphics - page is secure
