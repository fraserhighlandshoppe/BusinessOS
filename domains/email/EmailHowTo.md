## 1b. Source Incorporation: Email Foundations (Every Source Checked)

Every claim links to a source `.md` file. Where sources agree, a common paragraph links to all. Conflicts noted.

---
**Common paragraph — Email as owned channel + deliverability (DigitalMarketer + Pagan agree)**
Email = owned channel. Deliverability depends on engagement signals (open, reply, click, save, not-spam) + technical setup (SPF/DKIM/DMARC/TLS — `DigitalMarketer Email Playbook.md`; `Pagan Psychology of Conversion.md` / `Marketing_Mindsets.md`). List hygiene (suppress cold non-responders; segment by behavior) required by both (`DigitalMarketer Segments.md`; `Pagan Marketing Mindsets.md` — marketing as system, not ping).

---
**Source: DigitalMarketer — Email Framework (`knowledge_base/gurus/DigitalMarketer/Content/` + framework docs)**
- `101 Best Email Subject Lines.md`: self-interest = default; curiosity = when benefit unbelievable; urgency/scarcity when appropriate.
- `Email Marketing Playbook.md`: DMARC/SPF/DKIM/TLS required; engineer engagement signals.
- `Welcome Series.md`: first touch defines expectations; personalized greeting; deliver value immediately; never apologize.
- `Segments.md`: behavior grouping (engaged, buyer, VIP, cold); frequency/message differ.
- `Automation.md`: trigger flows mapped to funnel stage.
- `Amplify.md`: consistent cadence; single focus; mix story + offer.

---
**Source: Eben Pagan — Email / Marketing Framework (`knowledge_base/gurus/Eben Pagan/Email/Eben_Pagan_Email_Framework.md`; synthesized from 118 source docs)**
- Marketing begins with understanding customer needs; email must trigger ACTION (`Marketing_Mindsets.md`).
- Email nurture = contextually relevant automated follow-up; never generic ping (`Marketing_Mindsets.md` — process). Value creation = translate value, connect dots (`Marketing_Mindsets.md`).

---
**Source: Grant Cardone — Follow-Up / Omnipresence (`knowledge_base/gurus/Grant Cardone/`)**
- Follow-up checklist (`Simple Email Trick.md`): dream client defined; specific subject; personalized greeting; never apologize; do homework.
- Omnipresence includes email (`Unbreakable.md`); frequency matters (`Cold Prospecting.md`).

---
**Source: Joanna Lindenbaum — Referral in Email (`knowledge_base/gurus/Joanna Lindenbaum/Email/Joanna_Lindenbaum_Referral_Shame_Piece.md`)**
- Referral mechanics = delivery + ask (words, energy, timing, continuity). Use quarterly "Referral Events" in nurture sequences; separate self-worth from referral volume.

---
**Source: FHS Internal — SOP References (`domains/email/` + `domains/operations/sop/`)**
- `Welcome Series.md`, `Followup.md`, `Cold Email.md`, `Newsletter Strategy.md`, `Automation.md`, `Amplify.md`, `Segments.md` mapped above.

---
**Conflict / Agreement Note**
- Agreement: personalize; never apologize; deliver value before ask; measure; segment by behavior.
- DigitalMarketer: structured, metric-driven, automated flows.
- Pagan: process-first, audience-first, ethical appeal.
- Cardone: high-frequency, aggressive follow-up, omnipresence.
- Lindenbaum: referral ask mechanics integrated; self-worth separate from results.
