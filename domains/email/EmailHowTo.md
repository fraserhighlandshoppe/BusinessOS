## 1. Foundations

- Email = owned channel; deliverability depends on engagement signals (open, reply, click, save, not-spam) plus technical setup (SPF/DKIM/DMARC/TLS).
- Contextually relevant automated follow-up, NOT generic "ready to buy yet?" pings.
- Define the dream client before writing any sequence; every email must push Gain, Logic, Fear, or Scarcity.

## 2. Deliverability & Setup

- Technical: DMARC, SPF, DKIM, TLS configured (`Email Marketing Playbook.md`).
- Engineering: engineer engagement signals (open rate targets, reply prompts, segmentation by behavior).
- List hygiene: segments by engagement; suppress cold non-responders after defined window.

## 3. Sequences & Automation

- Welcome series (`Welcome Series.md`): first touch defines expectations; no apology, personalized greeting, deliver value immediately.
- Follow-up (`Email Followup.md`, `The Simple Email Trick That Makes Following Up Effective.md`): specific subject line, homework done, never apologize.
- Cold email (`Cold Email Marketing.md`): shorter, highly personalized, single clear ask, no attachments.
- Automation (`Automation.md`): trigger-based flows (signup, purchase, inactivity) mapped to the funnel stage (`Lead Generation and Conversion Funnel.md`).

## 4. Strategy & Segments

- Newsletter (`Newsletter Strategy.md`, `Amplify Your Email Marketing.md`): consistent cadence, single focus per issue, mix of story + offer.
- Segments (`Segments.md`): behavior-based grouping (engaged, buyer, cold, VIP); messaging and frequency differ per segment.
- Email types: awareness, promotion, list-building, collaborative, testimonial (`Types of Marketing.md` — marketing master reference).
