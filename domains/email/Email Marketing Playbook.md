# Email Marketing Playbook

Tags: #marketing #email

## Subject Line Types (DigitalMarketer, 101 Best Subject Lines)
- **Self-interest** (bread & butter - use most): direct benefit to the reader; pre-qualifies openers
- **Curiosity**: works for the opposite reason - withholds the benefit
- Others in the full list: specificity, urgency, etc. (see raw page + `gurus/DigitalMarketer` 101-email file)

## 4 Emotional Triggers That Increase CTR
Humans are hard-wired to respond to: **Gain** (what do I get?), **Logic**, **Fear**, **Scarcity**. Every email should push at least one.

## Follow-Up Execution Checklist
1. Define your dream client
2. Know your follow-up goal (desired outcome for you AND client)
3. Opt-in incentives - give the dream client what she wants/needs
4. Map your funnel
5. Balance information vs offers
6. Write clickworthy subject lines: not spammy, not generic, answer "so what", be specific ("123 XYZ did abc")
7. Personalized greeting; write as if speaking in person (one side of a conversation)
8. Never apologize in the email - it signals low confidence
9. Do your homework - never "I am under the impression that..." or "if this reached the wrong person..."

## Deliverability Checklist
- Shared IP OK under ~50k emails/month
- Mandatory authentication: DMARC, SPF, DKIM, TLS
- Big webmail providers score senders on interaction: reads, clicks, replies, moves out of spam, address-book adds, forwards, scrolls — engineer for positive signals

## Cold Email
99% of cold emails get deleted unread. Common killers: vague subject ("Special Offer"), generic greeting, me-first framing. See raw page critique examples.
