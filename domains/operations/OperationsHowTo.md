# Operations: Here's How To Run It

*Master guide for Fraser Highland Shoppe operations and internal SOP references.*
*Most content points to FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md; this guide organizes the reference points.*

Tags: #operations #operationsmaster #sop #fhsinternal #howto #masterguide

---

## 1. Foundations

- Operations = the system that delivers the brand promise. Every process should be documented in the FHS wiki (`FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md`) and referenced in SOP pages (`knowledge_base/wiki/`).
- Key domains for SOPs: marketing funnel, sales process, customer onboarding, support, finance, team roles (`Business Operations Essentials.md`).

## 2. SOP Reference Structure

- `FHS-INTERNAL-INDEX.md` indexes all internal SOP/reference pages without paraphrasing sensitive content.
- Domain master guides (`MarketingHowTo.md`, `SalesHowTo.md`, `BusinessHowTo.md`, etc.) reference SOPs for implementation details.
- Every SOP page should have: owner, last updated, inputs/outputs, metrics, reference to source guru/file.

## 3. Key Operations Areas

- Funnel execution: landing pages, checkout, delivery (`WebConversionHowTo.md`, `Checkout Page Essentials.md`).
- Team and leadership: roles, accountability (`Leadership and High Performance.md`, `Grant Cardone How to be a Leader.md`).
- Marketing operations: CashFlow Calendar (`Troy White CashFlow Calendar.md`), promotions (`MarketingHowTo.md`).
- Customer management: CRM (`Grant Cardone CRM System.md`), lead scoring, handoffs.
