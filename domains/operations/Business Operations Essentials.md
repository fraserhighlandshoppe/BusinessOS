# Business Operations Essentials

Tags: #operations #finance

## Budgeting
A budget = a plan in monetary terms: money in/out over the year. Purpose: prioritize objectives and reach financial goals.

## Cash Control
Cash (currency, coins, bank/chequing accounts) is the most liquid asset — and therefore the most vulnerable. Account for all spending; internal controls protect it (see raw page for control procedures).

## Revenue Recognition
- Time & materials contracts: simple — recognize revenue as billed, monthly.
- Fixed-price with milestones: costs flow monthly but income arrives only on milestone delivery → plan working capital accordingly.

## Cybersecurity
Executives set the tone via policies; commit to safeguarding customer data. Good cyber hygiene reduces weaknesses and risk.

## IT / Infrastructure Must-Dos
- Network checklist: documentation policy, topology map, server names/roles/IPs, change log per server (CMDB), software versions + license proofs, hardware inventory, AD documentation, backup strategy
- Use own domain email: big companies won't do big deals with gmail/hotmail primaries (~40% of advisors do)
- GIT for backup/restore of configs and code (ksfraser uses GitHub; credentials in KeePass)

## Professionalism & Accountability
Accept personal accountability for work and furthering business goals. Good intentions without follow-through → low morale and more errors.

## Work-Life Balance
Per Stewart/Corcoran/Beers: "balance" is largely fiction for entrepreneurs — admit it and design your life accordingly instead.
